## Long Description
*Yomi: The Land of the Dead* is Mytholore's first album of Japanese mythology, based on the *Kojiki* (712 CE), Japan's oldest surviving chronicle. Over ten tracks, Izanagi and Izanami stir the islands out of the sea, and lose their first child to the waves. Then Izanami dies giving birth to fire, and Izanagi descends into Yomi to bring her back. She asks him only one thing: *do not look*. He looks. What follows is a chase through the underworld, a boulder rolled across the pass between the worlds, and a final vow on either side of the stone, which became the law of life and death: *a thousand die each day, and fifteen hundred are born.* The album ends in purification and light, with the birth of the sun, the moon and the storm.

It is a record of creation, grief and forbidden looking, carried on taiko, koto, shakuhachi and biwa. It moves from a reverent opening through galloping battle and gothic horror to a triumphant symphonic finale.
