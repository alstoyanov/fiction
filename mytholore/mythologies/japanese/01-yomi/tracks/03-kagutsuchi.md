## 1. Story Behind the Song
After the islands, Izanami gave birth to the gods of the world: the gods of the sea and river mouths, of wind, of trees, of mountains and moors, of the boat of Heaven, and of food. Last of all she bore **Kagutsuchi**, also called Hi-no-Yagihayao, "the swift burning male": the god of **fire**. His birth **burned her**, and she lay down sick. Even from her sickness gods were born: from her vomit, the gods of metal; from her waste, the gods of clay; from her urine, the gods of water and of growth. Then Izanami **"divinely retired"**, the *Kojiki*'s phrase for the death of a god. She was the first being in the world to die.

Izanagi crawled around her head and around her feet, weeping. *"Oh, my beloved younger sister,"* he cried, *"that I should have given you in exchange for a single child!"* From his tears was born a goddess, Nakisawame, "the weeping marsh woman". He buried Izanami on **Mount Hiba**, on the border between the lands of Izumo and Hōki.

The song is the album's turn from creation into catastrophe. The mother of everything dies giving birth to the thing that will warm and destroy the world. Fire enters the world, and so does death. (*Kojiki*, Book 1, Chamberlain §§VI–VII. The *Nihon Shoki* gives variant versions of her death.)
