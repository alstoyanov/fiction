## 1. Story Behind the Song
In the beginning, says the *Kojiki*, Heaven and Earth had only just come apart. Below the High Plain of Heaven there was no land, only a formless something drifting "like oil floating on water, like a jellyfish". Gods came into being in Heaven one after another, alone at first and then in pairs. The seventh generation were a brother and sister: **Izanagi**, "he who invites", and **Izanami**, "she who invites". The heavenly gods gave them a single command, *make, consolidate and give birth to this drifting land*, and a single tool, the heavenly jewelled spear **Ame-no-nuboko**.

The two stood on the **Floating Bridge of Heaven** (*Ame-no-Ukihashi*) and pushed the spear down into the brine below. They stirred it, and the *Kojiki* records the sound it made as it thickened: ***koworo, koworo***. When they drew the spear back up, the brine that dripped from its point piled up and hardened into the first land, the island **Onogoro**, "self-curdling".

Nothing in the world yet had a name, a shape or an end. The song is that moment: the whole of creation depending on two gods, a spear and a sea, before anything had gone wrong. It is the calm before the album's fall. (*Kojiki*, Book 1, Chamberlain §§I–III.)
