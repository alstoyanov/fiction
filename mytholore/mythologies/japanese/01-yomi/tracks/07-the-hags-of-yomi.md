## 1. Story Behind the Song
Shamed and furious, Izanami sent the **Yomotsu-shikome**, the "ugly women of Yomi", after her fleeing husband. As they closed in, Izanagi pulled the **black vine headdress** from his hair and threw it behind him. It sprouted at once into **wild grapes**. The hags stopped to gorge on them, and he ran on. When they caught up again, he broke the comb from the **right bunch of his hair** and threw it down, and it became **bamboo shoots**. Again they stopped to pull them up and eat.

Then Izanami sent something worse: the **eight thunder gods** from her body, leading **fifteen hundred warriors of Yomi**. Izanagi drew his ten-hand-length sword and ran, **swinging it behind him** as he went. That is a strange, backward gesture, and scholars have long read it as a charm against the dead. He fled all the way to the foot of **Yomotsu Hirasaka**, the pass between the worlds.

The song is the album's chase and its fastest track: a god running uphill out of death, throwing away pieces of himself to buy a few more steps. (*Kojiki*, Book 1, Chamberlain §IX.)
