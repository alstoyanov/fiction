## Concept
The first story in Japanese myth, told as a descent. Izanagi and Izanami, the first gods to take shape, stir the islands out of the sea. Then fire is born and kills the mother. Her husband follows her into **Yomi**, the land of the dead. He breaks the one thing she asked of him, *do not look*, and sees what death has made of her. He flees with the dead at his back, and seals the road between the worlds with a stone. On either side of the stone they speak their last vow, and it becomes the law of life and death: *a thousand die each day, and fifteen hundred are born.* The album ends with his purification, and the birth of the sun, the moon and the storm, the gods of every story to come.

**Point of view:** mostly **Izanagi**, as narrator and grieving husband. **Izanami** sings in her own voice at three moments: the wrong words at the pillar (track 2), the plea from the doors of Yomi (track 6), and the curse at the boulder (track 8).

## Story Arc
1. **Creation (tracks 1–2):** the spear in the brine, the first island, the first marriage, and the first mistake.
2. **Catastrophe (tracks 3–4):** fire is born, the mother burns, and the father kills the child.
3. **Descent and horror (tracks 5–7):** the road into Yomi, the forbidden light, and the chase.
4. **Severance (track 8):** the boulder and the vow of the dead.
5. **Rebirth (tracks 9–10):** washing away death, and three noble children born from it.

## Sound
- **Base style:** the Japanese base prompt, pulled toward doom and death metal for the underworld, with a symphonic lift at the end:
  ```
  symphonic doom metal, melodic death metal, taiko drums, shakuhachi, koto, biwa,
  harsh male vocals, ethereal female vocals, ritual chanted choir, cinematic, dark, funereal
  ```
- **The shape of the album:** the sound darkens and slows from track 1 to track 8, then breaks open into light. Track 5 is the slowest and heaviest. Track 7 is the fastest. Track 10 is the only major-key, triumphant track.
- **Recurring motif:**
  - **Chant:** *"koworo, koworo"*, the sound the *Kojiki* gives for the spear stirring the brine. It opens track 1, returns under track 8 as the stone grinds shut, and closes track 10 as the gods are born.
  - **Phrase:** *"Do not look"*, sung by Izanami in tracks 6 and 8, and echoed by Izanagi in track 9.
