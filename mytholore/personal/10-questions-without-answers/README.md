## Concept
Humanity has spent centuries searching for answers to the biggest questions in existence: God, truth, purpose, justice, and what happens after death. But what if some questions are beyond our understanding? What if the search itself is the closest thing to an answer we'll ever find?

The album follows one man, the narrator, from one question to the next. When he was six, the power went out, and his father struck a match in the dark kitchen. The boy asked who lit the first one. His father laughed, shook it out and said *"ask me when you're older."* Now he is older, and he's asking everything. He sends prayers that get left on read, and gets a different version of the truth from every witness. He sits beside his father's hospital bed as the one man who could tell him dies without a word. He shouts at an indifferent sky, distrusts the saints and his own brain, measures himself against a universe that's ninety-five percent dark, and then watches every certainty he ever had collapse.

What's left after that is silence, a prayer to a room that may or may not be empty, and a hallway of doors that stay locked. The album ends on a roof during a power cut, with his own daughter asking him who lit the first match. This time he doesn't say *"ask me when you're older."* He says *"I don't know,"* strikes a match, and lets it burn out. In the dark there are twice as many stars.

The album never pretends to solve the mysteries it raises. Its final message is that **not knowing everything doesn't mean we can't live meaningfully.**

## Sound
- **Reference artist:** none. The sound is described from its parts, not modelled on anyone.
- **The room:** it should feel enormous, as if the listener were standing inside a cathedral at the edge of the universe.
- **Base style:**
  ```
  philosophical orchestral rap, epic cinematic strings, ominous brass, deep orchestral percussion, dramatic
  timpani, haunting piano, huge layered male and female choir, wordless choral textures, cosmic ambience,
  distant echoes, unsettling silences, intricate internal rhymes, rapid-fire male rap, vast cathedral reverb
  ```
- **Exclude styles (album default):** `pop, EDM, autotune, country, lo-fi, mumble rap, rock, distorted guitars, metal, growls, screamed vocals`
  - **No guitars and no trap production.** The weight comes from timpani, low brass, orchestral percussion and the choir.
  - **Shouted emotional outbursts are allowed** on tracks 2 and 9. Screams and growls stay excluded everywhere.
- **Vocals:** one male lead throughout: whispered, spoken, rapped, shouted and sung. The **choir** (male and female) is the voice of the universe. It is mostly wordless, it doubles the big choruses, and it never answers him in words.
- **Tempo:** a rap pocket of 86–98 bpm. **Track 9** is the fastest and most technically complex track (104 bpm, double-time and tempo shifts). **Tracks 4, 10 and 12** are slow (68–76 bpm).
- **Track 9 vs track 12:** track 9 is the most aggressive and complex song on the album; track 12 is almost its opposite: restrained, haunting and spacious.
- **The finale:** across track 12 the orchestra is stripped away until only a quiet piano, a distant choir and cosmic ambience remain.
- **Recurring motifs:**
  - **Sound:** the **struck match** (track 1 intro) returns blown out in track 4 and burns down to his fingers in track 12.
  - **Sound:** the **wordless choir chord** that opens track 1 closes track 12, still unresolved.
  - **Phrase:** *"Ask me when you're older"* (track 1) becomes *"I'm older now, Dad. I'm asking"* (tracks 1 and 4), and finally *"I don't know, kid"* (track 12).
  - **Image:** the **match and the dark**: every answer we light only shows more dark behind it (track 1). In track 12 he lets the match go out and sees more stars.
  - **Image:** **left on read**: the unanswered message (track 2), "I've left you on read too" (track 10).
  - **Image:** **doors and windows**: the locked doors of track 11, and the hallway that had windows the whole time.
  - **Callbacks:** track 9's intro is a collage of lines from tracks 1–8, and track 12 mirrors track 1 scene for scene: the power cut, the matchbox, the question.
