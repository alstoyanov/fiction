# 09. What If We're All Wrong?

**Album:** Questions Without Answers · **Type:** Personal · **Status:** Writing

---

## 1. Meaning Behind the Song
The album's most intense, aggressive and technically complex track: a psychological breakdown of certainty. It opens on a dissonant choir cluster and a collage of everything the narrator has been told so far (*"Ask me when you're older." "He always hears you." "Trust the facts." "He's home now." "I'm sure I chose."*) until he shouts the question the album has been circling: *what if we're all wrong?* The first verse is history's graveyard of certainties. They were sure the world was flat and never moved, that the sun went round us, that bleeding cured you, that the ether filled the dark, that the atom couldn't break. Every age before us was as sure as we are, with its textbooks, its temples and its maps of every star. *So what makes us so special that we've finally found the song?*

The second verse shifts into double time and turns the doubt on everyone equally: the believer and the doubter, the preacher and the louder atheist, the left, the right and the centre on the fence, his father, his teacher, the expert on TV, and himself. Every "obviously", every "clearly", every "everybody knows that". What if "good" is just a habit, "real" just a mood, and "me" just a story a billion neurons brewed? *What if we're all just babies pointing at the moon and calling it the sky?* The chorus deliberately pairs faith and reason: *every prophet, every proof, every temple, every lab.* The song spares nobody, and mocks nobody either.

The bridge strips everything away: *then what's left?*, repeated faster and faster until there's silence, and then one word: *breathing.* In verse 3 he empties every shelf of theory and scripture and spins in the rubble with his hands over his ears. The floor, the walls and the ceiling are gone, and he floats in nothing with a thousand thousand whys. He screams at the cosmos: *then what do I keep, and what do I forsake?* The song doesn't answer. The outro collapses to a whisper with the album's most unsettling and even-handed line: *what if we're all wrong... what if we're not?* That's the bottom of the album, and track 10 picks him up in the silence after it.
