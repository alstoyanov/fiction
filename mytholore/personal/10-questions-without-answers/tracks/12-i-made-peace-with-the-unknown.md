# 12. I Made Peace with the Unknown

**Album:** Questions Without Answers · **Type:** Personal · **Status:** Writing

---

## 1. Meaning Behind the Song
The finale, and almost the opposite of track 9: restrained, haunting and spacious. There's no grand revelation, no final explanation and no conclusion. The album's first scene comes back, one generation on. The narrator lies on the roof of his house on a blanket that was his father's, during another power cut, while his six-year-old daughter counts the stars, loses count and gets cross. Then she turns to him, as serious as only six-year-olds can be, and asks: *"Dad, who made all of it? And who made who?"* He feels the old pilot light from track 1, thirty years after a kitchen and a struck match. He has his father's matches in his coat (Grandpa's, to her), and strikes one. She asks who lit the first one, and he nearly gives her his father's line, *"ask me when you're older"*. *But I don't. Not this time.*

The second verse is what he tells her instead: *"I don't know, kid,"* the best place a good story can begin. Some people say God, some say it began with a bang, some say both, and some say nobody really knows; her dad is with that last group, but he never stopped looking up. He tells her you don't have to solve it all to live it well. There are still pancakes on a Sunday, still music, still rain, still somebody to hold, still meaning in the asking. *A mystery's not a hole, kid, not a failure, not a flaw; it's the reason that we look up, it's the reason for the awe.*

As the song goes on, the orchestra is stripped away. In the bridge, with only piano and a distant choir left, the match burns down to his fingers and he lets it go out. *In the dark, there were twice as many stars.* The answer he wanted all his life was a match, and putting it out lets him see further. Verse 3 is nearly whispered. He still doesn't know who lit the first match, where his father went, whether anyone hears when we call, what's true or right, or whether his mind is lying. But he knows he's here, she's here, and there's starlight in her eyes. *I made peace with the unknown, and the unknown feels like home.* She falls asleep. *I don't know. And that's okay.* The wordless choir chord from track 1 returns, still unresolved, and the album ends in silence.
