# Questions Without Answers: Album Cover Art

This file covers the **album** cover and its variants. Each song's art prompt is in its track file (§4). Every track prompt shares the cover's style line, so the set looks like one family.

**Concept:** a lone man standing in the nave of a vast gothic cathedral whose vaulted ceiling dissolves into the open universe. He holds up a single lit match, the only warm light in an ocean of stars.
**Visual palette:**
- **Colours:** deep cosmic indigo and black, with silver starlight, and **one accent: the warm amber of a single flame** (a match, a candle, a lamp, a lantern). Track 12 is the only image with no warm light at all: the match is out, and only the stars remain.
- **Motifs:** the match, the cathedral that opens into space, doors and windows, the shoreline, the stars. The figure is the same man in every track image (a boy in track 1, a father in track 12).
- **Style:** epic cinematic concept art, photorealistic, with vast scale, volumetric light and film grain.
- **Avoid:** text, logos, crosses and any specific religious symbols or figures (the cathedral is architecture, not a church of any one faith), angels, demons, skulls and gore. The album asks questions; the art shouldn't answer them.

**Final image:** `<file name>`. At least 3000×3000 px.

**Shared style line** (identical in every prompt for this album):
```
epic cinematic concept art, photorealistic, deep cosmic indigo and black palette with silver starlight and a
single warm amber flame, vast scale, volumetric light, film grain, high contrast, awe-inspiring, highly
detailed, square format, no text, no lettering
```
