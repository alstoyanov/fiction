## Long Description
*Questions Without Answers* is a twelve-track concept album that begins with a child in a dark kitchen asking his father who lit the first match. His father says *"ask me when you're older."* The boy grows up and goes looking:
- prayers that heaven leaves on read;
- witnesses who each have their own version of the truth;
- a father who dies without explaining where he's going;
- a universe that doesn't know his name;
- saints who were sure they were right;
- a brain that edits his memories;
- an island of knowledge in an ocean of the dark;
- and the collapse of every certainty he ever held.

What's left is a prayer to a room that may or may not be empty, and a hallway of doors that stay locked. And then a roof during a power cut, where his own daughter asks him the same question, and he finally gives the honest answer: *"I don't know."*

Rap verses carry the search and huge choral choruses carry the feeling. Massive strings, ominous brass, timpani, haunting piano and layered male and female choirs build a cathedral at the edge of the universe, from cosmic wonder through grief and defiance to a shattering collapse in track 9. Then they strip away until only a quiet piano, a distant choir and the stars remain.

It never pretends to solve the mysteries it raises. It only insists that not knowing everything doesn't mean we can't live meaningfully.
