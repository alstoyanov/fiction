# Masks and Mirrors: Album Cover Art

This file covers the **album** cover and its variants. Each song's art prompt is in its track file (§4). Every track prompt shares the cover's style line, so the set looks like one family.

**Concept:** a man in a dark suit in a baroque hall of mirrors, seen from behind, holding a cracked white porcelain mask at his side. In every mirror his reflection wears a different mask; only the nearest reflection shows his bare face.
**Tool:** Deep Dream Generator (deepdreamgenerator.com)
**Visual palette:**
- **Colours:** ink black and deep velvet crimson (the theatre), with **two accents: porcelain white** (the mask) and **tarnished gold** (the frames, the balconies, the bars). Track 4 and track 10 add a cold blue night light. Track 12 is the only image where grey daylight takes over.
- **Motifs:** the porcelain mask and its crack, mirrors and gilded frames, the spotlight, the theatre balconies, the window. The figure is the same man in a dark suit in every track image.
- **Style:** dark cinematic baroque concept art, photorealistic, with chiaroscuro lighting and film grain.
- **Avoid:** text, logos, skulls, gore, horror-film masks, clowns, demonic or occult imagery, and religious symbols. The album is about identity, not horror; the masks should be beautiful and unsettling, never monstrous.

**Final image:** `<file name>`. At least 3000×3000 px.

**Shared style line** (identical in every prompt for this album):
```
dark cinematic baroque concept art, photorealistic, ink black and deep velvet crimson palette with
porcelain white and tarnished gold accents, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, film grain, high contrast,
theatrical, highly detailed, square format, no text, no lettering
```
