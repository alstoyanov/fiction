## Long Description
*Masks and Mirrors* is a twelve-track concept album staged as a show that never ends. A man plays the lead in his own life, in a baroque theatre where every seat is full and everybody loves the mask. Track by track, the performance comes apart:
- the stranger in the dressing-room mirror;
- the perfect lie he tells so well that he believes it too;
- the man in the kitchen at three in the morning, when nobody's watching;
- the prison built out of compliments;
- the hypocrite with two faces;
- the critics who live in his head;
- the gold frame on an empty wall;
- a hall of mirrors where he can no longer tell which one is him.

Then he lies awake beside the one person he's most afraid to be known by, and starts to talk. On the same stage, in front of the same crowd, he takes the mask off. Some of them leave. He lets them.

Rap verses carry the argument, and huge sung choruses carry the feeling. Sinister harpsichord, staccato strings, eerie flutes, deep 808s and orchestral impacts move from a full house and a baroque minuet, through anxiety, hypocrisy and a shattering breakdown, to an empty stage, an ordinary grey morning, and the same minuet played once on a plain piano.

It doesn't pretend the face under the mask is perfect. It only insists that a face that's really yours is better than one nobody has ever met.
