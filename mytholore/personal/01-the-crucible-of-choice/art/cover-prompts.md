# The Crucible of Choice: Album Cover Art

This file covers the **album** cover and its variants. Each song's art prompt is in its track file (§4). Every track prompt shares the cover's style line, so the set looks like one family.

**Concept:** a lone hooded figure in a vast dark foundry, standing before a giant crucible of molten metal. In his hand is a glowing key, freshly forged from the bars of a broken cage that lie melting in the crucible.
**Visual palette:**
- **Colours:** charcoal black and ash grey, with **one accent: molten orange-gold**, the colour of metal in the crucible. Track 9 is the only image where the orange takes over.
- **Motifs:** the cage, the key, the pen and the page, fire and ash, iron becoming a blade. The figure is the same man in a dark hooded coat in every track image.
- **Style:** cinematic dark concept art, photorealistic, with volumetric light and film grain.
- **Avoid:** text, logos, skulls, gore, demonic or occult imagery, and religious symbols. The album is about choice, not horror.

**Final image:** `<file name>`. At least 3000×3000 px.

**Shared style line** (identical in every prompt for this album):
```
cinematic dark concept art, photorealistic, charcoal black and ash grey palette with molten orange
light, volumetric light, film grain, high contrast, dramatic, highly detailed, square format, no text,
no lettering
```
