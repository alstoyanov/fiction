## Long Description
*The Crucible of Choice* is a ten-track concept album that takes place over one night of reckoning. A man wakes in a cage and discovers he is its architect. Track by track, he throws each of his excuses into the fire:
- the hands that shaped him as a child;
- the warm, deadly comfort of being the victim;
- the paralysis of a crossroads where every road burns;
- the doubt that his will is even free;
- the life script handed to him at birth;
- the chaos he can never control.

Then he stands trial before the only judge who knows the whole story, his own future self. He walks out with the pen in his hand, and finds himself facing a blank page.

Rap verses carry the argument and metal choruses carry the feeling. Dark piano, orchestral strings, downtuned guitars and hard-hitting drums move from a whispered prologue, through industrial fury and a screamed climax, to a final spoken word and a single piano chord.

It doesn't pretend that what was done to us isn't real. It only insists that what we do with it is ours.
