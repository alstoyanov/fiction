# Chapter 2: No Storm Will Turn Them

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Mon Feb 17, 2110 (before dawn to night)
**Setting:** The inland road from the landing beaches toward the capital: the marsh road behind the gap, the ridge where Batteries 7 and 8 are dug in, a river crossing at a small mill town, and the company's position in a flooded railway cutting. Cold rain (real weather).
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
This is the first inland battle. The old weapons fail, and then they are turned round. Dmitri, a former Border Security agent, is attacked with the kind of weapon he used to carry. He also notices something about the enemy's route that he can't explain, and files it.

## Beats
- **Dmitri's watch log.** *Feb 17. 05:30. Company strength: ninety-four. Position: the cutting below the mill town.* He commands a **volunteer company** under Sokolov's coastal command: former Border Security men, lakeshore people, a schoolteacher, two ferrymen. He counts them the way he counts everything.
- **His lieutenant,** a former teacher from the lakeshore, unnamed on the page, carries a pack with a paper journal in it. He writes in it every night, which Dmitri respects and doesn't say.
- **Morning, Veridica's weapons.** Border Security brings two transmitter units up behind the river crossing and runs them into the SFW's line of advance. Dmitri has crewed units like these. It changes nothing. The SFW infantry come on at a steady walk, and no one among them looks at a road that isn't there. *No interfaces.* He writes: *Effect on enemy: none observed.*
- **The route.** The SFW columns don't take the marsh road, the one the ridge batteries cover. They come by the drainage tracks and the old orchard lanes, which keep them out of the arcs of **Batteries 7 and 8**. Those batteries are dug in, masked, and haven't fired once since the landing. The route bends round both arcs exactly. Dmitri notes it formally and doesn't draw a conclusion: *Enemy avoided ridge batteries 7 and 8. Batteries had not fired. Positions not visible from the coast.* He files it where he files things he will need later.
- **Afternoon, the turn.** At 14:00 exactly, with no build and no warning, a fact storm comes up over the cutting at full strength, like a switch being thrown. It is a manufactured storm and Dmitri knows it at once: it arrives all at once and follows no fact log. Then he knows the programme itself, a false history of the coast-road district from the Border Security test files, which he ran himself in another life. **The SFW is running two captured Border Security units** on Veridican troops (the two lost behind Battery 5 on Feb 12, Prologue).
- **What his soldiers know.** The cutting is also a road in one history: a paved road running level out of the flooded ground toward the town. Men who have interfaces *know* it is there. Two stand up to walk out along it. Keep it perceptual. The water and the embankment don't change; only what they know about them does.
- **Dmitri's anchor.** Without his brothers, he does what the bond does. He orders the company flat, hands on the ground ("The ground is real. Put your hand on it."), and has them call their names down the line in order, in rhythm, over and over. It is dull, procedural and shared, and the storm can't make it warm. It holds most of them.
- **The lieutenant** doesn't hear it. He goes out along the road that isn't there and into the open, and SFW fire kills him on the embankment. It is sudden and unshowy. One more of the company drowns in the flooded cutting, walking on a surface that exists only in one history.
- **The cost.** The company holds the crossing until dark and then withdraws on orders. **Nine dead, fourteen wounded.** The SFW take the mill town. Dmitri logs it all flatly: the time the storm began (14:00:00), and its end, at 14:41, when the SFW switched the units off to advance.
- **Night.** Dmitri writes the lieutenant's name and three lines, formally, for the farmhouse: *He was a teacher. He wrote every night. He went out onto a road that was not there.* It goes west with the next courier, addressed to Old Songs.

## Ending
**Night, a barn behind the new line.** Dmitri sits with the dead lieutenant's pack across his knees. The journal is inside, and he doesn't open it. He speaks to it formally, without a single contraction: ***"We have fought with facts for forty years. We have forgotten what they cost."***

## Notes
- **(Twist 1 clue)** The SFW knew which batteries to avoid (plot v2 Ch 2). Keep it to one logged observation. Dmitri doesn't think of Sokolov's plan and doesn't know it exists in detail.
- **Batteries 7 and 8** are invented here, and binding for Ch 9: the two ridge batteries covering the marsh road behind the gap between Batteries 4 and 5 (Prologue).
- **The captured units** are the two overrun on Feb 12 (Prologue, 05:51). SFW technicians run the units' **preloaded programmes**. Nobody explains how they learned the controls, and Nora Chen isn't implicated.
- **Storm rules:** a manufactured storm arrives at full strength, "like a switch", and follows no fact log. The triplets resist it through the bond; without his brothers, Dmitri uses shared procedure and a physical anchor (`fact-storms.md` §3, §5). The rain is weather; the storm is not.
- **Deaths:** the lieutenant and one other volunteer, both unnamed. Nobody of note dies.
- **Dmitri's voice:** formal, and no contractions in speech. He counts.
- **Ch 4 link:** the lieutenant's name reaches the farmhouse by courier. Old Songs sings it, but the page doesn't need to spell it out.
