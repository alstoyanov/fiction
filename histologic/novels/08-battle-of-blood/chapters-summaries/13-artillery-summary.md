# Chapter 13: Artillery

**POV:** Alexei Volkov
**Date:** Wed Mar 26, 2110 (dusk to about 05:30 on Thu Mar 27)
**Setting:** SFW-held farmland east of the capital's outskirts; a disused rail freight yard where the SFW has sited four towed guns; a drainage culvert under the embankment; a ruined barn two kilometres back, where Elena waits with the radio
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The raid the reader has been waiting for since the series began: the Phantom behind enemy lines, a job only he can lead, and every sign that this is where he dies. He doesn't. It costs one of Elena's people instead. Afterwards, wry and exhausted, he says the thing the reader is thinking. The chapter is a deliberate **misdirection**.

## Beats
- **Dusk, the reason.** Since Mon night the SFW's guns have been shelling the **Chronos field hospital** at the capital's eastern edge (Ch 6). The tents are clearly marked. Tanaka's count came through Nikolai on Tuesday, with no request attached: *four dead, all Chronos staff, eleven wounded.* Elena read it once. "Tonight."
- **The team.** Alexei and six of Elena's people from the coast cells, all unnamed, in the resistance's dark clothing. The explosives are Veridican engineer stocks the cells took on the coast. Elena stays at the barn with the radio; she runs operations and he runs the ground (her file: she coordinates, others extract).
- **Alexei's voice.** He is quick, wry and warm, and underneath it is the Phantom's patience. He hums the Lakeshore Air under his breath while they wait, the vault Old Songs gave him in Ashford's library ("They look for what you love…"). SFW soldiers have no interfaces, so nothing he learned about storms matters tonight. This is a war of mud and timing.
- **The approach.** A drainage culvert under the rail embankment, knee-deep in cold water. The yard holds the **four guns**, a crew asleep in two railway wagons, and sentries on a fixed round. He counts the round three times. It doesn't vary; the SFW is disciplined, which makes it predictable.
- **The work.** Charges on the breeches and the ammunition stack. It is slow, and his hands are steady. A sentry changes his round by one wagon's length, and the timing breaks.
- **01:50, the fight.** A flare goes up. There is automatic fire from the wagons. One of Elena's people, a woman from the coast cell, is hit crossing the open ground and dies there. Two more are wounded, one badly. The charges are set but not yet fired.
- **The last man out.** Alexei sends the others back through the culvert with the wounded, and stays at the embankment alone, firing to hold the crews' heads down until the timers run. Here the reader should be sure. A round tears through his pack, another through his sleeve, and he is thrown flat when the ammunition stack goes up. **The guns are destroyed.** He gets up, and walks back through the culvert water, and is alive. Keep the prose close and physical. There is no heroic speech. His thought is simple: *not yet, then.*
- **03:40, the barn.** Elena counts them in, left to right, the way she counts everything. *"Six. I sent seven."* He tells her where the woman fell. Elena writes nothing down; she will remember it. She dresses the graze on his arm herself, with her face very still.
- **The line.** Sitting on an upturned crate, soaked and shaking with cold, he laughs once. ***"I keep thinking this is the one."*** Elena doesn't answer at first. Then, hoarse: *"Don't."* He calls her **Aunt Lena**, which he never does in front of the cells. She lets him.
- **Texture.** He wonders what Dima would say about the timing. He decides Dima would have planned for the sentry, and it annoys him that this is true.

## Ending
**05:20.** The radio. **Nikolai**, from the war coordination floor: Isaiah has sent word through one of the coast cells that he wants to come across the lines, and he is bringing something. Alexei, still wet, with his eyes closed: ***"Of course he does."***

## Notes
- **(Misdirection)** Alexei survives (README decision 13). Every beat should invite the old death: the Phantom, the last man out, the rearguard. **Don't use an overpass, the ring road, or any image from Ch 19**, so that Alexei's real death on Apr 23 is its own scene. Don't foreshadow his last words, and don't mention "thirty seconds".
- **Casualties are credible and modest:** four Chronos staff dead at the hospital before the raid; one of Elena's people killed in the raid and two wounded. All are unnamed. The dead woman may go into "The Names" in Ch 16 without a name on the page.
- **The weapons are conventional** on both sides. The SFW has no interfaces, so there are no storms and no seeding here. The flare is only light, and it isn't weather.
- **Isaiah's message** sets up Ch 19 (it follows his decision in Ch 10). Its route through a coast cell to Nikolai is new.
- **"Of course he does"** is given to Alexei; the plot leaves the speaker open.
- **Elena:** she counts first and feels second, writes nothing, and her voice is hoarse. She doesn't cry here.
