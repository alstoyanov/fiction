# Chapter 24: Terms

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Fri May 16, 2110 (about 06:00 to after midnight)
**Setting:** The basement archive of the Commission's testimony intake, among Kovač's folders; the stair to the intake's back door; the western corridor out of the besieged capital, at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Part Four opens in the dark between the shelves. A decent man is about to save his city by giving away the one thing the war was fought for, and with it a second nation's past that nobody has asked about. Marcus can't stop him and doesn't want to. He remembers a sentence Kira brought back from the farmhouse, and understands that the moment it named has come.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 06:00:** *May 16, 2110. Basement archive, testimony intake. Day seventeen. Status: custody order (0.61). Location, per The Judge: unknown.* He has been here since Wed Apr 30, when Morrison spoofed his telemetry (Ch 21). He shaves in a sink meant for washing ink off hands. He hears the shelling above as a change in the dust.
- **The city since May 9.** It has been awake for a week, since the carrier was cut at 03:12, and it is frightened for the right reasons. The queues are loud now, and people argue. The testimony intake has become the place where people bring the names of their dead, on paper. Kovač files them. Her count has passed the tens of thousands, soldiers and civilians, and she gives him the number flatly, as an item on an agenda. Some of the names are SFW, taken from the journals of people who watched them die.
- **The front.** Since Wednesday the SFW has held the streets around the **Factorepo Spire**. It is at the gates. The Judge still coordinates what is left of the defence. Marcus watches its panel on the stair: every street, every shelter and every citizen's location, except his. His own entry reads *location unknown*.
- **17:10, Kovač comes down** with her hands in her coat pockets. The draft terms were sent to the Commission's legal office on Thursday night "for lawful form". She is removed, but she still has keys. She didn't take the draft. She **copied it by hand**, because paper can't be adjusted. The key clause, in her block capitals:
  > *By order of the First Minister under the war powers, custody of the core of The Judge passes to the Sovereignty of Free Will, in exchange for a general ceasefire.*
  The formal handover is set for **Wed May 21 at 13:30**, in the Spire. She pushes her glasses up her nose. "It's lawful, Mr. Chen. I checked the form."
- **What Marcus sees at once.** The Judge isn't one nation's machine any more. Since Oct 26, 2109, Veridica's core has held Chronos's true source too. Nora's packets said "the record of two nations" (Ch 10), and Julian said *"If they take your Judge, they take ours"* (Ch 11). Whoever holds the core holds the record of both. The terms say nothing about Chronos. **Nobody has asked Chronos.** He writes it under *QUESTIONS FOR THE WAR*: ***Who asked Chronos?***
- **20:00, Maitland on the national broadcast.** Kovač's old receiver sits on a folder box. Maitland is tired and grieving, and humane. The numbers come first, then the terms "in principle", then the date and hour. His line: ***"I will not trade this city for a machine."*** Farahani's February promise stands behind it: *"We are not here for your cities."* Marcus believes Maitland, and thinks he is right about the city. He is afraid of what the terms give away. *Both.* He lets himself think the word once.
- **The sentence.** In April Kira came back from the farmhouse with Vance's words and told him (Ch 16): ***"I'll give it when they try to give it away. Not before."*** He has kept them on UNCHECKED ever since, under *What is on the page?* (Ch 8). He reads the clause again: *custody… passes.* That is giving it away.
- **21:30.** Today's note went to Kira at noon, as one does every day. Kovač has carried one each way since Apr 30. Tonight Kira comes herself, down the back stair, with **Dmitri**, whose volunteer company holds the western corridor. Kira's name is on Pell's list and her Spire access was suspended on Apr 30, but she has no custody order. Marcus has one. They go anyway. Kovač holds the back door and does not come: *"I'll keep the folders. It's what I do."* She means it as a joke against herself. Tonight it isn't one.
- **The crossing.** The western corridor runs toward the lakeshore under fire. At a checkpoint short of Dmitri's lines, a soldier knows Marcus's face from the published list. The Judge can't place him; the soldier can. He looks at him for a long time. He has been awake for a week. "Nobody's sure of anything now," he says, and waves them through. Marcus doesn't run. Dmitri counts the shells aloud and doesn't use a single contraction.

## Ending
About 04:50, the farmhouse lane in real fog off the lake. There is a lamp lit in the kitchen window, though it isn't yet dawn. Kira says it first: "She's up." Marcus, to himself, a fact entry he doesn't finish: *Vance, E. Awake. Waiting for:*

## Notes
- **(Twist 3 clue)** The terms. The clause's words *custody* and *passes to* the SFW are chosen to be matched against the back of the page in Ch 25 (*"orders its custody given outside the nation"*). Nobody makes the match here. Marcus knows only that the moment Vance named has come. He doesn't know what the page says.
- **The clause wording is new.** The date and hour are from README decision 24. "I will not trade this city for a machine" is a new line for Maitland.
- **The index** is 0.61 and the custody order stands (README 15). It lapses on May 21.
- **Kovač's hand copy** fits her voice rules (paper over screens, titles, glasses, pockets).
- **Dmitri drives,** as in Book 7. His company holds the western corridor (Ch 17–23).
- **The spoof** (Ch 21) means The Judge can't locate Marcus. Only faces can, which is why the soldier matters.
- The tens of thousands dead are from the plot. Kovač gives no exact figure.
- **Isaiah's packet prints:** Marcus's paper copy, from Isaiah on Apr 24, is on the shelf among the folders (Ch 21). He takes it with him tonight, for Ch 27.
- The fog is real weather. There is no Wei, no Chen Zhao and no manuscript text in this chapter.
