# Chapter 9: The Wine Was Open

**POV:** Colonel Yevgenia Sokolov
**Date:** Mon Mar 10, 2110 (06:30 to about 23:00)
**Setting:** Coastal command's headquarters, withdrawn inland after the landing (a requisitioned district school); the road to the capital; Viktoria Sokolov's house in the capital; the First Minister's office, late
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 1.** The plan the SFW followed ashore was hers, and the one sentence in it that no one in command ever heard was said at her mother's table. Sokolov works it out the way she works out everything else, in the register of a report, and then she drives to the house. It is empty. She reports it in full, in the first person, and asks to keep her post.

## Beats
- **06:30, coastal command.** The courier's case reaches her from the capital through the war coordination's courier channel, still in Elena's oilcloth, with a cover slip from Kira's office, which forwarded it unopened (Elena writes nothing down). Inside: the SFW landing and advance plan, annotated in the SFW's own script, and behind it **an annex in Veridican military format**, as Elena saw.
- **The annex, read as an order.** Her POV lists what she sees and doesn't interpret it:
  - every battery she placed, **Battery 1 to Battery 9**, at her own grid references, each one marked in SFW script as *avoid*;
  - the three inland roads she meant to hold, each marked with an hour to be taken;
  - **the gap between Battery 4 and Battery 5**, marked as the first wave's beach (the Prologue's gap);
  - at the head of the annex, typed as its summary line: ***"The beaches are a door, not a wall."***
- **The check.** She opens the official coastal plan from her safe (adopted January 2110, README decision 7) and reads it line by line. The positions match. **The phrase is not in it.** It isn't in any briefing she gave. It is in her notebook, which has been in her coat since December, and she has said it aloud once: on Fri Dec 20, at her mother's table (Book 7 Ch 29). She writes the three facts in a column and initials them.
- **The second column: her promotions.** Major in Feb 2109 ("very quick", Book 6 Epilogue). Colonel on Dec 20, with the insignia already engraved. Coastal command in January, which made her private plan the official plan. She writes *arranged?* and crosses out the question mark.
- **Afternoon, the road.** She drives herself toward the capital, 140 km from the coast, in real weather: gray, a hard wind, convoys moving the other way.
- **The house.** The plain plate on the door still reads *Chancellor V. Sokolov*. The door is unlocked. The house is cold, with the heating off, and exact as always, with nothing out of place. The study desk has been cleared, as if for an inspection.
- **The table.** Two glasses stand side by side, clean. **The good wine** is there, the same as in December, cork out and **open**. She notes that it has gone flat, so it has been open for days. Propped against the bottle is a note in her mother's hand, one line: ***"You were always exactly where you should be."*** She hears December's sentence in it ("You have always been…") and doesn't let herself hear the difference.
- **"East."** She is Border Security, so she checks the one record she is entitled to. A private travel order for the former Chancellor, **eastbound, through unaligned territory**, dated **Thu Mar 6**, the morning after the courier was taken. She notes the date. She doesn't interpret it.
- **The hour.** She sits at the table for an hour in her coat and pours nothing. The narration gives the clock, the note, the glasses and her hands in her pockets, and not her feelings.
- **22:30, the First Minister's office.** **Maitland** is tired and courteous and receives her alone. She reports **formally, in full, in the first person**: the annex, the phrase, the dinner, the promotions, the house, the travel order. He asks: *"Was your mother part of whatever this is?"* She says: *"I do not know what 'this' is, First Minister. She was part of the plan's leaving the country."* He asks who else knows. *"You. And now it is written."* He says he will tell Marcus Chen in the morning: *"He has a list."* Then Maitland asks whether she wishes to be relieved of coastal command.

## Ending
Sokolov, flat, standing: ***"My mother has gone east, First Minister. I would like to stay where I am. I am apparently very good at it."***

## Notes
- **(Twist 1 reveal.)** Pays off Book 7 Ch 29, the promotions (Books 6–7), the Prologue's gap, Ch 2 and Ch 3. Sokolov never hears "the highest table"; Isaiah holds it.
- **Nobody learns that Viktoria is an Architect** (README decision 8). No mark, manuscript, Annex or heirs. Maitland's "whatever this is" is as far as anyone goes: the circle guesses she belongs to "whoever wants to be invaded".
- **The note's wording is exact** (README decision 8), and so is the phrase (decision 7). The note deliberately echoes December's spoken "You have always been exactly where you should be" (Book 7 Ch 29) and shifts it into the past tense: the written line is the spoken one, closed. Sokolov hears the echo and won't hear the difference. Don't correct either one.
- **The logic has to hold.** The annex's positions could have come from inside command, but the summary line could only have come from the dinner. That is how she knows.
- **New details (see handoff):** Batteries 1–9, the three inland roads, the inland HQ in a school, the cleared desk, the wine gone flat, the travel order of Thu Mar 6, and Maitland telling Marcus on Tue Mar 11.
- **Voice:** no confession, no interior speech; her real sentence comes last.
