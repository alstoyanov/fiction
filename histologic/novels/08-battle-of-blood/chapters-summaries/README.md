# Battle of Blood: Chapter Summaries

These summaries are based on `plots/08-battle-of-blood-full.md` (v2), with the drafting decisions below. The old summaries, chapters and documents followed the abandoned Nov 2025 plot and have been deleted (they remain in git history).

- **Structure:** a Prologue, 30 chapters and an Epilogue, in four parts.
- **Target length:** about 3,300–3,600 words per chapter, roughly 110,000 words in all.
- **Tone:** a war novel and conspiracy thriller. It should be physical and unsparing, and every chapter ends on a turn.
- **Canon rules:** `PROJECT-RULES.md` §4–6, `worldbuilding/`, `characters/` and `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md`. In short:
  - Storms are perceptual, never weather.
  - Seeding is silent.
  - Removing an interface kills.
  - The Judge gives probabilities, and above 0.60 means custody.
  - SFW citizens have **no interfaces**.
  - The manuscript reveal schedule is binding.
  - Wei never gets a point of view.
  - Singh does not appear.

## Drafting Decisions (Beyond Plot v2)

1. **The landing.** Lights appear on the horizon at **04:40 on Wed Feb 12, 2110**, below the headland of Book 7's Epilogue. The first wave lands **between Battery 4 and Battery 5**, exactly where Sokolov's plan left its gap. The capital is about **140 km inland**. The fog and wind are real weather.
2. **The SFW.**
   - **General Omid Farahani** commands. His one line to Veridica: *"We are not here for your cities."*
   - **Colonel Nora Chen** (38) handles the Veridican source.
   - **Colonel Beatriz Martinez** is Isaiah's ally.
   - **The attaché** is unnamed.
   - SFW soldiers can't be turned by storms or reached by seeding. The SFW also **turns captured Border Security storm units on Veridican troops** (Ch 2).
3. **Veridica at war.**
   - **The war cabinet:** Maitland, Chen Zhao, Wei (Head of Emergency Information) and Pell (Public Order). Marcus becomes civilian adviser on **Thu Feb 13**.
   - **Sokolov** holds coastal command.
   - **Dmitri** commands a volunteer company.
   - **The Judge** coordinates the defence through the national network. **Its core is in the Factorepo Spire**, in the capital.
4. **Wei's map.** On **Fri Feb 28**, Wei leaves Kira a **maintenance map of the core**. It shows the removal routes, and a **maintenance route** into the core that Marcus and Kira use on May 21. Wei's only words: *"You'll want this."*
   - **Route M** runs from a **service door in a disused transit tunnel under the Spire** to the **core's lower cable gallery**, beneath the core hall. The core is at or below plaza level. The map is physical only and doesn't show the join.
5. **The Chronos field hospital.** It arrives on **Mon Mar 3** at the capital's eastern edge. Tanaka comes with it, and so does **Morrison**, who travels on Chronos papers and is still officially dead in Veridica. They are "friendly" because Chronos believes the history Book 7 wrote.
6. **The courier.** Elena's cells, with Alexei, take him on the occupied coast on **Wed Mar 5**. His case holds the SFW advance plan, which is Sokolov's coastal plan turned inside out.
7. **Sokolov's coastal command.** She drafted the coastal plan privately in Dec 2109 (Book 7 Ch 29). After the headland visit (Book 7 Epilogue), she is given **coastal command in January 2110**, and the plan becomes the official defence plan.
   **The phrase at her mother's table.** At dinner with Viktoria on **Fri Dec 20, 2109** (Book 7 Ch 29), Sokolov said: *"The beaches are a door, not a wall."* It appears in the SFW plan.
8. **Viktoria's house.** Empty on **Mon Mar 10**: two glasses, the good wine, and a note in her hand: *"You were always exactly where you should be."* She has gone **east**. **Nobody learns she is an Architect.**
   - The note **deliberately echoes** her spoken line at the Dec 20 dinner, *"You have always been exactly where you should be"* (Book 7 Ch 29), shifted into the past tense. Both wordings stay as written; Sokolov hears the echo in Ch 9 and doesn't let herself hear the difference.
9. **Nora's packets (Ch 10).** They describe the core, the merged source, and "the record of two nations". Their source is "at the highest table".
10. **The third leg.** On **Tue Mar 18**, Nikolai finds a third leg in the join that "goes nowhere I can see". Julian's message arrives through Kovač: *"If they take your Judge, they take ours. I would like Chronos to be asked this time."*
11. **The Stabilisation of Sympathisers Order.**
    - **Pilot "stabilisation" broadcasts** began in **three capital districts on Mon Feb 24**, under emergency information powers, less than two weeks into the war. That is why Tanaka's patients say "We must be certain now" on Mar 3 (Ch 6).
    - **Signed** on **Mon Mar 17** under war powers. It **legalised the pilot after the fact** (its recital: "regularising the stabilisation measures in effect… since 24 February 2110", Ch 12). That it began before any law existed is part of Twist 2's horror.
    - **Broadcasting** then follows district by district from **Mon Mar 24**, about a week apart (four districts by Mar 29, Ch 14).
    - **Its full text is classified;** only a notice was public. Nobody reads the preamble before Ch 17.
    - **The order's file** is titled *Correction Over Punishment*.
    - **Its preamble** quotes Principle Four verbatim.
    - **Ashford** is to be reopened. **Kovač refuses on paper on Fri Mar 21** and is removed as Registrar.
12. **Reeves** is released under war powers to "direct stabilisation broadcasting". This is announced on **Sat Mar 29**. The broadcast centre is the **National Broadcast Directorate** by the Meridian Bridge.
13. **The artillery raid** (Wed Mar 26). Alexei's team destroys the SFW guns shelling the Chronos field hospital, and Alexei survives. This is a deliberate misdirection.
14. **The siege.** The capital is encircled on **Wed Apr 2**. A **western corridor** toward the lakeshore stays open under fire, and that is how the farmhouse is reached. The city is **strangely calm**, because it is seeded.
15. **Marcus's index.** 0.57 at the start → **0.59** (Fri Apr 4) → **0.61** (Wed Apr 30), when a custody order is issued → it lapses on May 21, when there is no Judge.
16. **Vance's riddle.** She gives it to Old Songs on **Wed Apr 9**. This wording is canon, and it is solved in Book 9:
    > *Look where they swept the shelf of us,*
    > *behind the number no one reads;*
    > *the old man peeled his apple there —*
    > *ask him the number. Ask him twice.*

    He sets it to the Lakeshore Air, so its last line lifts like a question. **Don't solve it or hint at the answer in this book.** Old Songs doesn't try yet.
17. **Diagnosis (Mon Apr 14).**
    - Tanaka's case notes record identical presentation and an absence of doubt that is clinically impossible.
    - **Morrison** recognises his calibration once more.
    - **Maya** brings the order's technical file. In it is a typed page, **copy numeral X**, **page 19** of Principle Four, with the chipped *e*. It carries **no mark**, because a principle's mark comes at its end, on p. 22, which isn't in the file. Nobody sees WZ·10 in this book.
    - **Tanaka reads it aloud in the linen room.**
18. **"Read your own book" nights** run from **Fri Apr 18**, in cellars. People read their journals from January and find doubts they no longer feel.
19. **Alexei's death.** **Wed Apr 23**, at an overpass on the eastern ring road, covering Isaiah's crossing. His last words, exact: *"Tell Elena I love her. Tell Dima he was first by thirty seconds, and I never minded."*
20. **Thirty seconds (Fri Apr 25).** Dmitri tells Nikolai: *"I was. By thirty seconds. He never minded."* Old Songs adds Alexei to "The Names", and **his voice breaks for the first time**.
21. **Pell's list (Wed Apr 30).**
    - **Who is on it:** journal keepers and "doubters", including Ruth Calloway, Kira and Marcus.
    - **Marcus:** a custody order is issued. **Kovač**, removed but still holding keys, hides him in the **basement archive of the testimony intake**.
22. **Maya and Reeves (Mon May 5).**
    - **Reeves:** *"I'd like to see what happens if you turn it off."*
    - **The weakness:** the carrier has a **timing dependency on the national clock**.
    - **At the door:** *"You still write that I didn't do it."* / *"You didn't. You did this."*
23. **Silence (Fri May 9).** The carrier is cut across the capital at **03:12**. The city wakes over several hours, and mass correction stops.
24. **The terms.** On **Fri May 16** the SFW is at the Spire's gates, and Maitland prepares to surrender the core. The **formal handover is set for Wed May 21 at 13:30**.
25. **The page (Sat May 17).** At the farmhouse, reached through the western corridor. Vance gives it to Kira.
    - **Front, Principle Seven, verbatim (§4.12):** *"The Judge must never become a weapon. Better destroyed than enslaved. I have built into it the means of its own ending: conditions, authorities and a cascade that cannot be reversed. I have told no one. If you are reading this, you are the one I did not tell."*
    - **Back, the mechanics, exact (canon from this book), in the same young hand:**
      > *If the lawful authority of the nation orders its custody given outside the nation,*
      > *and one who has read this page concurs,*
      > *it ends — and everything that restores from it ends with it.*
26. **Consent (Mon May 19).** Aubry comes to Julian in his recorded room. **Chronos consents**, and the consent is published by **self-disclosure** before it is sent. Julian adds a line for Marcus: *"This time you asked."*
27. **Wed May 21.**
    - **Morning:** Nora's SFW team enters the Spire ahead of the handover. Marcus and Kira come in by Wei's maintenance route.
    - **13:31:** Maitland's signature reaches the core's console, and the condition is met.
    - **13:31–13:35:** a **restore to a third site** runs and completes. It is the design's own transfer contingency; nobody sees it until Ch 30.
    - **13:40:** Kira concurs, and the cascade begins.
    - **13:44:** the cascade reaches the third leg, which is already empty.
    - **Final determination** on every Judge panel in both nations: *"Truth endures. I do not need to."*
    - **Silence by 14:00.**
    - **Nora dies** in the core fire, holding the door against her own technicians.
28. **Vance's death and burial.** She dies on **Sat May 24** at the farmhouse and is buried in the orchard on **Tue May 27**. Old Songs gives **his one speech** at the burial and sings **"Elise Vance"** into "The Names".
29. **Farahani** is killed on the coast road during the withdrawal on the morning of **Mon May 26** by a Veridican battery, cut off since February, that never got the ceasefire order. **Martinez** negotiates the rest from Tue May 27.
30. **After the Judges.** Veridica has courts with human judges only. **Chronos will elect a human council**, and it is elected in August. **Chen Zhao "confesses first"** and proposes a *Commission of Rebuilding*.
31. **Old Songs' voice rules** are binding:
    - He speaks rarely: **one speech (Ch 29)**.
    - He hums, and in wartime freedom he sings with words.
    - Every tune lifts at the end like a question.
    - Songbook lyrics only. "The Names" must be exact.
    - The riddle belongs to Vance. Give him **no riddle of his own** in this book.
32. **The manuscript in this book:** Principle Four (preamble and copy X p. 19) and Principle Seven (front and back). **Nothing more:**
    - No Annex text and no Register names.
    - No decoded mark.
    - No one learns that X, WZ·10 or Four belongs to Wei's father.
    - Vance's copy stays hidden behind the riddle.
33. **Settled in the summaries** (binding):
    - **Dmitri's company** holds the western corridor from **Wed Apr 2**.
    - **Apr 30:** Kira's **Spire access is suspended** (no custody order); **Morrison spoofs Marcus's telemetry** ("location unknown"). Nikolai keeps his access.
    - **The terms clause** uses the word **"custody"**: *"…custody of the core of The Judge passes to the Sovereignty of Free Will, in exchange for a general ceasefire."*
    - **Maitland is never told** about the page.
    - **Kira concurs** through the core's ordinary ***Concurrence*** field, by typing **the front of the page word for word**.
    - **The published consent** names no mechanism and no date. Its publication is what prompts the SFW to send Nora's team in early on May 21.
    - **Nora's last words:** *"Not you,"* then *"Go. I'm sorry about the record."*
    - **Ch 30:** the maintenance log store survives (it doesn't restore from the source). The third-site copy survives because *"This didn't restore from it. It became it."* Wei's read-only session (open from 13:41, no command) is **a log line only**, with no interpretation.
    - **Burials:** in the farmhouse orchard, under the **plum** trees. Alexei (buried in April, not shown); Vance beside him (Tue May 27).
