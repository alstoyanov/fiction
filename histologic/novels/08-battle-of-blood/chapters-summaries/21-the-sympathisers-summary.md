# Chapter 21: The Sympathisers

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Wed Apr 30, 2110 (07:00 to about 23:00)
**Setting:** Marcus and Kira's flat; the war cabinet room below the First Minister's office; the Chronos field hospital at the eastern edge; the streets of the seeded capital at dusk; the **basement archive of the Commission's testimony intake**
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The record comes for the man who checked it. Marcus refuses to run, and is persuaded that hiding isn't running when the alternative is to be made to agree. By nightfall he is a fugitive in his own besieged capital, filed among the folders.

## Beats
- **07:00, fact entry:** *Apr 30, 2110. Public registry. Stabilisation of Sympathisers Order, list of persons of stabilisation concern. Compiled by: Public Order (Pell, I.). Status:* The list runs to thousands of names across the capital and the lakeshore, sorted into three kinds:
  - **journal keepers;**
  - **"doubters"**, meaning people whose index rose after they spoke against Stabilisation;
  - people who **gathered**. Here Marcus recognises the source: The Judge counted the cellars (Ch 18). It couldn't read them, but it could count them.

  Listed persons must present for "stabilisation assessment" at the reopened Ashford. On the list are **Ruth Calloway** (Kestrel Point, a journal keeper), **Kira Osman**, and **Marcus Chen**.
- **Kira.** Her index is below the line, so she has no custody order yet. But her **Spire access is suspended pending assessment**, and she is off war coordination as of 07:30. They read it together at the table. She says nothing, and then: *"Nikolai still has his."*
- **08:30, the war cabinet.** Present are Maitland, Chen Zhao, Wei and Pell. Pell presents the list and reads from the order's preamble as if it were his own language: *"Certainty is care."* Marcus hears the page Tanaka read in the linen room, which he knows from Kira's copy, in a minister's mouth, and doesn't show it. Chen Zhao, warm, with tea in a glass: *"Restorative stabilisation, cousin. It is a kindness."* Wei is at the end of the table taking notes; Marcus's fact entry records him as *present, said nothing*. Maitland is tired and doesn't meet his eye: *"Your name is there because the record put it there, Marcus. I can't take it off."*
- **The argument.** Marcus argues against the order in order, as he did in Ch 15: the patients' identical sentence, the broadcast schedule matching the onset, the carrier. The public registry panel shows his index, **0.59**. At **10:52**, while he is still speaking, it becomes **0.61**. The list fed the record, and the record feeds the list. At **11:04** a custody order is issued automatically.
- **He does not run.** That is his rule, because running proves the charge (voice rule 7). He walks out of the cabinet room at an ordinary pace. **Kovač** is waiting in the corridor, in her dark coat, with her hands in her pockets. She read the list at 07:00. She was removed as Registrar in March (Ch 12), but nobody asked for the basement key: *"It isn't on a form."*
- **Her briefing,** flat and in order: *"You won't be tried, Mr. Chen. You'll be stabilised. There'll be nothing to prove afterwards, because you won't want to."* Marcus: *"I don't run."* Kovač: *"You're not running. You're being filed."*
- **Afternoon, the field hospital.** He goes as the war adviser on a routine visit. **Morrison**, apologising too much, spoofs his telemetry. His trace ends in a shelled block two streets east, where a relay failed at 15:10, and he is recorded as **location unknown**, neither dead nor defected. Tanaka puts a hand on his arm and says only, *"Mr. Chen."*
- **Dusk, the walk.** Kovač walks him across the seeded capital. The queues are orderly and the volunteers smile. Under the spoof the broadcast still reaches him (worldbuilding: a spoofed person still receives broadcasts), and he feels the calm pressing at the edges.
- **The archive.** Beneath the intake are rows of steel shelving holding the paper testimony of the Doctrine years: boxes, folders and dates. There's a camp bed, one lamp and a bucket. He puts **his paper copy of Nora's packets** (from Isaiah, Apr 24) on a shelf among the folders. Kovač will carry one note a day to Kira. He writes the first: *Filed. Safe. Read your book.*
- **Night.** Shelling shakes dust from the shelves. He reads his own journal by the lamp, to check that he still disagrees. He does.

## Ending
In the dark between the shelves, Marcus writes: ***Checked: the record wants me corrected. Unchecked: whether it's wrong.*** Then he crosses out "whether".
