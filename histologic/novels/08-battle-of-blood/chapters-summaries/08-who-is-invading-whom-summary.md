# Chapter 8: Who Is Invading Whom?

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Fri Mar 7, 2110 (morning to dusk)
**Setting:** The war cabinet room, Cabinet Office (09:30); the roads west out of the capital, full of refugees; Elena's farmhouse kitchen in the border zone (afternoon, by day); the car on the way back
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Part One ends with the pattern in plain sight. The enemy wants the machine, intact. Marcus asks the question nobody in cabinet wants asked, and then takes it to the one person who might know the answer. She tells him only that an answer exists.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 09:30:** *Mar 7, 2110. War cabinet. Involved parties: Maitland, O.; Chen, Z.; Wei, T.; Pell, I.; Chen, M. (adviser). Purpose: review of enemy objective.*
- **The pattern, laid out by Marcus in order,** like an argument about the record:
  - Farahani: *"We are not here for your cities."*
  - Kira's analysis (Ch 5): the axes converge on the capital, on the central district, on the Spire. Ports 0.05, industry 0.04.
  - The damage log: bridges, rail yards and coastal relays have been hit; **the trunk cables and the Spire's power corridors have not.**
  - The occupation (reports from behind the lines, by the paper chain): disciplined, paying for bread, handing out pencils.
  - His conclusion: they want **the Spire and The Judge, intact.**
- **What he keeps back.** Kira has told him about Wei's map (Ch 5). Wei sits four places away, courteous, tablet on the table. Marcus doesn't mention the map in cabinet. He notes that a map for removing the core means the core *can* be removed, and that anyone who holds the Spire can use it.
- **The exchange,** word for word:
  - **Marcus:** ***"We should know how to deny them the core."***
  - **Maitland:** ***"Destroy our own Judge?"***
  - **Marcus:** ***"Not yet. But we should know how."***
- **The room.**
  - **Maitland** looks at the window for a long moment and neither agrees nor forbids.
  - **Chen Zhao**, warm, with tea in a glass: *"Cousin, it is the one thing keeping us alive."*
  - **Pell**, for the minutes: *"The adviser at fifty-seven proposes disarming the nation."*
  - **Wei** writes one line on his tablet and says nothing.
  - The index stays **0.57**. Marcus notes it and notes Pell's noting.
  - Maitland closes the item: "Find out whether it can be done. Tell me before you tell anyone else." It is no authority, and it is no refusal.
- **Who could know.** The Judge's architecture belongs to Systems Architecture, and Marcus will not ask Wei. The only person alive who compiled the design is at the farmhouse.
- **The drive west,** by day. Marcus drives himself. At 0.57 he visits only by day and must be back before dark (Book 7). The roads are full of refugees moving the other way from the coast, in the order The Judge set for them. He counts the carts and loses the number. It is a small hole, and he lets it go.
- **The farmhouse, afternoon.** The barn still holds the refugees from the coast (Ch 4). **Vance is at the stove**, stirring something with both hands on the spoon to steady it. **Old Songs** sits beside the stove with a basket of potatoes. He doesn't hum.
- **Marcus asks her plainly.** He tells her the pattern. He asks whether, when she built it, she left any way to stop it being used by whoever holds it: *"A way to kill it, if we had to."* Vance corrects the word, gently, the way she once argued at the Archive: *"'Kill' is a word for living things."* A pause. Then: ***"There is a page."***
- **She won't give it to him.** He asks for it. "No." He asks what is on it. She turns back to the stove and doesn't answer. He asks where it is. She says nothing at all. Old Songs stays silent throughout and looks at neither of them. Marcus understands that the silence is not an argument he can win this afternoon, and he has to be on the road before dark.

## Ending
**Dusk, the car, a lay-by on the road east.** He takes out *QUESTIONS FOR THE WAR*. Under *Who is invading whom?* and *Then what are they here for?* he crosses out nothing. He writes the new question in his small pencil hand: ***What is on the page?***
