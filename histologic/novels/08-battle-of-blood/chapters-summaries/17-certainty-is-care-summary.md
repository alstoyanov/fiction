# Chapter 17: Certainty Is Care

**POV:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka
**Date:** Mon Apr 14, 2110 (06:10 to about 22:00)
**Setting:** The Chronos field hospital at the capital's eastern edge, inside the siege ring: the wards, Tanaka's office with its pinned paper map of the districts, and the **linen room** behind the laundry
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 2.** The calm is a treatment. Veridica is being corrected by broadcast under cover of the war, and the order that does it opens with the sentence Tanaka's torture was built from. Her case notes become a diagnosis with no author she can name.

## Beats
- **06:10, rounds.** Case notes from the first line: *Patient: male, 34, north district. Shrapnel, left thigh. Affect: calm, elevated. Asked whether he is afraid: "We must be certain now."* Forty-one times since Mar 3 (Ch 6): the same words and smile. Shells land and they don't flinch.
- **The old question** from her Ashford years: *"Is there anything you are not sure of?"* Every one says *"No"* in the same falling cadence.
- **The differential:** not confabulation (individual), not a storm (nothing flickered), not Book 5's seeding (one detail). *It takes something away and leaves a sentence.* Reeves, 2106: *erase, then seed.*
- **The map.** She has pencilled each patient's onset date onto the districts. The onsets don't spread; they arrive **district by district**. Three districts come first, with onsets from the **last week of February** (her Mar 3 patients, Ch 6). The rest follow about a week apart from late March. *Presentation: identical, forty-one patients. Doubt: absent. This is clinically impossible. Onset: district by district.*
- **11:00, Morrison.** He takes one reading with an assessment band, looks at the gain trace, and goes gray. His hands shake for the first time since 2106. *"It's mine. The Wing C calibration. On the national network."* Tanaka: *"James. Say that again."*
- **15:30, Maya** comes through the shelling with the copied **technical file of the Stabilisation of Sympathisers Order**. It came to her desk in the Commission's technical office for a routine network-load review. The routing slip has an office stamp and **no name**.
- **The linen room:** the one room no Chronos citizen enters in the afternoon, since every Chronos orderly logs what he hears. Present: Tanaka, Morrison, Maya.
- **The file.**
  - **Cover:** *Correction Over Punishment* (Ch 12). Signed Mar 17 under war powers; only a notice was ever public.
  - **The preamble:** legal language, then an unattributed quotation.
  - **The annex:** the broadcast schedule by district. Its first three lines are the three early districts, *continued from 24 February 2110 under emergency information powers*; then one district a week from Mar 24. It matches her onsets to within days. **The broadcasting began three weeks before the law that authorised it.** The Order legalised it after the fact. Tanaka writes that down as a finding, not a feeling.
  - **The technical sign-off:** *Dr. Reeves, Director of Stabilisation Broadcasting*.
- **The page.** Clipped behind the annex is **one typed page**: chipped *e*, copy numeral **X** in the lower right, page **19**. Maya: *"X. Like the intake pages."* (Book 6.) The circle's notes of copy VII's contents page put Principle Four at p. 18. The page carries no mark: this is the middle of the principle, not its end.
- **Tanaka reads it aloud,** slowly, the way she read the protocol at her kitchen table. It matches the preamble word for word (manuscript §4.6):
  > *"Do not punish the doubter; heal him. Punishment makes martyrs; correction makes converts. The aim is not obedience but belief: a citizen who, corrected, experiences his certainty as his own. Correction is compassion. Certainty is care."*
- **Recognition.** In *"experiences his certainty as his own"* she hears Continuity's aim, *genuine belief, experienced as self-originated*: the protocol she refused in 2105 was a paraphrase of this page. She sets the page down the way she once set down her cup. In *"Certainty is care"* she hears forty-one patients. Morrison: *"He used to say 'healing'."* Maya cries and is furious at once: *"Somebody put this in my queue."*
- **The why.** Principle Two, from copy VII: *"Controlled conflict answers every question before it is asked."* Under a war, correction is care and nobody asks. Morrison: *"Phase Two."*
- **Evening.** Tanaka copies the page by hand into her case book and into two journals by the pairing method, one of them for Kira. She keeps the typed page. Maya returns the file marked *reviewed*. Nobody asks after the page.

## Ending
Night, her office. She writes the diagnosis and stops at the last line: *Aetiology: a principle. Author: unknown. Treatment:* She leaves it unfinished.
