# Chapter 14: Release

**POV:** Maya Reeves
**Date:** Sat Mar 29, 2110 (07:12 to about 23:40)
**Setting:** Maya and Nikolai's flat in the capital; the public registry panel in the street below; the **National Broadcast Directorate** by the Meridian Bridge (the gate and the pavement outside); the war coordination floor that night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The man she visits in prison once a month is out, and he is doing the work the state needs most. She can't get in to see him, and he sends her one sentence, unsigned. The line she has written in her journal every month since 2107 stops being true.

## Beats
- **07:12:40.** Maya timestamps everything, and she timestamps this. The Public Order list of **releases under war powers** goes up on the public registry. Among the entries, flat and administrative: *REEVES, Dr. Released under war powers (Order of 17 March 2110) to direct stabilisation broadcasting. Posting: National Broadcast Directorate.* She reads it twice. There is no first name; there never is.
- **The monthly visit.** She last saw him on **Sat Mar 1**, her regular visit. He was thinner and courteous, and very interested in the war. He asked her whether the coast had fallen "cleanly". She didn't answer. She now finds that she can't remember whether he smiled, and it frightens her more than it should. She is 28.
- **Nikolai.** He comes in off the night shift. She shows him the list, and he goes still, the Volkov way of listening. He tells her about the reserved broadcast blocks he and Kira saw on Mar 18: *Civil stabilisation*, from the Directorate, from Mar 24 (Ch 11). Neither of them says what they are afraid it is. They don't have the words for it yet.
- **09:30, the Meridian Bridge.** She walks there. The Directorate is a pale building on the water by the bridge, with Public Order at the gate. The queue for the water point on the bridge approach is patient and orderly, and people are smiling. She notes it and doesn't know why she noted it. *(Twist 2 texture, very light.)*
- **The gate.** Her name isn't on any list. She gives the guard her Commission pass, her father's name and her own. The guard is calm and kind: *"I'm sorry, miss. It's for your own peace of mind."* She waits on the pavement for **2 hours 14 minutes**, timed.
- **11:48, the envelope.** The guard brings it out: a plain envelope, a single sheet, in her father's small, neat hand, and **unsigned**. He has never signed anything in his life (Book 5 Ch 21):
  > ***"They need someone who knows how. I am the only one who has done it."***
  She reads it standing on the pavement. *Done it.* She knows what he did: Isaiah's article, his code, the annex. She doesn't know what this is. Her anger and grief arrive together, in the same breath (voice rule 5). She doesn't cry in front of the guard.
- **Afternoon, the walk back.** Shelling to the east, distant and conventional. She counts the minutes between rounds. It's a habit, and it keeps her hands still. She passes the Cabinet Office and thinks of Marcus in there, arguing, and of Kovač out of a job since last Friday (Ch 12).
- **Evening, war coordination.** She takes her shift. On the network schedule the stabilisation blocks now cover **four districts**. The content is sealed. She looks at the timing and the origin and nothing else; she can't read more at her clearance. In the allocation history she notices that three of the four districts have carried a block of the same length, at the same hours, since **Mon Feb 24**, booked under Emergency Information's own powers and labelled only *public reassurance*. Only the fourth began on Mar 24. She logs the district list, the dates and the times without comment. Kira, beside her, reads the note once and hands it back. *"You'll want to keep that."* (Maya does: it matters in Ch 17 and 22.)

## Ending
**23:40, the flat.** She opens her journal to the month's page. Every month since her father's conviction she has written the same line, about Singh: ***He didn't do it.*** She writes it. She stops, crosses it out, and writes underneath: ***He is doing it now.***
