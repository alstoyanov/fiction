# Chapter 25: The Page

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Sat May 17, 2110 (about 05:00 to noon)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse in the border zone, reached through the western corridor: the kitchen table and the stove with the cracked door; the yard at the end
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 3.** Principle Seven needs no bomb. It needs exactly what Maitland is going to do on Wednesday, and one more hand. Vance gives that hand to Kira. On the back of the page is a sentence that reaches across the lake, and so the chapter closes on the lesson of Book 7.

## Beats
- **Kira's incident entry, 05:05:** *May 17, 2110. Farmhouse kitchen. Present: Vance, E.; Petrov, M.; Volkov, E.; Volkov, D. (door); Chen, M.; Osman, K. Lamp: lit before arrival.* Vance is dressed and sitting upright at the table. She heard Maitland on the receiver last night. Old Songs is at the stove, humming the **Lakeshore Air** low, and he goes on humming it under the whole scene.
- **Marcus asks,** plainly. Vance: ***"I said I would give it when they tried to give it away."*** She goes to her room and comes back with it. *(Don't show where it was kept.)*
- **Not to Marcus.** "Not you. You would carry it alone. You've done that." She turns to Kira: "You held a knife once and let it run, because stopping it would have been worse. You know what a hand on a thing is." Her tremor is too bad to hold the sheet still, so she lays it flat on the table and slides it across.
- **The object, in Kira's engineering eye.** It is one sheet, folded once. It is **handwritten** in the precise, slanting hand of the Compiler's Warning, which Kira knows from Book 6, younger and steadier than Vance's hand is now. The ink is faded. There is **no copy numeral, no mark and no page number**, and it isn't on the contents list. There is no chipped *e*, because it was never typed.
- **The front,** read silently first and then aloud at Vance's nod. It is Principle Seven, verbatim (§4.12):
  > *"The Judge must never become a weapon. Better destroyed than enslaved. I have built into it the means of its own ending: conditions, authorities and a cascade that cannot be reversed. I have told no one. If you are reading this, you are the one I did not tell."*
- **The back,** in the same young hand, three lines, exact:
  > *If the lawful authority of the nation orders its custody given outside the nation,*
  > *and one who has read this page concurs,*
  > *it ends — and everything that restores from it ends with it.*
- **The twist lands, in Kira's order: condition, authority, cascade.**
  - *Lawful authority… custody given outside the nation:* that's Kovač's handwritten clause, the terms and Wednesday at 13:30. **Maitland will meet the condition himself.** He's doing it to save the city, and he doesn't know it.
  - *One who has read this page concurs:* that's her, now.
  - *Everything that restores from it:* Kira sees the scope indicator of Oct 26 climbing past everything she wrote. One source. **Chronos's Judge ends too.**
- **Marcus's reasoning, aloud, as an argument about the record.** If they tell Maitland, he won't sign. Then the war goes on and the city dies. If he signs and nobody concurs, the SFW holds the record of two nations. If he signs and Kira concurs, the ceasefire stands and the prize is gone. Dmitri, from the door: "Then the general gets his ceasefire and nothing else." Elena says nothing.
- **Vance, briefly, in order, without asking to be forgiven.**
  - Why the condition: "A machine that decides for a people must never be handed to another people. It was the only thing I was sure of in 2066."
  - Why a second hand: "So that it would never be one person's decision. Not even mine."
  - How it was built into the Judge: "I had access once. I used it once." Nothing more *(elise-vance.md open question 7 stays open)*.
  - Why she wouldn't give it before (Ch 16): *"If I give it now, you'll use it to decide."* Now someone else has decided, and all that's left is to concur or not.
- **The practical line.** "It will ask for a concurrence, as it asks for every order that touches the core. Answer it with the front of this page, word for word. It knows its own words. No one else does."
- **Old Songs** stops humming for one bar when Kira reads *"the one I did not tell"*, and then starts again.

## Ending
Late morning, the yard. Kira has the page folded inside her journal. She looks back the way they came, toward the capital, and then out across the lake toward Chronos. Kira, to Marcus: ***"We decided for Chronos once."*** Marcus: ***"Then we ask them."***
