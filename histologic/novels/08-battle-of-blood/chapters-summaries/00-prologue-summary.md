# Prologue: Landfall

**POV:** Colonel Yevgenia Sokolov
**Date:** Wed Feb 12, 2110 (about 04:15 to 06:20)
**Setting:** The winter headland on Veridica's Atlantic coast from Book 7's Epilogue, a month later: her forward command post on the headland, the landing beaches below it, and Batteries 4 and 5 on either side of them. The capital is about 140 km inland.
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
This is the first hour of a war the nation wasn't built for. Every weapon Sokolov spent her career on is switched on and turns out to be useless. The hour also leaves one fact she writes down and won't interpret: the enemy came in through the gap in her own plan.

## Beats
- **04:15, the post.** It is a concrete observation bunker cut into the headland where she stood with Marcus, Kira and Dmitri on Jan 10. She notes the date and nothing else about it. It is real weather: a hard gray sea, wind off the water, and patches of sea fog lying on the swell. She has held **coastal command since January**, and her own plan is now the official defence plan. With her are a signals officer and two staff, all unnamed. She writes the watch log in a paper register, because she always has.
- **The plan, as a reader needs it.** Her plan places nine batteries, numbered 1 to 9. The beach between **Battery 4 and Battery 5** is the one stretch where their arcs don't overlap. That is deliberate, an economy: behind that beach lie marsh and a single road, and she means to hold the road inland. She doesn't think the phrase she once used for it. The reader may remember Book 7 Ch 29; the page says nothing.
- **04:40.** *Lights on the horizon, bearing east-south-east.* First a few, then a line of them. She logs it in exactly those words, with the time.
- **05:05.** The lights resolve into ships, and then into the low shapes of landing craft coming in under the wind.
- **05:12, the storm units.** On her order, the Border Security transmitter vehicles on the beach roads come up. These are her units, the fact-storm test programme her whole career was built on. They broadcast a false history of the shore over the true one: in one version there is no beach, only a drowned coast; in another, a sown field. **Keep this perceptual.** The sea and the sand are unchanged.
- **The first wave walks through it.** Through the glasses she watches SFW infantry come off the craft into the surf and up the sand at a steady pace. None of them looks at a road that isn't there. *No interfaces.* The broadcast has nothing to land on.
- **Her own soldiers flinch.** The gun crews at Battery 5 are in range of the unit behind them. A loader stops mid-movement, looking at a field where the sea is. A sergeant puts both hands flat on the concrete. Sokolov's forces are the only people on the beach who can perceive the contradiction. At **05:24** she orders every unit down. She logs it without comment.
- **The guns.** After that it is the old kind of war: conventional artillery and small arms, in the wind. Batteries 4 and 5 fire, and neither has the gap in its arc. Don't give casualty figures here. Show one concrete thing: a gun crew serving its piece in fog so thick it could be night.
- **05:51.** Two transmitter vehicles on the road behind Battery 5 are overrun before their crews can disable them. They are **captured intact**, and she logs it. *(This is what the SFW turns on Veridican troops in Ch 2.)*
- **Her POV stays restrained,** as in Book 7 Ch 29. She thinks in orders and observations. She notices, and doesn't interpret.

## Ending
**About 06:15.** The light comes up gray. The first wave is ashore and moving inland, and it came ashore **exactly between Battery 4 and Battery 5**, where the gap was, as if they had read the plan. She writes it in the report's register: *06:04. First wave ashore in strength, sector between Battery 4 and Battery 5. Enemy did not attempt the covered beaches.* She reads the line once, initials it, and sends it. She does not let herself think the rest.

## Notes
- **(Twist 1 clue)** The gap between Batteries 4 and 5 (README decision 1). It must read as a report entry, not a revelation. She doesn't ask who knew, doesn't think of her mother, and doesn't recall the dinner phrase.
- **Don't quote "The beaches are a door, not a wall" here.** It belongs to Ch 9. The gap's logic (hold the road inland rather than wall the beach) is enough.
- **Invented, and binding for Ch 2 and Ch 9:** behind the gap is marsh and a single road, covered by two inland **ridge batteries, 7 and 8** (see Ch 2); two transmitter units were captured at 05:51 behind Battery 5.
- **Weather:** the wind, sea fog and cold are real. Storms are perceptual; describe the broadcast only as what the gun crews *know* and see, never as weather.
- **SFW:** no interfaces. Conventional weapons only. Farahani doesn't appear.
- **Sokolov's age** isn't stated (39). This is her first POV chapter of two (the other is Ch 9). No confession, no interior speech about conscience.
