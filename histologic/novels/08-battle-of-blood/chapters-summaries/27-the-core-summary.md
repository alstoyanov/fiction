# Chapter 27: The Core

**POV:** Colonel Nora Chen (her only POV chapter)
**Date:** Wed May 21, 2110 (06:40 to 13:31)
**Setting:** The Factorepo Spire in the capital: the gates at dawn; the lower levels; the core hall, which lies below plaza level with the lower cable gallery beneath it, with rack aisles where the data shows as light, and the core console in a glass-walled bay at one end; the hall's main door onto the service corridor
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The liberator walks into the machine she crossed an ocean to take and meets the cousin she left behind in 2095. He doesn't argue with her. He shows her who paid for her war. She understands she was handed the prize by the people who wanted the war, and at 13:31 a lawful signature arrives that she can't read the meaning of.

## Beats
- **06:40, the gates.** The terms allow an SFW **pre-handover inspection team**, and Nora leads it: unnamed technicians with drive carriers and cutting tools. Since Monday's Chronos publication, headquarters has feared sabotage. *Chronos has consented to something, and nobody knows what.* Farahani's order, courteous and precise: **secure the drives; nothing leaves and nothing is touched until 13:30.** Veridican core staff stand aside as the terms require. The Judge's panels in the lobby still show the city.
- **Her interface.** It was installed when she was eight, in Veridica, and she is the only one in her unit who carries one. She has never removed it, because removing it would kill her. In the Spire she feels the national network again after fifteen years, an ordinary pressure she had forgotten was there. The SFW has always called her a security risk. She agrees with them.
- **Who she is.** She defected in 2095 after her father's correction. Marcus was seventeen then, and a believer, and he filed her away. In February she sent him a message through his journalist (Ch 3). She tells herself she doesn't know whether it arrived.
- **The core hall.** Kira's world, seen by an outsider: the light of the data in the racks, the quiet, and the console bay. Nora believes, without cynicism, that **the Judge must be taken from those who misuse it**, and held until Veridicans can do without it.
- **About 10:20.** Her technicians find two people in the rack aisles who came up from the core's lower cable gallery, a way that isn't on the handover plan. They are **Marcus and Kira**. They came in by **route M** on Wei's map (Ch 5): from a service door in the disused transit tunnel under the Spire, along to the core's lower cable gallery, and up the service stair into the hall. Kira's Spire access was suspended on Apr 30, and Marcus is a fugitive, so there was no other way in. Nora recognises him at once, older and thinner, with his glasses crooked. She has them held, not removed. She wants a witness, as she did with Isaiah (Ch 10). Kira sits by the console bay with a folded journal and a printed sheet, and says almost nothing.
- **The cousins, among the racks.** She makes her case: *"We are not here for your cities. We are here for the thing that corrected my father."* Marcus doesn't dispute it. He lays out paper instead, and Nora reads it:
  - **Isaiah's prints of her own packets** (Ch 10, 19). The seal on each is the same electronic **seam**, a chain closed backwards.
  - He has no paper for the next item, so he gives it from Isaiah's entry, dated and exact: the **RESTORATION account** the SFW received on **Mon Oct 14, 2109**, with the attaché's page headed *Phase: justification*, in Martinez's folder (Book 7 Ch 28). It has the same seam, and she can check it in her own service's files.
  - **Principle Two**, from Kira's photographs of copy VII: *"…and let there be enemies outside them all. Peace would raise questions. Total war would end the system. Controlled conflict answers every question before it is asked."*
  - **The Stabilisation Order's preamble**, which is Principle Four verbatim (Ch 17). *"They needed a war to do this. You were the war."*
- **Her question, and his.** She tells him the source never asked for anything: no money, no asylum, only that they come. Marcus: "Then it wasn't a leak. It was an invitation." She sees it whole: the war was given, the plan was given and the prize is being given. Whoever gives the prize has already decided where it will go. **She was handed the prize by the people who wanted the war.** Her father's correction and her own defection were both in someone's design. Marcus names nobody, and nobody says Wei.
- **What she does, and doesn't.** She sends her senior technician to hold the drive carriers at the far end of the hall, and doesn't say why. She doesn't radio headquarters. Marcus doesn't tell her about the page, and she doesn't ask what Kira is holding.
- **13:30, the radio.** The signing is in the Spire's great hall. Farahani's voice, courteous: "Signed." Maitland says something she doesn't catch.

## Ending
**13:31.** The order reaches the core console: lawful, under the war powers, *custody of the core of The Judge passes to the Sovereignty of Free Will.* Nora has won. She watches Kira instead of the screen. Kira looks at the folded sheet beside her, not defeated, and says very quietly: ***"It's met."*** Nora doesn't know what she means.

## Notes
- **Nora knows nothing of Seven.** The narration never explains "It's met". Ch 28 does, from Kira's POV.
- **The seam** is Wei's signature, but it stays unnamed, as in Book 7 Ch 28. **No source is identified.** There is no Viktoria, and nothing links "the highest table" to anyone.
- **The team's early entry** is motivated by Chronos's published consent (Ch 26). Farahani's order and the carriers set up Ch 28, where the technicians try to cut the drives out.
- **Her interface** follows the Continuity Traps in `morally-ambiguous-characters.md` §4. She never removes it, and the carrier has been cut since May 9, so she isn't seeded.
- **No restore is seen or hinted at.** 13:31 is also when the third-site restore begins (README decision 27). It is invisible here and first found in Ch 30.
- "I'm sorry about the record" is saved for her last line (Ch 28). Her Ch 3 message was delivered through Isaiah.
- **Route M** follows Ch 5: the transit-tunnel service door to the lower cable gallery. Marcus's paper copy of the packets is from Isaiah (Apr 24, Ch 21).
- Principles Two and Four are quoted from Kira's photographs of copy VII and Tanaka's hand copy of the copy X page (Ch 17). The preamble was never public.
- **Ages:** Marcus was born in Nov 2077, so he was seventeen in 2095, not a child.
