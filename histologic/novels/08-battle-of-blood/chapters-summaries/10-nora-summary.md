# Chapter 10: Nora

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Fri Mar 14, 2110 (morning into night)
**Setting:** SFW forward headquarters in a requisitioned seafront hotel in an occupied Veridican coastal town (unnamed); Nora Chen's office on the second floor; the promenade outside; the press office's darkroom; Isaiah's billet at night; the memory palace
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Nora wants a witness to her righteousness, so she shows him the prize. Martinez names what it means. Isaiah's rule of three is complete, and the witness stops being theirs.

## Beats
- **Opening, in the palace.** Isaiah files the day before it starts, as he does. His embedding is the condition of his asylum (Ch 3), and he has sent three weeks of dispatches as "the witness", each read by an SFW press officer before it goes. He notes the town:
  - the SFW's disciplined occupation, with paper and pencils handed out (Ch 7);
  - the quiet where the transmitter units would once have been;
  - no one here with an interface, **except Nora**. The SFW keeps her in rooms away from the captured storm units, because she is the one officer in the building a storm could turn.
- **Nora's office.** **Colonel Nora Chen** is 38, Marcus's cousin, and left Veridica in '95 after her father's correction. She is direct, warm when she chooses to be, and certain. She doesn't hide the scars behind her ears. She asks whether Marcus still keeps lists. Isaiah: "Columns." She almost smiles.
- **The packets.** She puts them on the desk: a stack of printed transmissions in Veridican, received through the source's channel over months. They describe:
  - **The Judge's core in the Factorepo Spire**: its storage floors, its guards and its power;
  - **the merged source**, one system since Oct 26, 2109 (Book 7 Twist 1), so that Chronos's true source now lies in Veridica's core;
  - the line Nora reads aloud and underlines with a finger: whoever holds the core holds ***"the record of two nations"***.
  Their source, as her handler writes it, is ***"at the highest table"***.
- **The seam.** At the foot of every transmission is the same electronic closing: a chain closed backwards. Isaiah saw it in Martinez's folder in December (Book 7 Ch 28), on the RESTORATION packet. He doesn't say so, and he doesn't say whose it is. He files it: *same channel.*
- **Nora's case, in her words.** She says they are not here to rule Veridica. They are here to take the machine away from the people who use it: "Nobody decides for them again. Not the Judge. Not the Ministry that corrected my father." She means it. Isaiah hears belief in how she says ***free***: the way people say *home*. She wants it on the record: *"Write it down, Mr. Okonkwo. I want someone to have seen why we came."* She lets him photograph the packets **with the camera the SFW press office issued to "the witness"**, "for the record, after".
- **Farahani,** briefly, crossing the lobby: courteous, precise, and busy. He asks Isaiah whether the dispatches are being cut. "Lightly, General." "Good." (No new line of doctrine from him.)
- **Afternoon, the promenade. Martinez.** She walks him along the sea wall in real wind, out of the building's hearing. She is cool and exact, and she has been watching him since Ch 3. Without preamble: ***"We are being given a war. Now we are being given the prize."*** She doesn't say by whom. She doesn't ask him what he will do, and he doesn't tell her.
- **The rule of three,** set out in the study of the palace like an editorial:
  1. *Phase: justification*, on the attaché's page (Book 7 Ch 5 and 28). The war was expected.
  2. The packets: the prize, supplied **from the highest table**, through the same channel.
  3. Martinez, an SFW colonel, saying it aloud.
  One fragment is a curiosity, and two is a pattern. He has three. He decides to **defect**, and knows exactly what that ends: the asylum, the passport, and every condition he never agreed to aloud. At the head of the parlour table his aunt is watching, and he doesn't look up.
- **Night, the darkroom and then the billet.** He makes contact prints of the packets under the red lamp, as the press office taught him to for dispatches. In his room he unpicks the lining of the coat he was arrested in, where his prints of Kira's photographs of copy VII pp. 5–12 have been since Sep 30 (Book 7 Ch 28), and his Wing C newsprint notes before that.

## Ending
He sews the packets' photographs into the lining with small, even stitches, as he did in 2106, and hangs the coat by the door where anyone could see it. Then he writes nothing down at all.

## Notes
- **The packets' wording is canon** (README decision 9): the core, the merged source, "the record of two nations", and a source "at the highest table". Don't add any further quoted line from them.
- **The seam** matches Book 7 Ch 28. Nobody names the sender, and nobody says "Wei". It sets up Ch 27, where Marcus shows Nora "the packets' source".
- **The source is not identified.** The reader, after Ch 9, may think of Viktoria's table. Isaiah can't: he doesn't know about Ch 9. Keep the two threads apart on the page.
- **Martinez does not see Principle Two here.** Isaiah doesn't show her his prints. When she learns the design is still open (`morally-ambiguous-characters.md` §3); flagged in the handoff.
- **Nora's interface:** she is the only interfaced SFW officer, and she is kept away from captured storm units (her file's Continuity Traps). She never removes it.
- **New:** the seafront hotel HQ, the press-office camera and darkroom, and "Columns."
- **Voice:** Isaiah classifies and counts, claims no more than he knows, and uses courteous titles. No borrowed maxims.
