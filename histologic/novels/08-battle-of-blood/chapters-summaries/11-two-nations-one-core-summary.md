# Chapter 11: Two Nations, One Core

**POV:** Nikolai Volkov
**Date:** Tue Mar 18, 2110 (01:40 to about 19:00)
**Setting:** The Judge's war coordination floor in the Factorepo Spire; a service stair and a cage-walk above the core's racks, reached with Wei's maintenance map; the floor's paper desk, where Kovač comes in person
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Book 7's discovery ("it was always one") becomes a military fact: take Veridica's core and you take Chronos. In the join Nikolai sees something that shouldn't be there, and files it where Marcus files things he can't prove. Then Chronos, through a ruined man, asks the question Nikolai has been avoiding since October.

## Beats
- **01:40, the night shift.** Nikolai is on war coordination with Kira; she is off shift, asleep on the cot in the anteroom until 06:00. The Judge coordinates the defence superbly and without a voice. Its panels show probabilities, routes, shelter assignments and battery supply. The SFW's lines are still bending toward the capital and the Spire (Ch 5).
- **The task.** On Fri Mar 7 Marcus asked Maitland how to deny the SFW the core (Ch 8), and Kira asked Nikolai to find out what "the core" now contains. He has **Wei's maintenance map** (Ch 5). It shows the physical racks, the power, the removal routes and the maintenance route, but not the logical structure. He works on the logical structure himself, from the source's own index.
- **The join, laid out in his notebook** as he traces it:
  - Veridica's true source and Chronos's are **one system** since Oct 26, 2109. Book 7 proved it; tonight he maps it.
  - **Chronos's true source lives in Veridica's core, in the Spire.** Its backups restore from here.
  - So if the SFW takes the Spire intact, it holds the past of both nations, 330 million people. He writes the figure and underlines it once.
- **The third leg.** The join has two restore legs, one to Veridica's backup systems and one to Chronos's. Then he sees **a third**. It is reserved and quiet, with no traffic, and it is permissioned in a form he can't read. Its endpoint resolves to nothing on the national address map, or on the Chronos map he rebuilt in October. He says it aloud to the empty floor: ***"It goes nowhere I can see."*** He doesn't guess. He writes it in his notebook with the time, **03:05**, and files it in Marcus's column, in capitals: ***UNCHECKED***. *(Closing-turn clue.)*
- **04:30, the racks.** He takes the service stair on Wei's map down to the cage-walk above the core. It is clean and cold, with row lights, and there is nothing to see. A rack doesn't tell you where its data goes. He counts the rows anyway, because Kira would. He thinks of his rooftop, the memory the cradle took (Book 4), which he holds now only as other people's testimony. A thing can be real and go nowhere he can see.
- **06:10, the schedule.** Kira takes over with coffee (black, no sugar). He shows her the join and the two-nation fact. The third leg is in his notebook, and **he doesn't show her yet**; it isn't a finding. On the network schedule she points to something else: blocks on the national broadcast network reserved from **Mon Mar 24** by Emergency Information for the **National Broadcast Directorate**, labelled *Civil stabilisation: district sequence*. The content is sealed. The Judge allocated the capacity without comment. *(Twist 2 clue, light.)*
- **11:20, Kovač.** She comes up in person in her dark coat, because paper should be carried. Julian's letter arrived at the testimony intake by lake courier, in his sloping hand. He is recorded at maximum (Book 7), so every word he writes is already public in Chronos; the letter only makes it paper. She reads it to Nikolai flatly, then hands it over:
  > ***"If they take your Judge, they take ours. I would like Chronos to be asked this time."***
  She adds nothing. As she leaves, in her briefing voice: *"There is a file on my desk I don't like, Mr. Volkov. I'll tell you about it when I've read it."* (This sets up Ch 12.)
- **The weight.** Nikolai ran the relay on Oct 25 (Book 7 Ch 16). He was the one who said "It's already there." The Chronos Awakening came through a line he held. Julian's sentence doesn't accuse anyone, and that is worse.

## Ending
**19:00.** He forwards Julian's words to Kira's desk exactly as written, with no comment of his own. He doesn't write what he is thinking, which is Book 7. Then he opens his notebook to the *UNCHECKED* line, looks at it, and closes it again.
