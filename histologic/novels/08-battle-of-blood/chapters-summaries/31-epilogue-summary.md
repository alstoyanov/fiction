# Epilogue: The Names

**POV:** Rotating: Julian, Dmitri, Isaiah, Maya, Marcus (Wei's journal line appears as a document, with no POV)
**Date:** Fri Aug 15, 2110
**Setting:** Chronos lakeshore city; the lakeshore checkpoint; the Chronos field hospital at the capital's eastern edge, as it is struck; the prison; the central plaza and the Lost Hour stone at 14:00
**Length:** ~3,600 words

## Purpose
This is what survives, counted person by person, three months on. Nobody is ruled on by a machine, and everybody has to decide what to keep. The last vignette gives the names to people who don't know whose song it is.

## Beats
- **Chronos, morning (Julian).** The count of the first human council is published: Chronos has elected people for the first time. **Julian is not on it and doesn't want to be.** One of its first acts **releases him from maximum recording**. He feels no difference, only knows it, as he knew the other. Adrian is beside him. Julian writes one line in the journal that is his own again, and closes it before the thought about Marcus forms.
- **The lakeshore checkpoint, midday (Dmitri).** Sokolov stops on her way back from the capital, as she always does. Formally: she has been offered the rank of **General**, and has refused. ***"I would like to stay where I am."*** Then, low, on the way back to the car, her real sentence: *"I was apparently very good at it."* Dmitri doesn't use a contraction. He counts the cars as he always has, and he counts one fewer brother, first by thirty seconds.
- **The field hospital, afternoon (Isaiah).** The Chronos field hospital is being struck to go home. In the **linen room**, where Tanaka read Principle Four aloud in April, Isaiah and Tanaka begin ***The Cost of Certainty*** together: his words and her case notes. He files the first page with a prefix, a sequence number and the date. She gives him one case note, unfinished at *Treatment:* as in April, and he leaves it unfinished on the page.
- **The prison (Maya).** Maya visits her father, who is back in prison. He is courteous and interested, pleased to see her. He asks one question: ***"Was it interesting?"*** She answers truthfully: ***"Yes."*** That evening she writes her monthly entry and writes neither *He didn't do it* nor *He is doing it now.* She writes the date, and leaves the line after it empty.
- **Wei (a page, no POV).** Presented only as a document, in a private hand, dated. The single line, exact:
  > *"They ended it themselves. That was in no one's design. Not even hers."*
  There is no scene around it, no reaction and no reader.
- **The central plaza, 14:00 (Marcus).** It is the 15th, and the plaza readings. The **Lost Hour stone** has **new names cut on its side**: the war's dead, from both shores and from the SFW, taken from the journals. Marcus finds *Alexei Volkov* and *Nora Chen* among thousands. Kira stands beside him. They are still together, and this time her hand finds his during the song. Chen Zhao's Commission of Rebuilding has its first sitting next week, and Marcus hasn't answered him.
- **The song.** **Old Songs**, officially dead, stands at the stone in the open air and sings **"The Names"**: *Alexei Volkov*, *Elise Vance*, *Nora Chen*, and the long list after them, every side. Use the exact lyrics (`old-songs-character.md` §3), one verse per name. Only a few verses need to be written out in full. **Children who don't know who he is join the refrain,** *name them, and they stay*, and go on singing it after he has stopped. Every phrase lifts like a question.

## Ending
The refrain, carried by voices that aren't his. Marcus has no fact entry for it.
**Final line:** ***"The machine is gone. The names remain."***
