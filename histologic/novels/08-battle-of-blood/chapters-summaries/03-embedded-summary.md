# Chapter 3: Embedded

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Thu Feb 20, 2110
**Setting:** The SFW beachhead on Veridica's occupied coast: the headquarters in a requisitioned coastal school a few kilometres inland; the rear area behind it; the study of Isaiah's memory palace, at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The witness is brought to the war as a condition of his asylum, and he is shown only what his hosts want seen. He meets the cousin Marcus never mentions. He hears one phrase that fits the letter he sent in December. And he understands that the objective has an address.

## Beats
- **The condition.** His asylum review was "brought forward" (Book 7 Epilogue). It has come back **conditional on embedding** with the SFW forces as *"the witness"*. He admires the precision of the word, as he once admired *conditional*. He came ashore on Tuesday with the second echelon. The coat hangs on him with its lining still sewn: his prints of Kira's photographs of copy VII, pp. 5–12, since Sep 30.
- **Filed on arrival,** with his prefix and the date: the school's classrooms, now map rooms; a child's drawing still pinned to one wall; SFW soldiers with no scars behind their ears; paper everywhere, because nothing here reports to anything.
- **The two marked heads.** In the whole headquarters only two people carry the thin white lines of an interface: Isaiah, and a woman in an SFW colonel's uniform. When the captured storm units are run forward (as on Feb 17, Ch 2), both of them are ordered back to the rear. A staff officer explains it courteously: they are the only people here a storm can reach.
- **General Omid Farahani,** about 55. He is courteous and precise and receives Isaiah for eleven minutes; Isaiah times it. He believes The Judge must be taken from those who misuse it, and he says so as a fact of procedure, not a boast. To Isaiah: *"You are here to see whether we fight cleanly. Write it if we don't."* Isaiah files the sentence.
- **Colonel Beatriz Martinez** is at the map table. She is cool with him and gives no sign of the room three floors down (Book 7 Ch 28), or of *Write carefully*. She is watching him, and he lets her. In public she says only, *"Mr. Okonkwo. You'll be shown what you need."* He hears how she says *need*.
- **The unnamed attaché** from the editor's lunch (Book 7 Ch 5) passes through with a folder under his arm. Isaiah notes it and doesn't look at the cover.
- **The colonel with the scars.** Late afternoon, behind the school, in the wind. She introduces herself: **Colonel Nora Chen**, SFW intelligence, 38. Isaiah knows the face before the name: it is Marcus's mouth, Marcus's way of looking at a thing as if checking it. Her line, exact: ***"I'm his cousin. I left in '95. Tell him I'm sorry about the record."*** Isaiah doesn't ask which record, and she doesn't explain. He files it verbatim.
- **Nora, sure of herself.** She is quick, warm and convinced. She believes the SFW is here to free Veridicans from the machine that decides for them, and she wants the witness to see it. She speaks of her father only obliquely ("I had a reason to leave. They corrected it."). Keep her sympathetic. She is not a villain.
- **The question.** Isaiah asks, as a journalist, how the Sovereignty knew where to land. She smiles, a little proud: *"We had help."* Then, of the Veridican source she handles: ***"A source at the highest table."*** Martinez, across the yard, has heard. She doesn't turn her head.
- **The map room at dusk.** On the operations map Isaiah sees no ports ringed, no factories and no power stations. The axes of advance run inland along the roads toward the capital and converge on one marker in its centre. He can't read the SFW labels, and he doesn't need to.

## Ending
**Night, the study of the palace.** He files the day in order, and lays three lines side by side: his own letter of Dec 16, *Someone in Veridica wants to be invaded*; Farahani's broadcast, *We are not here for your cities*; and Nora's *a source at the highest table*. He writes the one conclusion he can defend: ***The objective is not a city.*** Beneath it, a smaller line: *It is something in one.*

## Notes
- **(Twist 1 clue)** "A source at the highest table" (plot v2 Ch 3; README decision 9). **Leave it ambiguous:** a reader may hear the cabinet table, or a mother's dinner table. Nobody glosses it. Isaiah doesn't guess who it is.
- **Nora's line is exact.** "The record" is left undefined for Ch 27. Isaiah can't send the message yet, and nobody in Part One tells Marcus.
- **Nora has an interface.** She defected from Veridica in 2095, and removal kills (`morally-ambiguous-characters.md` §4). The SFW treats her, like Isaiah, as someone a storm or seeding can reach. That stays true in later chapters.
- **Don't reveal the target.** Isaiah sees a marker in the capital's centre, and doesn't know it is the Spire. Nora shows him the packets only in Ch 10. Kira's analysis (Ch 5) and Marcus (Ch 8) get there from the Veridican side.
- **Martinez** doesn't see Principle Two here. She learns the design later.
- **Farahani** says only this to Isaiah; his one line *to Veridica* is the broadcast (Ch 1).
- **SFW:** no interfaces, conventional weapons, paper records. The school, the attaché and the staff officer are unnamed.
- **Isaiah's voice:** editorial reasoning; he files with a prefix (*IO-*) and a date; he never claims more than he knows.
