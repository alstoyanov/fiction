# Chapter 16: Not Before

**POV:** Mikhail "Old Songs" Petrov
**Date:** Wed Apr 9, 2110 (afternoon to about 01:00 on Thu Apr 10)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse in the border zone by the lakeshore, reached from the besieged capital by the western corridor: the kitchen and the stove with the cracked door; the barn, where the coast refugees sleep; the yard at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Vance refuses the page again, and this time she says when she will give it, and why not now. Then, at night, she gives the old man the one thing she won't give anyone else, in the only form she trusts him with. He doesn't open it. He keeps it the way he has kept everything: in a tune.

## Beats
- **Afternoon, the stove.** He has been 72 since March. He opens the damper two fingers, puts on potatoes, and hums. The shelling is a long way east, over the capital, and the corridor road is under fire in daylight. **Vance** sits at the table in a blanket. She is 74 and very frail now. The tremor shakes the cup even with both hands on it, and she has stopped pretending it doesn't.
- **Kira arrives** by the western corridor, which **Dmitri's volunteer company** has held since Wed Apr 2, in a Commission car with a volunteer driver (unnamed). The car has a shrapnel scar on the door. Dmitri's people waved her through at the last checkpoint, and Dmitri sent a message with her, formal and without contractions: the corridor closes at dusk. She brings the capital in pieces, and he takes each one without comment:
  - the city calm under the guns;
  - Pell's first schedule;
  - Marcus at **0.59**, so he can't risk more than day visits, and today not even that;
  - Kovač gone from the intake.
- **The ask.** Kira asks for the page, plainly, the way she asks a system for a log (Ch 8: *"There is a page"*). Marcus sent her, and so did the siege.
- **Vance, exact:** ***"I'll give it when they try to give it away. Not before. If I give it now, you'll use it to decide."*** Kira asks, "Give *what* away? Who is *they*?" Vance doesn't answer. She holds the cup to still it. He hums at the stove, neither the rising yes nor the falling no. Kira doesn't press a second time. She files it, and he watches her do it.
- **Evening, the barn. "The Names."** The corridor closes after dark, so Kira stays. The refugees gather as they do every night. He sings "The Names" with the new dead from the journals, one verse each:
  - a Chronos orderly from the field hospital;
  - the woman from the coast cell who died in the rail yard (Ch 13), given to him by Elena's runner;
  - names from the siege.
  Quote only the refrain, exactly: *"The record may forget them, / the song will not forget; / name them, and they stay."* Kira sings the refrain with the barn. The song is as long as the dead, and it is getting longer.
- **Night, the kitchen.** Kira sleeps upstairs. Vance asks him to sit, lamp low, stove ticking. She speaks precisely, in short sentences. The rest of her copy is "where I left it". She won't write it down. *"You taught them to write everything down. This one I want kept the old way."* He understands that she means him.
- **The riddle.** She says it **once**, slowly, in rhyme, exactly:
  > *Look where they swept the shelf of us,*
  > *behind the number no one reads;*
  > *the old man peeled his apple there —*
  > *ask him the number. Ask him twice.*
  He says it back to her once, word for word. She nods. For one line she is twenty-seven again, arguing with him about rhyme: ***"It scans. You always said mine didn't."*** Then she isn't. *"For when I'm not here to be asked."*
- **He doesn't open it.** His POV hears every word and **lets none of them open**. He has had forty years' practice at hearing a thing and not looking at it: what you look at gets heat, and heat is what the machines search for. So he doesn't turn it over, doesn't guess and doesn't try. The narration mustn't follow any line of the riddle into memory, or into any place or person it might mean.
- **The yard.** 00:40. It is cold, with real fog coming off the lake and the far guns quiet for once. He fits the four lines to **the Lakeshore Air**, the tune that already carries the ferry verses, the lullaby and "Given Gently". He sings them under his breath with words, because here he is free. Now it is kept.

## Ending
He sings it through once more, alone in the fog, so that he won't lose it. The last line lifts at the end, as every phrase of that tune does, like a question. Nobody in the house hears it.

## Notes
- **(Twist 3 clue)** Vance's line is exact (plot v2): "when they try to give it away". Kira doesn't understand it, and nobody guesses the surrender. **Nothing of Principle Seven's text or mechanics appears.**
- **The riddle's wording is canon** (README decision 16). Quote it exactly, once in speech. **Do not solve it or hint at the answer.** No gloss on any line, and no memory, place or person attached to it in anyone's POV. The Ch 29 and Epilogue writers should hold the same rule.
- **Old Songs doesn't try yet** (README decision 16). His reason is in-world: the blind spot (`neural-interfaces-and-correction.md`), since keeping a thing unexamined keeps it cold. **He tells no one** about the riddle in this chapter.
- **Voice rules** (README decision 31): apart from saying the riddle back to her, he speaks no lines of his own here. He hums, and sings with words in freedom. Every tune ends on a question. **The riddle is Vance's; he has none of his own.**
- **Vance:** precise and brief, with no self-pity. She is 27 again for one line (her voice rule 6). "It scans" refers to their 2063 arguments about rhyme (Book 5 Ch 12). It is characterisation, not a clue.
- **"The Names" lyrics:** only the exact refrain is quoted, and the dead stay unnamed on the page. Alexei is **not** in it yet (Ch 20).
- **Kira** stays overnight because the corridor closes after dark (Dmitri's company holds it and sets its hours; Dmitri himself isn't on the page). Her index isn't affected, since the farmhouse isn't in the record.
