# Chapter 5: The Judge at War

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Thu Feb 27 (night) – Fri Feb 28, 2110
**Setting:** The war coordination room in the Factorepo Spire, where The Judge's defence outputs come up on the panels; Kira's desk there
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The machine they have spent five books fighting turns out to be the best ally Veridica has. Kira watches it save a town. She also watches it show her, by pure arithmetic, what the enemy wants. Then the man who once deleted her fact brings her a map, without comment, and the map is the wrong kind.

## Beats
- **Opening in incident format.** *Feb 27, 2110. 22:10. Spire, war coordination. Staff on floor: thirty-one. Outputs pending: four hundred and six.* Kira and **Nikolai** have run the room's link between The Judge and the commands since Feb 12. She counts nodes when the room gets loud.
- **How The Judge fights.** It has the national network, which means every road, every relay and **every citizen's location** by telemetry. It moves evacuees round columns, times convoys to the minute, and routes ambulances by where the wounded actually are. Its language never changes: formal, probabilistic, exact. It gives only determinations, which officials read out. **Its limits:** the SFW have no interfaces and are dark to it. It infers them from what they do to people who report. The coastal relays have been dark since the landing week. In the occupied strip The Judge is blind, and it says so as a number.
- **The town.** At 22:40 a determination comes up for a market town of about eleven thousand on the river road, unnamed: *Probability of enemy advance through the town within 30 hours: 0.84. Recommended: full evacuation by the north road, 23:30–04:00.* The command is reluctant; the town is a defensive line on paper. Kira pushes it through. All night she watches eleven thousand points of light move north in the order The Judge set. **Fri 06:15:** the SFW enters an empty town. There's no house-to-house fighting and no civilian dead. Kira logs it: *Town: evacuated. Civilian casualties: none recorded.* She is proud of it, and uneasy at how easily the machine moved them all.
- **The pattern.** Through the morning she and Nikolai run The Judge's analysis of every SFW movement since Feb 12. It gives the projected objectives as probabilities: *Ports: 0.05. Industrial districts: 0.04. Capital: 0.79.* Kira draws the projected axes on a paper map with a ruler, because a line she has drawn is one she can check. The lines don't bend toward the harbours or the factories. **They converge on the capital, and inside it on the central district, and inside that on the Spire.** She looks up at the ceiling of the building she is sitting in. Nikolai looks at her hands.
- **What they don't say.** Neither of them says *core* aloud. Kira begins a thought about what the core holds now, since October, and doesn't finish it, because the room needs her. *(Ch 11 lands it.)*
- **Evidence it wants it intact.** The Judge's damage log: SFW aircraft have hit bridges, rail yards and coastal relays. **They haven't touched the capital's trunk cables or the Spire's power corridors**, although both were within reach. The Judge reports it without inference. Kira infers it.
- **13:20, Wei.** **Thomas Wei**, Head of Emergency Information, crosses the floor with nothing in his hands but a folded paper sheet. He doesn't stop to talk to anyone. He puts the sheet on her desk, squares it to the edge, and says: ***"You'll want this."*** He leaves. There's no explanation, and she doesn't ask for one.
- **The map.** It is a printed **maintenance map of the core**, the Spire's core levels, which lie at and below plaza level, from Systems Architecture's archive. There is no author and no date. She knows these corridors from her Spire years (sixtieth floor, Book 4) only as far as the doors she was allowed through. It shows racks, cable galleries, freight lifts and service doors. One dashed line, keyed **M**, runs from a service door in the disused transit tunnel under the Spire to the core's lower cable gallery: a **maintenance route**.

## Ending
She reads the legend, the engineer's habit. A defence map would show approaches, choke points and fields of fire, where you stop someone getting in. This legend shows **how to get the core out**: *withdrawal routes, storage modules*; *disconnection order*; *freight lift 3, rated for rack load*; *route M, maintenance access*. It isn't a map for keeping the core. **It shows how to remove it.** She folds it along its old creases, puts it inside her jacket, and doesn't look toward the lifts Wei took.

## Notes
- **Wei's map** (README decision 4): **Fri Feb 28**. His only words are *"You'll want this."* **Route M** is the maintenance route Marcus and Kira use on May 21 (Ch 27). Its start (a service door in the disused transit tunnel under the Spire) and end (the core's lower cable gallery, beneath the core hall) are new and binding, so the core sits at or below plaza level (Ch 11, 27, 28 agree). Freight lift 3 and the legend's wording are new.
- **(Closing-turn texture, light)** A map that shows how the core can be *moved*. Don't underline it.
- **Wei ambiguity:** there's no hint whether the map is meant to help deny the core, help the capture, or something else. No interior, and one beat (the squared edge). His walk isn't described. Kira doesn't confront him.
- **Kira tells Marcus about the map off the page** before Ch 8, and Nikolai sees it tonight. Nobody tells the cabinet.
- **The Judge:** no voice, determinations only; it infers but never guesses aloud. Its coordination is superb (plot v2). The town and the figures are new. "Coastal relays dark since the landing week" is new and matches Ch 7.
- **Don't** have Kira or Nikolai conclude that capturing the Spire captures Chronos. That is Ch 11.
- **Timing:** the chapter opens on the night of Thu Feb 27 so that the town's evacuation can be shown. Wei's beat and the ending fall on **Fri Feb 28**, as the README requires.
