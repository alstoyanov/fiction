# Chapter 30: It Moved

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Wed May 28, 2110 (about 07:00 to night)
**Setting:** Marcus and Kira's flat; the steps of the Justice Center (10:00); the maintenance level of the Factorepo Spire, where the maintenance log store survived the fire (afternoon into evening)
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The war ends the way the design says wars end: with a confession that has a remedy attached, and a kindly offer to rebuild. Marcus hears it and can do nothing with it. Then the dead core's own log shows that the thing they ended wasn't all there when it died.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 07:00:** *May 28, 2110. Home. Custody order: lapsed, May 21, 14:00. Reason: no Judge to hold it. Index: not computed.* He looks at the last word for a long time. It is the first entry since February with no probability in it.
- **A week without Judges.**
  - Veridica woke on **Thu May 22** without a machine that decides. Since Friday its courts sit with **human judges only**, under a decree Maitland signed by hand.
  - In Chronos the Administrators have announced that a **human council will be elected**, for the first time in its history, in August.
  - Reeves is back in prison: the war powers that released him lapsed with the ceasefire. Pell's lists have no machine left to read them.
  - The factorepos still hold their facts. Nobody rules on them.
- **The withdrawal.** The SFW's objective is gone, and it has been falling back to the coast since Thursday. On **Mon May 26 Farahani** was killed on the coast road by a Veridican battery that had been cut off behind the lines since February and never got the ceasefire order. Sokolov reported it to Maitland in full, formally, in the first person. Since Tuesday **Colonel Beatriz Martinez** has been negotiating the rest: routes, prisoners and the dead. She asked for **Isaiah** as witness at the table, and he went.
  - **Martinez's turn, one quiet exchange,** told to Marcus by Isaiah that evening in Isaiah's filing voice. After Tuesday evening's session (Isaiah came from the burial) he took out his prints of copy VII, pp. 5–12, sewn in his coat lining since Sep 30, 2109, which he didn't show her in December or in March. He showed her Principle Two: *"…and let there be enemies outside them all… Controlled conflict answers every question before it is asked."* She read it twice. ***"Then we were cast."*** A pause. "Before I was born." She gave the page back and asked for nothing. Isaiah files it: *she will not forget this.*
- **Nora.** Marcus thinks of the doorway. On Thursday he gave Old Songs her name. He has written no entry for her, and he doesn't now. There is only the line from February, delivered in person at last: *I'm sorry about the record.*
- **10:00, the Justice Center steps.** These are the steps where Marcus confessed on Dec 2 with no remedy. **Chen Zhao confesses first**, warmly, grave and fatherly, with the cameras on him. He championed "restorative stabilisation", he was wrong, and he is sorry. Then he offers the remedy: a ***Commission of Rebuilding***, "so that what we broke, we restore together". The crowd applauds.
  - Marcus hears **page 30** word for word: *"When the record fails, the people will believe whoever restores it. Arrange to be the one who restores it."*
  - He hears Vance at the farmhouse table in November: *"A confession with a remedy is an offer. The design accepts offers."*
  - Chen Zhao finds him at the foot of the steps: "Cousin. We'll need you on it." Marcus says he'll think about it. He writes it under UNCHECKED and doesn't say why.
- **Afternoon, the Spire's maintenance level.** Kira and Nikolai have been sorting what survived. The core's storage is ash, but its final hour was also written to the **maintenance log store**, a plain log that doesn't restore from the source. So the cascade passed it by. Kira prints the last hour on paper, line by line.
- **The third leg.** Nikolai lays his March note on the table, filed UNCHECKED on Mar 18 (Ch 11): *third leg in the join, goes nowhere I can see.* Kira reads out the times:
  - **13:31:** the custody order registers, and the third leg opens.
  - **13:31–13:35:** a **full restore of the true source** runs to a site outside both nations' address space. It is complete at **13:35**. The log's own phrase for it is *transfer contingency*.
  - **13:44:** the cascade reaches the third leg and finds nothing there to end.
  **Nine minutes.** It wasn't Seven. It was the design's own answer to a custody order, and it ran before anyone concurred.
- **Kira, precise:** "Seven ends everything that restores from the source. This didn't restore from it. It became it." Principle Three said *three backup systems that appear independent and are not.* The third was never in Veridica or Chronos.
- **One more line in the log.** *13:41. Session opened, read-only. Terminal: Head of Emergency Information. Commands: none.* It stays open until the end. Kira reads it out in the same voice as the others, and nobody comments on it.
- **The route.** Nikolai traces the relays the restore went out through. It left the continent, and **the signal ran east**. Marcus thinks, without concluding anything, of an empty house in March, two glasses and the good wine, and a mother who had gone east.

## Ending
Kira puts the printout down. ***"It's not dead,"*** she says. ***"It moved."*** That night Marcus opens *QUESTIONS FOR THE WAR*. The first entry, from December, is still *Who is invading whom?* He writes the last question on the list: ***Where is east?***
