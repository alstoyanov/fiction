# Chapter 1: Questions for the War

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Wed Feb 12 – Thu Feb 13, 2110
**Setting:** Marcus and Kira's flat in the capital (before dawn, Wed); the streets; the First Minister's office and the war cabinet room on the top floor of the Cabinet Office (Thu); the flat that night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The war arrives as a fact entry. The state that fought Book 7 with deception now asks Marcus to help it fight with blood, because he "understands The Judge". The Judge turns out to be a superb general. Marcus keeps his list, and the enemy's first words to Veridica make the list's first question sharper.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry,** woken by the national alert at 05:20 on Wed Feb 12: *Feb 12, 2110. 05:20. Capital. Alert: enemy landing, Atlantic coast. Involved parties:* He stops. He doesn't know who they are yet.
- **Kira** is called to the Spire at 06:00 to help run The Judge's war coordination with Nikolai (see Ch 5). They are strained and still together (Book 7). At the door she says only, "Keep the list." He has it in his pocket, with Isaiah's letter: *Someone in Veridica wants to be invaded.* He has shown the letter to no one but Kira.
- **Wednesday, the city.** It is orderly: sirens, queues and screens. Nobody panics yet. On the panels The Judge is already routing the coastal evacuation. Every road inland is timed, and every town is emptied in sequence. It knows **where every citizen is**, and so it can move them without jams. Marcus admires it, and he notices that he does.
- **The first bulletin, 20:00 Wed:** *Dead since 04:40: 612, of whom 88 civilians. Enemy dead: no telemetry. Not recorded.* The Judge counts only what reports to it. Marcus reads the last line twice.
- **Thu Feb 13, 10:00, the war cabinet.** It meets in the room beside Maitland's office, at a long table with a map panel. Present:
  - **First Minister Oskar Maitland** (big, tired, well tailored, chin on his fist);
  - **Minister Chen Zhao** (69), with tea in a glass, warm and grave, who calls Marcus "cousin";
  - **Thomas Wei**, now **Head of Emergency Information**, courteous, who waits to be asked;
  - **Undersecretary Iver Pell**, now at **Public Order**.
  Sokolov reports from the coast by link, in the register of an order. The SFW is ashore and moving inland. She mentions no gap and no battery by number.
- **The appointment.** Maitland makes Marcus **civilian adviser** to the war cabinet: *"You understand The Judge."* Pell says, for the minutes, that the adviser's index is **0.57**. Maitland: "I know. I read it." Chen Zhao: "Our cousin has always been expensive, and worth it." Marcus accepts. He notes that Wei and Chen Zhao, absent on Dec 23, are both present now. He doesn't know who in the building Isaiah's letter is about, and says nothing.
- **What The Judge can do.** The panel shows the national network at war: every street, every citizen's location, every road. Evacuations are timed to the minute, and roads are cleared for columns. Its outputs are formal and probabilistic: *Probability of enemy advance on the coast road within 24 hours: 0.88.* It gives no voice, only determinations. **Show its limits too:** the SFW soldiers are dark to it. It infers them from what they do to Veridicans, and the empty spaces in its map are where the enemy is.
- **Marcus's list.** Under *QUESTIONS FOR THE WAR* the first entry, *Who is invading whom?*, is still the only one. In the cabinet he adds nothing. He watches who speaks for The Judge: Pell reads its numbers as certainties, and Maitland reads them as odds.
- **Wei's one beat.** An SFW broadcast is expected that evening, and the cabinet debates whether to carry it. Pell wants it blocked. Wei, mildly: *"Let them hear it. People fight better when they know who is speaking."* Maitland agrees. That is the only beat; no interior.
- **Thu evening, the flat.** Kira comes home late from the Spire, gray with tiredness, and they watch together.

## Ending
**21:00.** On every screen in Veridica: **General Omid Farahani**, SFW commander, about 55, in plain field uniform, speaking **Veridican** carefully, with an accent. He gives his name and rank, and one sentence: ***"We are not here for your cities."*** The broadcast ends. Marcus takes out the list and writes the second entry under the first: ***Then what are they here for?***

## Notes
- **(Book 8 set-up)** Farahani's line is his **one line to Veridica** (README decision 2). Keep the broadcast to his name, rank and that sentence.
- **The cabinet is exactly** Maitland, Chen Zhao, Wei and Pell (README decision 3). Marcus is appointed on **Thu Feb 13**.
- **The index is 0.57** (Book 7 Ch 25). It doesn't move in this chapter; it next moves on Apr 4 (0.59). Pell's remark begins his thread toward the lists (Ch 15, 21).
- **Isaiah's letter** (Book 7 Ch 28–30) stays in Marcus's pocket. Maitland isn't told.
- **Wei:** no POV and no interior, with one beat of double meaning (Principle Five's "external threats unify" sits under it; don't cite it). Nothing about the map yet (Ch 5).
- **The bulletin figures are new.** "Enemy dead: no telemetry. Not recorded" sets up Old Songs singing SFW names from paper (Ch 4). Keep later figures modest and consistent: the dead reach the tens of thousands only by May 16 (Ch 24).
- **Don't mention Nora Chen.** Marcus has filed his cousin away since 2095 (`morally-ambiguous-characters.md` §4). She is Ch 3's.
- **Nothing is revealed about Twist 1.** Sokolov gives no detail of the gap in cabinet.
