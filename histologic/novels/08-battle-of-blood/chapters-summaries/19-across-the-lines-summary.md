# Chapter 19: Across the Lines

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Wed Apr 23, 2110 (morning to 23:58)
**Setting:** The SFW forward headquarters east of the capital, in a requisitioned logistics depot; the occupied eastern suburbs at night (gardens, a rail cutting, a burned-out bus depot); the **overpass on the eastern ring road**; allotments on the Veridican side, and a volunteer picket
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The witness goes back to the side that expelled him, carrying the other side's secret. It costs the man who has spent his whole life getting people out. Alexei's death is an extraction, the thing he was for.

## Beats
- **Morning, the depot.** Isaiah is "the witness", filed and courteous. Since Mar 14 the photographs of Nora's packets have been sewn into his coat's lining (Ch 10), as in 2106. Farahani passes in the corridor, precise and polite. Martinez says *"Mr. Okonkwo"* and nothing more. He can't classify her look, and he notes that.
- **Nora.** She brings him coffee at noon and talks about the Spire as the end of the war. Of Marcus: *"When it's over I'll tell him myself."* She believes every word. He hears it in how she says *free*, the way people say *home*. He files her under *the sincere* and feels the weight of what he is carrying out of her building.
- **21:00.** He walks out on his standing witness's pass toward a forward aid post he has visited before, and doesn't arrive. At the burned-out bus depot **Alexei** is waiting in the dark with two of Elena's people, unnamed: a woman with a rifle, and a boy of about nineteen with the radio. Alexei: *"Mr. Okonkwo. You've sewn your coat again."*
- **The walk.** Gardens, fences, the rail cutting. Alexei is warm and wry, and he talks low the whole way because Isaiah needs it: Wing C, the soup Old Songs dropped, Dmitri being "the bossiest". He asks once whether Isaiah is sure. Isaiah: *"It was arithmetic."* Nikolai's voice comes through the boy's radio from war coordination, as the fixed point of the route. Beyond the siege line the network doesn't reach, so the Judge can't see any of them.
- **The ring road.** It's six lanes on an embankment, and it is the SFW's line. The underpasses are watched, and the one crossing is a service **overpass**, its concrete parapet chest-high. Across it is Veridican ground: allotments and a volunteer picket.
- **23:38, the flare.** They're halfway across when an SFW gun opens up from a position on the ring road below. They're pinned behind the parapet. Isaiah sees it as arithmetic: four people, one gun, forty metres of open deck to the far steps. Someone has to hold the gun's attention.
- **The choice.** Alexei takes the woman's rifle. *"We don't leave people. That's the whole point of me."* Isaiah starts to argue in order, like an editorial. Alexei quotes him back to himself: *"It's arithmetic, Isaiah. Most hard things are."* He sends the woman and the boy with him.
- **23:41. The last words, exact:** ***"Tell Elena I love her. Tell Dima he was first by thirty seconds, and I never minded."*** Then he moves along the parapet and fires, and the gun follows him.
- **The far side.** They reach the steps, the embankment and the allotments. Isaiah counts, because counting is what he has: *forty seconds of his rifle; then the other gun alone; then nothing.* The boy calls into the radio. Nikolai: *"Lyosha?"* There is no answer. There's no trace either, because the network ends at the line.

## Ending
**23:58.** The volunteer picket takes them in among the cabbages. Before he says anything, Isaiah takes out his paper and writes the entry, with its prefix and time: *IO. 23 APRIL. 23:41. Overpass, eastern ring road. A. Volkov:* and then the words, exactly, so that nothing is lost or read out of order. He stands in Veridica, alive, with the intelligence sewn into his coat, and he doesn't feel his hands.

## Notes
- **README 19 is exact:** Wed Apr 23, an overpass on the eastern ring road, covering Isaiah's crossing. The last words are verbatim. Every time in the chapter is before midnight.
- **The misdirection pays off:** Ch 13 had Alexei survive the artillery raid, and here the death comes as an extraction. It reverses "I have never once left someone at the fence" (`volkov-triplets.md`). The parapet echoes the triplets' rooftop (Alexei "in the middle on the parapet"), but **don't gloss it**. Isaiah doesn't know the roof.
- **Death confirmation is deliberately delayed.** The Judge can't see beyond the siege line, so Alexei is missing, not recorded dead, until Ch 20. Nobody sees him fall.
- **The packets** go to Marcus on Thu Apr 24 (offstage). Marcus keeps a paper copy (Ch 21, Ch 27).
- **Martinez stays ambiguous.** Don't say whether she knew he was leaving.
- **Voice:** counts, classifies, files; never claims more than he saw.
