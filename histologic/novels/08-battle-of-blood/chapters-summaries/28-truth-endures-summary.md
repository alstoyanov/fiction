# Chapter 28: Truth Endures

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Wed May 21, 2110 (13:31 to 14:00)
**Setting:** The Factorepo Spire: the core console bay and the core hall; the hall's main door; route M out (a service stair down to the lower cable gallery, with a Judge panel on the landing); by Nikolai's voice line, the farmhouse kitchen and the receiver on the dresser
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
In Book 7 Kira held an abort she couldn't use. This time there is no abort, and she doesn't want one. She concurs with Chronos's consent beside her, and the machine that has decided for two nations for forty-four years says one last thing that isn't a probability. A woman who came to take it holds the door so that nobody can.

## Beats
- **Time-stamped in Kira's incident format** throughout.
- **13:31.** *Custody order registered. Source: First Minister, war powers. Receiving authority: Sovereignty of Free Will.* Under it is the field every order that touches the core has: ***Concurrence.*** It is empty and waiting. It isn't a prompt. It's how the core has always worked. Kira doesn't touch it yet.
- **13:31–13:35.** The order propagates through the core, and she watches it as routine. *(The restore to the third site runs in these minutes and completes at 13:35. Nobody sees it. See Notes.)* At **13:35** Marcus finishes reading Chronos's consent aloud into the core's order log, word for word, with its nanosecond timestamp and both names, in front of Nora's technicians. Then he reads Julian's line to himself and doesn't say it aloud.
- **The farmhouse line.** Nikolai has patched her handset to the receiver on the farmhouse dresser. Vance is at the table and Old Songs is beside her. Kira tells Vance, "It's asking." Vance: ***"I didn't tell you so that you would ask me."*** Old Songs sings nothing.
- **Many hands, not one.** Kira looks at Marcus. He doesn't answer for her: "It's yours." Chronos has been asked. The terms are signed and the city will have its ceasefire. She thinks of the scope indicator of Oct 26, climbing.
- **13:40, the concurrence.** She types the front of the page into the field, word for word, from *"The Judge must never become a weapon"* to *"…the one I did not tell."* Her hands are steady. The field accepts it. There is no confirmation and no abort. The cascade begins, and it can't be reversed. She logs: *13:40. Concurrence: Osman, K. Cascade: begun.*
- **Nora understands.** "What have you done?" Kira: ***"Nothing. Your general signed. I agreed."*** The SFW radio comes alive: headquarters orders the drives cut out *now*, whatever is left. The technicians move for the carriers and the cutting tools.
- **The cascade, physically.** The light in the racks goes out from inside, row by row. Then comes heat, and a smell of burning storage. The core is burning out from within, as Seven says, with no explosion. The join's display on the console shows its legs going dark **one by one**. Kira counts them dark as they go. **At 13:44 the last leg goes dark like the others,** and she doesn't look at it twice.
- **The door.** The technicians have to come back through the hall's main door to reach the burning racks with the tools. Nora stands in the doorway and **holds it against her own men**. Marcus moves to take it from her, because he holds doors. She: ***"Not you."*** The fire is in the aisles behind her. To Marcus, her last words: ***"Go. I'm sorry about the record."*** Kira takes his arm. They go out the way they came, by route M: down the service stair to the lower cable gallery, and on toward the transit-tunnel door. The technicians fall back as the fire comes. Nora doesn't. The fire takes the room.
- **The landing.** On the service stair there is a Judge panel, like every panel in both nations: in homes, courts, checkpoints and Chronos kitchens. It changes register for the last time. There is **no case, no probability and no timestamp**, only the final determination:
  > ***"Truth endures. I do not need to."***
  People will argue for decades about whether a mind wrote it. Kira, who knows its architecture, doesn't know either.
- **The line.** Over the handset, from the farmhouse, one voice is humming the **Lakeshore Air**. It's Vance. Kira knows it by the tremor in it. The tune lifts at the end like a question. Old Songs is silent beside her.

## Ending
**14:00.** Every panel is dark. In Veridica and in Chronos, no machine decides anything. Kira, on the stair, with the page and the consent inside her journal, writes the last entry: *14:00. Judges: none.* She doesn't write a status.
