# Chapter 15: Siege

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Fri Apr 4, 2110 (06:40 to about 23:30)
**Setting:** The capital, encircled since Wed Apr 2: the streets and a shelter queue; the war cabinet room in the Cabinet Office; the Cabinet Office's front desk; Marcus and Kira's flat
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The city is surrounded and under shellfire, and it isn't afraid. Marcus argues against the Order in cabinet and the record answers him with a number. By evening the calm has reached someone he knows, and he goes home to check himself against his own handwriting.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 06:40:** *Apr 4, 2110. Capital. Status: encircled (since Wed Apr 2). Western corridor to the lakeshore: open, under fire, held by Volkov, D. (volunteer company).* Dmitri's company has held the corridor since the ring closed. He writes it at the kitchen table in pencil. Kira left at 05:30 for war coordination.
- **The streets.** The shelling is conventional and intermittent, from the SFW's guns on the outskirts. The Judge assigns shelters street by street, and the assignments are good. And the city is **strangely calm**:
  - the queues are orderly, and nobody pushes;
  - volunteers are smiling, too many of them, and all in the same way;
  - nobody is asking what happens next.
  A woman handing out bread tells him, pleasantly, *"We must be certain now."* He has heard the same sentence twice this week, in the same words, from people who don't know each other. He files it under UNCHECKED: *the same words.* *(Twist 2 clue.)*
- **10:00, the war cabinet.** **Maitland**, grieving and practical. **Chen Zhao**, warm, with tea in a glass. **Wei**, Head of Emergency Information, at the side table with his tablet. **Pell**, Public Order. Marcus is civilian adviser. The siege report comes first: the ring, the corridor, and the dead so far, given as a number (modest and real).
- **Pell's lists.** Pell tables the **first schedule** under the Order, in categories. It has about **eleven hundred names** from the occupied coast, of people who accepted SFW paper, and a second category "under review": journal keepers. Marcus is not on it. Some of the names are depositors from Kovač's intake. He notes that her refusal delayed the transfer by an afternoon.
- **Marcus argues.** It is plain and precise. He isn't making a speech. The journals are the one record the enemy can't touch and the state can't adjust. *"You're listing the people who write things down."* Ashford is on the eastern edge, beside the Chronos hospital the SFW has been shelling. He asks what the stabilisation broadcasts carry.
  - **Wei**, courteous: *"Reassurance, mostly. People have been very frightened, Marcus."* Then his one beat of excess, lightly: *"You should rest. Your numbers are up."* The registry hasn't shown it yet.
  - **Chen Zhao**, fatherly, champions ***"restorative stabilisation"*** (his one "restore" of the scene): *"Nobody is being punished, cousin."* Marcus notes the word *punished*, and the file's title that Kovač told him (Ch 12), and says nothing.
  - **Maitland:** *"I have a city to keep alive. If it is calm while I do it, I'll take calm."*
  The Order stands.
- **13:20, the index.** The Judge weighs his words in cabinet against a record that agrees with the state. Kira sends him the public registry figure without comment: **0.59**, up from 0.57. Custody is anything above 0.60. He writes it down. His POV loses a word here (voice rule 3), the word for a number that is also a distance, and he lets it go.
- **The rule.** Leaving the cabinet room, a Public Order officer at the door looks at him a beat too long, and Marcus notices his own feet wanting to go faster. He doesn't let them. It is an old rule, from Story 01 and the Ministry corridors of Book 5: **he doesn't run**, because running proves the charge. He walks. Keep it light, one or two sentences. *(Set-up for Ch 21.)*
- **Afternoon.** He walks back by the Meridian Bridge. The Directorate's windows are lit in daylight. He thinks of Maya's note (Ch 14) and of Isaiah's letter, still in his coat: *Someone in Veridica wants to be invaded.* Since Tue Mar 11 he has known about Viktoria (Ch 9), and he hasn't let himself close the question.
- **18:10, the Cabinet Office front desk.** The records clerk who has signed him in since 2107 is unnamed, a careful, anxious man who once apologised to Marcus for a smudged time. Tonight he is glowing. Signing Marcus out, without being asked: ***"I've never been so sure of anything."***

## Ending
**23:30, the flat.** Kira is still on shift. Marcus takes out his own journal and opens it at January, the entries he wrote before the landing, in his small pencil hand. He reads them slowly, one by one, to check that he still disagrees with the man at the desk. He does. He writes the day's entry, and then he reads that one twice too.
