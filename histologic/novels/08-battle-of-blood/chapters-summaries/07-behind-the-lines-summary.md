# Chapter 7: Behind the Lines

**POV:** Elena Volkov
**Date:** Wed Mar 5, 2110 (morning to night)
**Setting:** The occupied coast, behind the SFW beachhead: a fishing town under occupation; a safe loft above a net store; the coast road where it crosses a culvert between salt marsh and dunes; a ruined boathouse after dark
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Behind the lines, the enemy is disciplined, polite, and handing out pencils. Elena fights the occupation that gives Veridicans what she spent her life trying to give them. An ambush takes a courier, and in his case is a document that should not exist.

## Beats
- **Elena counts first.** *Cell strength: eleven. Present: nine. Alexei: here.* She is 46, hoarse and controlled, and she carries a notebook she never writes in. Since the landing week her cells have worked the occupied strip with Alexei.
- **The coast under occupation.**
  - **The relays are dark.** The SFW cut the coastal nodes, so for the first time in their lives the people here receive no broadcast. They walk about in a silence inside their own heads. Some find it unbearable. Some sleep for the first time in years. Nobody in the strip is tracked, including her cells.
  - **The SFW are disciplined.** They pay for bread, and they punish their own for theft. Their patrols are courteous. They carry paper everything: orders, maps, identity tags on cords.
  - **The paper.** At the town hall the SFW hand out **paper and pencils** to any Veridican who asks, with a printed card in Veridican: ***WRITE YOUR OWN HISTORY.*** The queue goes round the square.
  - **The irony is complete, and Elena sees all of it.** This is what she ran a resistance for: people keeping their own record, by hand, unchecked by any Judge. The invader is giving it away for nothing, and it works. She watches a woman she recruited in 2107 come out of the hall holding a pencil like a gift.
- **Alexei** is wry and quick, and asks the question she doesn't want to hear: *"What if they're the good ones, Aunt Lena?"* She: *"Then they'll forgive us."* Don't foreshadow his death heavily. He is tired, funny and alive.
- **The ambush.** Her cells have been watching the coast road for eight days: a courier rides the same route every second day, a motorcycle with a light escort vehicle. **Dusk, 17:40, the culvert.** Real weather: a raw wind off the salt marsh and fog in the dunes. The ambush is fast and flat. Elena counts it as it happens.
  - **The escort's two soldiers are killed.** She takes their paper tags. They will go west, to the farmhouse, to Old Songs.
  - **One of her cell is wounded** in the arm.
  - **The courier, an SFW officer, is taken alive.** He is unnamed and speaks careful Veridican. He doesn't struggle. He asks for water and gets it.
- **The case.** A hard document case chained to his wrist. Alexei opens it in the boathouse by torchlight. Inside:
  - an **SFW advance plan** in their own script, with overlays of the roads inland to the capital;
  - clipped behind it, an **annex in Veridican**, in the staff format of Veridica's own coastal command, with Veridican grid notation and Veridican road designations, and **the batteries by number**. The SFW's annotations in the margins turn every *hold* into *avoid* and every *covered* into *bypass*.
- **What Elena knows and doesn't.** She doesn't know Sokolov's hand, or her plan, and she has never seen the coastal defence plan. But she **knows a Veridican military document when she sees one**: the form numbers, the flat phrasing, and the way Veridica numbers its guns. A Veridican document in an enemy courier's case means someone in Veridica gave it to them.

## Ending
She reads the annex's first page twice in the torchlight and doesn't read it aloud. Alexei says what she is thinking: *"That's ours."* Elena: ***"It was."*** She wraps the case in oilcloth and writes nothing down. It goes inland at first light by the cells' paper chain, toward the capital, for coastal command.
