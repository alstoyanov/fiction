# Chapter 20: Thirty Seconds

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Fri Apr 25, 2110 (01:30 to night)
**Setting:** Dmitri's volunteer company post in the **western corridor** (a grain store beside the lakeshore road); the corridor under fire; Elena's farmhouse: the barn, the yard and the kitchen by the stove
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Grief spoken formally, without a single contraction. The brother who lost the memory asks the question the three of them argued over on a roof, and the eldest answers it for all three of them. Then the singer's voice breaks.

## Beats
- **01:30, the grain store.** Dmitri is on watch and counting: flares over the corridor every four minutes, shells per hour, vehicles through since dusk. His inner voice is a log. Since 06:10 on Thursday, when Nikolai came to him with Isaiah's entry, he has known that Alexei *stayed*. He asked Elena to let him go to the overpass. Elena: *"No. I go. You hold the corridor."* He held it, because it is the only road to the farmhouse and it would be needed.
- **What he did not feel.** At 23:41 on Wednesday he was counting trucks. He felt nothing. The bond is not telepathy, and he has always known that. It is the worst thing he learns this week.
- **02:14, the radio.** Elena's voice, hoarse: ***"I have him."*** Her cell went onto the overpass on the night of Thursday Apr 24, after the SFW gun pulled back. Then: *"Bring Kolya. Before dark."* Dmitri: *"Understood."* He hands the watch to his second, formally, and writes nothing.
- **Morning.** Nikolai comes out from the Spire along the corridor with **Isaiah**. Isaiah stands very straight and reads from his paper, because he will not say it from memory: *"He said: 'Tell Elena I love her. Tell Dima he was first by thirty seconds, and I never minded.'"* Dmitri's face does nothing. Isaiah folds the paper and puts it away.
- **The step of the grain store.** The two brothers sit. Nikolai lost the rooftop in Book 4 and holds it only as a story, from Dmitri's primer. He looks at his hands and says: ***"I don't remember who was born first."*** Dmitri: ***"I was. By thirty seconds. He never minded."*** They sit together. For once Dmitri counts nothing. After a long time Nikolai says *"Lyosha"* once, and it is not a reprimand.
- **Afternoon, the corridor.** They drive to the farmhouse under fire. Dmitri drives. Isaiah sits in the back with his hands folded.
- **The barn.** Alexei lies on a trestle under a sheet. Elena has washed him. Old Songs has sat with him since morning, humming; Alexei was the one who always asked him to sing. Vance sits at the kitchen table holding her cup in both hands to still it, and says nothing.
- **The yard.** From the kitchen window Dmitri watches Isaiah give Elena the first half of the message. She doesn't move.
- **Evening, the kitchen.** Old Songs sings **"The Names"**, as he does every evening, with the week's dead from the journals: Veridican, and one SFW soldier by name. Then the new verse. Quote it exactly (`old-songs-character.md` §3):
  > *Who went down to the water?*
  > *Name them, and they stay.*
  > *Alexei went down to the water —*
  > *name them, and they stay.*

  On the name, **his voice breaks, for the first time** that any of them has heard. He stops. Nobody moves. Then he goes on, and finishes the refrain:
  > *The record may forget them,*
  > *the song will not forget;*
  > *name them, and they stay.*

  The tune lifts at the end like a question.

## Ending
Elena doesn't come in. Through the window Dmitri watches her stand in the yard, in the dark, until the song is over.

## Notes
- **README 20 is exact:** "I was. By thirty seconds. He never minded." Dmitri **uses no contractions** in narration or speech. Nikolai and Isaiah may use them.
- **Old Songs (README table and decision 31):** his voice breaks here for the first time. He makes no speech, has no riddle and speaks no line. The lyrics are exact, and the tune lifts like a question.
- **The bond is not telepathy** (`volkov-triplets.md`). Dmitri feels nothing at the moment of death, and says so only to himself.
- **New, flagged for other chapters:**
  - Dmitri's company holds the **western corridor**, which is why he wasn't at the overpass.
  - **Elena recovered the body** on the night of Apr 24.
  - The time **02:14**.
  - **Alexei lies in the barn.** His burial is not shown; if it's needed, the orchard is the natural place (see Ch 29).
- **Isaiah delivers both halves of the message:** to Dmitri in person, and to Elena in the yard.
- **Vance is present and silent**, with her tremor. Nothing about the page.
