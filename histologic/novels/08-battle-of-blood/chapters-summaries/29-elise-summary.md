# Chapter 29: Elise

**POV:** Mikhail "Old Songs" Petrov
**Date:** Sat May 24 – Tue May 27, 2110 (her death on Saturday afternoon; the burial on Tuesday morning)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse: Vance's room, the kitchen and the stove with the cracked door; the old plum orchard behind the barn
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
She built the cage, and he lived in it for forty years. She dies beside him with her hand at last still, and he gives the one speech of his book over her grave. Then he sings her true name into the song that keeps the dead, the name no record in either nation now holds. What she left him stays a tune.

## Beats
- **Since Wednesday.** On Wednesday they heard the Spire at the dresser, through Nikolai's voice line patched to the farmhouse receiver (Ch 28). After the humming on the line, Vance went to bed and has hardly left it. On **Thursday night** Marcus came with a name and asked him to sing it. Old Songs did: ***Nora Chen***, into "The Names". Since then he has sung the song every evening, with the dead from every side. He hums at the stove and sings with words only when the dead need naming.
- **Sat May 24, morning.** Ordinary work: the damper on the cracked stove door, open two fingers, and tea. Her tremor is so bad that he holds the cup for her. For one line she is twenty-seven again, correcting a word in something he hummed, the way she argued at the Archive on Thursdays: "That's the wrong vowel. It always was." Then she isn't twenty-seven.
- **The afternoon.** He sits beside her and hums the ferry verses of the Lakeshore Air, not the lullaby. He **asks her nothing**, and he doesn't bring up what she gave him in April. Her last words, precise and brief: ***"I told someone."*** A little after four her hand goes still on the blanket, completely, for the first time since he met her again. He sits with it for a long time before he calls Elena.
- **Her name.** In Chronos she has been dead since January 2109, certified in Ward 9S under the name she lived under there. In Veridica she hasn't been anyone for forty years. He thinks the fact once and doesn't follow it.
- **Sun May 25.** Elena and Dmitri dig the grave in the orchard, under the plum trees in leaf, **beside Alexei's**. Elena brought his body back on the night of Apr 24, and he was buried here in April. Dmitri works formally, without a contraction, and counts the spadefuls under his breath. **Tanaka** comes out from the field hospital. She has sent word across the lake to Dr. Vale. **He can't come:** a Chronos man's eyes are logged, and his mother has been dead in Chronos since 2109. Tanaka says it as a case note, without finishing it.
- **Mon May 26.** The receiver on the dresser reports the SFW general killed on the coast road that morning, during the withdrawal, by a Veridican battery that never got the ceasefire order. That evening Old Songs adds ***Omid Farahani*** to "The Names", because the song takes the dead from every side.
- **Tue May 27, the burial.** Present: Elena, Dmitri, Nikolai, Maya, Kira, Marcus (his custody order lapsed with the Judge), Tanaka, Isaiah, Kovač, and the refugees from the barn, children among them. Nobody reads from any page.
- **The one speech.** It is plain and dry, spoken standing at the grave's head, and runs about 600–800 words. He uses no riddle and no rhetoric. Around the canonical core:
  - *"I argued with her for forty-seven years. For forty of them she wasn't in the room."* Thursdays at the Archive in 2063: poetry, rhyme, and the vowel she was always right about.
  - *"She was afraid, and she built a design for frightened people. Then she wrote down that she was wrong. Very few people write that down."*
  - *"I spent forty years inside what she built. One place after another. I'm not going to tell you I forgive her. It was never a thing for one man to give."*
  - **The key line, exact (plot):** ***"She built the cage, and she built the key into the cage, and she hid it in the one place her own design couldn't look: in someone else's hand."***
  - *"She wrote 'many hands' in 2066 and never practised it once, until a Saturday ten days ago, at that table."* He looks at Kira and doesn't name her.
- **"The Names."** He sings it through: all the dead since February, Alexei and Nora among them, and the last new verse, exact (`old-songs-character.md` §3):
  > *Who went down to the water?*
  > *Name them, and they stay.*
  > *Elise Vance went down to the water —*
  > *name them, and they stay.*
  >
  > *The record may forget them,*
  > *the song will not forget;*
  > *name them, and they stay.*
  He sings it alone. Nobody joins the refrain; that is saved for the Epilogue. The last phrase lifts like a question.

## Ending
Evening, alone at the stove. He hums the tune he set in April, what she gave him, low, **without the words**. He doesn't try to work it out yet. It lifts at the end like a question, and he lets it hang.

## Notes
- **The one speech** (README decisions 28 and 31). His voice first broke in Ch 20. Here it holds.
- **May 21** was heard at the farmhouse through Nikolai's patched voice line (Ch 28; README Old Songs table).
- **The riddle** (README decision 16) is **not quoted, solved or glossed**. Keep the speech clear of anything the riddle points toward: no Anselm Kerr, no apples, no shelves, no numbers. **Her Chronos alias is not named on the page**, because its surname would point at the riddle. The name beat says only that she was certified dead under another name. "The Archive on Thursdays" is plot wording and stays innocent. **The orchard is plum, not apple, on purpose.**
- **Nora's verse (Thu May 22) and Farahani's (Mon May 26)** are new dates. They give the Epilogue its list. Farahani's day falls inside README 29's range of May 25–27.
- **Alexei's grave:** the coordinator settled this. Elena recovered his body on Apr 24 (Ch 20), and he was buried in the orchard afterwards (not shown). Vance lies beside him.
- **Dr. Soren Vale** (Book 6–7) can't attend, which keeps him offstage and his certificate intact.
- **Present at the burial** assumes Maya, Isaiah and Kovač can reach the farmhouse after the ceasefire.
