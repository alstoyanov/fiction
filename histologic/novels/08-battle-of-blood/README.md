# Battle of Blood (Histologic, Book 8)

**Status:** plot v2 and chapter summaries done (Sept 2026). The chapters are still to be drafted. The Nov 2025 draft, its summaries and its documents were deleted; they are in git history.
**Set:** Wednesday, February 12 to Wednesday, May 28, 2110. The epilogue is on Friday, August 15, 2110.
**Follows:** *Battle of Truths* (Book 7). **Followed by:** *Architects of Chaos* (Book 9, the finale, provisional).

## Premise
An SFW fleet lands on Veridica's Atlantic coast. Its soldiers have no interfaces, so no storm can turn them and no seeding can reach them. The SFW has come for The Judge, which now holds the record of two nations. The heroes fight a real war and uncover who invited it and why. Under cover of the invasion, Veridica is being corrected. In the end the machine dies by its maker's hidden design, set off by the surrender that was meant to save the city. This time the heroes ask Chronos before deciding for it.

## The Twists
1. **The wine was open** (Ch 9): the SFW's plan is Sokolov's own, leaked by her mother.
2. **Certainty is care** (Ch 17): Phase Two mass correction is being broadcast, and the order quotes Principle Four.
3. **The surrender is the trigger** (Ch 25): Principle Seven fires on the handover. It also needs Kira's concurrence and Chronos's consent.
- **Closing turn** (Ch 30): the true source was copied east nine minutes before the cascade.
