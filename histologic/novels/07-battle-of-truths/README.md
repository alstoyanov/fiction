# Battle of Truths (Histologic, Book 7)

**Status:** plot v3 and chapter summaries done (Sept 2026). The chapters are still to be drafted. The earlier Nov 2025 draft, its summaries and its supporting documents have been deleted; git history keeps them.
**Set:** Wednesday, August 14 – Monday, December 23, 2109. The epilogue is set on Friday, January 10, 2110.
**Follows:** *The Distributed Truth* (Book 6). **Followed by:** *Battle of Blood* (Book 8, provisional).

## Premise
Two million journals are rewritten in a single night. The countersignature on the rewrite belongs to the Chronos Archive key, which is Julian Kovač's. Marcus Chen checks the record, and the record says what he fears. So he asks for the operation he refused in Book 6: RESTORATION, which means using the trust of the man he saved. It works far beyond its purpose. The two nations' true sources are one system, and Chronos wakes up believing Veridica's history. After that the truth arrives piece by piece, and none of it helps.

## The Twists
1. **One source** (Ch 16): the operation meant to cut out one hand rewrites a nation instead.
2. **The key wasn't his** (Ch 24): Julian was innocent, and a letter in his aunt's folder proves it.
3. **Enemies outside them all** (Ch 28): someone in Veridica gave the SFW the whole operation before it happened.
- **Turns:** the 2074 storm was scheduled ("The Two Maps", Ch 13), and Julian publishes his own log of every hour spent with Marcus (Ch 21).
