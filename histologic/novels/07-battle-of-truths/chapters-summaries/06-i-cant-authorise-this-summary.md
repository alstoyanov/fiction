# Chapter 6: I Can't Authorise This

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Aug 26, 2109
**Setting:** Marram End, a lakeshore village in the Veridican strip (dawn); the First Minister's office, top floor of the Cabinet Office, Veridica Capital (15:00); Marcus's flat that night
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
Marcus asks for the operation he refused in Book 6, for reasons a reader will share. The chapter should make the choice feel inevitable and also wrong. The state never orders it. It only declines to stop him, and Wei gives it a name.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, the casualty bulletin:** *Aug 26, 2109. 08:00. Commission casualty bulletin. Dead since Aug 14: seven.* One died on Saturday night on the shore road. **The seventh was a child at Marram End, on Sunday Aug 25.** The press gives no name.
- **Marram End at dawn.** Marcus drives out alone. There is a short stone slip and open water. In Chronos's history of the village a timber jetty runs out a hundred metres. In Sunday's storm the child walked out along it. Marcus thinks of the Kestrel entry Kira copied out last November: Wren at the edge of the drop, caught by the back of her coat. He knows the difference was one pair of hands. The village keeps a paper book. Its entry for Sunday is in four hands.
- **The sum,** in his head on the drive back:
  - The Rewriting carries the key that countersigned the Gap.
  - The roll says Julian has held it since 2104.
  - Julian showed it to him unasked, and said *asleep* as if it were nothing.
  - Chronos's Judge will believe Chronos's own record and nothing Veridica says.
  - So the only way to stop the storms is to change what Chronos's record says, and the only door to it is a man who trusts him.
  Nikolai's *I don't know the hand* doesn't come to mind. He notices, for a moment, that it doesn't.
- **15:00, the First Minister's office.** **Maitland** (big, tired, well tailored, chin on his fist) wants the numbers first: seven dead, the storm schedule, the week's casualties. Border Security has asked to put its transmitter units back in the field, and he has said *no, for now*. **Wei** is at the side table with his tablet, courteous, and waits to be asked. **Chen Zhao is not present.**
- **Marcus asks.** Give him the operation he refused. Use Julian's trust to get into the **Chronos Temporal Archive**. **Revoke the Archive key's rights in the true source.** **Restore Chronos's own records of the Rewriting and the Gap**, so that its Judge reads the truth and stops. The cover: a **joint Veridica–Chronos inquiry into the Rewriting**, co-chaired by Marcus and Julian, working inside the Archive. Maitland will propose it to Chronos in the morning.
- **Wei supplies the tools.** His office's restoration tooling, built for the Concordance "so a burned book could be restored", "may need adapting". Then the name, lightly: ***"Call it RESTORATION. That's what it is."*** Marcus **notices the word**: page 30, *whoever restores it*, and the Ministry folder Kira took from Ashford's data centre in 2106, *RESTORATION / XI*. He **accepts it anyway**, because refusing a word is a luxury for people with no dead.
- **Maitland,** after a long look at the window: ***"I can't authorise this. But I won't stop you."*** There is no paper and no signature line. Marcus recognises the deniability from KINDRED, and this time he is the one asking for it.
- **Wei at the lift,** pleasantly: "If you want Commissioner Osman's eyes on the tooling, I'd welcome them." That is one beat of excess: he already knows Kira will insist.
- **The walk home.** Marcus's holes: he reaches for the word for what he has just done and finds a smooth place where it should be.

## Ending
**The flat, 23:30.** Marcus writes the day for his cover, in pencil, in the format of desk 14-08: *Aug 26, 2109. 15:00. Cabinet Office. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Maitland, O.; Wei, T. Purpose: proposal of a joint Veridica–Chronos inquiry into the Rewriting. Status: agreed.* It is the first fact entry he has ever written knowing it to be a lie. He files it under **CHECKED**.
