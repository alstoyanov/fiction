# Chapter 12: The Diplomat's Page

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Mon Sep 30, 2109
**Setting:** The Sovereignty of Free Will: Isaiah's rooms (mid-morning, on the delayed link); the small conference room at his paper; the parlour and study of his memory palace (evening)
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Isaiah is given two more principles as insurance, and within hours his hosts ask him to vouch for the same pages. Someone else has them. In the parlour of his own mind, the one person who never speaks goes still. He ends the day holding more than a pattern, with no one to tell.

## Beats
- **The link.** Kira sends photographs of **pp. 5–12**: the chipped *e*, ***VII*** in every corner, and the marks ***HV·01*** and ***SO·04***. She took them a week ago at the farmhouse. Her note: *"Copy VII. Insurance. He doesn't know I've sent them."* She doesn't name him.
- **He reads them twice, like an editorial:**
  - *"Singular truth, mechanically verified, universally enforced… Democracy failed because it assumed humans could collectively determine what happened. We remove the assumption."*
  - *"A system without opposition appears tyrannical; a system with uncontrolled opposition becomes unstable. Let there be several Histologic nations, differing in method and agreeing in substance, and let there be enemies outside them all. Peace would raise questions. Total war would end the system. Controlled conflict answers every question before it is asked."*
  *Enemies outside them all.* He is sitting in one.
- **Filing.** In the palace study he files by copy, not by page: the Introduction (**no numeral**); Three and Five (**X**); page 30 (**XI**); and now **VII**. One document, in four sets of hands.
- **The paper.** The editors are still running the Rewriting as proof that "Histologic memory is worthless", and Isaiah hears *worthless* said the way people say *finished*. At noon he is called into the small conference room. The **military attaché** is there, the same man as in Book 6 and Ch 5: courteous, careful paragraphs, no interface.
- **The request.** The attaché lays photographs on the table: **pp. 5–12, copy VII**, the pages Isaiah received three hours ago and has shown no one. "Your authentication would be valued." They are **not Kira's photographs**. The lamp is different, and so is the table grain, and there's a thumb's shadow at one corner. Someone else has photographed the originals.
- **He refuses:** "I authenticate what I publish, when I publish it. I haven't." Where did they come from? "Channels." He remembers *Phase: justification* (Aug 23) and says nothing. His asylum is still *conditional*, and he admires the word's precision less than he did.
- **The link problem.** He drafts *Someone else has your pages* to Kira, and doesn't send it. If the pages came another way, the link may be the one clean thing left. If they came through the link, it isn't clean. He won't claim more than he knows.
- **Evening, the parlour.** He reads Principle Two aloud among the harmless true warm things: the pepper soup, the first byline over the fireplace. At *"differing in method and agreeing in substance"* **Aunt Sarah**, at the head of the table, is **suddenly very still**. She has never said a word in the palace, and she says none now. The narration doesn't comment.

## Ending
In the study he writes: *"One fragment is a curiosity. Two is a pattern."* He has **four copies** now, in four sets of hands, which is more than a pattern. He doesn't know whom to tell.

## Notes
- **(Twist 3 clue)** The SFW wants pages he hasn't published authenticated, and its photographs are not Kira's. The source stays open, and Ch 28 may route it through the Veridican seam. Point at nobody.
- **(SO·04 seed)** The aunt's stillness is unspoken, and nothing links her to the mark.
- **"Four"** (plot v3) means four copies: none, X, XI and VII. It is new.
- **Manuscript:** only One and Two are quoted. Kira didn't send p. 1, so Isaiah doesn't know or use the title yet.
- **Dates:** photographed Sep 23; originals back with Julian Sep 24. The attaché stays unnamed.
