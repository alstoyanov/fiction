# Chapter 23: Some of It

**POV:** Elena Volkov (her first POV in the series)
**Date:** Fri Nov 22, 2109
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse in the border zone (the kitchen and yard, midday); Nikolai and Maya's flat in the capital (the landing outside the door, late afternoon); the **central plaza** at dusk (the fountain, the Lost Hour stone beside it, and the blind spot of the colonnade)
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The recruiter, from inside, at last. The circle breaks along the lines each of them has always stood on. The one person who has done this to someone goes to the man she taught to do it, at the fountain where she did it to him. She gives him the part that was real, and one warning.

## Beats
- **Opening, in her voice:** fast, flat, counting. She counts the kitchen left to right the way she counts a patrol: Alexei, Dmitri, Kira. Nikolai is missing, and she writes nothing down. She remembers everything. She carries a notebook and has never written in it.
- **The fracture,** in the kitchen, a week after Julian's log went public.
  - Alexei, from the stove, too loud: ***"We did what was necessary."***
  - Dmitri, from the doorway, without contractions: ***"We became the thing we were used by."*** Neither brother gives ground. The bond is plain to see: a weight in the room.
  - **Marcus** arrives at noon. **Kira** gets up and goes out to the yard. They can barely be in one room: Julian's log shows what Julian felt, and it shows Marcus letting him feel it.
  - In the yard, Kira to Elena, levelly: "He let him hope." Elena: "That's the work." Kira: "You taught it to him." Elena: "Yes." That is all.
- **Her part.** She taught R-2 at this table from Sep 2 to Sep 13, and he was a good student. In September she told him *"Some of it was real"* and didn't say which part (Ch 9). She has known since then that she would have to.
- **16:30, the capital. Nikolai's flat.** Maya opens the door on the chain. He won't come out, and he has barely eaten since the 15th. Through the door, very quietly, Nikolai says what he has been saying to himself for a week: ***"I said it in February. 'I know the key. I don't know the hand.' Everybody heard the first half."*** Elena says "Kolya" once. The bond isn't hers, and she can't reach him through it.
- **17:40, the central plaza.** He is here, because this is where his habit puts him. He walks through the plaza most evenings, and he did in 2105 too. She read it in his file before she ever met him. It is dark and cold, with lamps on the water. She waits in the **blind spot of the colonnade**, counting the camera arcs. He is at 0.44, and an outlaw recruiter at his elbow on camera is exactly what put him in a white room once. The recruiter's opening line is ready, ***"I'm terrible with directions"***, and she decides not to use it. She says his name instead. It is the first time she has approached him without technique.
- **At the fountain,** with the Lost Hour stone a few steps away:
  - He already has **blue** under CHECKED, in her name, since September (Ch 9). He doesn't ask again. She gives him one more thing he never asked for: at the dinner by the river the dress was **green**. It is testimony, not memory, and it is all he can have of it.
  - **The part that was real.** It was the evening at the restaurant over the river, three days after the café. He told her about his parents, and she reached across and said, *"They'd be proud of you."* It wasn't in the plan. The plan was to agree with him. She said it because it was true, and because she wanted it to be true for him. It is the only thing she said in eight weeks that wasn't work.
  - **What it cost.** She couldn't do the rest cleanly after that. There were forty-seven interactions and a hundred and twenty-seven hours, and she remembers each one. In her correction cell she hid that dinner in the dullest thing she knew, the operation's own log, and kept it. She has never known why she kept the device in that drawer. And she still doesn't know about **the seven** she turned before him: whether she saved them or destroyed them. She knows their names and has never written them down.
  - **What he'll carry.** He will remember every hour, the way she does. The difference is that Julian's hours are written down, for everyone, by the man they were done to.

## Ending
She stands up to go before the camera arc comes round. Her last words, low and hoarse, and direct for once: ***"You'll want someone to forgive you. Don't ask him. It's the one thing you can't take from him too."*** She walks out of the colonnade's shadow and doesn't look back. He stays at the fountain.
