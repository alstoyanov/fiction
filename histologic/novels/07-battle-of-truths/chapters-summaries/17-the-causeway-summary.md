# Chapter 17: The Causeway

**POV:** Alexei Volkov
**Date:** Sat Oct 26, 2109, from about 04:00 to about 09:10
**Setting:** The marsh road on the Chronos shore; the streets behind the Temporal Archive; the causeway-head post; the **Drowned Causeway** in fog; the Veridican end; Elena's farmhouse kitchen
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Part Three opens on the way home from the victory. This is Alexei's craft chapter: routes, jurisdictions and exits. It is also the first look at a nation that woke up rewritten. The one man who could stop them at the lake lets them go, because his record says they belong on the other side.

## Beats
- **About 04:00, the marsh road.** Alexei has held the road since he drove the others in (Ch 16), with the lights off, counting the minutes the way Dmitri would. Through the bond he has felt Nikolai all night as **cold hands**, and it grows sharply colder a little after 02:40. It is sensation, never a voice, and he doesn't know what it means.
- **04:10.** He brings the vehicle to the Archive's back step. Four come out of the service door: Kira, Dmitri, Nikolai and Marcus. Alexei counts them twice. Kira carries the case with the image and doesn't look up. Marcus comes last, like a man who still has a key to hand back. Nobody tells Alexei what happened, and he doesn't ask yet. The fog is coming in off the lake on time: real weather, cold and ordinary.
- **The route out, as Alexei thinks it:** there is one jurisdiction in which all five are illegal, and one exit, the causeway, which only works in fog. He drives the empty streets. At a tram stop an early-shift worker checks something at the edge of his sight, frowns, and then stops frowning. That is the first thing Alexei sees of the Awakening, though he doesn't know it yet.
- **Behind them.** The reconciliation rooms were opened at 22:00 on Julian's grant, and a night desk at Temporal Defence has noticed. About 05:40, directorate lamps appear far back on the marsh road: two cars, moving slowly and sweeping the reeds.
- **The causeway-head post, about 06:00.** A young Chronos border guard stands alone under a floodlight that the fog turns white. Alexei has an answer ready for every question except the one he gets. The guard looks at five Veridicans walking towards the water, checks his log, and asks, puzzled and polite: ***"You're going home, then? It's your shore."*** This morning the lakeshore has always been Veridica's, and he can't think why he is posted at a border nobody disputes. He waves them on. **His interface logs all five faces.** Alexei knows it, and says nothing.
- **The Drowned Causeway, 06:05–07:25.** Alexei leads. He knows every gap from ten years as the Phantom. Dmitri takes the rear and counts them through the drowned stretches aloud.
  - **Halfway across, Nikolai stops** on a sleeper with water over his boots. Book 6's key match started all this, and he knows it. The cold in his hands hits Alexei like a blow. Alexei goes back, says "Kolya", takes his wrist, and walks him on one sleeper at a time. He makes no speech.
  - **Kira** speaks once, to nobody, and gives the final scope count as a figure. Nobody answers. Alexei understands it then: *all of it*.
  - **Marcus** looks back once at the Chronos shore dissolving in the fog, and then doesn't again.
  - The lamps stop at the causeway head. They don't follow into fog.
- **The Veridican end, about 07:30,** in first gray light. Elena waits with the truck. She counts, "Five", and asks nothing. On the drive Alexei tries a joke, and it dies halfway out.

## Ending
The farmhouse kitchen, about 09:10. Nikolai's receiver on the dresser is tuned, as it is every morning, to the Chronos broadcast that carries across the water. The newsreader is calm. There is a line about a reconciliation irregularity at the Temporal Archive "corrected from the true source", then: ***"Chronos Archive key-holder detained. Kovač, J."*** Nobody moves. Kira sets the case down on the table very carefully. Alexei, wry and hollow, to the room: ***"We won. Why does nobody feel it?"***
