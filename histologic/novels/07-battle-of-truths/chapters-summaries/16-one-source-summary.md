# Chapter 16: One Source

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Fri Oct 25, 2109, 20:50 – Sat Oct 26, about 04:10
**Setting:** The Drowned Causeway in fog (real weather); the marsh road on the Chronos shore; the Chronos Temporal Archive: its service door, the lower corridor, and the reconciliation rooms
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
**Twist 1.** The knife cuts nothing, because nothing is there to cut. Chronos's backup takes Veridica's master credentials as its own, and the narrow image restores a nation's past "from the true source", which was always one. Kira holds the abort and can't use it.

## Beats
- **20:50, the causeway head.** The fog is on time. Alexei leads Kira, Dmitri and Nikolai across the sleepers: "Step where I step. It's the only rule I've ever kept." He has a vehicle on the marsh road. He drives them in, then goes back to hold the road for the way out (Ch 17).
- **22:00, the service door.** Marcus stayed after the closing session "to finish the draft report" (Ch 15). He opens the door from inside on his co-chair access. Kira thinks it as a fact: *every door tonight writes Julian's name.*
- **Positions:**
  - **Dmitri on the doors.** Two night porters make rounds at 23:30, 01:30 and 03:30, and their logs will hold whatever they see. He keeps the team out of their sight lines, and counts.
  - **Nikolai on the relay:** a sealed line to the Veridican side, for the revocation half.
  - **Kira at the image** in the reconciliation room. The chipped cup is the one untidied thing in the room.
  - **Marcus holding Julian's access,** ready for the refusal branch.
- **The waiting.** She checks the scope list three times: two record sets, one revocation. The hard stop is armed and the scheduler is at **02:40**. Marcus keeps his lists folded. She thinks of Adrian and doesn't tell him.
- **02:40:00.**
  - **The revocation** completes on the Veridican side in eleven seconds, as written.
  - **The handshake.** Her hand is on the refusal branch. Chronos's backup ought to challenge a foreign credential. It **accepts the Veridican master credentials as its own**. There's no prompt, no breach and no seam: nothing to replace.
  - **Nikolai,** quietly: "It isn't crossing to them. It's already there."
- **The propagation.** The scope indicator reads *2*, and then it doesn't stop:
  - the lakeshore sovereignty records;
  - the Temporal War chronology;
  - the Gap, in Veridica's version;
  - the Rewriting;
  - then every log that refers to any of them, aligning entry by entry, towards 150 million.
  The restore runs from the true source, and the true source holds Veridica's account. She remembers Principle Three, *"three backup systems that appear independent and are not"*, which they published believing it meant *within* a nation. Then *agreeing in substance*. Then the mirror.
- **The knife.** The hard stop fires, and the abort is under her hand. Nikolai says it aloud: stopping now leaves every contested entry on both shores half-restored. Both records would be half-written, with a Gap in everything. She can't cut, and she lets it run. Marcus watches her face and asks nothing.
- **03:30.** The porter passes the corridor. Four people stand in the dark, and Dmitri counts him out.
- **About 04:06.** The indicator stops. The screen reads *Records reconciled*, then a number she doesn't read aloud, then *Source: master.*
- **04:10.** Dmitri counts them out into the fog. Alexei's lights are on the marsh road.

## Ending
On the Archive's back step Marcus asks her what happened. Kira, very level: ***"There was nothing to replace. It was always one."***
