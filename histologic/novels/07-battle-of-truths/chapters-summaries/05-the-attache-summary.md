# Chapter 5: The Attaché

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Fri Aug 23, 2109 (with a check on the morning of Sat Aug 24)
**Setting:** The Sovereignty of Free Will: the paper's offices; the editor-in-chief's lunch table in the private dining room upstairs; the study of Isaiah's memory palace
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
The SFW's favourite witness watches his hosts enjoy Veridica's catastrophe. He hears a number he shouldn't have heard, and he sees one line in a folder he has seen once before. It plants two Twist 3 clues, and Isaiah doesn't let himself conclude anything from either.

## Beats
- **The front page.** His paper runs the Rewriting across five columns: *HISTOLOGIC MEMORY IS WORTHLESS.* His own column runs underneath it, **cut**. His description of the Rewriting survives, but his last paragraph is gone: the one about the paper books that held, which ended on a question. The managing editor calls the cut "space". Isaiah files it as the fourth time this year.
- **What he knows,** filed in order: Kira's note on Thursday (*"Restore path. Paper held. Seventeen minutes, again."*); Veridica's official toll in the Commission's morning bulletin; Chronos's 0.91 of Wednesday; the storms. Veridica's Commission publishes its casualty bulletin **each morning at 08:00, capital time.** The latest, Thursday's, says **three dead** since Aug 14: the Gullhaven ferryman, and two storm deaths on Wednesday night.
- **Lunch.** The editor-in-chief invites him upstairs "as our witness". The **SFW military attaché** from Book 6 is there (**unnamed**): courteous, speaking in careful paragraphs, no interface behind his ears. The attaché talks about the Rewriting with professional appreciation, like a man admiring a well-made weapon he doesn't own.
- **The number.** Over coffee the attaché says, as a plain fact of the week: *"Five dead since Gullhaven."* He adds two lakeshore storm deaths from last night, Thursday into Friday, which the capital hasn't announced. In the Sovereignty it is lunchtime. **In the capital it isn't yet dawn.** Isaiah says nothing. He hears how the man says *five*: the way people say a figure they have *received*, not read.
- **The folder.** The attaché opens a slim folder to check a date. Isaiah knows its cover from March, from an office he was never meant to enter: ***HISTOLOGIC COORDINATION: STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS***. There is a new divider inside it, and on the first page after the divider, in the same typeface as the heading, is a single line.
- **Friday's bulletin** reaches the Sovereignty in the mid-afternoon (08:00 in the capital). It still says **three**, and the two deaths from Thursday night are "pending identification".
- **The palace.** That evening Isaiah files it in the study, under the date and the time: the attaché, the figure, and the hour it was said. Then he goes down to the kitchen, where the sources are kept, and doesn't find one for the number. The aunt at the head of the parlour table doesn't appear in this chapter.
- **The check, Sat Aug 24, 08:00 capital time.** The Commission's bulletin says **five**. Isaiah reads it twice and writes the difference in the study: *a day early.* He doesn't write *who told him*, because he doesn't know. He writes the question instead.

## Ending
The line he saw in the folder, which he has kept all day in the study with the others, where it waits for what it means: ***Phase: justification.***
