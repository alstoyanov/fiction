# Epilogue: The Coast

**POV:** Rotating: Julian, Tanaka, Old Songs, Kovač, Isaiah, Marcus
**Date:** Fri Jan 10, 2110
**Setting:** Chronos (where Julian is held at maximum); Ward 9S, Tanaka's hospital; Elena's farmhouse; Kovač's office at the Commission's testimony intake; the Sovereignty of Free Will; a winter headland on the Atlantic coast of Veridica
**Length:** ~3,600 words

## Purpose
This is the price, counted person by person, and then the new front. Every vignette is someone keeping a record that no system can use. The last one turns the whole series from the lake to the sea.

## Beats
- **Chronos, morning (Julian).** He is recorded at maximum, and every heartbeat goes to the directorate. He writes in his handwritten journal, the sloping hand of Book 6, the one thing no log can hold: ***"I am seen every second and nobody sees me."*** **Adrian** sits beside him and doesn't read it. Julian lets him stay. Kovač's reply to his letter of January 2108 is folded in the journal's back cover; it came nearly two years late *(see Kovač below)*. No thought of Marcus is spelled out. Julian closes the journal before the thought forms.
- **Ward 9S, midday (Tanaka).** A patient in 9S who was never logged tells her where the old ferry ran, whose shore it was, and that the old pier stood. Case-note form: *Patient: [initials]. Presentation: remembers. Onset: always. Aetiology:* She leaves it unfinished. The count from Ch 27 has grown, a little. She writes the number down and doesn't say it.
- **The farmhouse, afternoon (Old Songs).** Vance's tremor is worse: the cup no longer steadies it completely. She takes out **the page she won't give anyone** from where she keeps it (don't show where), reads it, and puts it back. Show no text, no heading and no numeral. Old Songs watches from the stove and **asks nothing**. He hums the Lakeshore Air, low, and it lifts at the end like a question he isn't asking.
- **The testimony intake, evening (Kovač).** She has written to Julian at last. The letter went by courier across the lake in December, to answer one dated 20 January 2108. She puts his original letter into a **new folder** and labels it in block capitals: ***ANSWERED***. She pushes her glasses up, looks at the shelf of folders she kept instead of acting, and puts this one at the end.
- **The Sovereignty (Isaiah).** A letter from the asylum office: his review has been ***"brought forward"***. He admires the phrase's precision, as he once admired *conditional*. He files it in the palace with the date. He doesn't write a question, because he knows the answer. The coat hangs by the door with the lining still sewn.
- **The Atlantic coast, dusk (Marcus).** A winter headland: gray sea, hard wind, salt on the glasses he keeps wiping. It is real weather, all of it. **Marcus, Kira, Dmitri and Sokolov** stand together. Sokolov brought them out to see the ground, because the lakeshore is not where this war will come. Alexei is at the farmhouse; Dmitri notes it once, in the way he counts people. Kira stands close to Marcus, not touching. Sokolov gives the coastline in a report's register: the landing beaches, the roads inland, the distance to the capital. Beyond the horizon, somewhere, is a fleet of people **no broadcast can reach**. Dmitri: *"No storm will turn them."* Sokolov: *"No."* Marcus thinks of his new list, and of the one question on it.

## Ending
The light goes. Marcus, to the three of them and the sea:
**Final line:** ***"We won the Battle of Truths. Now comes the Battle of Blood."***
