# Chapter 20: A Diplomatic Breakthrough

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Nov 4, 2109 (06:00 to about 23:40)
**Setting:** Marcus and Kira's flat (dawn and late night); the Commission offices; the great hall of the Justice Center for the evening reception; the corridor behind it; Elena's farmhouse kitchen (21:00)
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The victory is made official, and everyone applauds it, including the people who designed it. Marcus hears the whole thing as a page he has read. The only honest reaction all day is the old man's silence at the radio.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 06:00:** *Nov 4, 2109. Chronos determination, read on the Chronos broadcast. Veridica "bears no responsibility" for the Rewriting or the Chronos Gap. Probability: 0.93. Corrective broadcasting: ended, from noon.* Under it, from Friday night's bulletin: *Kovač, J. Recording: maximum. Office: removed.* He doesn't write a status for either.
- **12:00, the storms stop.** At noon the Chronos transmitters fall silent along the lakeshore. The Commission's bulletin carries the first report from the strip: no alert at the scheduled time. The dead stand at **seven**, unchanged since Marram End. Marcus reads it twice.
- **12:30, Maitland on every screen:** calm, well tailored, the numbers first. The Joint Inquiry into the Rewriting "has done its work". Chronos's own record now confirms that Veridica had no hand in either attack. He calls it ***"a diplomatic breakthrough"*** and thanks "our commissioners". He doesn't say how, and nobody asks him.
- **The capital cheers** the way it did last December. In the Commission corridor colleagues shake Marcus's hand for the second time in a year. **Kira isn't there.** She went out to the farmhouse on Saturday, and they have spoken since only about logistics.
- **18:00, the reception in the great hall.** Tea and glasses under the tall windows. **Chen Zhao** presides, warm and delighted, and lifts his glass: ***"To our restorers."*** The room drinks. Marcus hears page 30 in it word for word: *"When the record fails, the people will believe whoever restores it. Arrange to be the one who restores it."* He also hears Vance at the stove in August: *"They designed you to be the restorer."* He raises his glass, because not raising it would be a statement, and he has no statement.
- **Wei applauds with the others,** courteous, with his glass in his right hand. One beat: he catches Marcus's eye across the hall, and inclines his head the way a man acknowledges a good move. There is no interior, and nothing more.
- **The corridor behind the great hall,** with the tall windows and panels of lamplight on the floor. **Maitland,** privately, his big hand briefly on Marcus's shoulder: ***"You saved us."*** Marcus: ***"At what cost?"*** Maitland gives the answer he believes, which is a number: "Seven. And none since noon." Then he goes back in.
- **A trace of his holes.** Walking out across the plaza past the Lost Hour stone, Marcus reaches for the word for a victory you can't hand back, and finds a smooth place.
- **21:00, the farmhouse.** Dmitri drives him out without being asked, and will have him back in the capital before midnight. In the kitchen the receiver on the dresser carries the Chronos broadcast, repeating the determination in a calm voice. **Old Songs sits beside it and says nothing.** He doesn't hum, and he doesn't look round. He has hummed nothing at Marcus since October. **Vance** is at the table with her cup held in both hands, and she says nothing either. **Kira** looks at him once from the doorway to the yard: "They stopped." "Yes." It's the most they've said since Saturday.

## Ending
The flat, 23:40. The lists are on the table where he left them on Oct 24, folded, with CHECKED on the outside. He sits in front of them for a long time with the pencil in his hand. He doesn't open them.
