# Chapter 2: Julian?

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Thu Aug 15, 2109
**Setting:** The Old Customs House at Saltmarsh Crossing, neutral zone: the shielded conference room, and the walled garden with the bench under the pear tree (now in leaf); the train back; Marcus and Kira's flat, late
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Marcus meets the man his lists suspect, likes him as much as ever, and is given half a sentence that would have saved everyone. Then he checks the record instead of the witness, because the record says what he is afraid of. The chapter carries both Ch 2 plants for Twist 2.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry:** *Aug 15, 2109. 10:00. Old Customs House, Saltmarsh Crossing. Emergency session under the Lakes Talks' terms. Involved parties: Veridica; Chronos; Synthesis (Park, J.). Purpose: the Rewriting.* He leaves *Status* blank.
- **The venue in August.** The fog has burned off by nine. The marsh is loud with insects. The pear tree that was bare all winter is in full leaf now, with small hard green pears. The shielded rooms feel the same, and the Chronos delegates feel the same small deafness at the door.
- **The session.** **Professor Park** convenes it, warm and saying "perhaps". **Administrator Aubry** states Chronos's position, cool and courteous and in distributions: Veridica has used "credentials of the Chronos Temporal Archive" to attack Chronos's witness index, and Chronos reserves its response. Veridica's line, delivered by Marcus, is that the authority on the restore path was not Veridican. Each side holds up the same evidence as proof against the other. Marcus notices that this is so and doesn't take it further.
- **Julian** sits beside Aubry. He is gray with exhaustion, precise, and still funny, once. His networks' books are wrecked in the index. **The six keepers' paper books from the Night of Copies** agree with each other and with nothing the index holds.
- **The garden, at the break.** From habit Julian steers them out of the shielded room. Marcus recognises the steering, and this time it's only habit. They sit on the bench under the pear tree, where everything Julian hears is logged. Temporal Defence wants a full account of the key, every use and every hour. Then Julian says: ***"I don't understand how they used the key. I've only had it since—"***
- **Aubry** comes out through the garden door and cuts across him on a point of protocol: *"Senior Historian. The mediator is recalling the delegations. The doors close at the half-hour."* It's innocent, and she's busy. Julian gets up at once. **He never finishes the sentence to Marcus.** Marcus doesn't ask him to, either then or at the gate.
- **The February finding** sits in Marcus all day, unspoken: the key countersigned the Gap (Book 6 Ch 30), and now the key's habits are on the Rewriting. He hasn't told Julian about February. He looks at Julian's hands and hates himself for looking.
- **At the gate** Julian says he'll write, through Park's secretariat. He is visibly glad Marcus came. A trace of Marcus's holes: he reaches for the name of the green fruit on the tree and finds a smooth place.
- **The train,** in his fact-entry voice: Julian's openness in December, the slip, Nikolai's *"I know the key. I don't know the hand."* Marcus lets the second half of that fall away somewhere between stations.

## Ending
**The flat, 23:40.** Kira is asleep. Chronos publishes everything, so the **Temporal Archive's public roll of office-holders** is open to anyone. Marcus reads it:
*MERA, A. Senior Historian emeritus from 2104.*
*KOVAČ, J. Senior Historian. Key-holder since 2104.*
He does the arithmetic. 2104 is before December 2107, so Julian held the key at the Gap. He thinks of Helena Kovač. She would know her nephew's career, and she works in his own building, behind the testimony intake. He doesn't call her, because he doesn't want to make her a witness against Julian. He takes out the UNCHECKED list. *Julian?* is there, from February. He doesn't cross it out. He **underlines it.**
