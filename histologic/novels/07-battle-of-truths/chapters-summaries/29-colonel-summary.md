# Chapter 29: Colonel

**POV:** Colonel Yevgenia Sokolov (her only POV chapter in the series so far)
**Date:** Fri Dec 20, 2109 (09:00 to about 23:00)
**Setting:** Border Security headquarters in the capital (the promotion, and the intelligence brief); the lakeshore road and checkpoint at dusk; her mother's house in the capital, for dinner; the First Minister's office, late
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
A promotion that comes too fast, a mother who is never surprised, and a brief that makes the whole Battle of Truths irrelevant. Sokolov notices everything and explains nothing, even to herself. The war that is coming can't be fought the way the last one was.

## Beats
- **Her POV is restrained** (voice rules 1–3). She thinks in orders and observations, not feelings. She notices things and doesn't interpret them. The reader should feel her noticing without being told what it means.
- **09:00, the promotion.** She is made **Colonel**, ten months after Major (Feb 2109, Book 6 Epilogue), and given command of the **whole lakeshore**. The ceremony is short and conducted by an unnamed general. The citation praises her "judgement in the stand-down of December 2108". She notes the date: nobody is made colonel at her age for standing units *down*. She notes that the insignia was already engraved. She says, "Thank you, sir," and nothing else.
- **11:00, the brief.** It is her first as lakeshore commander, and it is in paper form. Three items:
  - An **SFW fleet** is assembling in the Atlantic: transports and escorts, more each week. Veridica holds the former East Coast. It shares **no border** with the Sovereignty.
  - There is **staging in unaligned territory**, with supply lines that were not there in the summer.
  - **SFW soldiers have no interfaces.** No storm will turn them and no seeding will reach them. She reads that line twice. Her whole career has been the transmitter units. She files, without comment, that every weapon she knows how to use is useless against them.
  There is a question in the margin in another officer's hand: *Why the lakeshore command, if they come by sea?* She doesn't answer it. She initials the page.
- **Dusk, the lakeshore checkpoint.** She drives out herself, as she always does. **Dmitri** is at the barrier with a Commission pass, on some errand of Kira's. He sees the insignia. *"Colonel."* Her formal line: *"Colonel Sokolov. Lakeshore checkpoint. Traffic normal."* Then, low, on the way back to the car: *"It was quicker this time."* He doesn't answer, and she doesn't wait for him to.
- **20:00, dinner with her mother.** **Viktoria Sokolov** is retired Chancellor of Veridica, seventy-three, elegant and upright, and still has the title on her door. Her house is warm and exact, with nothing out of place. Viktoria has the good wine already open when Yevgenia arrives; Yevgenia notes it, and that she hasn't yet told her. They talk about the lakeshore, a cousin, the cold. Viktoria listens to every answer completely. Near the end, touching the new insignia with one finger: ***"You have always been exactly where you should be."*** Yevgenia hears it as a mother's pride, and also as something else she doesn't name. She says, "Yes, Mother." She leaves the second glass half full.
- **22:30, the First Minister's office.** Maitland has sent for her to hear the brief in person. He is tired, courteous and quick. He asks whether the storms can be turned on the fleet. She tells him the truth in an order's register: there are no interfaces, so there is nothing to turn. He asks what they won in November, then. She thinks of the stand-down, the Chronos determination, and "a diplomatic breakthrough".

## Ending
Sokolov, formal and flat, to the First Minister: ***"We won the war of facts. They are not coming to fight that one."*** She lets a pause stand, and says nothing after it.

## Notes
- **Viktoria:** *nothing on the page says she is VS·09* (README cast; plot v3). There is no hint about the manuscript, the Annex or the heirs. The open wine is the most there is, and Yevgenia doesn't interpret it. Keep her proud, elegant, and never sinister.
- **One Sokolov POV only** (`yevgenia-sokolov.md` Continuity Traps). No interior confession, and no speech about conscience. Her real sentence comes last, quietly: the checkpoint line, and the silence after the ending line.
- **Her age:** don't state it (born the second half of 2070, so 38 or 39).
- **SFW geography:** the fleet is in the Atlantic, and there is no shared border (README decision 22). Fog, if it's used at the lakeshore, is real weather. Storms are perceptual; here they are only a subject of the brief.
- **Dmitri's checkpoint beat** echoes Book 6's Epilogue ("It was very quick"). It is optional if space is tight.
- **Maitland** summons Marcus and Kira three days later (Ch 30), and this conversation is what drives it.
