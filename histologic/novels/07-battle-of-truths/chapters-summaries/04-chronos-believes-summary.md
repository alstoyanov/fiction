# Chapter 4: Chronos Believes

**POV:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka
**Date:** Wed Aug 21, 2109
**Setting:** The teaching hospital in the Chronos lakeshore city: the storm-trauma ward (reopened), Tanaka's fourth-floor office, the ninth-floor corridor outside the director's office and Ward 9S
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Chronos resumes the war it stopped in December. Tanaka, a Veridican inside Chronos, watches it from the ward. The directorate starts pulling at the one thread that leads to her. Julian comes to the hospital, and she sees what the Rewriting has done to him.

## Beats
- **06:00, the determination,** on every screen and in every log. Chronos's Judge has read the collective will at **0.91**: Veridica has "attacked the memory of Chronos a second time", using "credentials of the Chronos Temporal Archive". **Corrective broadcasting resumes at noon.** Tanaka writes it as a case note, with the admission time left blank.
- **The ward reopens.** The four stabilisation crates from December are still sealed in the corridor ("pending review of the clinical record", Book 6 Ch 14). She checks the seals herself. Morrison checks them after her and says nothing.
- **From 14:00, the storms come back** to the lakeshore: Veridica's ordinary broadcasts still reach across, so the overlap flickers on the Chronos shore as well. The admissions come as italic case notes, with sensation and no weather:
  - a woman who knows two dates for her own wedding;
  - a boy who keeps walking toward a quay that is there in only one history.
  *Prognosis,* and she stops.
- **The directorate inquiry.** Two Temporal Defence officers are in **Dr. Vale's** office with the 9S **paper certification ledger**. It is part of a review of every unlogged record in the lakeshore province after the witness index was damaged. They are asking about **one certificate: "Mrs. Margit Kerr", certified dead in 9S on Jan 4, within the hour of officers asking for her by name.** They ask Tanaka, formally, whether she was the attending physician. She answers exactly and truthfully: *"I was her physician from August 2106. The certificate is Dr. Vale's."* The officers note it. Vale, tall and stooped, thanks them for their thoroughness.
- **9S** still runs on paper, and nothing is recorded inside. The bed by the east window is made and empty. Tanaka doesn't look at it for longer than she looks at the others.
- **17:00. Julian Kovač** comes to the storm ward as Chronos's liaison to the witness networks. Two members of his lakeshore networks were admitted this afternoon. He has a handwritten list of their names, the slope of the capitals she has seen in the New Foundation. He is **coming apart**, and exactly: he is precise about every time and has forgotten his coat. He has plainly not slept, and his log would know for how long.
- **The logged corridor.** Everything Julian hears goes into his log. In January he walked three men to her ward door under his own name (Book 6 Ch 20). Neither of them mentions January. Tanaka understands that this silence is the only courtesy Chronos allows, and gives it. He says only public things: his keepers' paper books agree with each other and with nothing in the index, and "every analysis says it was the Archive's key". He says *the Archive's*, not *mine*. She files it without knowing why.
- **Case note, not examined:** *Kovač, J. Not my patient. Visiting. Sleep: insufficient. Affect: exact. He asked after both patients by name and got neither name wrong. He asked after no one else.*

## Ending
Evening on the ninth floor. The officers have left with a copy of the ledger page. Vale stands at the window at the end of the corridor, stooped, courteous and calm. Tanaka asks what they wanted. **"They are asking who else I have certified. I have told them: many people, over many years. It is a hospital."**
