# Chapter 11: Copy VII

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Mon Sep 23, 2109
**Setting:** The farmhouse kitchen (afternoon into evening); the lakeshore checkpoint at dusk, in real fog under the floodlights, at the concrete bench
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The manuscript gets its name, and the contents page places every fragment. Principle Two says the war between the nations was designed, and Dmitri says the operation should stop. Marcus turns the argument round. Vance knows the numeral and says one sentence about it. Dmitri chooses to guard what he opposes.

## Beats
- **Dmitri's watch log:** eight in the kitchen, with Old Songs at the stove and himself by the door.
- **How the pages came.** On **Sat Sep 21** Julian gave Marcus a card folder in the reading room: "Read it first. Then tell me what it is." It is a **loan**: he wants the pages back by Tuesday, for box 204117, "where the record says they are". Dmitri drove Marcus across the checkpoint with the folder inside his coat.
- **The object.** Nikolai lays out nine typed sheets: **p. 1, pp. 5–8 and pp. 9–12**. The *e* is chipped, and ***VII*** is in the lower right of every sheet. At the foot of p. 8 is ***HV·01***, and at the foot of p. 12, ***SO·04***. Nobody can read the marks. Dmitri notes that their numbers don't follow the order of the principles.
- **The title, aloud.** Kira reads it: *THE FOUNDATION OF CERTAINTY. A design for the governance of a species that cannot agree on what happened.*
- **The contents,** with headings on the left and numerals and page numbers on the right:
  - *The Compiler's Introduction …… 2*
  - *The Necessity of Singular Truth …… I …… 5*
  - *Legitimacy Through Controlled Opposition …… II …… 9*
  - *Strategic Ambiguity Through Backup Control …… III …… 13*
  - *Correction Over Punishment …… IV …… 18*
  - *Controlled Conflict and the Calibration Grounds …… V …… 23*
  - *Narrative Preemption, or The Restorer's Authority …… VI …… 29*
  - *Implementation Annex …… 33*
  - *Register of Contributors …… 39*
- **Everything falls into place.** Kira lays the Book 6 strip against the right-hand edge, and *IV 18 / V 23 / VI 29* match. The Introduction, Three, Five and page 30 all fit. The Warning isn't listed. Kira: "It was never part of what they typed." Nikolai counts six principles, an Annex and a Register. **Vance says nothing.**
- **Guesses.** Alexei: "A register of what?" Marcus: "Names." He files it under UNCHECKED. The only count anyone says is the Introduction's seventeen. Then Four's title, ***Correction Over Punishment***. Almost everyone in this kitchen has been through correction. Nobody has the text.
- **Principle One.** Nikolai reads it: *"Singular truth, mechanically verified, universally enforced. All other principles derive from this necessity. Democracy failed because it assumed humans could collectively determine what happened. We remove the assumption."*
- **Principle Two.** Dmitri reads it aloud, formally, as he once read reports:
  > *"A system without opposition appears tyrannical; a system with uncontrolled opposition becomes unstable. Let there be several Histologic nations, differing in method and agreeing in substance, and let there be enemies outside them all. Peace would raise questions. Total war would end the system. Controlled conflict answers every question before it is asked."*
  He squares the page to the table's edge. ***"Then the war between the nations is not a war. The operation should stop."***
- **Marcus:** ***"Then the Chronos hand is theirs. Cutting it is cutting *them*."*** Alexei agrees with him. Elena is silent. Kira looks at *agreeing in substance* for a long time. Nikolai copies it into his notebook and underlines *agreeing*.
- **Vance** holds the edge of p. 1 so that her hand stays still, and looks at the *VII* for a long time. ***"That copy should not be in Chronos."*** Dmitri asks her formally whose it is. "No." Old Songs hums and says nothing, watching Vance watch the numeral.
- **Before they leave, as the light goes.** While Marcus is at the car, Kira photographs every sheet under the lamp. Dmitri sees that she doesn't tell Marcus, and says nothing.

## Ending
**Dusk, the checkpoint.** Major Sokolov comes out into the floodlit fog, hands in her pockets. "Volkov." He tells her nothing. "Whatever it is, I would rather not be told." Dmitri, to the fog: ***"I will protect it. I do not agree with it. I have been an instrument before."***

## Notes
- **(Twist 1 clue)** *agreeing in substance*: Kira's look and Nikolai's underline. Nobody connects it to the backups.
- **Manuscript rules:** §4.1, §4.3 and §4.4 wording. No mark decoded and no holder named, no "twelve", no text of Four, nothing about Seven.
- **The contents layout** is new and binding. It matches the Book 6 strip.
- **Custody:** Marcus returns the originals to Julian on **Tue Sep 24**, and they go back in box 204117, so copy VII stays in Chronos. From then on the heroes hold only Kira's photographs (which Marcus doesn't know about) and their notes, never the originals. When Marcus hands the folder back, he names no page and quotes nothing: the exchange is in Julian's log and in the disclosure (Ch 21). Kira's photographs feed Ch 12.
- Old Songs speaks no words. Dmitri uses no contractions.
