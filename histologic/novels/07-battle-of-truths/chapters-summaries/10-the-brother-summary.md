# Chapter 10: The Brother

**POV:** Julian Kovač (the first of his two POV chapters)
**Date:** Fri Sep 20, 2109 (opening on the evening of Thu Sep 19)
**Setting:** The Chronos lakeshore city: the Temporal Archive (the sealed holdings on the lower floor, the reading rooms, the end reading desk under its lamp); Julian's flat, with its window on the lake
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
This is the inside of the man being used. He is logged, frightened for his key, and happier than he has been in years, because Marcus comes through the door at eight every morning. His brother comes back. In a box nobody catalogued he finds the thing he will give away.

## Beats
- **Morning, logged.** He wakes at 06:11:52. He lay awake from 04:30, and he reads his log the way other people read the weather. He knows one entry by heart: at 03:12 on Aug 14 he was **asleep in this bed, every breath recorded**. *It proves where his body was. It doesn't prove where his key was.* Temporal Defence has questioned him twice.
- **The old man's habits in the building.** A **chipped cup** stands on the shelf above the reconciliation desk. Nobody uses it and nobody moves it. The sealed boxes are shelved **fore-edge out, labels turned in**, "the old man's way". The lamp at the end reading desk is left on at night. Julian notices all three and thinks about none of them. **Never explain.**
- **Marcus.** Julian's log shows his heart rate rise at 08:00:14 each morning, when Marcus comes in. Julian knows it does, and doesn't scroll back to it. The feeling stays unnamed, even to himself.
- **Tue Sep 17, recalled.** Over lunch Marcus mentioned the old gentleman who sings "The Names" every night for a wife and a little daughter, lost in a lakeshore storm "in the summer of '74". Julian wrote *summer 2074, lakeshore* in his annotation layer.
- **Thu Sep 19, 21:40, the sealed holdings.** He is tracing the key's use in the Rewriting through its authority history, working late and alone. At the end of a shelf is a box with **no catalogue entry**, marked only with an accession number: **204117**. Inside is a card folder tied with archival tape. Through the thumb-cut he can see the lower right corner of the top sheet, and a small typed numeral: ***VII***. It is the same style as the *X* on December's published pages. His hands go cold.
- **The two-officer rule.** An uncatalogued sealed item may be opened only with a second officer present, and both logs record it. He is alone. He enters the find in the holdings ledger in his own hand, locks the box in the Senior Historian's cabinet, and doesn't untie the tape.
- **Fri Sep 20.** He works beside Marcus all day and says nothing about the box. He wants to know what it is first, and doesn't ask himself why.
- **Evening: Adrian,** at the flat. It is their first dinner in eleven years. Adrian came across the lake by a route he doesn't describe. "Your log's got me now." "It had you already. We mirror Veridica's outlaw notices." At first it is awkward. Adrian takes the back off the kitchen clock, as he did at Aunt Helena's as a boy, and Julian laughs for the first time in weeks. Then it is tender: their father, and Helena's silence.
- **"The commissioner."** Adrian asks with an odd look: "Chen. Is he kind to you?" Julian says yes, too fast. Adrian: "Good." Later Julian scrolls back to that second, and still can't read it.

## Ending
**23:40.** Julian goes back to the Archive with his own keys, and for the first time in his career he breaks the two-officer rule. At the end desk, under the lamp, he unties the tape. There are nine typed sheets, with a nick out of every *e*. The top one is a title page with a list beneath it: ***THE FOUNDATION OF CERTAINTY.*** *A design for the governance of a species that cannot agree on what happened.* He decides to give it to Marcus tomorrow, because Marcus is the one person he trusts. His log records the decision. For once, he wants it to.
