# Chapter 1: Two Million Journals

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Wed Aug 14, 2109
**Setting:** The Commission's great hall in the Justice Center, Veridica Capital (emergency session, 11:00); the east corridor; Nikolai and Maya's flat that night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The center they refused in Book 6 has been used anyway, through the door Maya found in January. Every fact in the chapter is exact, and none of them leads anywhere Kira can follow. It ends on the one question nobody can answer, which is the book's first Twist 1 clue.

## Beats
- **Opening in Kira's incident-report voice.** *Aug 14, 2109. 11:00. Great hall, Justice Center. Emergency session. Cameras: eleven.* Minister **Chen Zhao** presides, grave and warm, with tea at his elbow. The benches are full.
- **Maya's log.** **Maya Reeves**, lead technician for the Concordance intake since January, reads out the restore-path log in times, which is her voice:
  - between **03:12:00 and 03:29** last night, about **2.1 million** registered journals were "restored from the master";
  - the operations ran through the Concordance's own **restore path from the master**, the component she wrote down by hand in January (Book 6 Ch 23);
  - every operation carried a **countersignature the path accepted in place of Technical Director authority**.
  Kira remembers Maya putting that component number on the floor of the flat in January: *restorable means editable.*
- **What was changed.** Samples go up on the panel. The edits are small and plausible and meant to set neighbours against each other: a debt, a quarrel, a face at a door. They carry **forged countersignatures** by real neighbours, which on a screen can't be told from genuine ones.
- **The paper holds.** By noon the keepers' reports come in along the pairing network. Wherever a registered page and an unregistered paper book record the same day, the **paper books agree with each other and not with the screens**. The paper books are untouched, and they are the proof. Kira counts the towns reporting. She doesn't count the journals.
- **Chronos, at noon,** on the monitored broadcast: the **witness books of Julian Kovač's networks**, logged in Chronos's **witness index**, have been altered in the same seventeen minutes. Temporal Defence calls it "a second Veridican attack on the memory of Chronos".
- **The minutes.** Kira hears *03:12–03:29* and does not move. They are the Chronos Gap's minutes, to the second. She looks for Wei's seam, the chain re-sealed backwards, and finds nothing. **Nothing was cut.** Every edit came in the front door, as a restoration.
- **Wei** is calm, helpful and appalled. He credits Maya by name ("Ms. Reeves found it first"). He takes the failure on his department, on the record. His line reads as candour the first time and as something else the second: *"The path did what it was built to do. It restored what it was given."* Asked how it accepted a foreign authority, he says, "I don't know yet." No interior.
- **Pell**, from the Ministry bench, gloats in the League's voice: *"The journals were never safe."*
- **Kovač** in the east corridor afterwards, hands in her coat pockets. "Julian's books too?" "Yes." She pushes her glasses up and says nothing more.

## Ending
**23:10, Nikolai's work table.** Maya is timestamping. Marcus is standing. Nikolai lays the countersignature from the restore operations beside the **profile he built in February** from the Archive's public reconciliation notices and the test slip dated *13 Dec 2108*. They match: how it closes a sealed block, and the interval before it signs. It has the **habits of the senior reconciliation key of the Chronos Temporal Archive**, the key that countersigned the Gap. Nikolai says what he said in February: *"I know the key. I don't know the hand."* Marcus takes out his lists and doesn't write anything. Kira asks the question aloud, and nobody in the room can answer it: **"How does a Chronos key reach a Veridican master?"**

## Notes
- **(Twist 1 clue)** Kira's closing question (plot v3). The path accepting a Chronos countersignature "in place of" Technical Director authority is the same clue in technical form. Nobody concludes anything from it.
- **(Twist 1 clue)** The Rewriting runs at **03:12–03:29**, the Gap's minutes. Kira notes it and files it. It pays off in Ch 14, where Wei's tools default to 03:12.
- **(Twist 1/2 texture)** *Restore path from the master* is Book 6 Ch 23's component, exactly. Wei's authorisation is not on the operations. The countersignature stood in for it.
- **Nikolai's caution** is his Book 6 Ch 30 line, repeated. It must stay "the key", never "Julian's hand". Marcus's failure in Ch 2 is to stop honouring the distinction.
- **The profile** was built from **years** of Archive reconciliation notices (Book 6 Ch 30). Don't comment on it here. It becomes quietly consistent with Twist 2 later: the habits belong to the key, not the man.
- **Vocabulary:** Chronos's **witness index**, never "register". The Concordance's journals are "registered", which is fine as a verb.
- **Wei:** no POV and no interior, with one beat of double meaning at most. **Chen Zhao** gets no "restore" in this chapter; save his quota. **Pell's line** is used here, and later chapters shouldn't reuse it.
- **Kovač** appears only briefly. Nothing about the key's dates.
