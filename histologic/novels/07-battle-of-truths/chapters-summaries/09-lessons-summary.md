# Chapter 9: Lessons

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Sep 2 – Mon Sep 16, 2109
**Setting:** The farmhouse kitchen table (evenings); the lakeshore checkpoint road; the Chronos Temporal Archive's reading rooms (Mon Sep 9) and front steps (Mon Sep 16); the capital (Sat Sep 7)
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Marcus is taught to do to Julian what was done to him, and turns out to be good at it. The cover goes up. Elena opens the Story 01 thread with one sentence. The chapter ends on the gift Marcus least deserves: Julian is glad to see him.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry:** *September second, 2109. Nineteen hundred hours. Farmhouse, border zone. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Volkov, E. Purpose: instruction. Subject:* He can't write the name, and leaves it blank.
- **The cover.** Maitland's office proposes a **Joint Inquiry into the Rewriting**. Aubry accepts within two days, because Chronos wants its key's misuse traced with Veridican witnesses present. The co-chairs are Marcus and Julian, in the Archive's reading rooms, from **Mon Sep 9**. Maitland's office passes on that Julian's first question was whether Veridica had named its co-chair yet.
- **The accreditation.** The Veridican delegation sits under the Lakes Talks' Synthesis protocol. There is **no Chronos monitoring**. They cross at the lakeshore checkpoint daily, are escorted in public, and are logged by every Chronos citizen who sees them.
- **The lessons,** at the kitchen table with the back-room door shut: in the afternoons from Sep 2 to 6, then in the evenings from Sep 9 to 13, after the crossing. Elena has her unwritten notebook. She briefs and doesn't explain:
  - *"You don't make him trust you. You make it easy for him to choose to. A man defends a choice. He doesn't defend a gift."*
  - *"Find what he's missing and be it. The same hour, the same chair."* In Chronos everyone Julian knows is on file. *"You're the one thing he has to take on trust."*
  - *"Tell him the truth about everything except the one thing. He'll keep every word to the nanosecond. Lie as little as you can, and never where it can be checked."*
  - *"Don't ask him for anything. Especially not the key. A man offers what he's never been asked for."*
  - *"Let him see you almost say something. Then don't."*
- **The exercises.** Elena plays Julian, using his published MERIDIAN log. "You pushed your glasses up. You do that before you check something. Don't check him in front of him." He asks how long it took, in 2105. "Forty-seven." It is the number of interactions. He is a good student, and his false cover entries (Ch 6) are getting easier to write.
- **Sat Sep 7.** Marcus passes the testimony intake, where Kovač sits. He thinks of asking her what Julian was like at nineteen, and doesn't. The record has already told him.
- **Mon Sep 9, the opening.** The reading rooms are long and pale, with nanosecond clocks. Aubry, cool: *"The probability that this inquiry finds what either of us wants is low. I would like it on record that I agreed anyway."* Julian, gray and formal across the table, doesn't look at Marcus all day.
- **Fri Sep 13, the last lesson.** They have the conversation Book 6's yard only began: the fountain, 17:43, milk and cinnamon, the flat with the blockers, the seven she turned before him. He asks which parts. ***"Some of it was real."*** She doesn't say which. He asks for one checkable fact instead, the one Continuity took: the colour of her dress at the fountain. "Blue." He files it under CHECKED, *Witness: Volkov, E.*, as testimony, not memory. At the door: "You'll be good at it. That's the part that costs."
- **The weekend.** Kira asks whether Elena is any good. Marcus: "I am."

## Ending
**Mon Sep 16, 07:58.** The delegations have gone home and the working phase begins. Julian is waiting alone on the Archive's steps, in his gray cardigan. He couldn't say it on the ninth. His log records it now: ***"I hoped it would be you."***
