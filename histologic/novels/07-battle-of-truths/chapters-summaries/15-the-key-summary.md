# Chapter 15: The Key

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Fri Oct 18 – Thu Oct 24, 2109
**Setting:** The Chronos Temporal Archive (the reading rooms, and the reconciliation rooms on the lower floor); Julian's flat, with its lake window; the lakeshore checkpoint road at night; the flat in the capital
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Everything Elena taught him works. The key comes to him unasked, from a man glad to give it. In the Inquiry's last week Marcus lets Julian hope, and that is the worst thing he does in the book. He knows it while he does it, and writes it down.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry:** *October eighteenth, 2109. Sixteen-forty. Chronos Temporal Archive, reconciliation rooms, lower floor. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Kovač, J. Event: access.*
- **Fri Oct 18, the gift.** Unasked, Julian takes him down to the reconciliation rooms. They are cold and clean, with a long desk and a **chipped cup** on the shelf that Julian doesn't use or move. He enrols Marcus's co-chair access on the doors and terminals, and signs the grant in the Archive's access ledger in his own name: "So you can see the key's history for yourself. Not through me." *A man offers what he's never been asked for.* Marcus feels it as success and as nausea together.
- **That night** he tells Kira in one line. She writes the access into the refusal branch without comment.
- **The evenings, Sat–Wed, at Julian's flat.** Julian cooks badly and cheerfully. Everything Marcus says goes into Julian's log, so he says almost nothing false. The lie is the whole of it, not any one sentence.
  - **The journal.** Julian reads him a page about a network keeper who rebuilt her book by hand after the Rewriting. He turns past the next page quickly, and Marcus sees a date and one initial: *M.*
  - **Adrian.** Julian tells him about the dinner happily: the clock, their father. "He asked about you." Marcus answers lightly.
  - **The garden, remembered.** They talk about the bench under the pear tree at Saltmarsh Crossing. Julian: "I'd like to sit there again when this is over." Marcus: "Yes." He lets it mean something.
- **By day** the last week is procedural. The co-chairs' draft report is due **Fri Oct 25**. Aubry sends a note of thanks "for a very orderly autumn". Over a file, Julian's hand brushes Marcus's, and neither of them moves it at once.
- **Wed Oct 23, 22:40, at the flat's door.** Julian: "Marcus, I—" He stops, then: "Drive carefully. The fog's in." Marcus lets the pause stand, puts his hand on Julian's arm for a moment, and goes. *Let him see you almost say something.* This time it was Julian who almost said it, and Marcus let him.
- **23:00, the checkpoint.** Floodlights in the fog, and his accreditation is checked. On the Veridican side he sits in the car for ten minutes before he can drive.
- **Thu Oct 24.** Kira finishes the image. At the kitchen table they go through the scope list, the hard stop and 02:40, courteous with each other, like colleagues.

## Ending
Late, alone, he opens his lists. Under CHECKED he writes the one entry he can verify: ***Oct 24, 2109. Chronos Temporal Archive. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Kovač, J. Event: I used him. Verification: me.***
