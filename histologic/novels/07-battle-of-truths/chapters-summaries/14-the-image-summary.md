# Chapter 14: The Image

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Mon Oct 7 – Mon Oct 14, 2109
**Setting:** A windowless former archive store on sub-level two of the Commission building in the capital; Historian's Lane at night (Oct 14); the flat
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Kira builds the knife, and the knife fits too well. Every Twist 1 clue the operation can give is here, and each has an innocent reading. Then the man who once did to her what Marcus is doing now asks her to stop it, and she keeps it from Marcus.

## Beats
- **Opening in incident-report voice:** *Oct 7, 2109. 08:00. Sub-level two, Commission building. Personnel: Osman, K.; Volkov, N.; Reeves, M. Objective: narrow restoration image. Scope: two record sets, one revocation.*
- **Wei's tools.** He brings the case down himself in his right hand, kind and unhurried: "They're yours, Kira. Change whatever you like." The manifest is headed ***RESTORATION***, the word from the folder in Ashford's data centre (Book 4 Ch 24). She files it.
- **The design:**
  - **Revocation:** strip the Archive key's signing rights in the true source. It is signed with the **Veridican master credentials** the tools carry, through the Concordance's restore path (Book 6 Ch 23).
  - **Restoration:** Chronos's *own* records of the Rewriting (Aug 14, 03:12–03:29) and of the Gap (Dec 13, 2107, 03:12–03:29), loaded through the reconciliation rooms under Marcus's co-chair access.
  - **Her rules:** nothing else is touched. The scope list holds two record sets, and there is a **hard stop** if the scope count goes over it.
- **Too clean.** The tools fit Chronos's backup as if they were cut for it: field names, block sizes, the handshake. There's a branch for the moment Chronos refuses a foreign credential and demands its own key, and nothing has ever run through it. Maya: "Either someone sloppy built this, or someone who never needed it." Kira builds the refusal branch herself.
- **03:12.** The scheduler's **default execution time is 03:12**: the Gap's minute, and the Rewriting's. Kira sits very still, then changes it to **02:40**. Maya timestamps the change unasked (14:06:22). Nobody asks why.
- **The mirror.** Nikolai maps Chronos's backup against Veridica's, which he knew from inside as Pieter Lang: ***"Their backup structure is like looking in a mirror."*** He says it uneasily, as a figure of speech. His Book 6 match started all this. He keeps three copies of every build, and says for the first time that he wishes he didn't.
- **Maya, a Reeves:** ***"Tools this good are built for the job they're used for."*** She means her father's cradles. Kira narrows the image again, to signing rights only and exact intervals.
- **At home** she and Marcus speak in logistics. He spends some evenings at Julian's table, and she doesn't ask what he says there.

## Ending
**Mon Oct 14, 21:30, Historian's Lane,** by the loose stone where it began (Story 02). **Adrian Kovač** is under a lamp, with the scar above his left eyebrow. It is the first time she has seen him since December 2105. He doesn't touch her. He had dinner with his brother three weeks ago. ***"Whatever he's doing to my brother, I did to you. Don't let him finish it."*** She says nothing quotable, and he goes. At the flat Marcus is asleep over his lists. She writes *Adrian. Historian's Lane. 21:30* in her journal, and doesn't tell him.
