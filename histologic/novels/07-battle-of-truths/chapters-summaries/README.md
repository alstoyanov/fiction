# Battle of Truths: Chapter Summaries

These summaries are based on `plots/07-battle-of-truths-full.md` (v3), with the drafting decisions below. The old summaries, chapters and documents followed the abandoned Nov 2025 plot. They have been deleted, and git history keeps them.

**Structure:** a Prologue, 30 chapters and an Epilogue, in four parts.
**Target length:** about 3,300–3,600 words per chapter, around 110,000 words in total.
**Tone:** a spy thriller and moral tragedy. It should be intimate, tense and unsparing. Every chapter ends on a turn.
**Canon rules:**
- `PROJECT-RULES.md` §4–6, `worldbuilding/`, `characters/` and `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md` all apply.
- Storms are perceptual, never weather.
- Seeding is silent.
- Removing an interface kills.
- The Judge deals in probabilities, and anything above 0.60 means custody.
- The manuscript reveal schedule is binding.
- Wei never has a point of view.
- Singh does not appear.

## Drafting Decisions (Beyond Plot v3)

1. **The Rewriting.** On **Wed Aug 14, 2109, from 03:12 to 03:29**, the same seventeen minutes as the Chronos Gap, about **2.1 million** journals registered in Veridica's Concordance are altered.
   - **The changes** are small and plausible, the kind that turn neighbours against each other, and they carry **forged countersignatures**.
   - **In Chronos,** the witness books of Julian's networks are hit the same way. Those books are logged in Chronos's **witness index**. Don't call it a "register"; that word is reserved for the manuscript.
   - **Paper books** kept by the pairing method are untouched, and they prove the tampering.
   - **The route in** was the Concordance's own **restore path from the master** (Book 6 Ch 23).
   - **The countersignature** has the **habits of the Chronos Temporal Archive's senior reconciliation key**, matching Nikolai's profile from Book 6 Ch 30.
2. **The Archive's public roll.** Chronos lists office-holders on a public roll. It reads: *KOVAČ, J. Senior Historian. Key-holder since 2104.* It also shows *MERA, A.* as "Senior Historian emeritus from 2104". **Both entries were edited**; the truth comes out in Ch 24.
   - **Julian's own log was edited to agree with the roll** (Ch 19): its system layer carries a countersignature every night since 2104, and his lived log for the January 2108 handover morning shows him alone at his desk. The edit predates RESTORATION; who made it stays open.
3. **Julian's cut-off line (Ch 2):** *"I don't understand how they used the key. I've only had it since—"* At that moment Aubry comes into the garden and cuts across him with a point of protocol. Julian never finishes the sentence to Marcus.
4. **Gullhaven.** A lakeshore town just north of Kestrel Point. A ferryman, left **unnamed** on the page, is accused by a registered journal of informing on his street in 2107. He is beaten on the evening of **Mon Aug 19** and dies in the night. Ruth brings the Kestrel Book's paper copy of that street's entries, which disproves the accusation, **four hours late**. She writes his name down for Old Songs, who adds it to **"The Names"** in Ch 7.
5. **The dead of the second war** are counted in the Veridican press: the Gullhaven ferryman, then storm deaths after Chronos resumes corrective broadcasting on **Wed Aug 21** (at 0.91). A child dies in the lakeshore village of **Marram End** on **Sun Aug 25**. **Seven dead** by Ch 6. Keep the numbers modest.
   - **The count:** the ferryman, Aug 19–20 (1); two storm deaths on the night of Wed Aug 21 (3); two on the night of Thu Aug 22, published Sat Aug 24 (5); one on the shore road on the night of Sat Aug 24 (6); the Marram End child on Sun Aug 25 (7).
   - **The last death is on Aug 25.** Nobody else dies before the storms stop on Nov 4 (Ch 20: *"Seven. And none since noon."*).
6. **RESTORATION.**
   - **Named by Wei** in Maitland's office on **Mon Aug 26**. Marcus notices the word and accepts it.
   - **Maitland says, word for word:** *"I can't authorise this. But I won't stop you."*
   - **Chen Zhao is not present.**
   - **The aim, as stated:** revoke the Archive key's rights in the true source, and restore Chronos's own records of the Rewriting and the Gap.
7. **The cover.** The **Joint Inquiry into the Rewriting**, co-chaired by Marcus and Julian, working in the **reading rooms of the Chronos Temporal Archive** in the Chronos lakeshore city.
   - It opens on **Mon Sep 9**.
   - It sits **Monday to Thursday**; Fridays are free, which is why Marcus can be at the farmhouse on Fri Oct 4 (Ch 13).
   - It is not the Lakes Talks. The Customs House is used only for the emergency session in Ch 2.
8. **Elena's lessons.** They run from **Sep 2 to Sep 13** at the farmhouse table. *"Some of it was real."* She doesn't say which part.
9. **Copy VII.**
   - **Found:** Julian finds it on **Thu Sep 19** in the Archive's sealed holdings, in a box marked only with an accession number.
   - **Handed over:** he gives it to Marcus on **Sat Sep 21**, and it is read at the farmhouse on **Mon Sep 23**.
   - **A loan, not a gift:** Marcus returns the originals on **Tue Sep 24**, to box **204117**, so **copy VII stays in Chronos**. Afterwards the heroes hold only Kira's photographs (taken Sep 23, without telling Marcus) and their notes. Nobody names or quotes a page in Julian's presence.
   - **Contents** (nine typed pages, chipped *e*, copy numeral **VII** in the lower right):
     - **p. 1**, the title page and table of contents;
     - **pp. 5–8**, Principle One, marked *HV·01*;
     - **pp. 9–12**, Principle Two, marked *SO·04*.
   - **The table of contents** lists the Introduction, then Principles One to Six **with their titles** and page numbers (§4 headings), then the **Implementation Annex** and the **Register of Contributors**. The handwritten Seven is not listed.
   - **What becomes known:** the manuscript's title; the existence of a Register and an Annex; Principle Four's title (*Correction Over Punishment*, p. 18). Principle Four's **text** stays unknown until Book 8.
   - **Vance:** *"That copy should not be in Chronos."* She will not say whose it is.
10. **The 2074 storm.** Julian finds the Chronos-side log of the lakeshore storm of **Mon Jun 11, 2074**: *"Calibration run 14. Coordinated with V. Border Broadcast Division."* He sends it through Marcus on **Wed Oct 2**, for "the old gentleman who sings", and Old Songs reads it on **Fri Oct 4**.
11. **The image.**
    - **Built:** by Kira, Nikolai and Maya between **Oct 7 and Oct 24**, in a basement room of the Commission.
    - **Wei's tools:** they are "too clean", and their default execution time is **03:12**. Kira notices and changes it.
    - **Nikolai:** *"Their backup structure is like looking in a mirror."*
    - **Adrian** visits Kira on **Mon Oct 14**.
12. **Access.** On **Fri Oct 18**, Julian gives Marcus co-chair access to the reconciliation rooms without being asked. He signs the grant in the Archive's **access ledger** in his own name (Ch 15), which is why he is detained (Ch 17, 19).
13. **The operation.** From **Fri Oct 25, 22:00** to **Sat Oct 26, about 04:10**, in the Temporal Archive.
    - **The team:** Alexei on the route over the Drowned Causeway; Dmitri on the doors; Nikolai on the relay; Kira at the image; Marcus holding Julian's access.
    - **The failure:** Kira runs the image at **02:40**. Chronos's backup accepts the Veridican master credentials, and the scope indicator climbs past everything she wrote.
    - **Propagation finishes at about 04:06.**
14. **The awakening in Chronos.** Chronos citizens remember by consulting their logs, and their logs now agree with Veridica's version.
    - **Nothing is seeded.** Chronos people simply trust their logs over their unaided memory.
    - **Who still remembers:** the **9S patients**, whose lives were never logged, and the **six Chronos paper keepers** from Book 6's Night of Copies. These are the only *Chronos memory*.
    - **Others who know the old history:** **Julian**, who stops deferring to his log once it lies about him (Ch 19, 21); **Aubry**, through her printed papers only (Ch 19); and **Tanaka** and **Morrison**, Veridicans whose interfaces don't record (Ch 18).
15. **Julian after the operation.**
    - **Sat Oct 26:** detained in the morning, because his access was logged. He is held in the directorate building by the harbour (Book 5 Ch 6).
    - **Fri Nov 1:** sentenced to **recording forced to maximum** and removed from office.
    - **Fri Nov 15, 14:00:** he publishes his log by **self-disclosure**, from detention. That right survives outlaw status.
    - **The disclosure's span:** every hour in Marcus's company from **Mon Nov 12, 2108** to **Fri Oct 25, 2109**, uncut (318 hours, Ch 22), including Aug 15 in the Customs House garden.
16. **Chronos's Judge.** On **Mon Nov 4** it determines, at **0.93**, that Veridica "bears no responsibility" for the Rewriting or the Gap, and the storms stop.
17. **Julian's letter** to Kovač, handwritten and dated **20 January 2108**: *"They've made me Senior Historian. I start in February. They've given me the old man's keys. I'm frightened of them, Aunt Helena."* She never answered it, and found it again on **Mon Nov 25**.
    - **When Julian got the key:** he **received the keys in January 2108** (by the letter's date) and **took office on Wed Feb 1, 2108**, both after the Chronos Gap (Dec 13, 2107). His own wording, to Aubry (Ch 19): *"the keys in January, the office in February."*
18. **Dr. Augustin Mera** (offstage).
    - **Career:** Senior Historian from 2088 until his retirement in **January 2108**.
    - **Death:** "died" on **Sat Feb 18, 2108**, and was cremated within a day.
    - **Who he really was stays open.** He may be the Chronos hand, or someone's instrument. Don't resolve it.
19. **Marcus's confession.** On **Mon Dec 2 at 14:00**, on the Justice Center steps, **with no remedy attached**. His contamination index rises to **0.57** and stays there. It was 0.44 at the end of Book 6.
    - **At 0.57, Marcus makes only daytime visits to the farmhouse** (Ch 25, 26). The farmhouse is paper and not in the record, so the index doesn't move; a night there would.
    - **After the confession, Marcus privately tells Chen Zhao** that Julian's pages carry copy numeral VII and that their contents list a Register of Contributors (Ch 25). From Dec 2 Chen Zhao knows that VII and a Register exist. He has seen no page.
20. **The Chronos testimony.** From **Dec 9 to Dec 13**, **11,000** signatures. On Fri Dec 13, the second anniversary of the Gap, Chronos's Judge records a **"minority pattern, 0.07"**.
21. **Martinez's folder.** The SFW received its account of RESTORATION on **Mon Oct 14**, eleven days before the operation. The attaché's heading reads *Phase: justification*. Martinez shows it to Isaiah on **Mon Dec 16**.
22. **The SFW threat.**
    - A **fleet** is assembling in the Atlantic, with staging in unaligned territory.
    - SFW soldiers **have no interfaces**, so they are immune to storms and seeding.
    - Veridica and the SFW share no border.
23. **Old Songs' voice rules are binding.**
    - He speaks rarely.
    - He has **one speech, in Ch 26**.
    - He hums.
    - Every tune lifts at the end like a question.
    - Songbook lyrics only: "The Names", "The Two Maps", and the Lakeshore Air **falling "no" phrase** from Book 4 Ch 14.
    - **No riddle** is required in this book. If one is used, use only one, and not the "twelve shepherds" riddle, which is reserved and would leak "twelve".
24. **No one says "twelve"** about the signatories. The Introduction's "seventeen contributors" is the only count anyone knows. The table of contents shows that a Register **exists**, not how many names it holds.
25. **Marcus and Julian.** Marcus uses trust and friendship. Julian's love is **never spoken**, and Marcus lets him hope. There is **no sexual relationship**, and Marcus and Kira stay together, strained.
26. **Isaiah's "four"** (Ch 12) means **four copies' worth of fragments**: none (the Introduction), X (Three and Five), XI (page 30) and VII (One and Two, from Kira's photographs of pp. 5–12). He never receives p. 1, so he doesn't know or use the title.
27. **"One more time. But this time, we do it right."** is spoken by **Marcus**, after Kira's nod (Ch 30).
28. **Marcus's age is not stated** in this book. He is 31 until he turns 32 in November 2109.
