# Chapter 30: Battle of Truths

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Dec 23, 2109
**Setting:** The First Minister's office (the same room as Ch 6); the corridor outside; Marcus's flat, night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The book closes where the betrayal began, in the office where Marcus asked for it. The man who said "I can't authorise this" now asks outright. Marcus says yes on one condition, which he can't yet define, and he starts a new list whose first question turns the war inside out.

## Beats
- **Opening entry, 09:40, on the stairs:** *Dec 23, 2109. First Minister's office. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Osman, K. Summoned by: Maitland, O. Purpose:* He leaves it blank. The last time he climbed these stairs was **Aug 26** (Ch 6). He notes that he came alone that time, and asked.
- **In his pocket:** Isaiah's letter, which arrived by the Synthesis courier on Saturday. One line: *Someone in Veridica wants to be invaded.* He has shown it to Kira and no one else. He touches it once on the stairs, and doesn't again until the end.
- **Kira beside him.** They are strained and still together (README decision 25). They came in the same car and didn't talk. She knows everything he did now, from Julian's log.
- **The room.** Maitland is behind the desk, tired and courteous. **Colonel Sokolov** stands by the window in new insignia, hands in her coat pockets (Ch 29). The brief is on the desk: the fleet, the staging, the soldiers with no interfaces. Wei and Chen Zhao are **not present**. Marcus notes their absence as a fact entry.
- **Maitland opens plainly:** ***"The SFW is preparing to invade. We need you."*** The exchange, word for word:
  - **Marcus:** ***"I'm done with deception."***
  - **Maitland:** ***"This won't be a war of facts."***
  - **Marcus:** ***"Then you need soldiers, not spies."***
  - **Maitland:** ***"I need someone who understands both."***
  Maitland adds only that he knows Marcus's index. *"Fifty-seven. I read it on the second. I don't care."* Sokolov says nothing throughout, until one formal line when Maitland asks her: *"The fleet is real, First Minister."*
- **The echo.** Marcus hears Ch 20 in it ("You saved us." "At what cost?"), and doesn't say it again. His POV loses a word here (voice rule 3), the word for a man who is asked twice. He lets it go.
- **The look.** Marcus looks at Kira. It is a long moment, and it's not forgiveness; she hasn't forgiven him, and he hasn't asked. She nods, once. **Marcus:** ***"One more time. But this time, we do it right."*** Maitland doesn't ask what "right" means, and Marcus notes that he doesn't know yet himself.
- **The corridor.** Sokolov passes them without stopping. Formal first: *"Commissioners."* Then, low, on her way out: *"They are not coming for the lakeshore."* Kira asks Marcus in the lift whether he'll tell Maitland about the letter. *"Not yet. I don't know who in the building it's about."* Kira: *"Then it's about the building."*
- **Evening, the flat.** He takes out his lists. CHECKED, UNCHECKED and HOPES are all full. He reads down UNCHECKED, where *Julian?* no longer stands (Ch 25), and the empty line is still empty. He considers the record for a long time: he checked it once, and it said what he feared.

## Ending
He takes a clean sheet and writes a new heading, in his small historian's pencil hand: ***QUESTIONS FOR THE WAR.*** He takes Isaiah's letter out of his pocket and lays it beside the sheet. Then he writes the first entry: ***Who is invading whom?***
