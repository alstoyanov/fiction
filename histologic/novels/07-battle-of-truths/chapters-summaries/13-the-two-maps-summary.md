# Chapter 13: The Two Maps

**POV:** Mikhail "Old Songs" Petrov
**Date:** Fri Oct 4 – Sat Oct 5, 2109
**Setting:** The farmhouse: the kitchen with the cracked stove door, the bench by the stove, the yard and woodpile in morning fog (real weather)
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The storm that took Anya and Katya was on a schedule, and both shores kept it. It is the one time in the series he is angry, and there are no words. Then comes the first new song since "Many Hands". Then he learns that the gift came from the man Marcus is using, and was paid for with his own grief.

## Beats
- **Friday afternoon.** The Joint Inquiry does not sit on Fridays; its sessions run Monday to Thursday, like the Lakes Talks before it. So Marcus can be at the farmhouse. He hands him an Archive envelope at the stove: "From the Archive. It's for you." Then he goes out to split wood, because he can't stay in the room. He hasn't met the old man's eyes since the falling phrase (Ch 7).
- **The object before the words.** Heavy Chronos paper, and a card in a **sloping hand**, unsigned: *"For the old gentleman who sings. I was told of your wife and your daughter. I found this in our holdings and thought it was yours to have. I am so sorry."* Under it is an extract from the Archive's sealed operational series:
  > *Mon 11 Jun 2074. Lakeshore sector, far shore. Calibration run 14. Coordinated with V. Border Broadcast Division. Onset 15:00:00. Duration 41 min. Outcome: recorded.*
  A second sheet is the Archive's mirror of Veridican death notices for 12 June 2074: the town, a short list, and among the names **PETROV, A.** and **PETROV, K.**
- **He reads it twice.** At three that afternoon he was at the school, marking copybooks. He has never known where they were when it came, only where they were found. Katya sang the ferry verse wrong.
- **What it means, in his terms.** He has known Principle Five since last November (*measured, and raised*), but not that his family was in it. *Run 14* means there were thirteen before. *Coordinated with V.* means both shores kept the timetable. Eleven days ago Dmitri read *agreeing in substance* at this table. He doesn't think in numbered principles. He thinks: *two maps*.
- **The anger.** He picks up his own brown-glazed morning cup and breaks it against the stove, below the cracked door. It is the only sound. He says no words. In the yard the axe stops, then starts again. Kira stays in the doorway.
- **Vance** comes from the back room and sits on the bench. She says nothing and doesn't apologise. After a long time she picks up one shard and holds it, so that her right hand stays still. They sit inside the silence until the stove burns down to red. He doesn't hum. That night, for the first time in thirty-five years, he doesn't sing "The Names", even silently.
- **Saturday, first light, at the woodpile.** It comes with words, because he is free and on this side of the lake:
  > *Two maps of the one river,*
  > *one for either shore;*
  > *one puts the ford by the willows,*
  > *one by the mill-house door.*
  He stops there. The last line lifts like a question. He sings it again, and it holds.
- **Kira** brings two cups of tea, and he notes that neither is his. She tells him plainly: the envelope came from **Julian Kovač**, who met them on the marsh road in January and walked them to the ninth floor. Marcus told him about Anya and Katya over lunch, and Julian went looking. Old Songs knows what Marcus is doing (Ch 7). Now he knows who paid for the gift, and with what.

## Ending
Across the yard Marcus is stacking the split wood, very neatly. Old Songs looks at him for a long moment and **hums nothing at all**. It isn't the Wing C silence, and it isn't the falling phrase. It is worse.
