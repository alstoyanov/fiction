# Chapter 25: No Remedy

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Fri Nov 29 – Mon Dec 2, 2109 (the confession at 14:00 on Mon Dec 2)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse kitchen (Fri); Marcus's flat (Sat–Sun); the main steps of the Justice Center (Mon, 14:00); the commissioners' room at the Commission (Mon, late afternoon)
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Part Four opens with the only confession the design can't use: the truth with nothing attached. It costs Marcus his index and gives him nothing back. Then the kindest man in the building offers to help, and Marcus says yes.

## Beats
- **Fri Nov 29, the farmhouse.** Marcus comes by day, with Dmitri driving, and has to be back before dark. His four-page draft ends in a remedy: an apology from the Commission, a petition for Julian's reinstatement, an inquiry and a fund. **Vance** reads the object first and then the words, holding the cup to still her hand. She does not quote the page. In her own words she tells him that **Six** holds more than page 30: if a thing is going to come out anyway, the design tells it before anyone else can, and brings the repair along with it. *"You watched the First Minister do it on the eighteenth of December. It worked. It was written to work."* She strikes out the last page with one line. *"A confession with a remedy is an offer. The design accepts offers."* Marcus asks what is left. *"The truth, and nothing to do with it. It has no page for that."* Old Songs is at the stove and does not hum.
- **Sat–Sun.** The draft gets shorter each time he rewrites it. Kira, strained since Ch 23, reads one version and says only: *"Don't name us. Ours are ours."* Kovač comes on Sunday: *"It's my folder. I'll hold it."*
- **Mon Dec 2, 13:55, the steps.** It is cold and clear. The crowd holds press, staff, the Day-of-Testimony regulars, and League placards at the edge. Near the back, hood up, is a white beard: Old Songs, officially dead, in the open air of the capital. Marcus starts a fact entry about him and doesn't finish it.
- **14:00, the confession.** There is no lectern. The key lines:
  - *"My name is Marcus Chen. Between the second of September and the twenty-sixth of October I used the trust of Julian Kovač, Senior Historian of the Chronos Temporal Archive, to enter his Archive. He gave me his access because I was his friend. I let him believe I was."*
  - *"We meant to cut one hand out of Chronos's record. We wrote our history into theirs."*
  - *"I checked the record. I did not ask the witness. The record said what I was afraid of, and I stopped checking."*
  - Kovač reads the letter dated **20 January 2108**: *"They've made me Senior Historian. I start in February. They've given me the old man's keys. I'm frightened of them, Aunt Helena."* Marcus: *"He did not hold that key in December 2107. His record says he did. I believed his record."* He says nothing about who did hold it.
  - *"I have no remedy. I'm not asking you for anything. I'm telling you so that it's written down."* He asks no one to forgive him, and Julian least of all (Ch 23).
- **The index.** The Judge weighs his words against a record that now agrees with Veridica everywhere. At **14:52** Kira reads the Commission's public registry at the foot of the steps: **0.57**, up from 0.44. It is still 0.57 at midnight, and the next morning. The back of the crowd is empty. At 15:30 the government issues a statement: *the Commissioner speaks for himself.*
- **16:40, the commissioners' room.** **Chen Zhao**, with tea in a glass, is warm and fatherly: *"Very foolish, cousin, and very brave."* Marcus has stopped keeping things back. He tells him that Julian's pages carry a copy numeral, **VII**, like the **X** on the intake pages and the **XI** on page 30, and that their contents list ends in a Register of Contributors. XI came out of the Ministry's own correspondence (Book 5), so he has come to the top of the Ministry. **Chen Zhao:** *"Then let me help you trace these copy numerals, find this Register of theirs. The Ministry's archive is at your disposal."* Marcus accepts, and thanks him. He shows Chen Zhao nothing: the originals went back to box 204117 in September, and Marcus has only his notes. Write it straight: kindness offered, and kindness accepted.

## Ending
The flat, late. CHECKED: *Dec 2, 2109. Kovač, J.: innocent. Verification: a letter. Witness: Kovač, H.* He moves *Julian?* off UNCHECKED at last. It has stood there since February, and he underlined it on Aug 15. Then he holds the pencil over the empty line. There is nothing to write in its place.

## Notes
- **Deviation:** Principle Six's second sentence is **paraphrased, not quoted**, and the paraphrase must not track its wording. Book 6 withheld it, and manuscript §5 doesn't schedule it. Vance speaks from her own knowledge. Page 30 is public.
- **Chen Zhao is dramatic irony only.** No hint, no held look, and no "restore" in this scene. **From Dec 2 he knows, from Marcus, that copy VII and a Register exist.** He hasn't seen the pages. Nobody knows the Register's contents or count.
- **No "twelve"**, no decoded mark, and Mera is not named.
- **The index** goes from 0.44 to **0.57** and stays there for the rest of the book. The figure comes from the public registry, because no session is sitting. Daytime farmhouse visits don't register, since the farmhouse isn't in the record.
- **(Twist 2 payoff)** "I checked the record" (Ch 24) becomes public.
