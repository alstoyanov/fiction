# Chapter 8: The Knife

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Fri Aug 30, 2109
**Setting:** The Commission building, Veridica Capital (morning: Kira's office, the Technical Director's corridor); Nikolai and Maya's flat (noon); the farmhouse yard (afternoon); Marcus and Kira's flat (night)
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Kira can't stop him, and she won't leave the cutting to Wei. Part One closes with the heroes agreeing to something, each for their own reason, and every reason is a good one. She sets the terms of the one thing she can control: the image.

## Beats
- **Two days since the farmhouse.** Marcus has slept at the flat both nights. They have spoken in facts only. Chronos has accepted the joint inquiry in principle, and it is to open in the Archive's reading rooms in September. Julian's letter came through Park's secretariat. Marcus left it on the table unopened for an hour before he read it. Kira noticed the hour.
- **Her incident log, morning:** the dead are seven. The Marram End entry in four hands. The storms keep a schedule again. Border Security's request to redeploy is pending. Her reciting compulsion starts (*layer one: key exchange*), and she stops it by counting the pages of the brief.
- **The brief.** Wei's office has sent the commissioners a two-page outline of RESTORATION's "technical approach", with no code in it: the restore tooling "adapted from the Concordance", a target described as "the Archive key's rights in the true source", and a note that "the image will be prepared by the Technical Director's office". She reads *prepared by* three times.
- **The corridor.** Kira tells **Wei**, levelly, that she will build the image herself, and that nothing will go into Chronos that she hasn't written or read line by line. Wei is pleased and generous: *"I'd hoped you would. You'll have the tooling the day you start."* That is one beat. His pleasure is the worst thing about it.
- **Nikolai's table, noon.** Maya is timestamping, as ever. Nikolai hasn't slept either. His profile started this: the February match, and Aug 14. He says it plainly: *"I said the key. I never said the hand."* Kira: "I know. He heard the key." Nikolai agrees to help, sick with guilt: he'll take **the relay**, because he wants to be the one watching the line. Maya says nothing, and Kira doesn't ask her yet.
- **The farmhouse, afternoon.** Old Songs is in the barn. **Elena** is counting trucks in. Kira tells her the condition she means to set. Elena hears her out without looking at her, then says the thing she wouldn't say on Wednesday: *"If he is going to do it, someone had better teach him properly. Or it will cost the other one more."* She will teach him, starting Monday, at the kitchen table. Kira understands that Elena is the only one of them who has done it, and doesn't ask what it cost.
- **Adrian,** for one paragraph in Kira's head: the bridge, two weeks of evenings, and the warehouse where he told her he had been sent. Julian is his brother. She files it where she keeps 7.3, and doesn't finish the thought.

## Ending
**The flat, night.** She lays out her condition in order, as she lays out every fault:
- She builds the image herself, and it is **narrow**.
- It does two things only: it **revokes the Archive key's rights in the true source**, and it **restores Chronos's own records of the Rewriting and the Gap**. Nothing else is touched.
- She reads every line of Wei's tooling before it goes near Chronos, and she has the right to stop it.
- She won't touch Julian. That part is Marcus's.
Marcus agrees to every point without arguing, which frightens her more than an argument would. Then Kira: ***"Then I'll hold the knife, so it only cuts what it should."***
