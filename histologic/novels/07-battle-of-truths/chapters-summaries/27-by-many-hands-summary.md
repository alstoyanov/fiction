# Chapter 27: By Many Hands

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Mon Dec 9 – Fri Dec 13, 2109 (ending on the night of Fri Dec 13, the second anniversary of the Chronos Gap)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse kitchen, with Nikolai's voice line patched into the receiver on the dresser; by link, Ward 9S in Tanaka's hospital, and the kitchens of the six Chronos-shore keepers; by courier over the Drowned Causeway, bundles of paper
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
Undoing it can't be done by one hand, least of all the one that did it. Kira builds the reverse of her image: a way for Chronos citizens to disagree with their own logs. It is slow, partial and real, and it ends in a small, true number.

## Beats
- **Mon Dec 9, the problem, in Kira's incident format.** *Population: approx. 150 million. Record: consistent with Veridica's on every contested event. The Judge: confirms (0.93, Nov 4). Unaided memory: present, but overruled by the log in every citizen who checks.* Chronos has no Doctrine of testimony. What it has is The Judge's reading of the collective will from **patterns in the facts**. So the answer has to become a pattern.
- **The pieces, laid out in rows on the table:**
  - **The six Chronos keepers** from the Night of Copies (Book 6 Ch 26). They hold paper books in Chronos hands, and those books still carry the old lakeshore, the Temporal War as Chronos told it, and the Gap as a Veridican attack. Their paper didn't change.
  - **The 9S patients,** whose lives were never logged. **Tanaka** records their testimony in 9S each night and reads it over the link at 20:00.
  - **Julian's published log** (Nov 15), now the most complete record of the operation: every hour, the access given, the night of Oct 25.
  - **Julian himself.** He is recorded at maximum, so everything he agrees to is public by itself. Adrian brings his answer across the lake on Monday: he will help. He asks one thing, that Commissioner Chen not contact him. Kira passes it on to Marcus in those words. Marcus says, "Yes." He stays away all week.
- **The bridge.** Kira designs it on one sheet of paper, called *the comparison*, with three steps:
  1. **Write first.** In your own hand, write what you remember of the lakeshore and the Temporal War *before* you open your log.
  2. **Then look.** Open your log, and read what it says you know.
  3. **If they differ, sign,** and **self-disclose the minute you signed.** That minute of your own log goes on the public record (the protected right, README decision 15), and so the difference becomes a fact The Judge has to read.
  
  The keepers copy the sheet by hand and pass it on by the pairing method, each copy into two others in different towns. There is no index and no center. Nothing goes out from a source: it is the image built backwards.
- **The count, logged daily,** from the keepers' tallies over the link: Mon, 212. Tue, 1,340. Wed, 3,900. Thu, 7,600. Many who write first and then look sign nothing, because their memory yields at once. Kira logs those too.
- **Each night at 20:00, 9S.** Tanaka's voice, flat and exact, reads a patient's testimony in case-note form: the ford, the ferry, the old pier standing, whose shore the lakeshore was. **Old Songs hums the Lakeshore Air** under it at the stove, the whole time, and nobody asks him to stop. On Wednesday a keeper on the Chronos shore hears it down the line and hums it back.
- **Julian's part,** heard only through the keepers. On Thursday he publishes, at maximum, a short statement that his log of Oct 25 is complete and true, and that he gave the access and was used. It is the most-read entry in Chronos that day. He never names Marcus.
- **One cost.** A Chronos keeper is visited by directorate officers on Thursday night and keeps writing, as one did in Book 6. Her kitchen is logged in their interfaces too. Nikolai, who counts such things: *"That's two more officers who have now watched a Chronos citizen write by hand."* It is small, and it spreads.
- **Fri Dec 13.** It is two years to the day since the Gap. By 18:00 the count is **11,000**. Alexei brings the last bundle over the causeway in fog (real weather).

## Ending
**22:10.** Chronos's Judge publishes its weekly reading of the collective will. It includes one line nobody at the table has seen before: *a minority pattern, 0.07*, of citizens whose unaided memory disputes the record. It is small, and it is there. Kira reads it twice, then says, to the room and to nobody: ***"It's not a restoration. It's a beginning of a disagreement. That's more than they had."*** At the stove, the humming lifts at the end like a question.
