# Chapter 7: The Falling Phrase

**POV:** Mikhail "Old Songs" Petrov
**Date:** Wed Aug 28, 2109 (afternoon to night)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse in the border zone: the kitchen with the cracked stove door, the yard at dusk, the kitchen table at night
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
The circle refuses, and Marcus goes anyway. Old Songs gives the refusal in the only language everyone from Wing C reads without thinking: one phrase of the Lakeshore Air, sung downward. Then he does what he has always done with the dead, and adds the first name of the new war.

## Beats
- **The stove.** He is seventy-one and has kept the damper open just so for three summers now. He hums the Lakeshore Air, every phrase lifting like a question. **Elise** sits at the table's end, holding her cup with both hands. Her tremor is worse this summer. The room is full: **Kira, Elena, Alexei, Dmitri, Nikolai**. Nikolai looks at his hands.
- **The slip.** Alexei brought it last night from the lakeshore run: a square of paper torn from an order book, in Ruth Calloway's square capitals, with the ferryman's name. It is in his shirt pocket. He hasn't sung it yet, because a name should go into the song in front of people.
- **Marcus comes to the stove** and tells them standing, in order, as a fact entry: the seven dead; the key and the roll; Maitland's office; RESTORATION; the joint inquiry; Julian. He doesn't soften it.
- **The answers, one by one:**
  - **Vance**, quietly, in exact words: ***"They designed you to be the restorer. This is the restoration."***
  - **Kira**, very level: ***"This is wrong, Marcus. This is what we fought against."***
  - **Elena** says nothing. She counts the room and looks at the table.
  - **Alexei:** "If it stops the storms…" He doesn't finish it, and hates that he doesn't.
  - **Dmitri** asks for time, formally, without contractions.
  - **Nikolai** says nothing. It was his evidence.
- **Julian, in Old Songs' memory:** the young man on the marsh road in January, "nine minutes early", who walked a dead man into Chronos under his own name and went back down alone. Old Songs owes him that crossing, and he knows exactly what it cost to give.
- **The phrase.** He doesn't speak. He hums the Lakeshore Air through once. At the last phrase, where every tune of his lifts, he takes it **downward**, low and slow. It is the Wing C signal for **no** (Book 4 Ch 14). Five people in the kitchen learned it through a meal slot, and their bodies know it before they do: Marcus, Kira, and the three brothers. Alexei shuts his eyes. Elena and Vance didn't learn it, but they read it in the faces. Vance sets her cup down.
- **Marcus hears it.** He stands at the stove a moment longer. Then he thanks them, which is worse, takes his coat, and goes. Kira doesn't follow him to the car. Through the window Old Songs watches the headlamps go down the farm track.
- **"The Names," at dusk,** in the yard, with everyone who stayed. He sings it with words, as he does in Veridica, where he is free:
  > *Who went down to the water?*
  > *Name them, and they stay.*
  He puts the name from Ruth's slip into the verse, *(—) went down to the water — / name them, and they stay*, and it goes down with the thirty-one from last winter. There are six more dead, and nobody has sent him their names. He will not sing a number. He hums the place where their names should go, and the tune lifts.

## Ending
Night. The kitchen is empty. He takes the farmhouse book from the dresser and writes in his slow archivist's hand, the third thing he has ever written on paper that he meant to keep: ***He asked. We said no. He went.***
