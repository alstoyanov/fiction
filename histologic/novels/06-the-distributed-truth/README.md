# The Distributed Truth (Histologic, Book 6)

**Status:** first draft complete (Sept 2026): 32 chapters, ~114,000 words, from plot v2 and the chapter summaries. HTML export in `html-export/`, EPUB `the-distributed-truth.epub`. The earlier Nov 2025 draft, its summaries and `docs/` were deleted and are kept in git history.
**Set:** Tuesday, October 30, 2108 to Thursday, February 14, 2109. The epilogue is on Friday, March 15, 2109.
**Follows:** *The Lost Hour* (Book 5). **Followed by:** *Battle of Truths* (Book 7, provisional).

## Premise
Seventeen minutes vanish from the life-logs of Chronos, and the scar of the Lost Hour is in the wound. The result is a war of facts across the lakeshore. Veridica asks its most trusted restorer, Marcus Chen, to end the war by manufacturing the trust of Chronos's senior historian, Julian Kovač. Marcus refuses and publishes everything, by many hands, in three nations. The war stops. Then the government confesses first and offers the remedy the heroes wanted: an index of every citizen's journal. The rest of the book is a race to keep "many hands" from becoming one record again.

## The Four Twists
1. **MERIDIAN** (Ch 8): Julian was sent to turn Marcus.
2. **The remedy was ready** (Ch 24): half the fragments were fed from inside, and the Concordance was drafted in advance.
3. **Mrs. Kerr** (Ch 21): the Compiler, Elise Vance, is Tanaka's own patient in a sealed Chronos ward.
4. **Two hands** (Ch 30): Julian's key countersigned the Chronos Gap.
