# Chapter 2: KINDRED

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Thu Nov 1, 2108, 21:00 to about 23:30
**Setting:** The First Minister's office, top floor of the Cabinet Office, Veridica Capital; the walk home
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The offer that looks like mercy. Marcus is asked to do to another lonely man what was done to him, and the method is so familiar that his body recognises it before his mind does. Set up the reconciliation key as the thing a state wants (a Twist 4 plant).

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry:** *November first, 2108. Twenty-one hundred hours. Cabinet Office, top floor. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Maitland, O.; Chen Zhao; Wei, T. Purpose: not stated.*
- **The room:** **First Minister Oskar Maitland** (about 60) is a big, tired, well-tailored man who listens with his chin on his fist and likes to be told the numbers first. Minister **Chen Zhao** is in an armchair, delighted to see Marcus, and makes the joke about the Chen families being "related somewhere". **Wei** is at the side table with a slim folder and his tablet, courteous, and doesn't speak until he is asked.
- **The public offer:** peace talks, the **Lakes Talks**, at the **Old Customs House at Saltmarsh Crossing** in the neutral zone, under Synthesis mediation (**Professor Park Ji-won**). Round one opens Mon Nov 12. Veridica wants its most trusted restorer as delegate. The cameras love him, and Chronos has read him.
- **The file:** **OPERATION KINDRED.** It is a Cabinet Office cover sheet with no signature line, which is Maitland's deniability made physical. The real task: befriend **Julian Kovač**, 34, Senior Historian of the Chronos Temporal Archive and **holder of its senior reconciliation key** to Chronos's backup architecture. His profile: lives alone, rarely leaves the Archive, and wrote a Chronos monograph on the Lost Hour testimonies that cites Marcus eleven times.
- **Wei's technical annex** is elegant, and Marcus can see that it is. A reconciliation run under Julian's key would let Veridica's technicians re-verify Chronos's own account of the Gap **"from the true source"**, inside Chronos's backups. Marcus notes the phrase as jargon and thinks nothing of it. Chronos's Judge would then read a record that no longer supports its 0.94 attribution, and the war would end by determination within a month. Wei: "There's one key, and one man holds it. Chronos trusts him with four million people's past." (A **Twist 4 plant**: the key is what the state wants.)
- **The approach sequence:** a second, unsigned page of seven questions to ask over the first meetings, such as:
  - "What did you want to be before you were what you are?"
  - "Who taught you to keep records?"
  - "Who still writes to you?"
  - "What do you do on the nights you can't sleep?"
- Marcus reads the questions and goes cold. **He recognises the method: Protocol R-2** turned outward, an attachment figure placed in front of a lonely mind to see what grows. In Wing C it was done to him. This time he is the seed. He doesn't say "R-2" aloud.
- **Maitland:** **"One man's trust, against four hundred casualties."** Chen Zhao, warmly: "You'd be restoring a peace, Commissioner. There are worse things to be trusted with." (This is his one "restore". Let it pass.)
- Marcus asks one precise question: what happens to Julian afterwards. Wei answers honestly: "That depends on Chronos." Nobody pretends otherwise.
- **Marcus asks for time.** Maitland gives him a week: "Thursday next." Nobody asks him not to tell Kira. Marcus notices that nobody asks.
- **A trace of Marcus's holes:** reaching for the name of Maitland's predecessor, he finds only a smooth place, and files it.

## Ending
Alone in the lift with the folder under his arm, Marcus opens it to the last page: **the target's photograph.** A narrow, attentive face; dark hair cut short; very blue eyes; a gray cardigan under an archivist's jacket; a man caught mid-sentence at some Chronos lectern, being kind to a question. The caption gives the surname. **KOVAČ, JULIAN.** Marcus knows one Kovač well. The file doesn't say whether they are related, and he finds he doesn't want to ask.

## Notes
- **"True source" (Twist 2 support):** the annex uses the phrase as unremarked jargon. In Ch 9 Kira reads the same two words in Principle Three and realises the annex's author knows how to reach Chronos's master copy.
- **Twist 1:** Marcus does not suspect Julian of anything. The seven questions come back in Ch 6, when Julian asks them, and in Ch 8, when MERIDIAN turns out to carry nearly the same list.
- **Chen Zhao** presides warmly and is not suspected. **Wei:** no interior. His menace, if any, is one beat of excess at most, such as knowing Marcus takes his coffee black.
- **The deadline is Thu Nov 8.** Ch 6 records how Marcus answers it: he accepts the delegation and says only that he will "study" the annex.
- **Julian's appearance** (dark hair, blue eyes, cardigan) is new here. The blue eyes are a Kovač family likeness (Adrian's are "startlingly blue", Story 02).
