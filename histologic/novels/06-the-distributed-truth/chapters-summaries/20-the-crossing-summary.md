# Chapter 20: The Crossing

**POV:** Alexei Volkov
**Date:** Wed Jan 2, 2109, night, into the small hours of Thu Jan 3 (flashback to Sun Dec 30, 2108)
**Setting:** The farmhouse kitchen; the marsh and the **Drowned Causeway** in fog; the marsh road on the Chronos shore; the teaching hospital's basement records hall, service lift and ninth-floor corridor outside **Ward 9S**
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
A dead man with no papers walks into the nation that records everything, because a set of case notes described a tremor. This is Alexei's craft chapter: routes, jurisdictions and exits. Its heart is a sound. On the Veridican side Old Songs sings with words. In Chronos he goes back to humming, and Alexei, who learned that hum through a meal slot, feels the change before he understands it.

## Beats
- **Flashback, Sun Dec 30.** Kira brings **Tanaka's case notes** to the farmhouse. Tanaka sent them the day before by Elena's courier, **with the patient's initials struck out**. The hospital and the ward are named, and the director is not. Her covering line: *Clinical, not personal. The patient asks after him daily. I would like to know why.* Since publication the patient has been eating less, and her tremor is worse.
- **Old Songs reads them** twice, as he read the Introduction. These lines are quoted from Ch 3 and Ch 14:
  - *"Patient [—], 72. Long-term, 9S. Lucid beyond her chart. Tremor, right hand."*
  - *"Reads every memorandum I write ('Write a twelfth')."*
  - *"Asks after 'the old man who sings on your broadcasts.'"*
  - *"Knows the lineage of the stabilisation technique better than its own protocol does."*
  
  He does the sum on his fingers: seventy-two. She was two years older than him. He stops humming. Alexei notices.
- **Jan 2, evening. The argument.** Elena lays it out in order: no papers, officially dead, and every Chronos citizen he passes will log his face. Tanaka has already refused. Kira wrote on Dec 31, and the answer was one word, *No.* Old Songs puts his coat on and says his only line of the night: *"The record thinks I'm dead. Let it."* Elena counts the room and starts planning.
- **The arrangements.** The fog is due after 22:00. Marcus has asked **Julian** to meet them, and Julian answered within the hour without questions. Dmitri comes: "I am coming because you will need someone to count."
- **The Drowned Causeway, 23:30.** The old rail causeway across the marsh can be seen from the Chronos shore in clear weather, and not at all in fog. The sleepers are awash in places. It was the Phantom's road for ten years, and Alexei knows every gap. The fog is real weather, cold and ordinary.
- **The sound.** Halfway across, Old Songs sings under his breath, with words: *"Down by the water the ferry is late, / lanterns are low on the pier…"* Near the Chronos end, headlamps show through the fog. The words stop mid-line, and he hums: the Lakeshore Air, low, lifting like a question. It is the exact sound of Wing C. Alexei's shoulders know it before he does: *captivity*. From here on the old man doesn't sing a word.
- **Julian on the marsh road,** by his car, gentle and precise: "Nine minutes early. I didn't mind." Alexei, blunt with outsiders: "Your log's recording us." Julian: "Everything I see. I thought you should know before you decided." He is walking three Veridicans into his nation's record **under his own name**. That is either courage or something else, and Alexei can't tell which.
- **The hospital, 03:40.** Julian's Temporal Archive card opens the **basement records hall**; the Archive audits institutional records. It is the only door he needs. Then a service corridor and a service lift to the ninth floor. Dmitri counts two cameras and one night porter, who looks at Julian's card and not at them. Julian stops at the end of the corridor: "Inside that door I'd drop out of my own log, and someone would ask me why." He goes back down alone.
- **The door.** Ward 9S. Old Songs hums. Alexei presses the night bell. The lock turns.

## Ending
Tanaka is on the other side, in a cardigan over her ward coat. She is on call because the patient had a bad evening. She is white with fury and fear, and keeps her voice very low: "You should not be here. Nobody should be here. That is the point of this room."
