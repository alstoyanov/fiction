# Chapter 29: Thank You

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Feb 11, 2109
**Setting:** The Old Customs House at Saltmarsh Crossing, neutral zone (the last session of the Lakes Talks; the walled garden, the bench under the bare pear tree); then the Commission corridor at the Justice Center, Veridica Capital, in the evening
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Two thank-yous from the two men who were set to use Marcus, one sincere and one unreadable. The chapter closes the KINDRED/MERIDIAN thread in grace. Then it hands Marcus the nearest thing to a confession Wei will ever give, which proves nothing.

## Beats
- **The last session** is a formality: the war has been over by determination since Dec 17. Professor Park closes it warmly ("perhaps", twice). Administrator Aubry is cool and courteous, and thanks the Veridican delegation "for a very informative winter". Marcus's index is 0.44, and nobody at the table mentions it.
- **The garden,** unshielded, where everything Julian hears is recorded. It has the bench under the bare pear tree where they sat with their orders in November (Ch 8), and where in December Julian showed him the key "so you can see there is nothing behind it". Marcus notes this once, as a fact entry, and moves on.
- **Julian's news:** Chronos has made him its liaison to the witness networks. He has started a journal *in handwriting*, which his colleagues at the Archive find deeply strange ("One asked if it was a medical condition"). He shows Marcus the first page. His handwriting slopes.
- **The line.** Julian, looking at the lake: ***"Thank you for not weaponising my loneliness."*** Marcus knows loneliness from before any of this ("colleagues but few friends", Story 01). He can't answer at once. Then: *"It's in your log now."* Julian: *"Yes. I wanted it to be."* This echoes Ch 8, and this time it is deliberate.
- **They part** at the Customs House gate. Julian's gratitude is completely unguarded. Marcus feels it as a weight he is glad to carry. Nothing on the page hints at Twist 4 beyond the key already recalled.
- **The train back** to the capital. Marcus updates his lists, and one item moves from HOPES to CHECKED: *The talks ended with nobody used.*
- **Evening, the Commission corridor** behind the great hall: tall windows over the plaza and panels of white light on the floor, as in Book 5 Ch 31. Wei is alone, coat over his arm, pleasant as ever. He hands Marcus a **folded page** from the small book Kira saw on Feb 4 (Ch 28).
- **The page,** in his private hand, holds two entries:
  - *"Nov 15, 2107. The Doctrine is law. The reformers are grateful, and trusted. Chen knows, and says nothing; he has begun keeping records of his own, with many hands, all checking each other. That was not in the design. Neither was the old man. I find I want to see what they build."*
  - under it: *"Feb 4, 2109. They refused the index. That was not in the design either."*
  
  Marcus notices, in fact-entry reflex, that there is nothing between the entries: fifteen months without a line. This is a man who writes only when he is surprised.
- **The exchange.** Marcus: *"You sent the X pages."* Wei: *"Did I?"* He inclines his head the way a man acknowledges a good move, and walks on through the panels of light. Keep it to that: no speech and no gloating.

## Ending
Marcus writes in CHECKED: *Feb 11, 2109. Commission corridor. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Wei, T. Event: he wanted us to see it. Verification: the page. Witnesses: none.* Then he goes to find one. (Suggested: Kovač, at the testimony intake the X pages came through.)

## Notes
- **The wording of Wei's Nov 15, 2107 entry is canonical** (Book 5 Epilogue). Use it exactly. "Chen" means Marcus. Let the double reading with Chen Zhao stand, and never resolve it.
- **Wei has no POV and no interior.** "Did I?" stays unanswered. Nothing here proves he sent copy X. The page is evidence that he *wanted them to see*, which is all the CHECKED entry claims.
- **Twist 4 must stay unrevealed until Ch 30.** The garden's key-showing is mentioned once, as a memory already given in Ch 17, with no new clue weight. Julian must read as entirely sincere.
- **Julian's journal** is set up here and pays off in the Epilogue letter, which is in the same handwriting.
- **Assumes Ch 17 places the December key-showing on this bench.** If Ch 17 puts it elsewhere in the garden, adjust the line.
- **Suggested witness:** Kovač. If the drafter prefers someone else (Dmitri, Kira), keep the line "Then he goes to find one."
