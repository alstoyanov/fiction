# Chapter 7: Aunt Helena

**POV:** Helena Kovač
**Date:** Sat Nov 17, 2108
**Setting:** Kovač's flat in the capital; Marcus and Kira's flat (Kira is out at a Commission listening session)
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
The state is using her family a second time, and this time she will not keep a folder. Kovač tells Marcus who Julian is and asks the question that points at Twist 1. The chapter ends with a one-line letter that turns the operation into a family matter.

## Beats
- **Saturday morning.** Kovač's flat is small and neat, with an overwatered plant and files on the table. She watches the weekly broadcast round-up of the Lakes Talks. **One frame:** Marcus and a Chronos historian on a bench in a walled garden, the historian laughing. She knows the face at once, though she hasn't seen it in person since **2093**, when he was nineteen. **Julian.** She understands in one frame: Veridica's most trusted restorer, sitting beside the man who holds Chronos's key.
- **The letter.** It came on **Wed Nov 14**, pushed under her door, unstamped, in Adrian's hand. It is the first letter he has written her in three years, and it is one line: ***They're going to use him. Don't let them.*** She didn't know who "him" was. She had thought, with a fear she is ashamed of, that Adrian meant himself. Now she knows.
- **Her job, briefly and in her voice:** Registrar of Testimony since mid-2107, after her dismissal from Ashford in Feb 2107 without charge.
  - The **public intake** is on the ground floor of the Commission's building, with a counter, writing desks and a steel deposit slot in the wall. It opens at **09:00**.
  - **Two cameras** watch the hall.
  - Every deposit is entered in the intake log, and she signs for releases on paper herself.
  - She trusts paper, because paper can't be adjusted.
- **The folder.** She thinks of the folder she kept for four years at Ashford instead of acting. *Not again.* She pushes her glasses up her nose and puts on her coat.
- **Marcus's flat,** Saturday afternoon. Hands in her coat pockets, standing by the door. She briefs; she doesn't make a speech.
  - **Julian is her younger brother's elder son, Adrian's brother.** At nineteen he left for Chronos to study at its Temporal Archive school, and never came back. The family split along the lake. Her brother stopped saying his name.
  - **His childhood:** a quiet, exact boy who **kept lists** of everything the adults said that didn't match. What Uncle said at Christmas, what the record said, what Mother remembered. He wanted to live somewhere that forgot nothing. So he went to the one country that doesn't.
  - Marcus hears himself in every sentence.
- **Her verdict:** **"The state has used my family once. Adrian was sent to Kira. Now you are sent to Julian."** (She knows Adrian's part from Kira's acquisition file, Book 4 Ch 18.) She does not ask what Marcus was ordered to do. She doesn't need to. Marcus doesn't deny it, and she notes that he doesn't.
- **She asks; she doesn't promise.** "What will it cost him, Mr. Chen? I'm not asking what it costs you."
- **Her question, the one she can't answer:** ***"Chronos never lets Julian out of the Archive. Why send him to talks now?"*** He has never travelled since he was given the key. She has followed his career from across the lake for fifteen years, from public registers and his monograph. Marcus has no answer, and files it: *UNCHECKED*.

## Ending
At the door she takes an envelope from her pocket and gives Marcus Adrian's letter. It is the first time she has let it out of her hand since Wednesday. He reads the one line: *They're going to use him. Don't let them.* "It was addressed to me," says Kovač. "I think it was meant for you." She leaves before he can answer.
