# Chapter 6: The Lakes Talks

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Nov 12 – Thu Nov 15, 2108 (round one)
**Setting:** The **Old Customs House at Saltmarsh Crossing** in the neutral zone, under the Synthesis flag: the shielded conference rooms, the front hall with the Synthesis press screen, the walled garden with a bench under a bare pear tree
**Length:** ~3,600 words

## Purpose
Marcus goes into KINDRED telling himself he is only studying it, and finds a man he likes. Plant all four Twist 1 clues so that each one reads as kinship. Make the reader like Julian as much as Marcus does.

## Beats
- **How he got here.** On **Thu Nov 8** Marcus accepted the delegation and said of the annex only that he would "study it". Maitland heard yes. Wei said, "That's all anyone could ask." Kira has known since the night of Nov 1. She asked one question, "Kovač?", which he couldn't answer, and has been quiet about it since.
- **Monday, the venue.** A pre-Histologic stone customs house on the marsh, in fog, flying the Synthesis flag.
  - **Inside:** shielded conference rooms where no Chronos interface can log. The Chronos delegates feel it as a small deafness at the door.
  - **Behind:** an unshielded walled **garden** and a bench under a **bare pear tree**. Everything a Chronos citizen hears there goes into their log.
- **Professor Park Ji-won,** the Synthesis mediator (74): small, white-haired, warm, fond of tea and anecdotes. She reminisces about reading in "the old capital Archive" as a young scholar, "perhaps in the sixties". She says **"perhaps"** constantly. Marcus likes her.
- **The positions.** Chronos wants a **joint verification of the December interval under the Temporal Archive's senior key**, with Veridican witnesses, so that Veridica can watch its guilt confirmed. Veridica says Chronos fabricated the Gap. **Both states want the same key used, for opposite reasons (a Twist 4 plant).** When the key is mentioned, Julian looks at his hands.
- **Julian Kovač:** gentle, precise and funny, with very dry jokes about Chronos's nanosecond clocks. At the first break he says, **"14-08, I think? I read your testimony."** Marcus checks it that night, and it is in the Commission's public archive: *CHECKED*. He is flattered, and ashamed of being flattered.
- **The questions.** Over the four days Julian asks, easily and one at a time, what Marcus wanted to be before he was a historian, who taught him to keep records, and who still writes to him. **They are the questions on Marcus's own approach sheet, nearly in order.** Marcus notices, and tells himself this is what lonely people ask each other. He asks Julian some of the same questions and hates himself a little for each one. Julian answers them all, generously.
- **The steering.** Julian's aide, a young Chronos protocol officer (unnamed) with a tablet, steers them into the shielded rooms at every break, as protocol requires. **Julian steers them out** into the garden each time: "I can't think in a room that doesn't remember." They talk on the bench in the fog.
- **Marcus's lists.** At night in the Synthesis guesthouse he keeps a KINDRED column: *Subject asks questions: yes. Subject lonely: yes. Subject trusts me:* and he leaves the status blank. He tells himself it's study. He knows what it is.
- **A trace of his holes:** reaching for the word for the pear tree's bare shape (*espalier*), he finds it gone. Julian supplies it without being asked.
- **Thursday, the Day of Testimony, 14:00–15:00.** Julian asks to watch the readings from the capital's central plaza with him on the **Synthesis press screen in the front hall**. The front hall is unshielded; Julian chooses it. On screen, ordinary people read out ordinary days at the Lost Hour stone. Kira is in the crowd with her book held against her coat. Watching her beside Julian, Marcus can't look at either of them for long.

## Ending
As the readings end, Julian says, quite simply, still looking at the screen: **"In Chronos nobody forgets anything. You can't imagine how lonely it is."** Marcus can. He has spent two years with holes where his life was. It is exactly the loneliness the annex told him to use. That is the problem.
