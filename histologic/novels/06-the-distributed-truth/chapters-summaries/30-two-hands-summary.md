# Chapter 30: Two Hands

**POV:** Nikolai Volkov
**Date:** Thu Feb 14, 2109 (04:50 to about 21:30)
**Setting:** Nikolai and Maya's flat, Veridica Capital (his work table); then Marcus's flat, in the evening
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 4.** Fifteen months of forensics come to one answer that nobody wants. The Chronos Gap was made by two hands, one on each side of the lake. The second hand's key belongs to the man who thanked Marcus three days ago. The book does not say whether Julian used it.

## Beats
- **04:50.** The comparison finishes on Nikolai's screen. He has worked on it since Nov 2107: since the reservation with the blank owner field (Book 5 Epilogue), and through Ch 18, when he still could not read the countersignature.
- **The first hand:** the seam in Chronos's own Jan 2108 Gap analysis. It is Wei's signature, the chain re-sealed backwards, the same as the Lost Hour. Kira recognised it within an hour of reading it. It was never enough on its own.
- **The second hand.** Sealing seventeen minutes inside Chronos's primary needed a **Chronos-side countersignature**, and nobody could read it without a reference. **The reference came from Julian:** during round 3 of the talks (Dec 10–13), in the garden, he showed Marcus the senior reconciliation key of the Chronos Temporal Archive, unasked, "so you can see there is nothing behind it" (offered in Ch 11, recalled in Ch 17). On Thu Dec 13 Julian made a harmless **test signature** with it, countersigning the one-line entry *Saltmarsh Crossing, garden, 13 Dec 2108*, and Marcus gave Nikolai the signed slip on Fri Dec 14. It has sat in the Gap folder since, uncompared (Ch 18). That slip gave Nikolai the key's **habits**: how it closes a sealed block, and the interval it waits before it signs, as individual as handwriting.
- **Why it took until February:** one demonstration was not a profile. Knowing what to look for, Nikolai found the same habits in years of the Archive's public reconciliation notices and built the profile from hundreds of them. Tonight the profile meets the Gap.
- **The match:** the key that countersigned the Chronos Gap is **the senior reconciliation key of the Chronos Temporal Archive, which Julian Kovač holds.** It proves the key was used, not whose hand held it.
- **Julian's log** for Dec 13, 2107, 03:12–03:29 shows the same **flat, content-free trace** as four million others, which for a sleeper reads *asleep, heart rate steady* (Julian said it himself, in December, as if it were nothing). Nikolai sets it beside any ordinary Chronos sleep log, which records every breath and every turn. The Gap's line records no night at all, only a state carried over: filler that nobody can check. And a Chronos log can lie only if someone holds the true source, and a key like this is how you hold it. The key that could make his log lie is his.
- **Julian's openness is what implicates him.** Without the garden there would be no reference. Nikolai looks at his hands for a long time.
- **He keeps copies**, as always: three, in three places. For the first time it doesn't feel like safety. It feels like a weapon being built in his own kitchen. One flash of dark humour: *"Many hands. Very good. Now there are three copies of the worst thing I know."*
- **Maya** gets up at 06:30 and finds him still at the table. He asks her to check the timings and not to tell him what she thinks until she is done. She timestamps everything. At 11:20 she says *"It holds,"* and then, furious and gentle at once, *"I hate that it holds."*
- **He sits with it all day.** He doesn't go to Kira. Julian is Adrian's brother, and he wants Marcus to hear it first, in person.
- **21:00, Marcus's flat.** Kira is away at the farmhouse with the copies. Nikolai lays it out plainly, in order, and adds: *"I want to say clearly that I don't know if he did it. I know his key did."*
- **Marcus** is silent for a long time. He thinks of the bench, the key offered unasked, "Thank you for not weaponising my loneliness", and the log that says asleep. He doesn't defend Julian and doesn't accuse him. Nikolai leaves at 21:30.

## Ending
Marcus stands at the window of the flat, facing the lake and the other shore beyond it. "Two hands," he says. "One on each side." He doesn't say the rest: *then the war was never between them.* He writes it under UNCHECKED, because he can't prove it:
*Feb 14, 2109. Chronos Gap, Dec 13, 2107, 03:12–03:29. Two hands: Wei's seam; the Archive's key. Holder of the key: Kovač, J. Julian?*
Yet.
