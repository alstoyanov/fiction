# Chapter 8: MERIDIAN

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Sun Nov 18 (farmhouse) – Thu Nov 22, 2108 (round two; the garden)
**Setting:** The resistance farmhouse yard; the flat; the Old Customs House at Saltmarsh Crossing; the walled garden, the bench under the bare pear tree, in fog
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
**Twist 1.** The target was also the hunter. Marcus refuses KINDRED in the one place where the refusal can't be unsaid, and learns that Chronos ran the same operation on him. Two states, two lonely men and one method. The refusal becomes a double publication.

## Beats
- **Kira's silence.** Since the Day of Testimony she has refused to talk about KINDRED. She writes in her journal at the kitchen table late, and leaves it open. The silence is her answer. Marcus understands it is also about Adrian's brother.
- **Sun Nov 18, the farmhouse yard.** Marcus drives out to the border zone. Old Songs is humming in the back room. (The Introduction went to Kira after his one night with it, Ch 5.) Elena is counting trucks back into the barn. She hears Marcus out without looking at him and says one thing: **"It always becomes real on one side."** Nothing else. He knows which side she means, and which autumn.
- **Round two, Mon Nov 19 – Wed Nov 21.** The pattern goes on: the aide steers them in, Julian steers them out, the questions, the garden. Julian mentions a younger brother he hasn't spoken to in years and changes the subject. Adrian's letter is in Marcus's inside pocket the whole time.
- **Wednesday night, the guesthouse.** Marcus lays out his lists. The CHECKED column has everything Julian has told him, all of it true. The KINDRED column has *Subject trusts me:* and still no status. He understands that he can't finish that entry, and that if he doesn't end it, it will finish itself.
- **Thursday, the decision.** Marcus chooses the garden **because** Julian's interface records everything there. A refusal in a shielded room can be denied. A refusal in Julian's log is in Chronos's permanent record the moment it is spoken.
- **The confession, on the bench in the fog.** Marcus tells him the truth, in order: **the orders, the key, the week**, and the seven questions, some of which he has asked him.
- **Julian goes white.** Then he **laughs**, helplessly, with his hands over his face, until he has to stop. He takes a folded printed directive from his jacket.
  - **DIRECTIVE MERIDIAN**, Administration of Temporal Defence, authorised by *C. Aubry, Administrator*.
  - **Objective: to win the trust of the Veridican restorer, and to turn him.** It is to end with a joint verification under the senior key, with Marcus's name on it.
  - It comes with an approach sequence. Several of its questions are **nearly word for word** Marcus's own. Neither man says what that means.
- **The clues fall into place, in Marcus's fact-entry voice:**
  - *14-08*: it was in Julian's briefing ("I did read your testimony too");
  - the questions;
  - the garden, because Aubry wanted every word logged;
  - Kovač's *Why now?*: this is why. Chronos let him out of the Archive for MERIDIAN.
- **Both nations ran R-2 on the same two men at the same table.** They sit side by side on the wet bench, each holding the order to seduce the other, and for a while neither speaks.
- **What was real?** Julian, quietly: the answers were true, all of them. So were Marcus's. Neither man can prove it, and both know that too.

## Ending
The fog, the pear tree, the two papers. Julian: **"It's in my log now. I can't take it back."** Marcus: **"Good. Neither can I."**

## Notes
- **Twist 1 lands here.** It pays off Ch 6 (14-08, the questions, the steering) and Ch 7 ("Why now?").
- **What happens next:** Aubry has the garden confession in Julian's log and wants to use it (Ch 11). Julian publishes his MERIDIAN orders from his own log under the right of self-disclosure on Dec 17 (README decision 9; Ch 15–16). Marcus reports nothing to Maitland in this chapter.
- **The near-identical questions** are texture for Twist 1. Later writers may let the reader recall them after Twist 4 (both sides working together). **No character draws that conclusion in Book 6 beyond a guess.**
- **Aubry** appears only as a name on the directive here. She is introduced in person in Ch 11.
- **Elena:** one line only. Her conversation about 2105 is Ch 15, and it must not happen here. She does not offer to run the operation.
- **Weather:** the fog is real weather. No storm happens in this chapter.
- **Seduction** means trust and friendship, not romance. Whether Julian's feeling for Marcus is only friendship stays tender and unstated.
- Kira does not appear on the page in the round-two section. How she hears the news is for Ch 9's writer, or can be left offstage.
