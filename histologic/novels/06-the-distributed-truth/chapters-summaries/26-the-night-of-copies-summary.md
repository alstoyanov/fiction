# Chapter 26: The Night of Copies

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Fri Jan 18 – Sat Jan 19, 2109 (21:00 to dawn)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse in the Veridican border zone (the kitchen, the yard, the gate onto the lakeshore road); by radio, the head of the Drowned Causeway
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
This is Kira's pairing method at full scale, and the book's answer to "one record". Vance confesses by dictation, so that the confession exists only as copies. Dmitri, who once guarded the single record, guards a night designed to leave nothing to seize.

## Beats
- **The method, as Dmitri's watch log:** every journal is copied by hand into two others in different towns, and each copy checks the other two. There is no index and no center, and no seizure matters.
- **21:00, the setup.** Nikolai has patched a voice line through old relays and moves the relay point every forty minutes. **Twelve keepers in twelve towns, six on each shore,** answer the roll call by town. Ruth answers for Kestrel Point. The other eleven are unnamed. Twelve is Vance's choice, and she will not say why. When Kira asks, she says only: *"It is a number I owe."* (A seed for later books. Never explain it in this book.) Beside each keeper sits a second hand, copying line by line. Those second copies will cross the lake before dawn, so that each shore ends the night holding the other shore's handwriting.
- **Marcus is not there:** at 0.58, one night among outlaws would mean custody (Ch 25). Kira keeps the roster on paper at the end of the table.
- **21:30, the dictation.** Vance sits by the stove, small and very upright, and speaks each sentence twice, because people write slowly. Old Songs sits beside her and does not hum. Between paragraphs he hands her the cup, because her right hand is steady only when it holds something. The confession covers:
  - the 2060s;
  - the seventeen contributors, unnamed;
  - the Introduction she wrote in winter 2064;
  - that the storms and deletions were planned;
  - the Warning;
  - that she has lived quietly since, without saying where, under what name, or who kept her. She protects Soren, Tanaka and Morrison, and in Chronos's record "Mrs. Margit Kerr" died in 9S on Jan 4 (Ch 22).
  
  Quote only a few lines (see Notes).
- **Security.** Dmitri logs three approaches, the barn and the lakeshore road. Elena is at the gate with a rifle she never raises. **01:40:** a League car slows, and two men get out and look up the farm track. Headlamps appear down the road: a Border Security patrol, entirely routine. The men leave. Sokolov speaks on the radio, formal line first: *"Border Security. Routine patrol, lakeshore road, until morning."* Then, low: *"Tell her to go slowly. People write slowly."*
- **02:35, a Chronos-shore town.** Over the line come a knock and the voices of border directorate officers. The keeper says: *"Wait. I'm writing."* There is a silence. Then she asks Vance to repeat the last sentence. The officers stay in her kitchen and listen. Nikolai: *"That's two more logs."*
- **02:50–04:10, the read-back.** Each keeper reads a paragraph and the others check it. Four places differ, and the differences are written in the margins in the other hands. At one of them Vance corrects a word: *"I said 'afraid'. Not 'frightened'. They are different words."*
- **04:15.** Alexei crosses the causeway in fog (real weather) with the Veridican second copies. **04:50, the near-miss:** directorate lamps at the Chronos end. He lies in the reeds for eleven minutes while Dmitri counts him through on the radio. It is Dmitri's only contraction of the night.
- **07:20.** Alexei is back, wet to the knees and grinning, with six bundles in the other shore's hands.

## Ending
Dawn. Twelve towns, twelve hands, one confession. Nobody holds the original, because there isn't one. Old Songs begins, very low, to hum something Dmitri has never heard. It lifts at the end like a question.

## Notes
- **The confession's opening** is a new, canonical detail that later chapters may quote: *"My name is Elise Vance. In the winter of 2064 I began to assemble a design for the government of frightened people. I was twenty-eight. I was afraid, and I was good at my work, and I believed those were the same qualification."* It echoes her Ch 22 line.
- **Reveal schedule:**
  - The confession names no contributor and decodes no mark.
  - It never uses "Register", "Annex" or "heirs", and it does not mention Principles One, Two or Four.
  - It does **not** mention the withheld page or copy X.
  - Nobody says the manuscript's title (a Book 7 reveal). Call it "the design", "the document", "the pages" or "the manuscript".
  - **Don't say anyone signed anything.** The Register and any signatures are later reveals (Book 7 and Book 9). The only figure Vance gives is the Introduction's own "seventeen contributors". Her only reason for twelve keepers is "It is a number I owe."
- **The Chronos keepers' and officers' interfaces log every word,** and erasing a log is sacrilege (README decision 9). Hint at this; don't explain it.
- **Old Songs'** round is taught in Ch 27, so no lyrics appear here.
- **Assumed from Chs 20–22:** "somebody knew" and the causeway crossing method. Tanaka is not present. **Vance is officially dead in Chronos** (Ch 22: Morrison's spoofed flatline, Vale's certificate for "Mrs. Margit Kerr"). The directorate and League men are hunting the living woman whose confession is on the line, Elise Vance; nothing on the page links that name to the dead ward patient.
