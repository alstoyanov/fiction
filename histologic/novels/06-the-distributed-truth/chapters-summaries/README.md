# The Distributed Truth: Chapter Summaries

These summaries are based on `plots/06-the-distributed-truth-full.md` (v2), with the drafting decisions below. The old summaries, chapters and `docs/` followed the abandoned Nov 2025 plot and have been deleted; git history keeps them.

- **Structure:** a Prologue, 30 chapters and an Epilogue, in four parts.
- **Target length:** about 3,300–3,600 words per chapter, roughly 110,000 words in all.
- **Tone:** a political thriller and conspiracy mystery. It should be tense, intimate and morally exact, and every chapter ends on a turn.
- **Canon rules:** `PROJECT-RULES.md` §4–6, `worldbuilding/`, `characters/` and `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md`. The main ones:
  - Storms are perceptual, never weather.
  - Seeding is silent.
  - Removing an interface kills.
  - The Judge gives probabilities, and above 0.60 means custody.
  - The manuscript reveal schedule is binding.
  - Wei never gets a point of view.
  - Singh does not appear.

## Drafting Decisions (Beyond Plot v2)

1. **The venue.** The Lakes Talks are held at the **Old Customs House at Saltmarsh Crossing**, a pre-Histologic stone building in the neutral zone flying the Synthesis flag.
   - **Inside:** shielded conference rooms, where no Chronos interface can log.
   - **Behind:** an unshielded walled **garden** with a bench under a bare pear tree, where everything a Chronos citizen hears is recorded.
   - **Schedule:** sessions run Monday to Thursday in rounds (Nov 12–15, Nov 19–22, Dec 10–13, then irregular), with the last on **Mon Feb 11**.
2. **Kestrel Point.** A lakeshore town in the claimed strip, on the Veridican shore.
   - Landmarks: Harbour Street, the ferry office where Ruth works, a church hall, and the **old chandlery** reading room where the Kestrel Book is kept by day.
   - The **old pier** at the end of Harbour Street: according to Veridica it was demolished in 2089, and according to Chronos it stands.
   - **The Kestrel Book** was begun in **April 2108** from a hand copy of Kira's method, carried by a courier. It has 17 contributors at the start of the book and 43 by the Epilogue.
3. **The farmhouse.** Elena's resistance farmhouse in the border zone, with its yard and barn, is where Old Songs lives (Book 5). **Vance lives there from Jan 5, 2109.**
4. **The Drowned Causeway.** An old rail causeway across the marsh to the Chronos shore, usable only in fog, which is real weather. It is the smugglers' route for Alexei's crossings (Ch 20, 22, 26).
5. **Tanaka's hospital.** The teaching hospital in the Chronos lakeshore city.
   - Tanaka's office is on the fourth floor (Book 5 Ch 22).
   - The **sealed cognitive ward, Ward 9S**, is on the ninth floor beside the director's office. It is the only place in Chronos where patients' life-logs are clinically sealed, meaning nothing is recorded.
   - **Dr. Soren Vale**, the director, is tall and stooped, with beautiful manners (Book 5 Ch 6).
   - **"Mrs. M. Kerr"** has lived in 9S since 2098. Tanaka has been her physician since August 2106.
   - In Tanaka's case notes she is always **"Patient M.K."** The full ward name, **Margit Kerr**, is spoken only in Ch 21.
6. **The two delivery routes.** These are the engine of Twist 2; keep them exact.
   - **Vance's route** is three items from the Compiler's copy (no copy numeral), hidden in the bindings of old border songbooks and carried by Tanaka "for the old gentleman":
     - **Nov 5:** two songbooks. The **Introduction** (pp. 2–4) is in the second binding and is found on **Nov 9** (Ch 5).
     - **Dec 3:** a third songbook containing the **table-of-contents strip**, found on **Dec 5** (Ch 13).
     - **Dec 7:** "the last one", containing the handwritten **Warning** (p. 40), found on **Dec 9** (Ch 15).
   - **The inside route** is two anonymous deposits at the Commission's public testimony intake, which Kovač runs. Both are typed with the chipped *e* and carry copy numeral **X**:
     - **Mon Nov 26:** **Principle Three** (pp. 13–17, mark *YP·02*, five pages). The intake log timestamps the deposit at **06:58**, before the intake opens at 09:00, and the camera shows **no visitor**.
     - **Wed Nov 28:** **Principle Five** (pp. 23–28, mark *JM·08*, six pages). The same thing happens at **06:51**.
7. **The table-of-contents strip** is torn lengthwise. It shows only the right-hand edge of three lines, the numerals and page numbers: `IV …… 18` / `V …… 23` / `VI …… 29`. **The headings are missing**, which keeps the principles' titles for Book 7's full table of contents (Principle Four's text stays for Book 8). V at 23 matches the Principle Five pages (pp. 23–28), so the strip is genuine. VI at 29 makes Singh's page 30 part of **Principle Six**.
8. **The marks.** Characters see *YP·02* (Three), *JM·08* (Five) and, from Book 5, *CZ·11* (page 30). They notice the pattern of two capitals, a raised dot and a number no higher than 12, and **cannot read them**. Nobody decodes a mark, names a contributor, or knows whose copies X and XI are. Marcus may note that XI came from the Ministry's own correspondence.
9. **Chronos rules.**
   - Every citizen's interface logs continuously, and citizens have read access to their own log.
   - Publishing one's own log is a protected Chronos right, the **right of self-disclosure**. This is how Julian publishes MERIDIAN (Ch 16).
   - Erasing a log is the worst sacrilege Chronos knows.
   - Chronos officials are **Administrators** who implement The Judge's reading of the collective will. Chronos has no ministers.
10. **The contamination panel.** At a public session before The Judge, each speaker's current contamination index is shown on the chamber panel, the Doctrine's "weighted for known contamination" made visible. Marcus sits at about 0.20 when he starts speaking in Ch 25 and rises live to **0.58**. After the Feb 4 ruling it falls to **0.44**.
11. **KINDRED was built to be refused.** This is implied in Ch 24 and never proven. The Concordance drafts date from **early November**, days after KINDRED was offered on Nov 1. Either branch serves the design: if Marcus complies the war is won, and if he refuses and publishes, the remedy is ready.
12. **How Twist 4 is found.** Chronos published its own analysis of the Gap in Jan 2108 to accuse Veridica. The analysis includes the seam structure, which is how Kira recognised it, but the countersignature is unreadable without a reference. In Ch 11 Julian offers to show Marcus his key, and he does so during round 3 of the talks (Dec 10–13), in the garden. Marcus recalls it in Ch 17. Nikolai sees the key's habits. **Julian's openness is what implicates him.**
13. **Old Songs' voice rules** (`characters/main-characters/protagonists/old-songs-character.md`) are binding:
    - He speaks rarely and hums instead.
    - He has **one speech in this book, in Ch 22**.
    - Every tune lifts at the end like a question.
    - He uses **at most one riddle** in the book, in Ch 27: *"A song dies with the singer, unless it has many singers."*
    - **In Chronos, where everything is recorded, he goes back to humming**, and the reader should feel the change.
    - Use songbook lyrics only.
14. **Numbers stay modest.** Four hundred-odd casualties and thirty-one dead in the war. Seventeen contributors to the Kestrel Book, forty-three by March. Nobody can count the journals, and that is the point.
15. **No one says the manuscript's title.** *The Foundation of Certainty* appears on its title page, which is a Book 7 reveal. In Book 6 the characters, Vance included, call it "the design", "the document", "the pages" or "the manuscript". The heroes' reply is "the Reply" or "the New Foundation".
16. **What the Gap looks like in a log.** Every affected log shows a **flat, content-free trace for 03:12–03:29**: the last state carried over, with no breaths, words or movement. For sleepers it reads *asleep, heart rate steady*. The clue is the contrast with ordinary Chronos sleep logs, which record every breath (Ch 11). Ch 1, 11, 17 and 30 agree. Never call it a "flatline", which is the word for a spoofed death.
17. **How Vance leaves Chronos.** Outside 9S her interface would log her. On Fri Jan 4 **Morrison spoofs her telemetry** (the canon flatline), and **Dr. Vale certifies "Mrs. Margit Kerr" dead in 9S**. She leaves in the mortuary van as a dead woman, as Old Songs once left Veridica, and from then on she is **officially dead in Chronos**. Vale, Tanaka and Morrison stay at the hospital. Her confession never says where she lived or under what name. Who told the directorate about "Mrs. Kerr" stays open.
