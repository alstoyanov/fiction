# Chapter 3: The Sealed Ward

**POV:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka
**Date:** Mon Nov 5, 2108
**Setting:** The teaching hospital in the Chronos lakeshore city: Tanaka's fourth-floor office (shared with Morrison), the ninth floor, and **Ward 9S**, the sealed cognitive ward beside the director's office
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Chronos is building its own Continuity under a medical name, and Tanaka is the one person who knows what it is. Introduce **Patient M.K.**, the most important person in the book, as one entry in a clinician's case notes. Every Twist 3 clue here must read as ordinary clinical detail.

## Beats
- **Opening case note** in Tanaka's italics: a storm-trauma admission from the lakeshore, then the day's circular.
- **The circular:** the Administration of Temporal Defence proposes **"stabilisation"** as wartime treatment for acute storm trauma. It means cradle sessions to "settle" contradictory memory. Last year's "salience therapy" proposal was withdrawn after her eleventh memorandum; this is the same thing in new wording. Tanaka reads the protocol and recognises **Continuity under a Chronos name**: the hood, the gain curve and the word *baseline*. She sets her tea down very carefully. Morrison, at his desk, reads it over her shoulder and says nothing for a long time.
- **Dr. Soren Vale on the ninth floor.** He is tall and stooped, with beautiful manners. He meets her outside his office and says the order will not be carried out in his hospital "for now". He does not explain why. He never has. Tanaka still assumes he read Isaiah's article and has a daughter (her guess from Book 5 Ch 6).
- **Ward 9S:** the only place in Chronos where nothing is recorded.
  - Patients' life-logs are **sealed by clinical order** on the director's authority, and the walls are shielded, so that anyone inside drops out of the record until they leave (their logs read *CLINICAL SEAL*).
  - The ward runs on **paper**: charts, menus, library books. Its day-room screen receives broadcasts and records nothing.
  - Patients are listed **by initials only**. It is the ward's one courtesy.
  - It smells of the ninth floor's own **disinfectant**, sharp and cold, faintly green, like cut stems. It isn't what the rest of the hospital uses. Tanaka notices it on her cuffs every evening.
- **Patient M.K.** is in the bed by the east window, facing the lake. Small and very upright, in plain clothes, with white hair cut short. Admitted **2098** under the director's seal. Chart: progressive cognitive decline. **Tanaka has been her physician since August 2106**, when Vale assigned the case to her personally.
- **The round,** as scene and case note at once:
  - M.K. is **lucid beyond her chart**: exact, dry, and interested in everything.
  - Her **right hand** has a **tremor**. It stops when she holds something: her cup, or a pencil.
  - She reads **every memorandum Tanaka writes**, in printed copies, since Chronos filings are public. Last November she read the eleventh and said **"Write a twelfth."** Tanaka began one and kept it in a drawer (Book 5 Epilogue). Today Tanaka tells her about the circular. M.K. says again, **"Write a twelfth,"** and then, "Finish it this time."
  - She corrects one word in Tanaka's draft, gently: not "unprecedented" but "**unrepeated**". "It has a precedent. It hasn't happened *here*." Tanaka makes the change without asking why the distinction matters to her.
  - She asks after **"the old man who sings on your broadcasts."** She saw him sing at the plaza memorial last November on the day-room screen. "Is he well?" Tanaka: "He is well." That is all she says.
- **Tanaka's inner voice:** she doesn't ask patients about themselves beyond the chart. **Confidentiality is the one wall she built herself**, and in a country that records everything it is the most valuable thing she can give anyone.

## Ending
As Tanaka leaves, M.K. takes **two old border songbooks** from her bedside locker: small pre-Histologic printed collections in faded cloth, one green and one blue. **"For the old gentleman, from an admirer. Paper should go home."** Tanaka turns them over. They are lakeshore songs, and the spines have been mended neatly by someone patient. She puts them in her bag. In the lift, going down, she can smell the ninth floor on them.

## Notes
- **The case note must be close to canon:** *"Patient M.K., 72. Long-term, 9S. Lucid beyond her chart. Tremor, right hand. Reads every memorandum I write ('Write a twelfth'). Asks after 'the old man who sings on your broadcasts.'"* **(Twist 3 clues: the tremor, "Write a twelfth", "the old man who sings".)**
- **Disinfectant (Twist 3 clue),** set up here for Ch 5: the ninth floor's smell is distinctive, and only Tanaka would know it.
- **The re-glued spines (Twist 3 clue, very light).** The Introduction is in the second (blue) book's binding, found Nov 9 (Ch 5). Tanaka does not open the bindings and suspects nothing.
- **"Unrepeated" (Twist 3 clue, very light):** Vance's love of exact language (voice rule 3). It is also an early echo of Ch 14, where she knows the technique's origin.
- **The name:** never "Kerr" in this chapter. Only "M.K." The full ward name is spoken only in Ch 21.
- **Vale's protection (Twist 3, carried over from Book 5 Ch 6):** he blocks stabilisation and gives no reason. Ch 14: Temporal Defence overrides him.
- **Floors:** Tanaka's office is on the fourth floor (Book 5 Ch 22), and the director's is on the ninth (Book 5 Ch 6).
- **Age check:** Tanaka is 48. M.K. is 72 (Vance, born 2036).
