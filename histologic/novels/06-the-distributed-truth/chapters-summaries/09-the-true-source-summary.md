# Chapter 9: The True Source

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Mon Nov 26, 2108
**Setting:** The Commission's public testimony intake and Kovač's office behind it, in the capital; Marcus and Kira's flat that evening
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Part Two opens with the first fragment from the inside route. Kira reads the mechanism of everything that was done to her and to Marcus, typed in 2066. The chapter plants Twist 2's first hard clue: a deposit logged at 06:58, with no visitor to make it.

## Beats
- **08:52, a call from Kovač:** "Commissioner Osman. There is a deposit I would like you to see." It is the Monday after round 2 and four days after MERIDIAN. On Friday, at Kira's insistence, Marcus sent Wei's office one written question about the KINDRED annex: *how does a restoration route reach Chronos's true source from outside Chronos?* The reply was promised for Monday.
- **The intake.** A public hall on the ground floor with a counter, writing desks, two cameras, and a steel deposit slot anyone may use at any hour. It opens at 09:00. Kira counts as she walks in. Kovač waits by the inner door with her hands in her coat pockets.
- **The log.** It shows the deposit at **06:58**, under the ordinary public-deposit code: one item, five sheets. The clerk found it in the receiving tray at 08:40. Kovač plays the camera over the slot from 06:00 to 08:40. The hall is empty. Nobody crosses it, and the slot never moves. "Either the camera is wrong or the log is. Neither has been wrong before."
- **The pages.** Five typed sheets, **pp. 13–17**, headed *PRINCIPLE THREE: STRATEGIC AMBIGUITY THROUGH BACKUP CONTROL*. There is the chipped lowercase *e*, as in the Introduction. Every lower right corner carries the copy numeral **X**. At the foot of p. 17 is the mark ***YP·02***: two capitals, a raised dot, a number. She can't read it, and she files the pattern.
- **The text** (quote in full):
  > *"Maintain three backup systems that appear independent and are not. Should outsiders corrupt the record, it may be restored from the true source. Should insiders reform it, it may be restored from the true source. Ambiguity, controlled, is both defence and instrument."*
- **Kira reads her own case**, in incident-report form:
  - Protocol 7.3, entered Nov 18, 2105 at 22:00 and gone without a trace.
  - Marcus's 0.58, typed as 0.73.
  - The Lost Hour, erased from all three backups at once, which only works if the three are not independent.
  - Wei's annex uses the same two words, *true source*, for Chronos. Her question has been answered, and not by Wei.
- **The compulsion** returns for a moment (*layer one: key exchange*), and she stops it by counting the pages again. Five.
- **Procedure.** Kovač's rule: anonymous deposits are held unpublished pending verification, and they may be released to a Commissioner against signature. Kira signs, and Kovač countersigns: *AUTHORISED: H. KOVAČ.* Kovač lets no one into the intake log itself: "I would like it to stay the one thing in this building nobody has touched."
- **Adrian, briefly.** Kira: "He wrote to you." Kovač: "One line. Mr. Chen has it." Neither says Julian's name.
- **Evening.** Kira copies Principle Three by hand into the first journal, so that it exists twice. Wei's office sends no reply. Her columns:
  - **CHECKED:** the pages, the *e*, the X, pp. 13–17.
  - **UNCHECKED:** *06:58. No visitor.*
  - **UNCHECKED:** the Introduction has no numeral, these pages have X, and page 30 has XI. One sender, or two?

## Ending
She lays Singh's photograph of page 30 beside p. 17, one finger on each corner. Marcus: "XI came out of the Ministry's own correspondence." Kira: "X, not XI." A pause. "Someone else has a copy."
