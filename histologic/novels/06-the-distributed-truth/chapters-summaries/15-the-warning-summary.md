# Chapter 15: The Warning

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Sun Dec 9, 2108
**Setting:** The resistance farmhouse (day); the Commission corridor in the capital (night)
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
The Compiler tells whoever finds her copy, in her own hand, that reform will be absorbed. Marcus chooses a publication with no center: many hands, three nations, one hour. Everyone who backs him is sincere. The one person who is pleased should frighten the reader.

## Beats
- **The last binding.** Alexei brought the fourth songbook last night from Tanaka's handover. At the kitchen table (Marcus, Kira, Elena, Alexei, Dmitri) Old Songs slits the spine and finds one **handwritten** sheet folded twice, in a young woman's precise, slanting hand. *40* is written top right, and *March 2066* at the foot.
- **The hand.** Old Songs knows it before anyone reads a word. He lays two fingers beside the ink, not on it. "Her hand."
- **Marcus reads it aloud.** Quote it in full:
  > *"To whoever finds this: I assembled these principles because I was afraid. I was wrong. The system contains the seeds of its own corruption: Three enables the war of facts and Five encourages it. Reformers will try to fix it. They will fail, because the design anticipates them: it will make whistleblowers into criminals and reformers into its own restorers. There is no reform path, only replacement. Distribute truth. Distribute verification. Accept uncertainty. Build by many hands."*
- **What he hears.** *Reformers into its own restorers* is page 30, the strip, the Commission: him, already. *Build by many hands* is Old Songs' "Many hands" from the safe house in January 2107 (Book 5 Ch 30), written in 2066. His columns: **CHECKED:** Three and Five. **UNCHECKED:** *there is no reform path.*
- **The decision.** Not the Commission, and not one outlet, because either would be a center. **14:00, Monday Dec 17**, in three nations:
  - **Veridica:**
    - sealed paper packets carried through the week by Elena's couriers and the keepers to every journal they know, marked *not before 14:00*;
    - at 14:00, Kira enters the set into the **Commission's public archive**, and Kovač enters the two X deposits as testimony received, which is her procedure;
    - at 14:00, Marcus reads his KINDRED orders aloud on the **Justice Center steps**, with Dmitri beside him.
  - **Chronos:** Julian publishes MERIDIAN and the Nov 22 garden conversation from his own log, under the right of self-disclosure. Marcus will give him the hour tomorrow, in the garden, where it is recorded.
  - **The SFW and Synthesis:** Isaiah, in the same hour. It will be evening where he is.
  - **What goes out:** the KINDRED orders and Wei's annex; the Introduction, Three, Five, the strip and this page; Singh's photograph of page 30, now identified as Principle Six.
  - **What doesn't:** any name, including Elise's. Old Songs hums one falling phrase, *no*, and that settles it. Nothing about songbooks, the patient, or crossings. Page 30's margin-noted original stays with Kovač.
- **Why 14:00:** the Lost Hour began then, and the Day of Testimony readings begin then. Kira: "They'll know it's on purpose." "Good. It is."
- **Kira backs him without being asked,** already counting couriers. Neither of them says that Julian is Adrian's brother.
- **Elena, in the yard.** They have their first conversation about 2105 in three years, and it stays partial. "I did this to you. I know what it costs on both sides." He doesn't answer the part he can't answer, and she doesn't ask him to.
- **Dmitri** will tell Sokolov the hour and nothing else. "What will she do?" "I do not know. I have never once known."
- **Night, the capital.** There is a light on in the Technical Director's office on a Sunday. Marcus tells Wei in the corridor, as a courtesy, because the public archive is Wei's system: Monday week, 14:00, the orders and the pages. Wei: "Good." The smile is held a beat too long.

## Ending
At the flat Marcus writes Monday's entry in advance, in pencil, in the format of desk 14-08: *Dec 17, 2108. 14:00. Justice Center steps; Commission archive; Chronos; the Sovereignty. Involved parties: many hands. Event: publication. Verification:* He leaves the line blank.
