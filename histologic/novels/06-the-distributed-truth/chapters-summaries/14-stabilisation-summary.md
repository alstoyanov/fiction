# Chapter 14: Stabilisation

**POV:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka
**Date:** Fri Dec 7, 2108
**Setting:** The Chronos teaching hospital: the storm-trauma ward, Tanaka's fourth-floor office, and the sealed ward 9S on the ninth floor
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Chronos's own Continuity arrives in crates. Tanaka stops it a second time with a twelfth memorandum built on a clinical history only one person alive could dictate from memory. She notices that her patient knows too much, and she doesn't ask. This is the strongest Twist 3 clue before the reveal, kept behind the wall of confidentiality Tanaka built herself.

## Beats
- **07:30, the override.** A Temporal Defence Administration order overrules the director: "stabilisation" of storm casualties begins at once, "in view of wartime need". Dr. Soren Vale comes down from the ninth floor to tell her himself, tall, stooped, beautifully mannered. "I'm afraid I have been overruled. I wanted you to hear it from me." As before, he doesn't say why he fought it.
- **The cradles** reach the storm-trauma ward at ten: four Chronos-built hoods on jointed arms. The protocol summary on the crate lid gives the aim as *stable, self-originated certainty, experienced by the patient as their own*. She sets her cup down very carefully, as she did at her kitchen table in 2105. Morrison reads the calibration sheet and goes gray, because it descends from his own profile. "Sorry," he says, to nobody.
- **Case notes,** in italics, on the ward: a ferryman who can't choose which shore he lives on; a girl of nine who asks which pier is real. *Prognosis,* and she stops.
- **9S.** It is sealed, with nothing logged, next door to the director's office. Patient M.K., 72, sits upright by the window, holding a cup in both hands to still the tremor in the right. She has already heard. In 9S news travels by nurse.
- **The dictation.** Tanaka came to take a pulse. Instead M.K. dictates the technique's clinical origin from memory, in order and in short declarative sentences:
  - where "salience reduction" was first proposed;
  - which early trials failed, and when (the late 2060s);
  - how it crossed the lake inside hardware;
  - that the crate-lid aim is "a paraphrase of something old, about compassion".
  She says no more, and Tanaka doesn't ask. **Continuity's aim, under a Chronos name.**
- **A word corrected.** Tanaka writes *derived from*. M.K.: "Not derived. Descended. Derived means someone did the arithmetic."
- **The twelfth memorandum,** written at her desk with Morrison checking the calibration history. It is plain and devastating in wartime: Chronos's "stabilisation" is Veridica's Continuity, which Chronos itself condemned in August 2106, carried across the lake in Chronos's own crates. It is the twelfth she began a year ago. Filed at 18:10.
- **Case note, written and not examined:** *Patient M.K., 72. Recall of 2060s clinical literature: precise, dated, first-hand in tone. Knows the lineage of the stabilisation technique better than its own protocol does. Source of knowledge: not asked.* She files it where she keeps things. Confidentiality is the one wall she built herself.
- **21:40.** An Administration notice: the order is suspended "pending review of the clinical record". The Judge has read the memorandum. The crates stay sealed.

## Ending
Late, she climbs to 9S to tell her. M.K. is awake with the lamp on, and nods as if she knew. Then she takes a small old songbook from her bedside drawer and holds it out with both hands: "For the old gentleman. The last one, I think." Tanaka puts it in her bag, and carries it.

## Notes
- **(Twist 3 clue)** She knows the origin too well, and Tanaka doesn't ask.
- **(Twist 3 clue)** The right-hand tremor, stilled by holding things. Vale's personal apology and his unexplained stand.
- **No Principle Four.** "A paraphrase of something old, about compassion" is the limit. The crate-lid aim echoes Continuity's aim (Book 4), not the manuscript's wording.
- **Name rule:** only **"Patient M.K."** Never write "Kerr" in this chapter. Tanaka speaks to her without using a name. The ward name is first spoken in Ch 21.
- **Timeline:** Ch 3 has "Write a twelfth", and the Book 5 Epilogue has the twelfth begun. Tanaka hands the book to Alexei at the lakeshore checkpoint on Sat Dec 8, and Old Songs opens it on Sun Dec 9 (Ch 15: "Alexei brought the fourth songbook last night").
- **Voice:** italic case notes, unfinished lines, exact distinctions, formal address, and the cup set down with care.
- **Setting:** Book 5 Ch 6 and Ch 22 (fourth-floor office with Morrison; the director on the ninth floor).
