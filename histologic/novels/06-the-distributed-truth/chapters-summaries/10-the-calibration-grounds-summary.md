# Chapter 10: The Calibration Grounds

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Wed Nov 28, 2108
**Setting:** The Commission intake in the capital (morning); the resistance farmhouse (afternoon); the lakeshore checkpoint in the fog (dusk)
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The second X deposit is Principle Five. For the Volkovs it is their father's death on paper, twenty-four years early. For Sokolov it is her job, written down in 2066. Two formal people sit on one bench, and one of them begins to change without saying so.

## Beats
- **Why Dmitri is there.** Since MERIDIAN, Chronos has had the garden conversation in Julian's log (Veridica's government has not been told), and Elena has put Dmitri on Marcus and Kira as their shadow. He counts the intake's cameras (two) while Kovač shows Kira the log.
- **The second deposit.** It happened the same way, at **06:51**: the ordinary public-deposit code, before opening, **no one on camera**. Six sheets, **pp. 23–28**, copy numeral **X**, the chipped *e*, and the mark ***JM·08*** at the foot of p. 28. It is headed *PRINCIPLE FIVE: CONTROLLED CONFLICT AND THE CALIBRATION GROUNDS*.
- **The timing.** Yesterday the talks secretariat published round 3's agenda: *the claimed strip and the broadcast overlap*. The border is to be argued in twelve days, and this morning the page about borders arrives. Kira notes it. Dmitri files it.
- **He reads it over her shoulder.** His face does nothing. Quote:
  > *"External threats unify more reliably than internal messaging. Fact warfare between Histologic nations is not failure but function, so long as it remains a war of facts and not of blood. Border regions of broadcast overlap should be preserved, not resolved: they are where a population's tolerance for contradiction may be measured, and raised."*
- **Papa.** In 2089 Ivan reported that the border overlap was no technical limitation. He was corrected that spring, and a year later he died in a storm as Test Subject 2471, *lethal threshold confirmed*. *Measured.* Dmitri counts: twenty-four years after the page was typed. It needed no name.
- **Custody.** Kira signs the pages out under Kovač's rule, and Dmitri carries them in a card folder inside his coat. Kovač: "Bring them back in the condition they left."
- **The farmhouse.** Nikolai rode out with them. The brothers read the pages in order at the kitchen table, Dmitri, Alexei, Nikolai, the way they sat on the parapet. Old Songs hums the Lakeshore Air at the stove, their mother's tune, every phrase lifting.
  - **Alexei** wants to burn something: a depot, a van. "Lyosha," says Dmitri. That is the whole reprimand.
  - **Nikolai** wants to know who is sending the pages. There were two deposits, at 06:58 and 06:51, and nobody on camera either time. Kovač refused him the log this morning. His hands are cold, and the brothers feel it as weight.
- **Dusk, the lakeshore checkpoint.** The fog is real, and the floodlights turn it white. She comes out alone, tall, in the long dark coat, hands in her pockets. "Volkov."
- **The bench.** It is concrete, under the floodlight. He gives her the folder: "Page twenty-eight. Then the rest." She reads all six, standing and then sitting. Her units are deployed along this shore tonight, and one waits in the bay behind her with its mast folded. Neither of them uses a contraction:
  - "This was written before I was born." "Yes."
  - "It describes my orders." "Yes."
  She returns the folder squared at the edges, and she doesn't ask where it came from.

## Ending
Sokolov, quietly, to the fog: "Then I am an instrument." Dmitri: "So was I. It is not a permanent condition."
