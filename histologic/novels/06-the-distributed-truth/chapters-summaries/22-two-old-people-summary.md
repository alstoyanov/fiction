# Chapter 22: Two Old People

**POV:** Mikhail "Old Songs" Petrov
**Date:** Fri Jan 4, 2109 (noon to first light on Sat Jan 5)
**Setting:** Ward 9S (Vance's bed by the east window, curtains drawn); the service lift; the basement mortuary and its van bay; the marsh road; the Drowned Causeway in fog
**Length:** ~3,600 words

## Purpose
The emotional heart of the book: the woman who built the prison and the man who survived it. **His one speech of the book** is spoken in the only room in Chronos that does not remember, which is why he can speak at all. He neither forgives nor condemns her. He asks the one question, and she answers it. Then the world outside finds her by name.

## Beats
- **The room.** He has hummed since the causeway, as he did in Wing C. At noon Tanaka tells him again: *nothing here is recorded*. This time he believes it. Elise sits upright by the east window, holding a cup to still her right hand. He takes the visitor's chair. The other patients are asleep.
- **His speech.** Plain and cracked, dry in places, with no rhetoric. The drafter expands it to about 700–900 words around these lines:
  - *"We met again on a Thursday. I noticed. You'd have noticed too."*
  - *"You came in on Thursdays for two years, from the systems department, with ink on your fingers. You read the gray journal the moment it came in, slowly, making notes, the way you read something you mean to build on. I never once asked what you wanted with it. I was twenty-five. I thought wanting to understand a thing was the same as being on its side."*
  - *"You said rhyme was a compression algorithm. I said it was a kind of prayer. You gave me the prayer and won anyway. You always gave the first point. In November I read three typed pages that gave the first point in every paragraph, and I knew you before the end of the first one."*
  - *"Anselm ate his apple at eleven. Gloves off, pocketknife, one long curl. He asked where the box had gone. Politely. In writing. Twice. When they gave him back he still peeled it in one curl, and I said the shelf mark to him, Periodicals, Governance, one hundred and fourteen, and he asked me if I was sure I had the number right."*
  - *"So I sang it. Forty years of it: correction rooms, meal slots, the trolley, the hood humming behind my ears. You were right about compression. That's why it lived. The machine skims a tune like furniture. I kept those essays alive with your argument."*
  - *"Since sixty-six I've carried one question for you, and it was the wrong one. I wanted to know where you'd gone. Since November I've had the right one."* A pause. **"Did you know what it was, when you were writing it?"**
- **Her answer, exactly:** **"Yes. That is what I have to live with. Not that I didn't know. That I did, and was frightened, and thought frightened was the same as wise."**
- **Why this room.** *"The one room in Chronos that does not remember. In Veridica they could have corrected me. In Chronos they would have logged me. Here there is nothing to correct and nothing to log. A record can be restored. Nobody can restore what was never written."* Then: *"And Soren was here."* On the years before 2098: *"Later. That's a long list, and you'd want it checked."*
- **Why she took the name:** *"Because he asked where the box went, and I didn't."* She says nothing more about Kerr.
- **The Warning.** She wrote it by hand in March 2066 in her own copy, when the document was already typed and the copies weren't hers any more. She told no one: *"Who would I have told? Anyone who could have acted on it was in the document. Anyone who couldn't would have been helped, like Anselm."*
- **Afternoon, with Alexei, Dmitri and Tanaka present.** Alexei lays out Kira's photographs of the five published items. Vance reads them as an archivist reads, the object before the words. She taps the corners of Three and Five: **"Copy X isn't mine."** Dmitri, who builds cases, asks formally whose it is: **"Names would make you a court, and a court is one record."** She refuses every name among the seventeen. Tanaka asks whether anything else in her copy should cross the lake: **"There is one page I will not give anyone yet."** Old Songs notices only that she says *page*, and that her hand is steady while she says it.
- **16:20.** Vale comes in, still courteous. Two officers of the Chronos **border directorate** are in the lobby asking for **"Mrs. Kerr"** by name. They haven't asked for "the patient in 9S", or for intruders. The ward lists initials only. *Somebody knew.* Nobody says who could have. Alexei glances once at the service lift they came up in two nights ago, on Julian's card, and then away.

## Ending
The fog came in at dusk. Outside 9S, Elise's interface would log her, so she has to leave it as a dead woman, the way Old Songs left Veridica. Tanaka sends for **Morrison** from the fourth floor. In the linen room, on a ward tablet, he does what he did at the fence (Book 4, Book 5 Ch 6): he pushes the signature of a real flatline through the telemetry relay for the patient in the east-window bed, and from then on the relay reports zero. Old Songs knows him from Wing C. Morrison doesn't look at the patient. Vale then **certifies "Mrs. Margit Kerr" dead in 9S**, in his own hand, the cause consistent with her chart. He takes them down in the service lift to the **mortuary**, cold and white and, at this hour, empty of the living. He gives Dmitri the keys to the mortuary van: *"Nobody in Chronos stops the dead."* He straightens his mother's collar. *"Go up and be charming to them,"* she says, and he goes up to the lobby with the certificate. **Tanaka stays on the ward,** because a sealed ward that stops running gets noticed. Morrison goes back to the fourth floor. She hands Dmitri a folded copy of her case notes for Commissioner Osman. They leave the van at the marsh road. On the causeway Dmitri carries Vance over the drowned stretches without a word. Old Songs hums the whole way. At the Veridican end, in first light on **Sat Jan 5**, he sings with words again, the ferry verse. Elise has never heard those words. She listens to the last line lift: *"when there is no one left here?"*

## Notes
- **The one speech (README decision 13)** is spoken only inside 9S. The memories are exact from Book 5 Ch 12 and 30, and Ch 5:
  - Thursdays for two years, in whispers, with Kerr pretending not to hear; the systems department; the ink on her fingers.
  - Compression against prayer (in Ch 5 she concedes the prayer and wins).
  - Kerr's apple at eleven, his cotton gloves off, peeled in one curl with his pocketknife, both before and after the correction; "politely… in writing… twice".
  - *Periodicals, Governance, 114*, and the numbers that now run 113, 115.
- **Deviation:** plot v2 says the question was "carried since 2066", but in Book 5 he never suspected her. So from 2066 his question was *where did you go*, and it became *did you know* on Nov 9 (Ch 5).
- **Don't use "A song dies with the singer."** Ch 27's riddle completes it. **Anya and Katya are not linked to Principle Five** (that is Book 7).
- **Reveal schedule:**
  - No names and **no "twelve"**. No Register, Annex, heirs or title; she says "the document" (decision 15).
  - Nothing about Kerr's part in the journal.
  - The withheld page is unnamed, with no hint of its content. Her copy is neither shown nor located.
- **(Twist 2 clue)** "Copy X isn't mine." Tanaka's notes carry this and "three documents" to Kira (Ch 24). Alexei brought the photographs from Kira.
- **Only Vance, Old Songs, Alexei and Dmitri cross.** Vale, Tanaka and Morrison stay at the hospital.
- **Vance's exit (settled, README decision 17):** Morrison spoofs her telemetry with the canon flatline (worldbuilding `neural-interfaces-and-correction.md`), and Vale certifies "Mrs. Margit Kerr" dead in 9S on Fri Jan 4. She leaves in the mortuary van as a dead woman. From then on she is **officially dead in Chronos**, as Old Songs is in Veridica. What the officers make of a death certified within the hour of their asking stays offstage. Her confession (Ch 26) never says where she lived or under what name, which protects Soren, Tanaka and Morrison.
- **"Somebody knew" stays open.** The only hint allowed is Alexei's glance at the service lift: Julian walked them in under his own name, so his log holds the route (Ch 20). Never state it or weigh it. Don't imply anyone is Vale's father.
- **Vance's "twenty-seven again" beat** is saved for Ch 27.
