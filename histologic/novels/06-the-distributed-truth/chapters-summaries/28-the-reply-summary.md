# Chapter 28: The Reply

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Mon Jan 28 – Mon Feb 4, 2109
**Setting:** The farmhouse kitchen table (Jan 28–31); by courier and causeway, pages from Kestrel Point, the checkpoint, the capital and the Chronos shore; The Judge's chamber, Justice Center (Feb 4)
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
The heroes answer the manuscript point for point, by many hands, leaving blank what they haven't seen. The Judge then rules that it can't call a concordant nation contaminated. The victory is partial: the Concordance survives, voluntary.

## Beats
- **Mon Jan 28.** Kira lays out the known manuscript on the kitchen table in rows, as she lays out everything: the Introduction, Three, Five, page 30 (Six), the TOC strip (IV/V/VI) and the Warning. She logs it in incident-report format. Under stress the reciting compulsion comes back once. The idea is a reply that mirrors the manuscript's structure, with each answer in a different hand and no author.
- **The New Foundation,** as its numbered lines stand by Feb 1:
  1. **Introduction** (collective, drafted over three days, each sentence countersigned Kestrel-style): *"This reply is written by as many hands as will write in it. Truth, we have learned, cannot be decided for a public. It has to be checked by one, and the public must be able to disagree."* Vance reads the draft and corrects one word, gently: the draft said "allowed to disagree". *"'Allowed' is a word for whoever holds the keys."* Isaiah's page comes by courier from the SFW. He has struck out eleven words and added none.
  2. **To One:** blank. *"We will answer these when we know what they said."*
  3. **To Two:** blank, with the same line.
  4. **To Three** (Kira): *"Keep copies no one can recall: on paper, in many houses."*
  5. **To Four:** blank, with the same line.
  6. **To Five** (Sokolov, handed to Dmitri at the checkpoint, folded once, without comment): *"A border is where two peoples compare notes, not where they are measured."*
  7. **To Six** (Marcus, from the capital, because he stays away at 0.58): *"Trust no restorer, including us."*
  8. **For Chronos** (Julian, in handwriting, which Alexei brings back over the causeway): *"Record less. Remember more."* Kira sees the slope of his capitals and thinks, for one beat, of Adrian's.
  9. **For the children** (Wren, fourteen, sent by Ruth): *"Teach children to check, not to believe."*
  10. **To the Warning** (Ruth): *"Where we disagree, write both down, each in our own hand."*
  11. **To itself** (Alexei, who wanted to burn something in Ch 10): *"If this ever becomes one record, burn it and start again."*
- **An open register** of blank pages follows, headed: *Anyone may sign. There is no last page.* Vance signs it on the second page, in the middle and not first. She says nothing about why that matters to her. (Readers will understand it in Book 9.)
- **Old Songs writes no answer.** Inside the back board, after the blank pages, he writes the four lines of "Many Hands" in his own slow hand, with a line of short rising strokes under them so that whoever reads it will know the tune goes up. He does not sign it.
- **Fri Feb 1.** It is published nowhere in particular and copied everywhere: into the confession towns first, then along the pairing network. Kira can't count the copies, and she notices that she has stopped trying.
- **Mon Feb 4, The Judge's chamber.** The same panel as on Jan 14. The determination is read in probability language: keeping an unregistered journal is **contamination-relevant at 0.41, below threshold**. The unregistered books' testimony agrees with the registered ones, and The Judge cannot class a large, concordant part of the nation as contaminated. **The Concordance survives, voluntary.** Many register anyway, and many don't.
- The panel recomputes the indices of the Jan 14 speakers under the new classification. Kira watches Marcus's line.
- **One beat of Wei:** on the technical bench after the ruling, he writes a single line in a small book with his right hand and puts it away. Kira has never seen him carry it. She files it. (It pays off in Ch 29.)

## Ending
Marcus's index on the panel reads **0.44**. Kira: *"I'd have liked it lower."* Marcus: *"I'd have liked it checkable."*

## Notes
- **Reveal schedule:** the blanks for One, Two and Four say nothing about what those principles contain. The open register is framed as the answer to Vance's refusal of names ("names would make you a court"): anyone may sign, and no list is closed. **Nobody may frame it as mirroring a Register in the manuscript,** whose existence is a Book 7 reveal.
- **Never name the original by its title** (a Book 7 reveal). The reply is "the New Foundation" or "the Reply", and the original is "the design" or "the manuscript".
- **Wren's is the ninth line**, as the plot's character list requires. The numbering above is canonical for later chapters and the Epilogue.
- **New details:** the Introduction wording, Ruth's and Alexei's lines, Vance's correction and signature, Isaiah's eleven struck words, and the register heading. The lines from Kira, Sokolov, Marcus, Julian and Wren, and the self-warning, are plot v2 wording.
- **The Wei notebook beat** is a single glance with no mind-reading. It plants Ch 29's Feb 4 entry.
