# Chapter 18: The League of the Record

**POV:** Nikolai Volkov
**Date:** Sat Dec 22 – Mon Dec 24, 2108
**Setting:** The central plaza in the capital, by the Lost Hour stone; a lakeshore district town on Sunday; Nikolai's terminal in the capital; Kestrel Point on Christmas Eve, reached by line and through the bond
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
This is the loud extreme that makes the Concordance look like the sensible middle. The fear under it is real. The money on top of it is not. Nikolai finds a pattern he can't prove and files it beside the other thing he can't prove. Then the extreme does what extremes do, and the chandlery burns.

## Beats
- **Sat Dec 22, the central plaza.** The League of the Record fills the square around the Lost Hour stone. The banners repeat Ch 4's leaflets: *THE JOURNALS ARE CONTAMINATION.* **Pell** speaks: burn the journals and restore the pure record. *"A nation of memories is a nation of liars"* (his Book 5 line). Nikolai watches from the edge with Maya.
- **The genuine fear.** An older woman beside him is crying under a hand-lettered sign. She says to nobody that she misses knowing what happened yesterday. After seven months of storms people want one thing that doesn't argue with them. Nikolai understands her better than he'd like. He lost a memory once and was given it back as testimony (Book 4).
- **The professionals.** Stewards with earpieces work the crowd in shifts. The placards come from one print run, with the same misaligned crest on every one, and coaches have come from three towns. Maya times the stewards' rotations unasked: every twenty minutes, to the second.
- **Sun Dec 23, a lakeshore district town.** It's a smaller rally with the same stewards and the same placards. That night Nikolai traces the **stewards' channel**. It runs on a network block provisioned from a **Ministry "public order" budget line**, a general heading for "support to lawful public assemblies".
- **Pattern, not proof.** The line is generic and legal. Pell is a Ministry undersecretary, so of course the Ministry supports his rallies, and that story would be true. But since publication the line has paid for nothing except League rallies. Nikolai makes his three copies. Maya: "That isn't evidence." Nikolai: "No. It's a shape."
- **The other folder.** He files it beside his oldest unfinished work, the **Chronos Gap's countersignature** (Book 5 Epilogue). He has its shape but not its grammar, and it is still unreadable. Julian's signed test slip from Dec 13 is in the same folder. One demonstration is a sample, not a profile. He looks at it once, for something else, and puts it back.
- **Mon Dec 24, Christmas Eve,** the first holiday without a storm since May. Kestrel Point's contributors hold an evening reading in the **old chandlery reading room**. Alexei has been carrying hand copies of the fragments to the lakeshore towns since the 17th, and stays for it. Nikolai is in the capital with Maya.
- **The bond, about 22:15.** Alexei arrives as **heat and pressure behind the eyes**, sudden and climbing. It is sensation, never a voice. Nikolai is on his feet before he knows why. He says "Lyosha," and tries the line three times.
- **22:41. Alexei calls,** coughing, and for once he tells it in order. After the reading Ruth left with the book to walk an elderly contributor home, and Wren stayed to stack chairs. The front of the chandlery caught all at once. That morning someone had pinned a League leaflet to its door. Alexei got Wren out through the **back window over the water**. Nobody is hurt beyond the smoke. The reading room is gone.

## Ending
Nikolai asks the only question that matters to the town. Alexei, still coughing, laughs once. "The Kestrel Book's in Ruth's coat."
