# Chapter 23: The Drafts

**POV:** Maya Reeves
**Date:** Mon Jan 7, 2109 (memory of Sat Dec 15, 2108)
**Setting:** The Commission building in the capital: the technicians' floor, the Technical Director's document store, and Kovač's public intake on the ground floor; Maya's flat, evening
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
A technician is asked to build the remedy and reads its timestamps. The Concordance was drafted before the disease was named, and the anonymous pages came from inside. The worst line comes from the one man she can't stop visiting.

## Beats
- **08:30. The assignment.** Wei's office makes Maya lead technician for the **Concordance intake**: scanning, sealing and registering journals at national scale, live by March if the Act passes on Jan 14. Wei is kind and generous with credit: *"You're the best we have at intakes. Take your time."* (*Take your time* is what he said in Book 5 Ch 14, when he had given her the drive himself. She notices, and doesn't know why it bothers her.)
- **The design documents.** She pulls the architecture Wei presented on Dec 18 from the Technical Director's document store, with its full history, because she always reads history first. Times are her voice:
  - First draft created **Tue Nov 6, 2108, 08:14**, in the Technical Director's office. That is five days after KINDRED was offered.
  - **Forty-one revisions** follow. The last is **Mon Dec 17, 11:40**, two hours and twenty minutes before the publication it answers.
  - The Dec 18 speech isn't in the store. She doesn't need it to be.
  - One component makes her stop. Every registered page is held as a sealed master, with a **restore path from the master**, authorised at Technical Director level, so that a burned book "can be restored". She reads it twice. *Restorable* means *editable*. She writes the component number down by hand.
- **The sizing exercise.** One revision, dated **Tue Dec 4**, cites a load estimate: "testimony, all sources, including handwritten, national scale". Maya wrote that estimate herself. Wei's office gave it to her that week as "archive capacity planning". She thought it was about the Commission's own archive, and did it in two days. She has been building the Concordance since before it had a name.
- **The intake.** The Concordance will absorb the Commission's public testimony intake, so its records must be merged. Maya goes down to **Kovač**, who has kept the intake log closed to everyone (Ch 9). Kovač reads the merge order twice, pushes her glasses up her nose, and prints the log for her on paper herself. She briefs flatly: "You'll want the twenty-sixth and the twenty-eighth."
- **06:58 and 06:51.** On the screen, the two deposits carry the ordinary public-deposit code (Ch 9, Ch 10). In the routing layer beneath, which the intake desk never sees, both records were created by **a terminal inside the Ministry network**, not by the slot's scanner, which stamps every public deposit. Maya can say where the record was typed. She can't say which office, and she can't say how the paper got into the tray. Kovač, quietly: "The tray has two sides." Neither of them says more.
- **The memory.** At home that evening she opens the notebook Nikolai gave her. She writes up every visit to her father on the 15th, as she has done since Book 5. **Sat Dec 15, 2108, 10:40.** She had told him about the dull capacity job: every journal in the country, indexed, so it could be weighed. He listened with his terrible precise interest and said: ***"Give them a trusted index and see what grows. I'd have loved that for Continuity."*** Under it, in her hand as always: *He didn't do it.* She reads it three times. At the time she took it for his usual professional envy.
- **Anger and grief together** (voice rule 5). She is furious with Wei, with herself for doing it in two days, and with her father for seeing it at a glance from a prison chair.

## Ending
She doesn't send it. At 21:10 she copies the timestamps, the component number and the two routing entries by hand onto six sheets, dates each one and signs it, and takes them across the city to Kira. On paper.

## Notes
- **(Twist 2 clue)** The drafts begin **Tue Nov 6, 2108**, early November and days after KINDRED (README decision 11). The last revision is **Dec 17, 11:40**. It is implied, and never proven, that KINDRED was built to be refused. Maya doesn't draw that conclusion. Ch 24 lets the dates sit side by side.
- **(Twist 2 clue)** The X deposits were entered from a Ministry-network terminal. No office and no person is identified. This keeps Ch 29's "Did I?" open. Ch 9 set the log up to look ordinary, and kept Nikolai out of it so that this find is Maya's.
- **Deviation for date consistency:** plot v2 has her describe "the Concordance" on Dec 15, but it wasn't announced until Dec 18. So on Dec 15 she described the unnamed **sizing exercise** (assigned **Tue Dec 4**) that later turns out to be the Concordance.
- **The restore path from the master** is the technical form of Principle Three for Ch 24. Maya names no principle. She reads it as a technician.
- **Reeves** appears only in memory (brief rule). His line is exact from plot v2. The notebook is the one Nikolai gave her (Book 5 Epilogue).
- **Wei:** one beat of excess at most, the echoed "Take your time". No interior.
- **These drafts are never put before The Judge** (Ch 25): the metadata could be called forged, and it would expose Maya.
