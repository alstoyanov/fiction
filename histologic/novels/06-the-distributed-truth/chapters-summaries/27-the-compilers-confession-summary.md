# Chapter 27: The Compiler's Confession

**POV:** Dr. Elise Vance (her only POV chapter)
**Date:** Mon Jan 21, 2109 (the readings at 14:00)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse, Veridican border zone: the kitchen, by the stove with the cracked door; the yard
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The designer, from inside, in exact language with no self-pity. Her confession is read on both shores while she sits beside the man she argued with on Thursdays. She learns that "many hands", which she wrote and never practised, can't be done alone. The book's only riddle appears here.

## Beats
- **Morning.** She has been at the farmhouse for sixteen days. She notes how far Mikhail opens the damper on the cracked stove door. Her right hand trembles. She steadies it by holding things: a cup, the table edge, once his sleeve.
- **13:55.** Nikolai's line is patched into a receiver on the dresser. **At 14:00 her confession is read in town squares on both shores.** Two readings come through at once: Ruth by the ferry office on Harbour Street, and a Chronos-shore keeper whose words are logged into every listener's life. They are half a sentence apart, so she hears her opening (*"My name is Elise Vance…"*) in two strangers' voices, out of step, like a round. The Kestrel reading uses one word she did not say. She lets it stand.
- **The 2060s, precise.** Thursdays at the capital's Historical Archive; a junior archivist with a notebook of lakeshore songs; the gray journal at *Periodicals, Governance, 114*; rhyme as a compression algorithm against rhyme as prayer (Book 5 Ch 12); Kerr at the green lamp, peeling his apple in one curl. In winter 2064 she began assembling seventeen people's principles, and was proud of the Introduction's *"decided for"*. She does not let herself think any of the names, even here: a thing thought often enough is eventually said.
- **Kerr.** She chose the alias for him because he was their teacher and "the last man I knew who thought an archive was where things are not lost". She knows what was done to him in 2067. She thinks about it for exactly one paragraph, and no further.
- **The Warning, March 2066.** She wrote it in her own copy and told no one: *"I was afraid. I was wrong."* Then she went quiet for forty-three years. **The one page she will not give anyone yet** is a weight she feels and turns away from. Its contents and location stay out of her thoughts on the page.
- **Why the design can't answer this.** It was built to anticipate reformers, and it modelled a reformer as someone with a center: a leader, a commission, one record to capture or restore. It has nothing to hold on to in a confession that exists only as copies, or a reply it did not write. She thinks it calmly, and is not comforted.
- **Her tremor, and the time she has.** She has stopped asking Tanaka for numbers. One thought of Soren, who signed her death certificate in his own hand on Jan 4 (in Chronos she is dead), unspoken.
- **Mikhail** hums the new tune from dawn on Saturday (Ch 26). She asks what it is. He does not name it. He says only the riddle, the only one in the book: ***"A song dies with the singer, unless it has many singers."*** Then he teaches her **"Many Hands"**, line by line, exactly:
  > *One hand writes and one hand checks,*
  > *one hand holds the light;*
  > *one voice fails and one voice mends,*
  > *and many make it right.*
- **It is a round.** He starts, and she enters on the second line. Her voice is thin, exact and slightly flat. It lifts at the end like a question because the tune does. With two voices it keeps collapsing, because the third entry has no one to carry it. She tries to hold two lines at once and cannot. For one line she is twenty-seven and arguing with him about rhyme. Then she isn't.

## Ending
Kira comes in from the yard with cold on her coat and a satchel of copies. She stops in the doorway, listens for one pass, and comes in on the third entry, off-key, the first time she has sung in front of anyone. The round holds.
