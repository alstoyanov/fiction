# Epilogue: The Round

**POV:** Rotating: Ruth, Kira, Dmitri, Isaiah, Maya, Tanaka, Marcus
**Date:** Fri Mar 15, 2109
**Setting:** Kestrel Point; the central plaza of Veridica Capital (the Lost Hour stone by the fountain); the lakeshore checkpoint; the SFW; the prison visiting room; Tanaka's hospital, Chronos; Marcus's flat
**Length:** ~3,600 words

## Purpose
This is a partial, plural and uncountable outcome, with the Book 7 threads laid in quietly. The dead are named from both shores. Then the round, which no single voice can carry, becomes a crowd's. The book ends on one question mark Marcus will not cross out.

## Beats
- **Kestrel Point, morning (Ruth).** The ferry season reopens. At the end of Harbour Street the old pier is only water, and everyone agrees. Wren walks to the edge and looks at nothing, calmly. **The Kestrel Book has forty-three contributors,** and three copies are kept in three other towns. Ruth writes the morning's entry, countersigned twice.
- **The central plaza, 14:00 (Kira).** Since the Day of Testimony in November, people have taken to coming to the stone on the fifteenth of every month without anyone arranging it. Today there are more of them. Old Songs stands at the stone, still officially dead, and sings **"The Names"** with words, for **the war's thirty-one dead from both shores**. The names are taken from the journals, because each nation's record lists only its own dead as victims of the other's attacks. A Veridican name and a Chronos name go into the same verse. Quote the refrain exactly: *"Who went down to the water? / Name them, and they stay."* Don't invent a list of names on the page. The song runs as long as the dead.
- **Then the round.** Old Songs starts "Many Hands", and Vance enters on the second line. She is dead in Chronos's record, as he is in Veridica's, and nobody in the plaza knows her face. Kira takes the third, off-key and not caring. Then there is a dozen: Alexei, Maya, a tram driver, a boy of about ten. Then the crowd picks it up without knowing who wrote it. It lifts at the end like a question, over and over, and nobody resolves it. Marcus sings too.
- **The lakeshore checkpoint (Dmitri).** Sokolov wears a **Major's** insignia, promoted in February, faster than anyone is promoted. Her formal line: *"Major Sokolov. Lakeshore checkpoint. Traffic normal."* Then the quiet last line: *"It was very quick."* She notices, and so does he. Neither says why.
- **The SFW (Isaiah).** In an office he was not meant to enter, he sees his published fragments in a military briefing folder headed *HISTOLOGIC COORDINATION: STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS*. He files it in the palace, and writes a question he can't answer: *Who else is reading this, and for what?*
- **The prison (Maya).** It is the fifteenth, so she visits her father. He has read the New Foundation, and has only one note: *"No index. Clever. They'll hate that."* He says it with the terrible, precise interest he gives everything. He still tells her about the letter opener. On the tram home she writes, as every month, *He didn't do it.*
- **Chronos (Tanaka).** She starts a **twelfth notebook**, in 9S, where the bed by the east window is made and empty and nothing is recorded. The first entry is in case-note form, and she leaves its last line unfinished.
- **Marcus's flat, night.** A letter arrives by hand, in the sloping handwriting of Julian's new journal: *"My brother wrote to me. First time in eleven years. I'm going to answer."*

## Ending
Marcus at the window with the UNCHECKED list. *Julian?* He looks at the question mark for a long time and does not cross it out.

**Final line:** *"The design could anticipate every reformer. It could not anticipate everyone."*
