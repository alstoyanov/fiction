# Chapter 13: The Table of Contents

**POV:** Mikhail "Old Songs" Petrov
**Date:** Wed Dec 5, 2108
**Setting:** The resistance farmhouse: the kitchen, the stove, the yard at dusk
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
A strip of paper a finger wide identifies page 30 as Principle Six. The heroes see that the Commission they serve is a principle, and that they are its restorers. Old Songs lets go of a false hope about Park and begins, without words, the first new tune he has made in forty years.

## Beats
- **The third songbook.** Last night Alexei brought it from Tanaka's handover at the lakeshore checkpoint, "for the old gentleman who sings on your broadcasts". It is a small pre-Histologic book of lakeshore hymns with a neatly re-glued spine. In the morning Old Songs opens the binding with a kitchen knife.
- **The strip** is folded once along the inside of the spine. It is torn lengthwise, and only the right-hand edge of three lines survives, the numerals and page numbers:
  > `IV …… 18`
  > `V …… 23`
  > `VI …… 29`
  **No headings.** He looks at the torn edge where the words should be. He knows a contents page when he sees one, having catalogued thousands.
- **Marcus and Kira drive out.** Kira lays out the arithmetic:
  - Three ends at p. 17, and IV begins at 18.
  - The X pages of Five run 23–28, and V begins at **23**. **The strip is genuine.**
  - VI begins at **29**, so **Singh's page 30 is Principle Six.**
- **What that means.** The sentence at the centre of page 30 is the one they know: *"When the record fails, the people will believe whoever restores it. Arrange to be the one who restores it."*
  - The Lost Hour failed the record, and the Doctrine restored it. The Commission restores it daily, trusted, and the Commission is them.
  - Marcus notes that XI came out of the Ministry's own correspondence. Nobody goes further. The Commission is Six in person, and nobody suspects the Minister himself or can say who wrote the page.
  - Kira counts the gap: Four runs pp. 18–22. Those are five pages nobody has seen, under a title nobody knows.
- **The broadcast.** On the evening screen, ahead of round 3, is **Professor Park Ji-won**, 74, the Synthesis mediator, warm, recalling her reading days in "the old capital Archive", "perhaps in the sixties, perhaps a little later". For three weeks he has half-believed it: the same city, the same room. He listens with his eyes shut to how she turns an argument. She doesn't turn it. She agrees, then qualifies.
- **He lets it go.** "No." Everyone looks up. "Elise never said 'perhaps' in her life." In memory: Thursdays, ink on her fingers, rhyme as a compression algorithm or as a prayer, her way of winning by conceding the first point, and Kerr's apple at eleven, peeled in one curl.
- **The new tune.** Later, at the stove, he hums something nobody has heard. It is short and lifts like a question. He stops and starts again, and it doesn't work, because it needs a second voice to come in under it. He stands listening to the place where that voice should be. Kira, in the doorway, hears him stop in the same place three times.

## Ending
Late, at the table, Marcus: "We were always going to be the restorers." Old Songs, without turning from the stove: "Then stop restoring. Start copying."
