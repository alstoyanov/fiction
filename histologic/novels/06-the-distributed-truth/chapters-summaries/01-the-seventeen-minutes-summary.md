# Chapter 1: The Seventeen Minutes

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Thu Nov 1, 2108
**Setting:** The Historical Integrity Commission's session in the great hall of the Justice Center, Veridica Capital; the east corridor afterwards; memory of a night in January 2108
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Establish the cost of Book 5's silence. Kira knows whose hand made the Chronos Gap. She agreed with the others to say nothing, and now there are thirty-one dead. Wei is kind, reasonable and wrong, and he is the only person in the hall who knows exactly how wrong he is.

## Beats
- **Opening in Kira's incident-report voice:** the session, the benches, the broadcast light, the eleven cameras she counts on the way in. Minister **Chen Zhao** presides, warm and courteous, with tea at his elbow. He has one short, neat line about grief (no "restore" in this chapter).
- **The casualty report** comes first. Since corrective broadcasting began on **Wed May 2**: **over four hundred** cognitive casualties, and **thirty-one dead on both shores**, since the Commission counts Chronos's published figures too. Closed schools and evacuated streets. A clerk reads out a list of lakeshore towns, one of them **Kestrel Point**. Kira writes nothing down, because the hall is recorded, and counts instead.
- **Wei's "attribution analysis" of the Chronos Gap:** Tue Dec 13, 2107, **03:12–03:29**, seventeen minutes in which the life-logs of the whole Chronos lakeshore province, about four million people, hold a **flat, content-free trace**: no breath, no word, no movement, only the last state carried across the seventeen minutes. For the sleepers, most of the province at that hour, it reads *asleep, heart rate steady*. He is clear, generous with credit and unhurried. His two hypotheses:
  - Chronos fabricated a gap in its own record to justify the war;
  - or the Lost Hour technique, which the Commission itself published in its case archive, has been copied by persons unknown.
  - Either way, not the Veridican state. It is reasonable, kind and wrong.
- **He credits Kira by name:** "Commissioner Osman noted in January that the structure resembles the Lost Hour. It does." A pleasant beat. "Resemblance isn't authorship." (Wei voice rule 3: decency on first reading, the design on the second.)
- **Flashback, three layers:**
  - **Nov 15, 2107,** in the blind spot of the plaza colonnade: Nikolai's blank-owner reservation "some weeks ahead". *It's the same hand.*
  - **Dec 13, 2107:** the news of the Gap. The seven knew what it was before anyone announced it.
  - **A night in January 2108,** when Chronos published its own analysis of the Gap to accuse Veridica. Kira read the seam structure in it and recognised it **within an hour**: the chain re-sealed backwards from the far edge, and the tombstones laid down in the order of removal. It is the same habit she found around her own fact, the 73 and the Lost Hour. She wrote a short technical note saying the Gap "**resembles**" the Lost Hour, and filed it. None of the seven said the name. Their reasons from Book 5 still hold: the proof is thin, and exposing seeding would void the Doctrine. They kept writing journals instead.
- **Now:** thirty-one dead. Kira does the sum she has refused to do all year and does not finish it. Her reciting compulsion starts up under the stress (the 7.3 spec, silently), and she stops it.
- **Kovač in the east corridor** afterwards, hands in her coat pockets. She is the Commission's **Registrar of Testimony** now and runs the Commission's public testimony intake on the ground floor, which opens at nine. "Ms. Osman." One quiet question: **"Has he written to you?"** Adrian. He hasn't. Kovač nods: "Nor to me." The storms are worst in the strip, where outlaws live. Neither woman says that.

## Ending
Across the corridor a Cabinet Office messenger hands Marcus a card: the First Minister's office, **21:00, tonight, alone.** Marcus reads it and puts it in his inside pocket. Kira watches him do it. Wei passes between them on his way out, tablet in his right hand, and says pleasantly to Marcus, **"Good luck."** He doesn't break stride. Kira files the fact that he knew.
