# Chapter 11: Seventeen Minutes Asleep

**POV:** Julian Kovač (his only POV chapter)
**Date:** Fri Nov 30, 2108
**Setting:** The Chronos lakeshore city: the Temporal Archive, the Temporal Defence Administration, Julian's flat
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
This is the inside of the man both states wanted to use: gentle, exact, and lonely in a country that forgets nothing. He chooses to publish with Marcus, not to turn him, and he gives the reader both Twist 4 plants without knowing it. His openness is what implicates him.

## Beats
- **Morning, logged.** He wakes at 06:10:04 and knows it to the second. **Set this up early:** Chronos logs sleep breath by breath. Last night's log shows every turn, a waking at 03:47 he doesn't remember, and his heart rate like a tide. Citizens read their own logs the way others read the weather.
- **His flat** is small, orderly and quiet, with a window on the lake. For fifteen years he has kept Veridica's shore out of the frame. He keeps lists in his log's annotation layer (Kovač's "boy who kept lists", Ch 7).
- **The Archive.** He is Senior Historian and holder of the **senior reconciliation key**. It is routine to him: he countersigns the Archive's nightly backup reconciliation the way a clerk initials a ledger. Show one countersignature in passing. His Lost Hour monograph sits on a shelf.
- **Adrian.** His younger brother is an outlaw in Veridica, and they haven't spoken in more than ten years. There is a list of things to say to him, never sent.
- **The debrief.** Administrator Constance Aubry, of Temporal Defence, is cool and courteous and thinks in distributions. She has already read the garden conversation of Nov 22 in his log, and she wants to use it. Continue MERIDIAN, with a shared confession as the new intimacy:
  - "A man who has confessed to you has chosen you."
  - "They want your key, Julian. We knew they would."
  - "The probability that Commissioner Chen's confession was itself a move? Near a third."
  She never tells him what to do. She tells him what is likely.
- **The decision.** Walking home, he thinks of the **right of self-disclosure**: any citizen may publish their own log. If he publishes the directive and the garden, nobody can take it back and nobody can use it. He decides to publish with Marcus.
- **The scroll back.** The Gap's first anniversary is thirteen days away, and the Archive is preparing the observance. Before publishing, he checks his own record, as a historian does. He scrolls to **Tue Dec 13, 2107**, as every citizen did that week. From 03:12 to 03:29: ***asleep**, heart rate steady*, and nothing else. It is the same flat, content-free trace four million people have. He slept through the sacrilege like the rest of the province. He has seen it before and never thought about it, and he doesn't now.
- **Marcus.** The fog, the bench under the bare pear tree, "Good. Neither can I." Nobody in Chronos has ever said anything to him that couldn't be looked up. What he feels stays tender and unstated.

## Ending
He writes to Marcus through Professor Park's secretariat, the only post that crosses the lake in wartime. He dictates it into his log first, so that it exists. Unasked: *"If it helps, I can show you the reconciliation key. So you can see there is nothing behind it."*

## Notes
- **(Twist 4 clue)** Seventeen minutes *asleep*, set against the breath-by-breath sleep log from the morning. Neither Julian nor the narration notices the contrast.
- **(Twist 4 clue)** He offers the key unasked. Aubry's line echoes the clue that both states want the key (Ch 2, 6).
- **(Twist 4 texture)** The routine countersignature gives the key "habits" (README 12). Don't show the key in close-up. The showing in the garden during round 3 is recalled in Ch 17 and Ch 30, not shown here.
- **What the Gap looks like (settled, README decision 16):** every affected log shows a flat, content-free trace for 03:12–03:29, the last state carried over, with no breaths, words or movement. For sleepers it reads *asleep, heart rate steady*. The clue is only the contrast with ordinary Chronos sleep logs, which record every breath. Ch 1 and Ch 30 match this. Don't call it a "flatline" (that word is the spoofed death, Ch 22).
- **New dating:** Dec 13, 2108 is both the Gap's first anniversary and the last day of round 3.
- **Chronos rules:** read access to one's own log, the right of self-disclosure, and erasure as sacrilege. Officials are Administrators, and there are no ministers.
- **Family:** Julian is Adrian's **elder** brother and emigrated at nineteen. The Epilogue's "eleven years" means the last contact was about 2098.
