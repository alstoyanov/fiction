# Chapter 24: The Remedy Was Ready

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Wed Jan 9, 2109
**Setting:** Marcus and Kira's flat in the capital: the floor, the table, paper in rows
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 2.** Every clue since Ch 9 is laid in one row, in dates, and read as a system. Someone fed them the fragments in the order that made publication inevitable, and had the remedy waiting. The Concordance is page 30 performed, and it is built out of the principle the heroes themselves published. It ends on the book's answer, in six words.

## Beats
- **19:00.** Kira, Maya, Nikolai and Marcus. Kira clears the floor and lays everything out in rows, left to right by date, the way she lays out every fault. Her reciting compulsion threatens (*layer one, key exchange*). She stops it by counting the sheets.
- **The row**, as Kira's incident log:
  - **Thu Nov 1:** KINDRED offered.
  - **Tue Nov 6, 08:14:** the Concordance's first draft, in the Technical Director's office (Maya's sheets).
  - **Mon Nov 26, 06:58:** Principle Three, copy X. No visitor. It arrived on the morning Wei's office owed an answer about the annex (Ch 9).
  - **Wed Nov 28, 06:51:** Principle Five, copy X, the morning after round 3's agenda named the border (Ch 10).
  - Both records were **created from a Ministry-network terminal** (Maya).
  - **Tue Dec 4:** Maya's unnamed "capacity planning" estimate, which one of the drafts cites.
  - **Thu Dec 6:** Varley's first payment, from "public outreach". Kira has it from Ruth's written account, which describes both cards. She catches what Ruth didn't: it came **twelve days before** the Concordance was announced.
  - **Sun Dec 9:** Wei, told of the publication plan: "Good." (Ch 15.)
  - **Mon Dec 17, 11:40:** the last revision, two hours and twenty minutes before publication.
  - **Tue Dec 18, 12:00:** Maitland's speech, twenty-two hours after publication, with numbered provisions and a budget (Ch 17).
  - **From Dec 18:** the League's rallies, paid from a Ministry public-order line (Nikolai, Ch 18). Varley's second payment comes on a Concordance card. Nikolai lays the two lines side by side: *two budget lines, one ministry.*
- **The other sender.** Tanaka's case notes, carried back by Dmitri (Ch 22): *Patient states she sent three documents.* And: *"Copy X isn't mine."* Vance sent the Introduction, the strip and the Warning. Someone else sent Three and Five. Marcus: "XI came out of the Ministry's own correspondence. X came in through the Ministry's own network." That is a guess, and Kira files it as one. Nobody names whose copies they are.
- **The mechanism.** Kira reads Principle Three aloud from their published copy: *"Should insiders reform it, it may be restored from the true source."* Maya puts down the component number: **a restore path from the master.** The Concordance registers every journal. That makes it **a list of everyone who remembers**. It holds each page as a master copy that can be restored, and that means edited, from the source. *"If a book burns, we restore it"* (Wei, Ch 17). The chandlery burned on Christmas Eve, and by January the Concordance's offer sounds like mercy.
- **Page 30,** now known as Principle Six: *"When the record fails, the people will believe whoever restores it. Arrange to be the one who restores it."* The Concordance **restores the record by absorbing the journals into it.** The League is the extreme that makes it look moderate. Varley was paid to make the free books look unreliable.
- **KINDRED, implied.** Marcus looks for a long time at Nov 1 and Nov 6 side by side. If he had said yes, the war would have been won their way. He said no and published, and the remedy had been begun five days after the offer. He doesn't say *designed*. Kira doesn't either. **Never proven.**
- **What they don't do.** Maya asks whether they take it to The Judge on the 14th. Marcus says no: the metadata can be called forged, it would expose her, and a court is one record. They won't beat an index with a hearing. Nikolai, looking at his hands: "Then we beat it with copies."
- **Kira's sketch.** While they talk she draws three small circles on the back of a sheet and joins them, then another three, then more: books checking each other in threes, with no line to the middle. It is the seed of the pairing method (Ch 26). She doesn't explain it yet.

## Ending
Kira, very level: **"They didn't stop our publication. They finished it."** Marcus, very quietly: **"Then we don't give it a center."**
