# Chapter 12: The Third Fragment

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Mon Dec 3, 2108
**Setting:** The Sovereignty of Free Will: Isaiah's rooms and his paper's offices; the parlour of his memory palace
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Isaiah's rule of three is met, and the publication gains its third nation. His hosts love the story too much, which is the Book 7–8 setup. He makes sure no single nation owns the truth by setting a condition on the hour.

## Beats
- **The link.** It is mid-morning in the Sovereignty and early morning in the capital, and the link is long, delayed and crackling. Kira sends photographs of three typed pages: the **Compiler's Introduction**, pp. 2–4, with the chipped *e* and no numeral. Her note: *"The old man knows the voice. There is more. Not yet."* She sends no name, and he doesn't ask for one.
- **He reads it twice, like an editorial.** Quote:
  > *"This document assembles principles from seventeen contributors across political theory, systems architecture, social psychology, historical methodology and information security. Our goal is a post-democratic order that prevents the chaos of the Fracture Years while preserving the appearance of participation. Truth, we have learned, cannot be decided by a public. It must be decided for one, and the public must be allowed to believe it decided."*
- **The rule of three,** filed:
  - **One:** page 30.
  - **Two:** the sung essay.
  - **Three:** this.
  "One fragment is a curiosity. Two is a pattern." The third says what the others only implied: *assembles principles*. The system was built.
- **The editors.** The managing editor who spiked his Doctrine column is delighted: "The Histologic world confesses." The delight worries him. Isaiah hears belief in how people say words, and the man says *confesses* the way people say *surrenders*.
- **The attaché.** An SFW military attaché is in the office "by coincidence": courteous, careful paragraphs, no interface. He asks for "the full set, for assessment." Isaiah: "There is no set. There are three fragments." Attaché: "Then the three." Isaiah declines: he may read them when everyone does. His asylum is still *conditional*, and he admires the word's precision.
- **His condition.** Kira has told him, in outline, that Marcus and Julian mean to go public together once they know what they hold. Isaiah's condition: he will publish, but only in the same hour as they do, so that no one nation owns it. It goes out in his SFW paper and through the **Synthesis Institute for Historical Methods**, the channel that carried his Wing C account in 2106. The hour isn't set yet. He sends Kira one line: *"Your hour is mine. Not before."*
- **Evening, the palace.** He writes the headline where he always writes a first sentence, in the parlour, among the harmless true warm things: the pepper soup, the first byline framed above the fireplace. It is a question, as his pieces end on one: ***"Decided For Us?"*** The standfirst: *pages written before the Founding, describing a nation that would be governed through its record, and allowed to believe it chose.*

## Ending
He sets the headline on the parlour table and looks up. At the head of the table sits his Aunt Sarah, sharp and elegant, as she always has. In the palace she has never said a word. She is watching him.
