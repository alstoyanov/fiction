# Chapter 17: The Ally

**POV:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka
**Date:** May 2106
**Setting:** The white room, a medical check-up, a corridor
**Length:** ~3,000 words

## Purpose
Turn someone inside the staff. Tanaka fails with the person she trusted, and succeeds with the one she read correctly.

## Beats
- The alliance needs staff. Tanaka is the one who can read them. She has worked beside them.
- **First attempt: Reyna Moss.** At a routine medical check, Tanaka speaks as one doctor to another: "Reyna, you've seen what it does. You held Marcus's hand while it happened." Moss is shaken and sincere, and *immovable*: "It's hard, Yuki. Healing is hard. I pray you'll see that again." She doesn't report her, because she thinks Tanaka is sick, not dangerous. It's worse than betrayal. Tanaka learns that the gentlest believers are the hardest walls.
- **Second attempt: Dr. James Morrison.** Tanaka has watched his tremor for eight months. In a session, with Reeves's voice briefly off-channel, she speaks: "You don't believe in this. Your hands tell me every time."
- He's startled and defensive: "Be quiet. They record everything." But he doesn't report her.
- **Next session:** He is gentler and less invasive. He turns the probe depth down by a margin no one else would notice, but she does.
- **The corridor**, days later, during an escort change. Morrison, barely audible: "I'm sorry. I built the cradle calibration for this. I thought it was for trauma patients. I don't know how to stop it."
- Tanaka: "You can help us. You can be on the right side of this. And you can start by telling me what the triple cradle will do to the Volkovs."
- He tells her in one sentence: *simultaneous integration will either break the bond or kill all three; Reeves considers both outcomes informative.*

## Ending
Tanaka codes the report that night: *Morrison is ours, or nearly. Moss is not, and never will be. Be kind to her anyway; she'll be the one who raises the alarm.* Then she adds a line only for herself: *I refused once and they made me a subject. He's about to refuse. I owe it to him to make it survivable.*

## Continuity Notes
- Moss's arc pays off in Ch 24–25, when she raises the alarm sincerely.
- Morrison becomes the "defector" of Book 5.
- The detail about the triple cradle raises the stakes for the triplets' August "integration".
