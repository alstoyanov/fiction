# Chapter 18: The Breaking Point

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Late May – early June 2106
**Setting:** The white room (intensified), Director Kovač's office
**Length:** ~3,600 words

## Purpose
Marcus's hardest test: Reeves tries to take Kira from him. Then an ally at the very top. This is the midpoint turn of the book.

## Beats
- The micro-drift has grown. Reeves won't risk his showcase before August: **"Re-baseline C-1."** Sessions go back to intensive. Moss is worried and kind. Morrison is grey-faced.
- **The session:** For the first time Reeves is *in the room*: an unremarkable, courteous man in his fifties with clean hands and interested eyes. It is the first time we see his face. He doesn't hunt Elena anymore. He hunts the new warmth. "There's someone new in here, Marcus. Let's see her."
- The interface searches for Kira. Marcus runs his vault: fact entries, one after another, with Kira folded inside the formats. *"Personnel entry: Osman, K., Senior Architect. Location: Wing C, 7-K. Status:"* and he never finishes the line, because finishing it would make it salient.
- It nearly fails. He loses a real memory in the struggle: their first whispered conversation in the library, gone for good. Reeves feels the resistance and smiles. "Interesting. You've learned to hide. Who taught you?"
- Marcus plays the flattened patient with everything he has: the lag, the blankness, a forgotten question. Morrison fumbles a calibration, maybe on purpose, and the session aborts early "for subject safety".
- **Director Kovač's office, days later.** Kovač, 52, precise and weary, summons him "for a routine stability interview". There are no cameras; she has turned them off.
  - "Marcus, you were almost corrected. Then you came back. Why?"
  - Marcus takes the risk: "Someone gave me a reason to. And because correction isn't healing. It's erasure."
  - Her expression flickers. "You think I don't know that?"
  - She shows him *her* files: years of documenting failures and damage. People like Samuel Ferris who never came back.
  - "I thought I was helping people. Continuity… it's gone too far."
  - **Her own turn:** "You were our first success. Then the garden cameras showed me a man who was supposed to be empty looking at a woman like *that*. Nobody who is erased looks at anyone like that." And more: she pulled Kira's file and found the "record anomaly", and the named "person of interest", **Adrian Kovač, her nephew**. "Reeves stole a fact to take her, and used my family to make it believable."
- She doesn't promise anything. She asks one question: "If there were a way out, could you all be ready by August?"

## Ending
Back in 7-H, Marcus tries to recall the library, the reference shelves, and her whisper, and finds only a smooth blank where it was. He codes to her: *I lost the first day. Tell it to me again.* Her reply comes on the next cart, in pinpricks: the whole conversation, word for word, ending with the line she said then: *I'll remember for both of us until you can.*

## Continuity Notes
- Kovač is Adrian's **aunt** (her character file). Adrian stays offstage in this book.
- **Twist 1, reread:** Reeves isn't trying to delete Kira. He is *measuring how deeply she has taken root*. "You've learned to hide" is delight, not frustration. Marcus believes he won this round, and Reeves lets him. Write the scene so both readings hold.
- Kovač doesn't know about R-2. Her reading ("nobody erased looks at anyone like that") is sincere, and it is also exactly what Reeves hoped someone would see.
- A memory that is *permanently* lost here makes the trauma real. He never gets it back.
- Kovač's cameras-off trick is how the alliance meets in Ch 19.
