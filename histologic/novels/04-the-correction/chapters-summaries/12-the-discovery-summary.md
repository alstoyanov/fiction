# Chapter 12: The Discovery

**POV:** Nikolai Volkov
**Date:** Late Feb 2106
**Setting:** Technical wing, maintenance room B-4, the Correction Registry
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
Nikolai, the anxious bridge, finds what Continuity is and when it ends. This forces the triplets' hand.

## Beats
- Nikolai maintains Wing C's interface cradles and the facility's data link to the national **Correction Registry**. He is trusted for being perfectly compliant, perfectly competent and perfectly incurious. He hates how good he is at it.
- **The hidden files:** Every night for months he has visited his own buried partition in the Registry: the three brothers' true memories, which he hid at the border facility (Story 03). They survived the transfer because every facility writes to the same Registry. He reconstructs a little more each night. Tonight he restores the roof, the fog and the argument about who was born first. It is a devotional act.
- He notices a restricted directory whose access pattern sits right next to his partition: **CONTINUITY**. He has never dared touch it. Isaiah's "C-7" has reached him through Dmitri and the bond, as a nagging sense of a number.
- The technical breach is plausible and small: a maintenance certificate that Reeves's team forgot to revoke. The dread is real. Every access is logged somewhere.
- **What he finds:**
  - Seven subject files, C-1 to C-7, each with a "mind type": BELIEVER, ARCHITECT, BOND ×3, CLINICIAN, WITNESS.
  - The stated aim: *"to produce stable, genuine belief, not compliance, across all tested cognitive profiles."*
  - The status line on C-1 (Marcus): *"SUCCESS. Stable baseline. Reserved for demonstration."*
  - The status line on the BOND files: *"Primary research interest. Integration method: simultaneous triple cradle (untested)."* They plan to correct all three at once, in one room.
  - The schedule: **FINAL INTEGRATION, ALL SUBJECTS: 10 AUG 2106. MINISTRY DEMONSTRATION: 11 AUG 2106.**
  - An acquisition note on C-2 (Kira) that he doesn't understand yet: *"Acquired via record anomaly (ref. R.)."* And under it: *"Placement 7-K per protocol R-2."* The **R-2** file sits in Reeves's personal directory, and Nikolai can't open it.
- He copies what he can into his own partition, disguised as corrupted memory fragments, and backs out. His hands won't stop shaking.

## Ending
Walking back to Wing C under escort, Nikolai reaches for the bond and doesn't need words. **Across the facility, in the garden and the library, both his brothers stop what they are doing at the same instant.** That night, for the first time in nine months, all three break protocol and look directly at one another through the glass. Dmitri gives a single nod: *Now.*
