# Chapter 23: The Breakout

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Aug 10, 2106, ~00:30 – 01:20
**Setting:** Wing C, the service tunnels, the data centre
**Length:** ~3,800 words

## Purpose
Action with moral cost. The evidence is taken, the antagonist confronted, and people left behind.

## Beats
- **Samuel:** Marcus kneels and tells him, "We're leaving. You can come." Samuel smiles politely, because he doesn't understand the concept. He asks if it's time for his session. Kira has to pull Marcus away. Marcus: "He was the first one I saw. I promised him with my hand." It's his first real grief of the book, and they can't stop for it.
- **Who comes and who can't:** Some general-population inmates follow the triplets. Some are too damaged to understand a door. **Two** Wing C inmates join the group (to be named during drafting). Both die in Ch 25.
- **Down the service tunnels to the data centre.** Kira is in her element: this is Spire-grade architecture, and she helped write its standards. Nikolai strips the drives. Kira breaks the archive encryption *with a key from her own old protocol*, the one Continuity deleted from the factorepo, which they then quietly reused. The irony is exact and she savours it. *The stolen fact, put back to work.*
- **Dr. Reeves is there.** He came back early from the Ministry when the storm alerts reached him, and he is calm inside it. He says he trained his interface on storms: "Where do you think we learned what a mind can bear?"
- **His revelation of how deep it goes:**
  - The border fact storms were **Continuity field trials**. Ivan Volkov, 2471, was early data.
  - Phase Two is already approved at Ministry level. Test carrier signals are *already* running in parts of the broadcast network.
  - "You're not escaping a prison, Ms. Osman. You're escaping a *pilot*. The country is the facility."
- He tries to reason with Marcus, his success: "You were at peace. Don't you remember?" Marcus: "No. You took that too."
- **Dmitri subdues him.** It's controlled, not cruel. A blow and restraints. Tanaka is hurt in the struggle, cut on the right temple by a falling rack: the **scar** from her character file.
- **The copies:** one set to Nikolai's partition, one physical drive to Elena, and one to Isaiah through Kovač's director channel.
- **Isaiah and Kovač stay** in the data centre as the others go. Isaiah: "Go. I'll protect the data from here. I'll publish everything." Kovač begins typing the official record: *cognitive failures*.
- **Moss appears** in the tunnel mouth, dazed, storm-lit and sincere. She sees them and understands. She says, "I'm sorry. I have to save you," and hits the manual alarm. She isn't a villain. She's a believer.

## Ending
At the tunnel exit Marcus looks back at Ashford. Its outline is flickering between a hospital, a prison and an empty field. *He was almost lost here forever.* Kira: "Come on. We have to go." He goes, and the alarm follows them into the dark.

- Moss's alarm is what triggers the immediate pursuit in Ch 24.
- Kira reusing her own stolen protocol pays off her Story 02 arc.
