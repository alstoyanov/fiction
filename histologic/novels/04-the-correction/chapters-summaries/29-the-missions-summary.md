# Chapter 29: The Missions

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mid-Sept 2106
**Setting:** The safe house, the last night together
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
They scatter because they have to, not in triumph. Marcus's mission is born, desperate rather than confident.

## Beats
- Elena lays out the reality. Isaiah's exposé has made Continuity a scandal, but Phase Two isn't dead, only delayed. Being officially dead is a weapon: *dead people aren't tracked.* But a group of seven dead people is a pattern, and patterns get found. They have to split up.
- **The paths:**
  - **Dr. Tanaka → Chronos**, with Morrison. She will stop correction technology being used there, and build a network to get defectors out. (This sets up Book 5: "helps defectors escape".)
  - **Alexei and Dmitri → the Resistance**, with Elena, to fight and to extract other correction subjects. The Phantom and the enforcer now work *together*.
  - **Nikolai → back into Veridica** as Elena's mole, under a new identity forged on top of a dead man's. It's his own choice and the most dangerous one. "I've spent my life standing in the middle. At least now I'm choosing which middle." (He keeps a partial Registry backup, as Book 5 needs.)
  - **Old Songs** goes with the resistance, "to keep the songs where people need them".
  - **Marcus and Kira → the border zone**, to document, gather evidence and reform from within, starting from outside.
- **The triplets' parting:** For the first time since Story 03 they choose to separate, and it's their choice. They find that the bond doesn't need glass or a triangle. Before they part, Alexei and Dmitri tell Nikolai the rooftop story one last time, out loud, together, and he answers the passphrase from their telling: "Dima. By thirty seconds. It doesn't count." It's a memory rebuilt from testimony, and it's enough. Nikolai: "Promise." Dmitri and Alexei: "Promise."
- **Marcus's mission isn't confident anymore.** It's desperate: *"I have to believe this can be fixed. If I don't believe that, then correction won. They erased the part of me that had hope. I can't let that be permanent."*
- Kira: "Then I'll hope for both of us. And I'll remember the parts you can't."
- **Living with the false field (Twist 2):** Marcus now keeps two lists, *checked* memories and *unchecked* ones, and asks the others to confirm things. A historian who has to have his own life verified by witnesses. He finds it humbling, not humiliating: "This is what the factorepo should have been. Not one record. Many witnesses."
- Marcus faces the 0.58 one last time. The system that condemned him was falsified by people. **That's the only reason reform is possible.** A system that's truly infallible can't be fixed, but a corrupted one can. It's a new kind of faith: in correction by people, not of people.
- **Marcus accepts himself:** damaged, not defeated. The man who arrived at Ashford would have been horrified by who he's become. The man he is now will fight.

## Ending
The last night. They sit together in the safe house for the last time as seven minus one. Someone leaves a chair empty for Isaiah without saying anything. In the morning the vehicles leave in different directions. Marcus and Kira stand in the fog and watch until there's no one left to watch.

## Continuity Notes
- The idea of many witnesses instead of one record anticipates Book 5's Lost Hour Doctrine and Book 6's distributed truth.
- The destinations match Book 5's starting positions (Marcus is an outlaw in hiding, Tanaka is in Chronos, Nikolai has a backup, Elena is coordinating).
- Marcus's "correction by people, not of people" is the thesis of the book and seeds his Book 5 arc from reformer to official reformer.
