# Chapter 4: The Arrival

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Dec 7–10, 2105
**Setting:** Intake chamber, then Wing C Cell 7-K (facing 7-H), then the white room
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
Introduce Kira as a fresh, fighting mind, the opposite of Marcus at this point. Start her resistance. Give the first clue that someone in Wing C is awake.

## Beats
- **Open the morning after *The Stolen Fact*.** She spent the night in the small white intake chamber ("I entered that fact. I know I did."). Don't retell the story.
- Dr. Moss's assessment. Words are flashed at her: *Adrian. Spire. Fact. November eighteenth.* Kira realizes the machine is watching her *reactions* and starts, instinctively, to reduce them.
- She is moved to Wing C and walked down the glass corridor. She glimpses inmates staring at walls, and some catatonic. Hayes checks her tablet at the junction, hesitates, and says, "7-K. Yes, Dr. Reeves was specific." Then **7-H, directly across from her new cell**: the broken young man with glasses, head in hands. He is the same man she glimpsed through the observation window the night before. He looks familiar, but she can't place him.
- **Cell 7-K.** Two transparent walls, silence. She takes it apart the way she'd take apart a system: sightlines, camera domes, the rhythm of the lights, the meal trolley. She counts. It keeps her sane.
- **Her first session** (Reeves's voice; Moss; Morrison). The interface goes straight for Adrian: the bridge, the kiss, the warehouse confession. On pure instinct Kira drags her attention into **Spire architecture**: encryption layers, node addresses, the spec of protocol 7.3 recited line by line. The probing slides off. The voice pauses, then says: "Interesting."
- She leaves shaking, nauseous and **intact**. She now has a hypothesis: the machine follows feeling, not information.
- **Patterns she logs over three days:** who is taken, when, and for how long, and who comes back changed. The triplets leave on work details and move with a synchronisation that is too precise. The man in 7-H only moves when he's taken, and comes back unchanged because there's nothing left to change.
- She thinks of Adrian: he loved her, and he lied to her. Both things are true. Holding both, she decides, is a skill the machine can't read.

## Ending
The book slot opens. On the tray is a library book she didn't ask for, a dull maintenance manual for pre-Histologic water systems. On page 47, one word is faintly underlined in pencil: **REMEMBER.** She looks up. The corridor is empty except for the old trolley-man walking away, humming.

## Continuity Notes
- Her technique ("think technically") is an early, untrained version of the rote-material blind spot.
- The book was sent by Alexei (Ch 6) and delivered by Old Songs' trolley. Kira doesn't know who sent it until Ch 14.
- Kira has not yet recognised Marcus from the news. That happens in Ch 10.
- **Twist 1 clue:** "Dr. Reeves was specific." Her cell was chosen for its line of sight to Marcus.
