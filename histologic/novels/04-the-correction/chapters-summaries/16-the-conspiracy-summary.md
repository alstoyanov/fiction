# Chapter 16: The Conspiracy

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Late Apr 2106
**Setting:** Cell 7-H, the garden, the white room
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The truth about Continuity comes out, and it hits Marcus hardest. The last support of his old faith (the Judge was right, even if cruel) is knocked out *by a fact*.

## Beats
- **Elena, first:** Marcus absorbs that the woman who seduced, lied to and warned him is the triplets' aunt, and that her brother was Ivan Volkov. In the garden, Alexei, through Dmitri, sends him the family's line: "She recruited seven people and never knew if she saved them. She's coming for eight." Marcus doesn't know whether he's angry, grateful or afraid. He is all three at once, which he has been learning is allowed.
- **Nikolai's files, decoded and shared piecemeal:**
  - Project Continuity aims to create genuine belief, not compliance. The seven were selected to test every type of mind: if it works on a Believer, an Architect, a Bond, a Clinician and a Witness, it works on anyone.
  - Phase Two: delivery through the **national neural-interface broadcast network**. *Not a prison technique. A population technique.*
  - **Kira's "record anomaly":** Her fact was deleted *by Continuity* to get her convicted. She was chosen, then manufactured into a criminal. Her fury is cold and total. (Who in the factorepo can do this is left open, a signature she'll recognise in Book 5.)
  - They note the locked **R-2** reference and move on. Kira assumes it's a technical protocol for Architect-type subjects. It's a reasonable guess and the wrong one.
  - **C-1:** *"Contamination index 0.58 (below threshold). Adjusted to 0.73 for acquisition."*
- **The blow:** Marcus reads it three times. Fifty-eight percent. Below sixty. **He would have gone home.** The Judge did not calculate his guilt: someone *typed* it. Everything he gave up doubt for ("the facts are the facts") was falsified at the exact point where it decided his life.
- He doesn't break. He is surprised that he doesn't. What he feels is clean, cold, historian's anger: *someone changed a fact.* It is the first fully *his* emotion since October.
- **"I was their first success."** He sees it clearly now. If Kira hadn't arrived, he would be gone, and Reeves would present his emptiness to the Ministry as proof. He owes what's left of himself to her.
- The triplets survived through connection. Kira through a technical vault. Himself through Kira. **Connection is the counter-technology.**
- In the white room that week, Reeves's voice notes that his "stability readings show micro-drift", nothing much yet. Marcus fills his mind with fact entries and Kira-in-the-formats, and holds.

## Ending
In the garden blind arc, Marcus says aloud, for the first time: "We're lab rats. But some of us have already proved we can't be broken." Kira takes his hand below the bench, in full view of nothing. Four months until August.
