# Chapter 25: The Cost of Freedom

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Aug 2, 2106, ~01:20 – 02:00
**Setting:** The service road, the perimeter fence, Elena's vehicles
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
The escape itself, the reunion, and the price. Dmitri is the tactical mind carrying the emotional weight.

## Beats
- **The perimeter:** Dmitri leads with an enforcer's economy. The storm is thinning at the edges, and so is their cover.
- **The flatline:** At the fence line Morrison, on a stolen tablet, and Nikolai trigger the spoof. One by one, their interface telemetry reads *cognitive failure: flatline*. Dmitri feels the moment his own record dies. It feels like nothing, which is what frightens him.
- **The people they leave:** An inmate who followed them from general population sits down at the fence and refuses to go on. The flickering world outside is worse than the one he knows. Dmitri makes the enforcer's calculation and hates it. They leave him. It echoes Marcus and Samuel.
- **Elena:** Only two proper vehicles on the service road, plus a battered delivery van her people found in ten days. It's the rushed plan made visible. A woman in tactical grey steps out: *Commander Elena Volkov*.
  - The reunion with her nephews is not sentimental at first. She counts heads, like a soldier. Then, by habit, she tests them with the family passphrase: *"Who was born first?"* **Nikolai opens his mouth and has nothing** (Ch 21). The silence lasts one terrible second. Dmitri answers for him: "I was. By thirty seconds." Alexei: "That doesn't count." Elena understands what was taken, and she breaks. She holds all three at once, which only works because they stand like one person.
  - She tells them in one line what she never told them as boys: "Your father didn't change. They changed him. I should have told you."
  - **Elena sees Marcus.** "Is he…?" Kira: "He's recovering. But they almost got him." Elena and Marcus face each other. Neither of them has the words. She says, "I told you to remember that conversation." He says, "I didn't. They took it. Kira gave some of it back." The history between them is left unresolved on purpose.
  - Old Songs is helped into a vehicle, humming.
- Dmitri assigns the two Wing C escapees to the delivery van because it's the only room left. He will remember making that choice.
- **The alarm:** Moss's alarm has reached Border Security. The spoofed flatlines complicate the picture, since officially these people are dying, but a live pursuit has been dispatched anyway.
- **Pursuit begins immediately.** Headlights on the ring road. Elena: "East. Into the zone. They won't follow us far into a storm." Dmitri: "They will. I would have."

## Ending
The vehicles pull out. Dmitri takes a last look at Ashford through the rear window: flickering, lit up, emptying. He thinks of Sokolov, whose storm unit they just used in her country's capital, and knows *exactly* who will be sent after them.
