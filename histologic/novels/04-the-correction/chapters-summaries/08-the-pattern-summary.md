# Chapter 8: The Pattern

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Mid-Dec 2105. Six weeks as a subject.
**Setting:** Cell 7-E, a white-room session, his memory palace
**Length:** ~3,000 words

## Purpose
The investigator's view. Isaiah turns the seven into a *question*, gives the reader the first hard clue (the word "Continuity" and the number 2471), and ends Part One on mystery.

## Beats
- **His craft:** A journalist's memory palace. He mentally furnishes a house from his childhood, and every fact goes in a room. In sessions he leads the machine into the front parlour, full of surface memories (his work, his aunt's dinners), and never opens the cellar.
- **Why he's here:** He was investigating a pattern of deaths in the border zone that matched fact-storm onsets too closely. In a Border Security procurement leak he found test-subject numbers. One of them, **2471**, was annotated "lethal threshold confirmed". He was arrested three days before publishing, for "unauthorized historical inquiry".
- **His taxonomy of the staff:** true believers (Moss), jobholders (most orderlies, Hayes), the disturbed (Morrison), and the one he can't classify (Reeves, who seems to believe in something other than Veridica).
- **The clue:** During a session, Morrison's tablet is angled wrong for three seconds. Isaiah reads the header upside down: **PROJECT CONTINUITY: SUBJECT C-7 (WITNESS)**. He files it in the cellar.
- **His census of Wing C's "special" inmates:**
  - The triplets: arrested together, here about a year. In the library he overheard the librarian's surname: **Volkov**.
  - Tanaka: four months. He heard an orderly call her "Doctor".
  - The broken man, Marcus Chen: two months. Isaiah recognises him from a small news item about a factorepo historian who reported an outlaw.
  - The new woman: days. He has heard an orderly call her "Osman". Years ago he covered the Spire's opening, and he remembers a young architect of that name on the press list. The system's own builder is now inside the system's prison.
  - Himself: six weeks.
- *Seven.* All arrived within about a year, from different arrests. Most of Wing C's other inmates are general-population overflow who come and go. **These seven stay.** If he is "C-7", someone else is "C-1".
- Why these seven? A believer, a builder of the system, three brothers, a doctor, a journalist. Is it a collection? A sample?

## Ending
Late at night, the name comes together in the cellar of his palace: **Volkov**. Test Subject 2471 in the procurement leak was listed with a surname. *Ivan Volkov.* Isaiah looks across the atrium at three identical men asleep in a triangle. He realises he may be holding the proof of how their father died, and has no way to tell them.

## Continuity Notes
- Isaiah is 38 and dignified. His notebook habit has become the memory palace.
- The Continuity header and "C-7" pay off in Ch 12 and Ch 16. The link to 2471 pays off in Ch 15 and Ch 23.
- Close Part One on this note of mystery and grief.
