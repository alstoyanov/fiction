# Chapter 21: The Single Node

**POV:** Nikolai Volkov
**Date:** ~Jul 18–20, 2106
**Setting:** Treatment room C-1, fitted with the new triple cradle, of which only one seat is used
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Twist 3: the bond gets its real test, and it costs something. This is the book's darkest turn before the storm, and it forces the escape forward.

## Beats
- **Interrogation first:** Reeves questions Nikolai in person, courteous and curious. What did you open? Why? Nikolai plays the compliant technician who was "running an integrity sweep". Reeves doesn't believe him and doesn't need to: "It doesn't matter what you took, Nikolai. It matters what you are. And I've waited a year to find out."
- **Reeves's decision:** Instead of the full simultaneous session on Aug 10, he runs a **"single-node test"** now. He will integrate one brother alone while the other two sit in their cells, and measure whether the bond carries the correction to them or protects the one in the chair. "If the bond is a network, let's see what happens when one node goes down."
- **The session:** Morrison runs the console, helpless, and turns the probe down wherever he dares. Moss is present, sincere, and holds Nikolai's hand the way she held Marcus's.
- **Inside Nikolai's head,** the bond is presented as sensation, not telepathy. Across the facility, Dmitri in the garden and Alexei in the library both stop, sit down and *hold*. In Wing C, Kira sees Alexei's hands clench on a book cart and understands without code.
- **The fight:** The machine goes for Nikolai's guilt, as it did in Story 03 ("I tried to hold us together"). Then it goes for the brothers themselves. Nikolai runs every vault technique he has: code, maintenance logs, Old Songs' tunes. It isn't enough alone. At the worst point he feels his brothers *pull*, a synchronised pattern that Morrison's console shows as three peaks where there should be one. Reeves leans in, delighted.
- **The bond holds, just.** Nikolai comes back as Nikolai.
- **The cost:** Back in his cell he reaches for the memory he restored so lovingly in Ch 12, the **rooftop**: fog off the lake, the argument about who was born first. **It's gone.** He knows it existed and what it was about, but the memory itself is missing. The Volkov passphrase, *"Who was born first?"*, is now a question he can't answer.
- **Reeves's conclusion,** overheard by Morrison and passed on through Tanaka: the bond *resisted*, so the full simultaneous session must come earlier, while he knows how to break it. **Final integration for all subjects is moved to Aug 2.** Reeves will not be leaving for the Ministry. He'll be in the building.
- The escape they had planned for Aug 9–10 now has to happen on the **night of Aug 1–2**. Elena gets ten days' notice instead of three weeks.

## Ending
That night Dmitri sends a coded page to Nikolai's cell. It isn't the plan. It's the rooftop, written out in full, every word they said as twelve-year-olds, pricked into a school primer. Nikolai reads it three times. It's a story now, not a memory. He accepts it anyway, because his brothers remembered it for him, the same thing Kira did for Marcus in Ch 18.

## Continuity Notes
- The lost rooftop memory is permanent. It pays off at the reunion with Elena (Ch 25) and in the parting (Ch 29).
- This chapter creates everything that is rushed later: the storm unit is only partly calibrated, there are only two proper vehicles plus an improvised third, and Reeves is present during the escape.
- The escape stays in August, as Book 5 requires ("August 2106 – correction facility escape").
- Nikolai now has three POV chapters.
