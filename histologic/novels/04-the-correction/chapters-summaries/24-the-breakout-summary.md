# Chapter 24: The Breakout

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Aug 2, 2106, ~00:30 – 01:20
**Setting:** Wing C, the service tunnels, the data centre
**Length:** ~4,000 words

## Purpose
Action with moral cost, and **the book's central twist (Twist 1)**. Reeves claims the love that saved Marcus was his design. Kira has to keep going while she wonders whether it's true.

## Beats
- **Samuel:** Marcus kneels and tells him, "We're leaving. You can come." Samuel smiles politely, because he doesn't understand the concept. He asks if it's time for his session. Kira has to pull Marcus away. Marcus: "He was the first one I saw. I promised him with my hand." It's his first real grief of the book, and they can't stop for it.
- **Who comes and who can't:** Some general-population inmates follow the triplets. Some are too damaged to understand a door. **Two** Wing C inmates join the group (to be named during drafting). Both die in Ch 26.
- **Down the service tunnels to the data centre.** Kira is in her element: this is Spire-grade architecture, and she helped write its standards. Nikolai strips the drives. Kira breaks the archive encryption *with a key from her own old protocol 7.3*, the one Continuity deleted from the factorepo and then quietly reused. *The stolen fact, put back to work.*
- **As the archive opens, she sees R-2.** Protocol R-2: **SEEDING**. *"Following erasure to baseline, introduce a compatible attachment figure to test formation of genuine belief in an emptied profile. C-2 selected for compatibility with C-1 residual profile. Placement: 7-K (line of sight). Privileges synchronised."* She reads it twice and keeps copying.
- **Dr. Reeves is there.** He never left for the Ministry (Ch 21), and he is calm inside the storm. He says he trained his interface on storms: "Where do you think we learned what a mind can bear?"
- **His revelations:**
  - The border fact storms were **Continuity field trials**. Ivan Volkov, 2471, was early data.
  - Phase Two is already approved at Ministry level, and test carrier signals are *already* running in parts of the broadcast network. The method is **erase, then seed**: remove doubt, then plant loyalty. "You're not escaping a prison, Ms. Osman. You're escaping a *pilot*. The country is the facility."
  - **Twist 1:** "Erasure was never the goal. Anyone can empty a mind. The question was whether something *real* could grow in the empty space. So I stole your fact. I chose your cell. I gave him room. I timed your library hours. And it worked better than anything in the program. Did you think it was *luck*?" He turns to Marcus: "What you feel for her is my best result. It's the proof of Phase Two."
- **The ambiguity is the point.** The file proves Reeves *arranged the conditions*. Nothing can prove he *caused the feeling*. Reeves may be telling the truth, or he may be trying to break his escaping subjects with the one weapon he has left. Kira can't tell. Neither can the reader.
- **Marcus**, shaking, with no counter-argument: "You took my memories. You don't get this too." It sounds like a hope, not a certainty, and he knows it.
- **Dmitri subdues Reeves.** It's controlled, not cruel. A blow and restraints. Tanaka is hurt in the struggle, cut on the right temple by a falling rack: the **scar** from her character file.
- **The copies:** a partial set to Nikolai's partition, the physical drive to Elena (it holds only part of the archive, but it includes R-2), and the rest left for Isaiah to send through Kovač's director channel over the coming days.
- **Isaiah and Kovač stay** in the data centre as the others go. Isaiah: "Go. I'll send everything from here." Kovač begins typing the official record: *cognitive failures*.
- **Moss appears** in the tunnel mouth, dazed, storm-lit and sincere. She sees them and understands. She says, "I'm sorry. I have to save you," and hits the manual alarm. She isn't a villain. She's a believer.

## Ending
At the tunnel exit Marcus looks back at Ashford. Its outline is flickering between a hospital, a prison and an empty field. *He was almost lost here forever.* Kira says, "Come on. We have to go." Then, quieter, the question she will carry all the way to Ch 28: *Was I sent to you?* He takes her hand anyway. They go, and the alarm follows them into the dark.

## Continuity Notes

- Nobody tells Isaiah about R-2 in person. He finds it himself while sending the archive (Ch 27).
- Moss's alarm is what triggers the immediate pursuit in Ch 25.
