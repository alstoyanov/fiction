# Chapter 19: The Alliance Forms

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Mid-June 2106
**Setting:** Maintenance room B-4, 02:00, cameras "down for scheduled maintenance"
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
All seven in one room for the first time, with Kovač and Morrison. Share everything, put the evidence together, and set the goal.

## Beats
- Nikolai schedules a fake maintenance outage. Kovač signs it off. Morrison escorts the "sleep-study subjects". Old Songs keeps watch at the corridor junction, humming the all-clear.
- **The first time all seven are together** in one room, with no glass. Kira sees them properly as people for the first time. Isaiah is taller than she thought. Tanaka is smaller. The triplets are unsettlingly identical until they speak, and then utterly different. Marcus stands next to her, and his hand finds hers without either of them looking.
- **What each brings:**
  - Nikolai: the Continuity files.
  - Isaiah: 2471 and the fact-storm test program.
  - Tanaka and Morrison: the clinical mechanics, and the triple cradle.
  - Kovač: the politics. The Ministry demonstration on Aug 11 is real. Reeves has backing high up (she doesn't know how high). Phase Two would deliver belief through the national broadcast network. "The government wants a weapon: belief on demand. You are the proof of concept."
  - Kira: the technical reading. The broadcast network and the factorepo share infrastructure she helped design. *She helped build the road they plan to use.*
- **Elena's message, relayed through Nikolai's partition.** She has been in contact since April (explain the route here: her network rides the Registry's outbound link). She offers an extraction on the night of Aug 9–10 and a way to crack the facility open: "a storm".
- **The debate:**
  - Alexei: "We escape. Tonight if we could."
  - Marcus, still damaged, but steady: "We *expose* this. If we just run, they'll do it to the next seven."
  - Dmitri: "Exposure without escape is suicide."
  - Isaiah, carefully: "Evidence needs a witness who survives."
  - Kovač: **"You do both. Escape with the evidence."**
- Kira's own moment: she speaks about the fact they stole from her. "They taught me the system could be wrong. Tonight I want to prove it can be *corrected*, by us."

## Ending
The outage has four minutes left. As they file out, Kovač stops Kira: "My nephew. Adrian. Is he…?" Kira: "He lied to me and he loved me. Both." Kovač nods as if that's the most honest answer she's heard in years. Kira realises the director is as trapped as they are.
