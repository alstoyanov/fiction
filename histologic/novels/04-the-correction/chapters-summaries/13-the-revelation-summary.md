# Chapter 13: The Revelation

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Early Mar 2106
**Setting:** The garden, inside the blind arc
**Length:** ~3,000 words

## Purpose
The triplets reveal themselves. Dmitri, the one who argued hardest for silence, is the one who breaks it, which completes his small arc of letting go of control.

## Beats
- Open with the brothers' decision, made the night before through glances, the bond and a single sign. Dmitri insisted it be him: if it goes wrong, the enforcer takes the blame. "It is the only use left for what I was."
- Dmitri's view of Marcus now: better, and dangerously better. His posture is too upright on good days, and his eyes track Kira too openly. Dmitri has seen Reeves's orderlies note it.
- **The approach:** Blind arc. Dmitri kneels at the bed beside Marcus's bench.
  - "We are not corrected. None of us. We have been awake the whole time."
  - Marcus nearly turns and gives them away. Dmitri, without looking: "Do not move. Look at the bench. Good."
  - "The correction failed on us. Three of us. It could not break what three minds hold together."
  - "Sixteen months since they took us. Nine of them here, pretending. Watching."
  - "You were almost erased. You are coming back. We can help you, but you must stop looking at her like that. They are writing it down."
  - "Nikolai reaches the systems. Alexei moves books through the whole building. I work outside. We can carry messages. But you must trust us."
- Marcus, damaged but sharp for a moment: "Why now?"
- Dmitri tells him the one fact that matters: **"August the tenth. They mean to finish all of us. You first."**
- Marcus asks, "How many others?" Dmitri: "Seven of us. All chosen. You'll see."
- The arc is about to close. Dmitri uses his last twenty seconds on something personal. "I hunted my brother for three years and did not know him. I watched you for five months and did not help you. I am sorry." Then he's blank.
- Later, from Dmitri's cell: the triplets' first *argument by glance*. Alexei is triumphant, Nikolai frightened, Dmitri unexpectedly lighter.

## Ending
That night, Marcus's book-slot delivery is a school primer of arithmetic tables. Faint pinpricks under certain numbers spell out one word: **WELCOME.** Across the atrium, Kira holds up her own book to the glass. It has pinpricks too.

## Continuity Notes
- "Sixteen months" counts from the arrest in Nov 2104. "Nine here" counts from the June 2105 transfer to Ashford.
- The pinprick code is Alexei's, set up in Ch 14.
- Mark Dmitri's apology. It closes his arc from Story 03 about seeing and not seeing.
