# Chapter 27: The Recovery

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** ~Aug 31, 2106 (three weeks after the escape)
**Setting:** The border-zone safe house (the old factory)
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
Trauma, not triumph. The love story is confirmed out of shared damage.

## Beats
- **The survivors:** Marcus, Kira, the triplets, Tanaka, Morrison and Old Songs. Elena comes and goes. They're officially dead and actually broken.
- **Scars:** Memory gaps, flashbacks, and doubt about which memories are real. Tanaka has a flinch whenever a door hisses. Morrison can't sleep. Nikolai checks his partition obsessively. Dmitri sleeps in his boots.
- **Kira's own scar,** which she hasn't admitted: she can't write code anymore without reciting it aloud, because the vault technique has become a compulsion. The thing that saved her has changed her. She's frightened to find that she was damaged too.
- **Marcus struggles most.** "They almost erased me. Part of me *is* still erased. I can't remember so much." He has gaps in his memory of Elena, of his parents, of his work, and of who he was before. He sits with his head in his hands again, and this time it's grief, not emptiness.
- He shows Kira the list he's been keeping of things he knows are gone, written as fact entries. Among them is the library conversation (from Ch 18).
- **Kira:** "Then we remember for you. That's what we do now. We remember for each other." She has also been keeping a list: the things she remembers *about him* that he doesn't. She reads it to him.
- **The confession** comes from trauma, not triumph. Two broken people choose each other, knowing what they are. Marcus: "I don't know which parts of me that love you are the old me and which are the new." Kira: "I don't care. They both showed up."
- **The ambiguous recognition, revisited:** Marcus: "In the corridor, I said I knew you. I still don't know how." Kira: "Maybe you didn't know me. Maybe you just decided to." He likes that better.
- Marcus: "I don't know if I can do this. Reform Veridica. I don't even remember who I was." Kira: "Then we'll figure out who you are now. Together."
- **Old Songs,** that evening, sings the lullaby from Ch 11 to the whole room. Marcus sings the second verse, which he didn't know he knew.

## Ending
Late at night Kira watches Marcus sleep and does something she hasn't done since the Spire: she enters a fact. It isn't for any factorepo, only for herself, spoken aloud in a whisper: *"Kira Osman. August thirty-first, 2106. Border zone. With him."* No system will verify it. She knows it's true.
