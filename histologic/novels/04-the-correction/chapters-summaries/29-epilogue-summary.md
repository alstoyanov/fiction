# Epilogue: Seven Paths, One Truth

**POV:** Rotating short vignettes, ending with Marcus
**Date:** Nov 14, 2106 (three months after the escape)
**Length:** ~2,500 words

## Purpose
Show where everyone is, the cost carried forward, and a direct handoff to *The Lost Hour* (Nov 15, 14:00–15:00).

## Vignettes
- **Isaiah:** Still in maximum security. His messages still get out somehow. That evening he's given an official notice: *"Expulsion from the territory of Veridica. Nov 15, 14:30. Border transfer."* No one will say which border or why. (Kovač has arranged it quietly.) Isaiah sews his notes into the lining of his coat. *He'll cross tomorrow with documents he can't prove he legally carried.*
- **Dr. Tanaka in Chronos:** Working inside the medical system, fighting to stop correction being adopted there. Tomorrow a group of defectors will cross with her help.
- **Alexei and Dmitri with the Resistance:** In a mobile command post with Elena, planning an extraction for tomorrow afternoon. They argue like brothers, about who goes first.
- **Nikolai in Veridica:** Under a new name, working as a mid-level factorepo systems contractor. Each day is a risk. He notices something odd in tomorrow's maintenance schedule: a large block of system activity booked for **14:00–15:00**, with no owner. He flags it to Elena.
- **Director Kovač:** Still at Ashford and still under inquiry, walking the empty glass corridor of Wing C. Samuel Ferris is still in 7-G. She sits outside his cell for a while.
- **Marcus and Kira in the border zone:** Documenting, gathering evidence, barely surviving. Marcus has a flashback, the white room and the cradle, and Kira talks him through it with a fact entry.

## Final Scene
- A message from Elena: *"Tomorrow. 14:00 to 15:00. Everything happens in that hour. Be ready."* Marcus is to upload the Continuity evidence to secure servers during the window.
- Marcus's fear: "What if I document it and then can't remember? What if correction took that from me too?"
- Kira: "Then I'll remember. We'll remember together."

## Final Line
*"Tomorrow, history will be made. And even if they erase it, even if they try to correct it, we'll remember. We survived erasure once. We'll survive it again."*

## Continuity Notes
- Every vignette maps onto a Book 5 operation during the Lost Hour: Marcus's upload, Isaiah's crossing, Tanaka's defectors, the extraction, and Nikolai's monitoring.
- "Seven paths": Isaiah, Tanaka, Alexei, Dmitri, Nikolai, Marcus and Kira. Plus Kovač's vignette as a coda.
