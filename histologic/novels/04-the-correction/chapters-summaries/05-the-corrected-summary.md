# Chapter 5: The Corrected

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Dec 2105 (overlaps Ch 4). Thirteen months since the arrest.
**Setting:** The Ashford garden (winter), the escorted work detail, Cell 7-A
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
Reveal that the "cured" triplets are fully awake. Give their history since *The Divided Truth* from Dmitri's disciplined point of view. Pose the question of whether to reveal themselves.

## Beats
- Dmitri rakes frozen beds in the small walled garden. His face is blank and his movements economical: perfect compliance. Inside, his mind is counting cameras, guard rotations and the four-minute blind arc of the east pole camera. He was an enforcer, and he still is one.
- **Recap woven through memory (no info-dump):** The border facility. Six months of white rooms. The moment the technicians realized the three brothers answered the machine *as one*. Isolation that didn't work. The director's verdict: "correction failures, permanent detention". Captain Sokolov's lingering look at the monitors.
- **Then something new:** In June 2105 they were suddenly transferred to Ashford. The triangle of cells was rebuilt *on purpose*. The doctors here don't want to correct them. They want to understand *why they can't be corrected*. Continuity. Dmitri doesn't know the word yet, only that they are being studied.
- The privileges came in stages: garden (Dmitri), library (Alexei), technical maintenance (Nikolai). They are rewards for perfect compliance, and they are how the brothers see the whole facility.
- The bond: he doesn't "hear" his brothers, and there is nothing supernatural about it. He *knows*: Alexei's impatience like a pressure behind the eyes, Nikolai's fear like cold hands. It is what kept them whole.
- **Captain Sokolov's monthly visit.** She reviews "her" file through the garden glass. She looks at Dmitri a beat too long. He gives her nothing. It is the hardest acting he has ever done.
- He watched the broken man in 7-H go under over two months, and thinks: *They're finishing him.* He thinks of the new woman in 7-K, who is still fighting and doesn't yet know she's being watched by the watchers.
- **The disagreement:** Their rule has been to survive, wait and trust no one. Dmitri still thinks revealing themselves would kill all three brothers. Through the bond he senses that Alexei has already broken the rule (the book in Ch 4) and is furious and afraid.

## Ending
That night, Dmitri watches Kira's cell. She is holding a book to the light. He closes his eyes and sends the only thing the bond can carry to his brother: *What have you done?* Across the atrium, Alexei doesn't look up, but his hand on the bed forms their childhood signal: *Promise.* (Their mother's last words: "Save each other.")

- The bond is framed as uncanny but grounded: shared patterns, not telepathy.
- The east pole camera's blind arc pays off in Ch 11 and Ch 13.
