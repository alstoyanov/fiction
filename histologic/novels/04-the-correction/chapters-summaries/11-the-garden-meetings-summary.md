# Chapter 11: The Garden Meetings

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Late Jan – Feb 2106
**Setting:** The walled garden (winter giving way to early spring), the white room
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Slow recovery, with gaps. The four non-triplet subjects form a secret circle. Marcus learns to lie to the machine. The love story begins, fragile and unequal.

## Beats
- Marcus is given garden privileges: an hour, three times a week, with Kira, Tanaka, Isaiah and a few general-population inmates. The gardener (Dmitri) works the beds, blank-faced.
- **The system:** They sit on separate benches within whispering distance, faces angled away from the east pole camera. They speak only during its four-minute blind arc. (Kira worked out the arc. Dmitri, unseen, has been making sure she could.)
- **Tanaka explains** what was done to him, clinically and kindly: "They tried to erase you. They got close to what I call the floor. But you're still here. Salience maps can be rebuilt. Not all of them." She warns him that some things won't come back.
- **Isaiah shares his census:** "Seven of us. All kept. Someone's calling us by numbers. I'm C-7." Marcus struggles to follow. The idea of a *pattern* excites the historian in him and exhausts the patient.
- **The gaps:** Marcus can't remember his mother's first name. He remembers Elena's words but not her voice. He remembers *that* he believed, and not what belief felt like. Kira fills in what she can from the news and from what he tells her, and writes it back into him in the whispered minutes.
- **Old Songs** hums near the garden gate, the same tune from Ch 1. This time it *lands*: suddenly, whole, Marcus remembers his mother singing a pre-Histologic lullaby she wasn't supposed to know. It's the first memory to come back with feeling in it. Tanaka's eyes sharpen: *music survived*.
- **The white room, reframed:** Using Kira's rule, Marcus learns to stock the front of his mind with **fact-entry formats**, thousands of them, the ritual of his old job ("Traffic incident, corner of Fifth and Main, fourteen-oh-two…"). The machine skims them. His first successful lie: Reeves calls him "flawless". He goes back to his cell and shakes with the knowledge that he fooled it.
- **Falling:** He is falling for Kira, and he knows it's tangled up with need. She is his anchor to reality. He's damaged and he knows it. When he tells her ("I don't know if what I feel is real or if I just need it"), she says she knew someone who told her both things at once, and they were both true.

## Ending
In the blind arc, the gardener kneels to reset a stone border right beside Marcus's bench. Without looking up, without moving his lips much, he says four words: **"You're doing well. Careful."** Then the arc closes and he's blank again. Marcus stares at the man he thought was the most corrected person in Ashford.

- The tune and the lullaby prefigure the Ch 14 technique and are a callback in Ch 27.
- Dmitri's four words are a teaser. The full reveal is Ch 13.
