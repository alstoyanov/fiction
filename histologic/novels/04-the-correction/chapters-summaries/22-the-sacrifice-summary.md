# Chapter 22: The Sacrifice

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Jul 21–31, 2106
**Setting:** The garden, maintenance room B-4 (last night meeting), Cell 7-E
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
Isaiah chooses to stay. The rushed plan turns his choice from noble into *necessary*, and the book gets its voice after the storm.

## Beats
- **Opening image (moved from Ch 20):** Isaiah sets a final stone in Dmitri's garden layout, a circle of seven with one stone apart. Dmitri sees it and understands.
- **The rush changes the arithmetic.** With ten days instead of three weeks, there's no time to copy the whole Continuity archive out safely before the storm. The raw archive in the data centre is too large for the physical drive Elena can supply. Someone has to stay at a terminal *after* the others are gone and send the rest out through Kovač's channel over days.
- **Isaiah lays out his reasoning like an editorial:**
  - If six escape and the evidence leaves with them, Veridica calls it outlaw forgery and hunts them.
  - If it's published **from inside**, by a subject, through the Director's own channel, it's a *witness account*, and it can't be traced to the six "dead".
  - Someone has to finish sending, and to make sure the evidence gets out if the escape fails.
  - "I'm a journalist. That's the job: stay where the story is."
- **Protests:**
  - Alexei: "We don't leave people. That's the whole point of me."
  - Tanaka: "They will put you in maximum security. You know what the cradles are like there."
  - **Marcus**, struggling for words but clear: "We don't leave people behind." (He is thinking of Samuel.)
  - Isaiah to Marcus, gently: "You're not leaving me. I'm staying by choice. They *took* your choice. I'm *making* mine. And you need to heal. You can't do that here."
- Nikolai, still shaken from Ch 21, is the last to protest and the first to accept: "Somebody stays in the middle. I've always been that. For once let it be someone who chose it."
- Kovač will stay too, as Director, and cover the story: storm, chaos, cognitive failures, one surviving subject.
- **It's decided:** six subjects escape, with Old Songs and Morrison, and one remains. Everyone has a part. The plan is cut down to what can be done in ten days, and everyone can see the gaps.
- **Late that night, in 7-E:** Isaiah's memory palace. He walks through the house for what may be the last time before maximum security, and moves everything into the cellar. He composes the first sentence of his article. He's afraid, and he lets himself be.

## Ending
The book-slot opens. It is Old Songs, humming a tune Isaiah doesn't know. The old man leans in and whispers, his longest speech in the book: *"The one who stays is the one who remembers. That was always my job. Now it's yours."*
