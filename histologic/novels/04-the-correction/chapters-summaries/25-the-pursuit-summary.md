# Chapter 25: The Pursuit

**POV:** Alexei Volkov
**Date:** Aug 10, 2106, ~02:00 – dawn
**Setting:** Highways east of the capital, then the fog of the border zone, a checkpoint, a safe house
**Length:** ~3,600 words

## Purpose
The chase. Alexei, The Phantom, is home in the border zone. The fact storm that was a weapon is now terrain. The price is paid in lives.

## Beats
- Alexei drives the lead vehicle with Elena navigating. He knows these roads: the Phantom's routes, the jurisdictions that flicker, the places the maps disagree.
- **Pursuit:** Border Security vehicles, and a drone overhead for a while. As they enter the border zone, **natural (manufactured) storms** are running. Streets bend between Veridica's and Chronos's histories.
- Pursuit drivers crash when the road flickers into a building. One pursuer's vehicle simply stops, its driver screaming at a history he can't hold.
- **Inside the cars:** The triplets keep each other coherent by calling out their childhood rooftop, like a chant. Marcus struggles as the storm gets into his gaps again. Kira keeps him grounded: "Look at me. Stay with me." She uses the fact-entry trick again. Tanaka, her temple bandaged, treats the injured in the back.
- **The losses:** The third vehicle, carrying the two Wing C escapees, takes the wrong fork at a moment when the storm shows two roads. It goes over an embankment that exists in only one history. Both are killed. Alexei sees it in the mirror and can't turn back.
- **The checkpoint:** The same kind of fog-bound checkpoint as Story 03's ending. **Captain Yevgenia Sokolov** is there in person. Her floodlights catch the lead vehicle. For a long moment, through the fog and the windscreen, **she looks at Dmitri's face**, a dead man's face according to the Registry.
  - Her motives are deliberately double. She knows the stolen storm unit came from *her* program, so arresting them would put her program on trial. And she remembers the monitors: "They really loved each other." *"Or the solution."*
  - She radios: *"No contact. Checkpoint clear."* She lowers the barrier.
  - Alexei drives through. Dmitri whispers, "She let us go." Alexei: "She let *herself* go. We were just in the way."
- **Dawn at the safe house**, an old factory in contested jurisdiction, like Story 03. Head count: two missing. Old Songs sits by the window and sings, quietly, for the two who didn't make it. It's the first time the others hear him sing, not hum.

## Ending
Alexei stands in the doorway as the fog lifts. He helped people cross this zone for years and always told them the cost. *Now he knows it.* He goes inside to his brothers, and for the first time since they were twelve, the three of them fall asleep in the same room.
