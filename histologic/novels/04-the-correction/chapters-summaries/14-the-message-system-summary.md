# Chapter 14: The Message System

**POV:** Alexei Volkov
**Date:** Mar 2106
**Setting:** Library, trolley routes, garden, cells: the whole facility as a network
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
Build the secret communication network, and teach the others (and the reader) *how to survive correction*. The book's methods chapter, written as the Phantom's joy at running a network again.

## Beats
- **The network:**
  - *Books (Alexei):* Pinpricks under letters, keyed to the page number (a simple rotating substitution). Book choice is a signal in itself: arithmetic means "code inside", poetry means "no news, hold".
  - *Routes (Nikolai):* He adjusts maintenance schedules so the carts pass the right cells at the right times.
  - *Placement (Dmitri):* Stones and plant markers in the garden give times and "danger" flags, readable from the benches.
  - *Sound (Old Songs):* Humming at the meal slot. One tune means yes, another means no, silence means "they're watching".
- **Kira cracks it first**, in a day. She sends back a corrected key, because Alexei's has a statistical weakness. He laughs out loud in the library and has to turn it into a cough. She also, finally, learns who sent page 47.
- Tanaka decodes next, then Isaiah (who at once turns it into a filing system). Marcus is slowest; Kira teaches him in the garden, and he's proud of every word.
- **All seven are now communicating.**
- **The survival teachings**, sent in fragments by code:
  - How the interface works: it follows emotional salience and the associations around it.
  - The blind spot: rote, rhythmic and procedural material is skimmed. So "*hide what you love in what you've memorised*" (Old Songs' rule).
  - Everyone's personal vault: Kira uses protocol specs, Isaiah the rooms of his palace, Tanaka surgical checklists, and Marcus fact-entry formats, with **Kira** folded into them line by line.
  - How to look flattened: slowed responses, a lag before the eyes move, deliberately forgetting small tasks. (The triplets learned this from watching real flattened patients. Tanaka adds clinical precision.)
  - Rule: never all look up at once.
- Alexei, through code: *"We survived because we were three. You can survive because you are seven."*
- The cost, shown in one near-miss: an orderly picks up a coded book and flips through it. Old Songs "drops" the trolley, spilling soup. The orderly curses, and the book is forgotten.

## Ending
In the library that night, Alexei holds a book that has passed through all seven sets of hands. It's the same image as Story 03's epilogue, but no longer something he can't explain: *"he felt other hands touching the same book."* He isn't imagining it now. He decides it is time to tell them where the brothers came from and what it cost.

## Continuity Notes
