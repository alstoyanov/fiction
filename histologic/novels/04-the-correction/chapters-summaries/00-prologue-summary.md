# Prologue: The Conviction

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Night of Oct 7 to morning of Oct 8, 2105
**Setting:** Enforcement Center holding chamber, then the transport, then arrival at Ashford
**Length:** ~2,500 words

## Purpose
Pick up exactly where *The Believer's Fall* ends, without retelling it. Move Marcus from "correction is healing" to the first sight of what Ashford really is. Plant the warnings.

## Beats
- The door opens and Dr. Moss comes in with "her instruments of mercy". This is the last line of Story 01, replayed. It turns out to be an **assessment**, not a correction: a band of sensors, a list of words (*Elena. Veridica. Doubt. Father.*), and his reactions recorded.
- Moss is kind and sincere. She tells him his case is "of interest", and that he is lucky, because he will go somewhere with "better resources".
- Alone, Marcus replays the Judge's figure: **seventy-three percent**. He tests himself on whether he believes, and finds that he does. The irony is the heart of the prologue. His faith is intact, and it is exactly what they want.
- Through the door he overhears Moss on a call: *"Yes, C-1 profile confirmed. Transfer to Ashford, Wing C. Dr. Reeves will want him before the end of the week."* He doesn't understand it.
- Dawn transport. A second enforcer (not Kade) reads the transfer order and goes quiet. "Ashford? What did you *do*?" Ominous warning #1.
- The van passes the Factorepo Spire and the Justice Center. Marcus still finds the ordered city beautiful.
- Ashford from outside: white, low and landscaped, like a hospital or a university. It reassures him.
- **Intake with Supervisor Hayes.** She is sympathetic and tired, and won't answer questions. "Wing C is… specialised. Do what the doctors ask." Ominous warning #2. When he asks how long, she looks away.
- The corridor to Wing C passes the ordinary wards. Through observation glass, inmates in gray, some reading, some talking. Then a double door, a keycard, and silence.
- **Final image:** A corridor of glass cells, lit like an aquarium. Faces turn toward him. Some are alert. Many are blank in a way that makes his stomach drop, calm the way a switched-off screen is calm. Three identical men sit in three cells arranged in a triangle, and not one of them looks at him.

## Last Line (target)
Marcus had believed his whole life that everything in Veridica was known. Walking into Wing C, he understood for the first time that some things were only *kept*.

## Continuity Notes
- Moss appears in Stories 01–02. She works at Ashford and comes to the Enforcement Center for priority assessments.
- Keep the story's own vocabulary: "contamination", "corrective custody", "the facts are the facts".
- Samuel Ferris, in the cell next to Marcus (7-G), is first seen here: a blank face at the glass.
