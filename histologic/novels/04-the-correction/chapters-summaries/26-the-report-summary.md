# Chapter 26: The Report

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo, as his published article, with short interleaved present-tense fragments from his cell
**Date:** Article published Aug 13, 2106. Fragments Aug 10–20.
**Setting:** The text of the exposé, and a maximum-security cell
**Length:** ~3,000 words

## Purpose
Change the scale from escape to scandal. The story leaves Ashford and enters the world. Isaiah's voice at its best.

## Form
The article is presented in full, a long-form piece titled something like **"Continuity: A Witness Account from Wing C"**. Between its sections come short fragments of Isaiah in maximum security: the new cradle, a smaller cell, the cellar of his palace.

## Article Beats
- Opening line (composed in Ch 21): a restrained, devastating first sentence about seven people who were chosen.
- **Project Continuity exposed:** its purpose (genuine belief across all mind types), its subjects C-1 to C-7, and Phase Two (delivery through the national broadcast network).
- **The evidence:** excerpts of the files. The **C-1 index adjustment (0.58 to 0.73)**. Kira's "record anomaly". The triple-cradle protocol, with its note that "both outcomes are informative".
- **Test Subject 2471**, named: Ivan Volkov. The border storms as field trials.
- **Marcus Chen as the prime example,** in Isaiah's words: *"nearly erased, recovered only through human connection."* He describes the man at the glass with his head in his hands, and then the man who learned to lift it.
- **The fact storm of Aug 9–10**, the first ever inside Veridica, which the government is calling an "infrastructure malfunction".
- **The official record:** six subjects (and others) logged as *deceased: cognitive failure during fact storm*. Isaiah reports this deadpan, exactly as the Registry states it, with no comment. Readers who can do arithmetic will notice he is the only subject counted as alive. (He protects the escapees without lying.)
- The closing paragraph: the witness remains inside so that the record cannot say he fled.

## Fragment Beats
- The piece goes out through Kovač's director channel, routed via Synthesis relays (Isaiah's international contacts). It is picked up by Synthesis and Chronos media within hours. It's an international scandal.
- Veridica calls it fabrication. The Ministry demonstration is "postponed". Reeves is not seen publicly.
- Isaiah is moved to **maximum security** the same day. His fate is unknown to the outside world.
- Kovač is under internal inquiry. She survives for now, because anyone who wants her gone would have to explain in public what she knows.
- In the new cradle, Isaiah goes down into his cellar and finds everything still there.

## Ending
A guard slides a tray through the slot. Under the bread is a scrap of paper in pinpricks, in a code only seven people know: *We read it. We're alive. —K.* Isaiah eats the paper. Then he laughs for the first time in ten months.

## Continuity Notes
- Isaiah's international contacts point toward his Book 5 role (border crossing and asylum).
- Leave open exactly *how* the note reached him. It hints that Kovač or Nikolai still has reach inside.
