# Chapter 23: The Fact Storm

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Aug 1, 2106, 23:40 – Aug 2, ~00:30
**Setting:** Wing C, then the corridors of Ashford, as reality comes apart
**Length:** ~3,800 words

## Purpose
The horror set piece. The first fact storm ever to hit the interior of Veridica, and the most vulnerable mind in the building has to get through it. It also plants Twist 2: the storm leaves something behind in him.

## Beats
- **23:40:** Waiting in 7-H. Marcus runs the escape as a list of fact entries. Everyone knows the plan is cut down, the storm unit only partly calibrated, and Reeves somewhere in the building preparing for tomorrow's integration. Old Songs' humming comes through the slot: the signal. Across the atrium, Kira puts her palm to the glass. He does too, the way he did with Samuel on the first night.
- **23:47: The storm hits.** It is harder and less even than planned, because the calibration was rushed. A pressure change and a sudden wrongness (Story 03's language). Then every interface in Ashford receives contradictory histories of *this building*:
  - *Ashford: a hospital, founded 2081.*
  - *Ashford: a prison, founded 2079.*
  - *Ashford: a field. Nothing was ever built here.*
- The walls flicker. For whole seconds, Marcus's cell *isn't there*: he's standing in wet grass under stars. Then the white is back.
- **The staff panic:** An orderly screams that he's never worked here. Hayes sits down in the corridor and weeps, because the storm has handed her a version of her life in which she was a nurse, and she wants it.
- **Morrison triggers the lockdown release.** The glass doors hiss open along the whole wing.
- **Marcus's particular horror:** His gaps are where the storm gets in. It *fills the blanks* with false memories, and they're warm. A version where Elena really loved him. A version where his mother is alive. A version where he never reported anyone, and the café at 17:43 went on for years.
- **The one that stays (Twist 2 seed):** In the "field" history there is no building, only grass and stars. For a stretch that feels like hours, he is *lying in that grass with Kira*, talking quietly, in no hurry, free. It's the most peaceful memory he has ever had. In reality it lasts a few seconds, while Kira is dragging him down a corridor. Write it so that the reader, too, half-believes it happened.
- **Kira reaches him.** "Focus on me. Focus on what's real." He can't tell which Kira is real, because the storm has offered him three of her. She does the one thing the storm can't fake: she recites, flatly, the vault entry he built for her in May and coded to her on the cart. **"Personnel entry. Osman, K. Location: here. Status: with you."** It's boring and procedural and true. He grabs it.
- **The triplets move through the corridor together,** calling out one another's names in rhythm, anchored and coherent. Nikolai falters: the storm finds the hole where the rooftop was. His brothers close in on either side of him. People instinctively follow the three of them.
- Isaiah stands in the atrium in his open cell doorway. He isn't escaping. He is **documenting**, saying what he sees aloud into his memory palace, room by room.
- Tanaka works like a field medic, pulling general-population inmates out of seizure postures.

## Ending
Marcus comes out of 7-H for the last time. At 7-G, Samuel Ferris is sitting calmly on his bed while the walls flicker, completely untouched. The storm has nothing to show a mind that has nothing left. Marcus stops. Kira pulls at his arm: "Marcus, the data centre, now." He doesn't move. *Five seconds.* Then he steps into Samuel's cell.

## Continuity Notes
- The false "field" memory pays off in Ch 28 (Twist 2).
- The interior storm is politically enormous. It is referenced in Ch 27 and later books ("fact storms reached the capital").
- Keep the physics: it is perception flickering through the interface, not reality itself (Story 03).
