# Chapter 2: The White Room

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Oct 9, 2105
**Setting:** Continuity treatment room C-2
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
Marcus's first correction session. Introduce Dr. Reeves, Dr. Morrison and the mechanics of the interface. The horror is mental violation, not physical pain.

## Beats
- A white room with a white reclining chair. A neural cradle fits over his head and connects to the interface he has carried since childhood. Present are **Dr. Moss** (who holds his hand and means it), **Dr. Morrison** (who runs the console and doesn't meet his eyes), and a voice from behind mirrored glass: **Dr. Reeves**, calm and pleasant, never raising his tone.
- Reeves explains, courteously: "We're going to help you see clearly. You'll see your memories as we see them. Don't try to hide. There is nowhere to hide." He uses Story 03's ritual phrasing ("State your name. State your crime.") and extends it: *"Do you believe?"* Marcus says yes. Reeves says, "I know. That's why you're here."
- **The process:** Memories surface as though projected inside his skull. The first café with Elena, 17:43. Her hand on his arm. The dinner by the river. Each one is *examined*: Marcus feels it lifted, turned and judged, as if a stranger were reading his diary aloud.
- The interface homes in on emotional heat. It skips the fact entries and routine days and pulls hard at **Elena**, at the moment he felt doubt ("so why did he feel like he'd made a terrible mistake?"), and at his parents. (Plant the blind spot quietly: the dull memories pass unnoticed.)
- The voice: **"These memories are corrupted. They must be corrected."**
- The worst moment: Elena's line, "When they come for you too, remember this conversation", is played back to him. Then it plays again, *slightly wrong*, with a different inflection. Then a third time, with words missing. He cannot tell which version was real.
- Marcus tries to resist and doesn't know how. Clenching doesn't work. Arguing doesn't work. Belief doesn't work, because the machine isn't arguing with him.
- Morrison's hands shake once on the console. Moss notices and frowns.
- Back in his cell he is disoriented and nauseous, and vomits into the steel sink. He curls on the bed with his head in his hands.

## Ending
He tries to picture Elena's face and gets it, but it looks *filed*, like a photo in a record. He realizes this will happen again, and again, until there's nothing left to file. Across the atrium, all three triplets are awake and facing the same direction, toward him.

## Continuity Notes
- The phrase "These memories are corrupted. They must be corrected." echoes Story 03 on purpose.
- Reeves is only a voice for most of the book. His face is first seen clearly in Ch 18 or Ch 23.
- Establish the rhythm: intensive sessions every two to three days during Marcus's first two months.
