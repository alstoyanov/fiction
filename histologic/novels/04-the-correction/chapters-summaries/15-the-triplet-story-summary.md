# Chapter 15: The Triplet Story

**POV:** Nikolai Volkov (framing present, told in flashback fragments "sent through the code")
**Date:** Late Mar – mid-Apr 2106
**Setting:** Cell 7-C, the Registry partition, and memory: the border facility and the Volkov apartment
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
Tell the triplets' story to the others: why the bond resists, and what their father's fate really was. Then bring Elena back into the book.

## Beats
- **Frame:** Nikolai writes their story in pinpricks, night after night. Writing it is also restoring it; he takes each fragment from his hidden partition.
- **Fragments (don't re-narrate Story 03; refract it):**
  - Arrested together after their mother died in the storm. The triangle of cells at the border facility. Unable to speak, watching each other suffer: the torture of proximity.
  - Six months of correction. *When they corrected Dmitri, Alexei and Nikolai felt it. When Alexei resisted, the others' patterns synchronised.* Three parts of one consciousness.
  - Isolation, higher intensity, separation. Nothing worked. The bond went beyond distance.
  - Declared "correction failures". Sokolov: "They really loved each other." The choice between sedation and detention.
  - Nikolai's partition: hiding their true memories in the system, one byte at a time.
  - June 2105: the transfer to Ashford, and the triangle rebuilt. *They want to learn how to break a bond.*
- **Isaiah answers through the code:** He knows the number **2471**. He tells them their father was a test subject. He was killed, not by accident, and "lethal threshold confirmed". Alexei already knew (Story 03), but Nikolai finds something even Alexei didn't know.
- **Nikolai's new discovery in the Registry:** An old correction record for **Ivan Volkov**, two years before his death. Papa was *corrected*. The quiet, un-laughing father of their teenage years was not grief or overwork. The boys never knew. **They are living their father's fate, and resisting it where he couldn't.**
- The record lists his next of kin: wife Sofya, and a sister, **Elena**.
- **Mid-April:** A message appears inside Nikolai's own hidden partition, which should be impossible. It is signed with the Volkov childhood phrase: *"Who was born first?"* It's from **Aunt Elena**. She is out, she knows where they are, and she's coming.

## Ending
The chapter's last coded page reaches every cell: *Our aunt Elena is alive and she is outside. She was the one who warned us Papa had changed. She never told us why.* In 7-H, Marcus reads the name. A fragment of his damaged memory surfaces clearly: a woman on a couch saying **"my brother questioned a minor discrepancy… when he came back, he wasn't my brother anymore."** He understands who Elena is, and whose brother that was.
