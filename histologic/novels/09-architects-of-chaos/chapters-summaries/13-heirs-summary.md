# Chapter 13: Heirs

**POV:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka
**Date:** Mon May 13, 2115 (about 08:00 to 22:00)
**Setting:** The teaching hospital in the Chronos lakeshore city: Tanaka's fourth-floor office, shared with Morrison; the storm-trauma ward; the linen room; her flat at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Two physicians find that the hands they used to heal and to harm were, on paper, family business. Tanaka does what she has always done with something she can't bear: she writes it up as a case. Her question isn't whether blood is destiny. It is whether a clause can make a choice. It can't, and she writes that down.

## Beats
- **Case note, 08:00,** in italics: *Subject: Tanaka, Y. Relation: great-niece of MT·06, Director Michiko Tanaka (the Technologist; the Implementation Annex). Deceased 2092.* She has carried p. 39 in her case book for three weeks, beside the **copy X page, p. 19**, which she has kept since Book 8.
- **Great-Aunt Michiko,** remembered exactly. Tanaka met her four times. She was a small, upright woman at a New Year table who asked the child Yuki what she would be, and said "Good" to "a doctor". When Yuki qualified, a card came: *Minds are the only instruments that matter.* Tanaka still has it. She reads it again against the Annex: *"belief delivery at population scale."* It is the same hand's idea, fifty years apart. *Kinship: established. Influence: unknown.* She leaves the line open.
- **09:30, Morrison.** He comes in with the ward list, sees her face, and sits. She tells him as she tells patients: she asks permission first, and then tells the truth. *"James. There is a name on the page. May I?"* **JM·08, Colonel Jerome Morrison**, author of Principle Five: the calibration grounds, *"where a population's tolerance for contradiction may be measured, and raised."*
- **His hands shake,** for the first time since Book 8's linen room. By his own rule, if they shake it means something. *"He was my grandfather. My father didn't speak to him."*
- **"Was I prepared?"** Morrison lists it the way he lists faults, in a low voice with his eyes on the floor:
  - he designed the **gain calibration for Continuity's cradles** (Book 4);
  - he recognised it on a broadcast node in 2106 (Book 5);
  - he recognised it in Chronos's stabilisation crates in 2108 (Book 6);
  - he recognised it on the national network in 2110 (Book 8).
  Four times it was his work, and every time it served *his grandfather's page*. His first post after training was a calibration unit at the border facility, offered to him **before he had applied**. *"Did somebody put me there?"* She doesn't know, and she says so.
- **The ward.** She does her rounds. There are storm-trauma patients, fewer every year since the Judges ended. The work steadies her.
- **The foundation (light).** At noon Morrison hands her a letter he received in March and never answered. It is on the letterhead of **the Liu Wei Endowment**, in Synthesis. It invites him to a funded fellowship "on the reconciliation of national records". Tanaka reads the founder's name at the foot: *Chairman Liu Wei*, **LW·03**. It is a dead man's foundation, still writing letters. *Heir: possibly not a person.* She files it unlabelled, in the place where she keeps things.
- **The linen room, 16:00.** This is where she read Principle Four aloud in 2110. She lays p. 19 of copy X, *"Correction is compassion. Certainty is care,"* beside p. 39. She and Morrison read the Register once more together. The Annex clause: *"Each contributor shall prepare an heir."* Her question is clinical: does a clause produce an heir, or only the conditions for one? Kira's old rule from R-2 answers it: **the conditions can be arranged. The choice can't.**
- **Her own line.** She refused the Continuity trials in 2105, when Reeves asked. She writes that fact down too, and doesn't call it virtue.

## Ending
Late afternoon in the office, with the light going. Morrison, with his hands flat on the desk to still them: ***"He never met me. He died when I was fourteen."*** Tanaka: ***"Then the clause failed with you."*** That night, in her flat, the last case note: ***Case note: heir, declined.***
