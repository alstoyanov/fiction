# Chapter 21: The Peace Everyone Wants

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Jul 15, 2115 (07:00 to about 21:00; the publication at 14:00)
**Setting:** The First Minister's office (morning); the central plaza and the Lost Hour stone on the fifteenth (14:00); Marcus and Kira's flat, in front of the evening broadcast
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The day they meant to reveal everything, and everything has already been revealed. The World Record spreads like relief, and the people Marcus trusts are leaning toward it. The heroes publish anyway, by hand, as planned. What would have been a revelation is now a confirmation. Then the man whose mark is on page 39 goes on the air and thanks them.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 07:00:** *Jul 15, 2115. Home. Publication: 14:00, as planned. Status: redundant?* He files the question mark under UNCHECKED. He is 37. Tamsin is two and asks for the "boat song". He doesn't have it.
- **A week of relief.** Since Jul 8 the Register has been in every paper, and so has the remedy. So has **Chen Zhao's confession** of that afternoon (Ch 20), and it has made him more trusted, not less. The papers call him "the Architect who stayed". The papers are gentle about the Architects and warm about the World Record: *no nation can ever be written by another again.* Letters pages, radio call-ins and the journal networks all show it. People are ashamed of the design and grateful for the repair, both at once. The Commission's own proposal for an advisory **Counselor** (Ch 1) is being described as "Veridica's natural seat" in the World Record. One paper asks who will chair the World Record, and names nobody. At the Commission's sitting last Monday morning, before 14:00, the deputy chair at the Minister's right stood empty, as it has since Aug 2110 (Ch 1). Marcus notices that he noticed, and files nothing.
- **09:00, the First Minister's office.** Marcus is called in as the Commission's dissenting member. **Maitland** is tired and practical, and inclined: *"It's the first thing in five years anyone has offered me that isn't a hole to fill."* He doesn't forbid the publication. *"Publish. It can't hurt now."* He doesn't know how true that is.
- **Martinez, by link.** She will keep her word: the SFW's town assemblies read the pages aloud at 14:00. She is also inclined to the World Record, and she says so plainly. She has met the Synthesis envoy (Ch 18). *"I have signed four hundred letters, Marcus. If they can guarantee I never sign another, I have to listen."*
- **Chronos's council**, by Kovač's report, is **divided**. One side wants peace. The other remembers that Chronos was **written once by another nation**, in October 2109 (Book 7), and doesn't want to be written again by anyone. Julian holds no seat and says nothing publicly, but the six paper keepers will read at 14:00.
- **Marcus's lists.** Under HOPES he has nothing new. Under UNCHECKED: *World Record: who holds the reference source?* Kira has been inside the Schedule for a week and says only "not yet". She doesn't guess.
- **14:00, the stone.** It is the fifteenth, as it has been every month for eight years. The names are cut in the stone's side, **Tanaka** is there with her notebook, and the crowd is some thousands. The hand-copied sets go out from folding tables. Copyists sit beside their own sets, with their countersigners. Isaiah's facsimile run is stacked in bundles. At the same hour, by Kovač's list, the pages are read aloud or handed out in Chronos, in the SFW's assemblies, in the small circles in Memoria, Axiom and Synthesis, and, by Elena's word, forty sets start walking east.
- **What people do with them.** They **compare**. They hold the hand copy beside the Synthesis facsimile from last week and find that the two agree. Some nod, some thank the copyists, and many go home early. It's a confirmation. Only **Kerr's page** is new, and Marcus watches a few people read it twice: *"Keep it by many hands."* A woman asks him what it has to do with the World Record. He starts a fact entry and can't finish it.
- **The copy numerals.** The hand copies were made from photographs that show the Compiler's copy's blank corner, and the heirs' facsimiles are cropped. Marcus notices it, files it, and doesn't follow it.
- **Evening, the flat.** Kira, Tamsin asleep. The 20:00 broadcast.

## Ending
**Chen Zhao** on the national broadcast. He is silver-haired and grave, with tea in a glass on the desk beside him. He speaks in short, gentle sentences. He has been a confessed Architect for a week (Ch 20) and is still Minister. He repeats it without flinching: *"I have never hidden from a page."* He thanks ***"our commissioners' diligence"*** for placing the pages in every hand today, and then, warmly, he **endorses the World Record**. Marcus looks at the man on the screen, whose own mark is on page 39, and writes a fact entry: *Jul 15, 2115. 20:04. National broadcast. Chen Zhao, CZ·11: endorses the remedy to his own principle.* It is the shortest entry he has written all year.
