# Chapter 16: RA·12

**POV:** Nikolai Volkov
**Date:** Mon Jun 10, 2115 (about 06:00 to 23:00)
**Setting:** The back room of Nikolai and Maya's flat in the capital, where the open checking tools run; the farmhouse kitchen at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 2.** The one name on p. 39 that nobody could place turns out to be the most public name in the east. Nikolai proves it the way he proves everything, with two independent records that were never meant to be laid side by side. The design didn't flee to the enemy in 2110. It went home.

## Beats
- **06:00, the back room.** Maya's asleep, and so is their son. Nikolai has had *RA·12, Rustam Aslanov* under UNCHECKED for seven weeks (Ch 10). No factorepo holds the name, and no principle carries the mark. His wallet still holds the primer page with the rooftop on it (Book 4), a memory he has only from testimony. He keeps a copy of everything, as always.
- **The paper, first.** On Friday **Elena's first test courier** came back across the eastern border from the unaligned side. It was the dry run for the routes (Ch 19). With him came a **CNT civic primer**, printed on paper, because the CNT has no interfaces and teaches from books. It is for children of about eight. Nikolai reads the translation Elena's courier pencilled in the margins:
  - *The Founder, **Rustam Aslanov**, who in 2068 called the first Supreme Council against the Histologic expansion.*
  - A portrait. Died 2093.
  - The last page: *Our Chairman, **Timur Aslanov**, the Founder's son.*
  In Veridica the CNT has only ever been "the Council" and "the Chairman". Nikolai lays it beside his own primer page. *Arithmetic for young citizens.* Two schoolbooks from the same design.
- **The traffic, second,** worked in his own careful language:
  - **The Book 8 printout:** the maintenance log store's record of **May 21, 2110, 13:31–13:35**, the transfer contingency. The receiving site's acknowledgements carry **its own clock**, and it runs a fixed **0.41 seconds** behind the national clock.
  - **The garden.** The one reconciliation notice logged whole on Mar 25 (Ch 3) carries the same offset. The tending bursts, which they can see only as timing and volume, all leave by the same last relay.
  - **The CNT Supreme Council's external bulletin service** is the one CNT network the outside world can hear. It broadcasts the Council's statements to foreign missions on a published schedule. He has recorded it for three weeks: **the same 0.41 seconds, and the same last relay.**
  - *Same clock. Same door.* The third site isn't in some anonymous facility in the east. It is **CNT Council infrastructure**.
- **Maya checks him.** She wakes at seven and runs his comparison cold on her own tools without being told the answer. She reaches the same relay. *"Tools this good are built for the job they're used for,"* she says again, as in Book 7, and this time she means the Council's.
- **The foundation (light).** The relay between Synthesis and the eastern relays is leased, and the lease is billed to a **"reconciliation research network"** of **the Liu Wei Endowment** (Ch 13). Nikolai files it under UNCHECKED next to the letter Tanaka told them about. He doesn't know what it's for yet.
- **The shape, in his head, in order:**
  - **Principle Two:** *"let there be enemies outside them all."*
  - **The Annex:** the dashed outer border dated **2068** (Ch 9).
  - **RA·12,** signed in 2066, two years before he founded the Council.
  - **Book 8:** *the signal ran east.* Viktoria went east in March 2110.
  - The CNT was the design's **enemy outside them all, made on purpose.** Its founder signed the Register. Its Chairman is his heir. And since May 21, 2110, the true source has been sitting on the enemy's own machines.
- **His one dark joke,** to Maya: *"For forty-seven years we were afraid of them. Apparently we should have been afraid of their filing."*
- **Evening, the farmhouse.** The circle comes: Marcus, Kira, Dmitri, Elena, Isaiah, Kovač and Maya, around the kitchen table. Nikolai lays out the primer, the printout and three weeks of bulletin logs in a row, and walks them through it without looking up. Kira checks the offset herself. Elena says only that the primer cost her courier nine days. Across the table Marcus unfolds *QUESTIONS FOR THE WAR* and draws one line through *Where is east?* He writes nothing beside it. Nikolai sees it and doesn't comment.

## Ending
Nikolai, to the circle, with his hands flat on the printout: ***"They didn't run to the enemy. They made one, and moved in."***
