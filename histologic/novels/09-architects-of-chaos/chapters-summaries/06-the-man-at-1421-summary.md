# Chapter 6: The Man at 14:21

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Thu Apr 4, 2115 (about 19:00 to 23:30)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse kitchen, the one room in the country that isn't in any record; the yard afterwards
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 1.** The Book 5 question, whether her credentials were stolen or she was complicit, gets its answer: both, and neither. She was used, then spared, then chose silence, and now she has chosen to come back. The man who spared her was the design's own man. Marcus finally writes the entry he left unfinished in 2107.

## Beats
- **Who is present:** Marcus, Kira, Dmitri (by the door), Nikolai and Maya, Elena, Tanaka, Isaiah and Kovač. Tamsin is at home with a sitter. **Old Songs has gone up to bed before it starts,** on purpose. He doesn't testify (Book 5 Ch 19), and this account is theirs to write down. Singh sits at the table with her glasses on their cord and her sleeves rolled up. She asks for **three people to write** as she speaks. Kira, Isaiah and Kovač take pens.
- **Her credentials.** For four years as Deputy Minister, **Minister Chen Zhao's private office routed every scheduling request through her login.** Every Deputy's calendar was worked that way, and it was routine. She never questioned it; it was easier not to. The four entries at 14:00, the "only slot" offered to Kovač's office, the deep-cover warning, the defectors' window, Elena's tip and the **14:02 confirmation** all came from her credentials. "Not from me. I'd like that written down both ways."
- **13:59:58, the knock** (resolved here). There were two short knocks at the inner side door, light and unhurried. It was the knock the Minister's private office always used. The **Minister's private secretary** (unnamed) brought the afternoon's folder, as he did most days, and said the Minister would be grateful if she kept to her desk this afternoon. She said she always did. Her face did something because she had just written *Watch who restores it* in the margin. Then it settled, because this was routine. She initialled its routine papers; none of them touched the hour. He left by the side door at about 14:05.
- **The hour.** She felt nothing when the record was erased; nobody did. At **14:23** by her wall clock (the **door log had the card at side entrance B at 14:21:07**, where Dmitri saw him, and Marcus reconciles the two aloud), the side door opened without a knock. It was **Thomas Wei**, whom she knew by sight as a senior systems architect: a dark jacket, a small tablet in his right hand, and his **left hand bandaged**, already dressed when he came in. He said one sentence:
  > ***"Die in the record, or die."***
  She understood both halves at once. She chose the record. He touched the tablet. At about 14:23 her telemetry was **spoofed to flatline**, the canon method, and on her own screen her Registry status went to zero. She felt nothing then either.
- **The walk out.** She took her gray coat from the stand. They went down the side corridor and the east stairs and out of the side entrance, then on foot through the seeded district, **while forty people remembered someone else going in**. They passed two staff on the stairs, and neither looked at her. At the district's edge a car was waiting, with a driver she never saw clearly. Wei turned back without a word. She was in unaligned territory by night.
- **The body.** When they left, her office was empty. She doesn't know who brought the woman in, or when. In **2113** the Civic Record printed the restored paper registers of the Ministry's hospitals. In one mortuary day-book she found: *15 Nov 2106. Unclaimed female, approx. 50. Released on Ministry order, 13:10.* There is no name, only a number. It was an unclaimed woman from a Ministry hospital mortuary, released before the hour began. **She never learned whose body it was.** "Someone was burned in my name. I have looked for hers for two years."
- **Watching who restores it.** She wrote the margin note for herself. For nine years she watched who restored the record, and it was **Chen Zhao**: the Commission in 2107, and the Commission of Rebuilding in 2110. Then she looks at Marcus. "Mostly, Mr. Chen, I watched you."
- **Her part.** "I chose it. I have had nine years to decide whether that was cowardice. I haven't decided." Isaiah writes it word for word.
- **Maya.** Her father was convicted of a murder nobody committed. He stays in prison for Continuity and Phase Two. Maya says, very quietly, the line she has written every month: ***"He didn't do it."*** Nikolai's shoulder is against hers.
- **The entry.** Kovač says it isn't proof against the Minister: "It's a witness." Singh says, "Yes. That's what I came to be." Marcus unfolds his lists. He opens *Entry 6* from Book 5 Ch 29 (*What happened in the office: unknown.*) and writes the amendment he has waited nine years to write:
  > *Entry 6, amended. November 15, 2106. 14:21:07, side entrance B; about 14:23, the Deputy Minister's office, side door. Wei, T. Left hand bandaged. Event: Singh, A., telemetry spoofed to zero; walked out through the seeded district. What happened in the office: witnessed. Witness: Singh, A.*
  He moves one item from UNCHECKED to CHECKED.

## Ending
Late, in the yard, with the fog down, Marcus: ***"Why did he let you live?"*** Singh: ***"I've asked myself every day. I think he wanted someone to see it."***
