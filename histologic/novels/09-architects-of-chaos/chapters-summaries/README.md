# Architects of Chaos: Chapter Summaries

These summaries follow `plots/09-architects-of-chaos-full.md` (v2), with the drafting decisions below. The old summaries, chapters and documents followed the abandoned Nov 2025 plot and have been deleted; git history keeps them.

- **Structure:** a Prologue, 30 chapters and an Epilogue, in four parts. This is the series finale.
- **Target length:** about 3,300–3,600 words per chapter, roughly 110,000 in total.
- **Tone:** a conspiracy thriller and a reckoning: grave, humane and precise. Every chapter ends on a turn.
- **Canon rules:** `PROJECT-RULES.md` §4–6, `worldbuilding/`, `characters/` and `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md` (now fully revealed). In short:
  - Seeding is silent.
  - Removing an interface kills.
  - There are **no Judges** in Veridica or Chronos. Memoria, Axiom and Synthesis still have theirs, and Synthesis has a **Meta-Judge**.
  - SFW and CNT citizens have **no interfaces**.
  - Wei has no POV.

## Drafting Decisions (Beyond Plot v2)

1. **The world in March 2115.**
   - **Veridica:** a human-run **Civic Record**, rebuilt from testimony and journals and backed by paper, overseen by Chen Zhao's **Commission of Rebuilding**. Marcus is its **dissenting member**. The Commission proposes an advisory **Counselor**, not yet built. Nikolai's **open checking tools** compare records and never decide.
   - **Chronos:** a human council, now in its second term.
   - **Status:** Maitland is still First Minister. Martinez is President of the SFW (since 2111). Timur Aslanov is Chairman of the CNT.
2. **Families.**
   - **Marcus and Kira** married in June 2112. **Tamsin Chen** was born on Sun Apr 9, 2113, and turns two on **Tue Apr 9, 2115**, between Ch 7 and Ch 8.
   - **Nikolai and Maya** married in 2111 and have a son, unnamed on the page.
   - **Isaiah** lives in Veridica again. *The Cost of Certainty* (with Tanaka) came out in 2112.
   - **Elena** is no longer an outlaw, since there is no Judge to hold the record against her.
3. **Singh.** She is about 58, and still wears a gray coat and reading glasses on a cord.
   - **The knock at 13:59:58** was the **Minister's private secretary** (unnamed), with the afternoon folder. He left by the side door at about 14:05.
   - **The escape (Nov 15, 2106):** Wei's card was read at side entrance B at **14:21:07**, and at **about 14:23** he came through her side door and gave her a choice: *"Die in the record, or die."* Her telemetry was **spoofed to flatline** at about 14:23 (the canon method). He walked her out through the seeded district.
   - **The body:** the one cremated within a day was an unclaimed woman from a Ministry hospital mortuary. Singh never learned whose it was, and it haunts her.
   - **The nine years:** she spent 2106–2112 in unaligned territory, and from 2112 lived in Chronos under another name (unnamed on the page).
   - **Her credentials:** Chen Zhao's private office routed every request through her login.
   - **The margin note:** she wrote *"Who restores it? Watch who restores it."* for herself, just before the knock (Book 5 Prologue).
   - **What she believes of Wei:** *"I think he wanted someone to see it."*
4. **The riddle, solved** (Ch 2 and 7). The wording is canon from Book 8:
   > *Look where they swept the shelf of us, / behind the number no one reads; / the old man peeled his apple there — / ask him the number. Ask him twice.*
   - **"The shelf of us"** is Periodicals, Governance: *us* means Vance, Mikhail and Kerr.
   - **"The number no one reads"** is **114**. The numbers run **113, 115**.
   - **The apple** is Kerr, at eleven, in the reading room.
   - **"Ask him twice"** means Kerr's two written inquiries.
   - The **correspondence registry** (Historical Archive, basement, files boxed by year) kept them. The catalogue was purged; the inquiry about the purge was simply filed.
   - The registry card reads: *Kerr, A. Inquiry re: Periodicals, Governance, 114. 1st letter. 2nd letter.* It is **the only place in Veridica where the number still exists**.
   - **The visits:** the first is Mon Apr 8, when the card is found. On Thu Apr 11 Kovač opens the file.
5. **The Compiler's copy, as found (Thu Apr 11).** It sits behind Kerr's **second** letter. It carries no copy numeral and has the chipped *e*.
   - **Missing from it**, because Vance sent them to Old Songs in Book 6 or gave them to Kira in Book 8:
     - **pp. 2–4**, the Introduction;
     - **the torn strip** of p. 1's contents;
     - **p. 40**, the handwritten Warning;
     - **the inserted Seven page**.
   - **What remains:** p. 1 (torn), and **pp. 5–39**. That includes Principles One to Six, the **Annex (pp. 33–38)** and the **Register (p. 39)**.
   - **Complete again:** when the heroes lay the Book 6 pieces and Kira's Seven page beside it, the copy is whole for the first time since 2066. That scene is in Ch 9.
   - **Custody from Ch 9:** **Kovač** holds the Compiler's copy (with Kerr's page clipped to it) and the Book 6 pieces in her card folder. **Kira** keeps Seven in her journal, in her desk drawer. Everyone else works from Kira's photographs and from hand copies. Nothing moves until Ch 30.
   - **Clipped to the front:** **Kerr's page, "On Keeping"**, one typed sheet with his struck-through mark **AK·15**. Its last lines:
     > *"A record kept by one hand is a confession waiting for its torturer. Keep it by many hands."*
6. **The Register (p. 39).** Signed in 2066, with seventeen marks.
   - **The twelve signed**, each with its copy numeral beside it (I–XII, matching the mark number). This is the canon roster in `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md` §6:
     - HV·01 Hedda Voss
     - YP·02 Yuri Petrov
     - LW·03 Liu Wei
     - SO·04 Sarah Okonkwo
     - AV·05 Arkady Volkov
     - MT·06 Michiko Tanaka
     - TK·07 Tobias Kovač
     - JM·08 Jerome Morrison
     - VS·09 Viktoria Sokolov
     - WZ·10 Wei Zhang
     - CZ·11 Chen Zhao
     - RA·12 Rustam Aslanov
   - **The five struck through** with a single line (still legible, unsigned, no numerals):
     - **SA·13 Dr. Sigrun Ahlgren**
     - **EO·14 Professor Emeka Obi**
     - **AK·15 Anselm Kerr**
     - **LB·16 Dr. Lorenzo Bassi**
     - **MR·17 Dr. Matthias Roe**
   - **Ahlgren and Bassi** are the "two others" who asked with Kerr and were corrected in the spring of **2067** (Book 5 Ch 30). The other two withdrew; what happened to them is unknown.
7. **The Annex (pp. 33–38)** is quoted from §4.9:
   - mandatory interfaces;
   - *"belief delivery at population scale"*;
   - the Judge's **deliberate weaknesses**;
   - the Correction Registry;
   - the **placement of Histologic capitals and borders**;
   - *"Each contributor shall prepare an heir."*
8. **Principle Six's second sentence may now be quoted** (the whole manuscript is revealed): *"When a truth cannot be hidden, disclose it first, in your own words, with your own remedy attached…"*
9. **Copy X (Mon May 27).** Wei brings his father's copy, **less the pages that already came out of it**:
   - pp. 13–17 (Three) and pp. 23–28 (Five), both in Kovač's intake custody since Book 6;
   - p. 19, which Tanaka holds (Book 8).
   Everything else matches the Compiler's copy page for page. That leaves **27 typed pages** (p. 1 intact; no p. 40, no Seven). After the cross-check Kovač holds copy X with its intake pages. It goes to the court as evidence for Wei's trial, and stays there (Ch 30).
10. **RA·12.** On Mon Jun 10, Nikolai matches the traffic from the third site (Book 8, May 21, 2110, 13:31–13:35) to **CNT Council infrastructure**.
11. **The disclosure.**
    - **The heroes' date** was **Mon Jul 15, 14:00**, by hand in every nation.
    - **The heirs publish first,** through Synthesis, on **Mon Jul 8, 14:00**, with the *Statement of the Heirs* and the **World Record** attached.
    - **What the World Record is:** one merged record under Synthesis's Meta-Judge, restoring from a "reference source" whose reconciliation traffic runs to the CNT site.
    - **Funding:** LW·03's foundation funds it.
12. **The vote.** **Thu Aug 15**, at Synthesis's Council of Nations.
    - **For:** Memoria, Axiom and Synthesis.
    - **Against:** Veridica, Chronos and the SFW.
    - The CNT is not a member.
    - The World Record runs in those three nations only.
13. **The flight east.** **Mon Sep 2**, by sea and air from the Atlantic coast, through Sokolov's coastal command. **Chen Zhao and Sarah Okonkwo** go, with others left unnamed. **Tobias stays** in Chronos. **Viktoria and Mera** are already in the CNT. **Wei refuses to flee**: he is tried by a human court in the autumn and sentenced to twelve years.
14. **The broadcast from the CNT.** **Mon Oct 7.** Timur Aslanov, Viktoria, and **Mera**, alive. *"You just changed the enemy."*
15. **Containment.** The cabinet meets on **Tue Oct 15**. Paper goes in by Elena's routes. The first report back: a hand copy of **p. 39** pinned to a Council noticeboard in a provincial CNT town.
16. **The Day of Testimony.** **Fri Nov 15, 2115.** Old Songs sings the **Lakeshore Air, complete**, with the new last verse (`old-songs-character.md` §8). The original copy is shelved at the Historical Archive at **Periodicals, Governance, 114**, the number restored: the whole Compiler's copy, the Book 6 pieces and Kerr's page, with **Kira placing Seven last**. Old Songs writes the card. The numbers run 113, 114, 115. Many hand copies exist everywhere.
17. **Old Songs dies** on **Mon Jan 20, 2116**, at the farmhouse. He is buried in the plum orchard between Vance and Alexei. Vance was buried "beside" Alexei in 2110 with a strip of grass left between the graves (Ch 19); he lies in that space (Epilogue).
18. **Old Songs' voice rules** are binding:
    - He speaks rarely. His **one speech** is in **Ch 25**.
    - He hums, and sings with words when free.
    - Every tune lifts at the end like a question.
    - Songbook lyrics only.
    - **The book's one riddle** is his, in **Ch 10**, as the Register is read: *"Twelve shepherds tend the flock. Who tends the shepherds?"* The riddle has been reserved until "twelve" is known.
19. **Wei:** no POV and no interior. He is seen only from outside, and his lines are exact:
    - Ch 6, reported by Singh (Nov 15, 2106): *"Die in the record, or die."*
    - Ch 15: *"You'll want to check it against hers. Two copies are a pattern."*
    - Ch 15: *"Because it's finished. And I would like it to have been finished by you."*
    - Ch 15: *"Singh is well?" / "Good."*
    - Epilogue: *"Nobody designs everyone."*
20. **Chen Zhao's exact lines** (Ch 24): *"You are my heir, Marcus. You always were."* and *"We made you a hero. We needed you."* The second has **no "Marcus"** in it.
21. **Names:** every name in the Register is settled (decision 6). No other new named characters. Walk-ons stay unnamed.
22. **Numbers stay modest.** Crowds are counted in thousands, not millions.
23. **The chair kept open.** The Commission's **deputy chair**, at Chen Zhao's right on the dais, has been empty since the first sitting in Aug 2110. *"Still yours, Marcus. I keep it open. I'm a patient man."* (Ch 1.) Marcus sits at the far end as dissenting member. Ch 15 and 21 call back to it. In Ch 24 Chen Zhao offers Marcus **the chair of the World Record**, and the empty deputy chair, the heir's seat, is the clue. There is no "head seat".
24. **Line ownership.**
    - *"The cycle breaks here. We defend, but we don't attack."* is **Martinez's**, in Ch 29 only.
    - *"You united against us. You need an enemy. We have only given you a better one. You just changed the enemy."* is **Viktoria's**, all four sentences, in Ch 28. Timur speaks only of refuge and RA·12.
    - *"If we invade, we prove them right"* is Marcus's (Ch 29).
25. **Smaller settled points.**
    - **Dmitri holds no title** in 2115 (Ch 22, 29).
    - **Morrison** was born about 2067. His line in Ch 13 is *"He never met me. He died when I was fourteen."*
    - **The green-shaded lamp** and the drawer that stuck were at **Mikhail's** desk (Book 5 Ch 30), in Ch 7 and Ch 25.
    - **Timur Aslanov** is not named before Ch 16. The Register is not read before Ch 10. Mera is not known to be alive before Ch 28.
    - **"Where is east?"** is crossed through on Jun 10 (Ch 16).
