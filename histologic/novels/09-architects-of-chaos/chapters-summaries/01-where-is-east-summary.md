# Chapter 1: Where Is East?

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Mar 18, 2115 (about 06:30 to 21:40)
**Setting:** Marcus and Kira's flat; the Commission chamber (the Commission of Rebuilding's Monday sitting, 10:00); the flat again at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Five years after the Judges ended, this chapter shows the world that has to be put at risk: a record kept by people, a family, and a kind old minister. It plants the Counselor and the empty chair. It ends with the first of the dead calling in.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 06:30:** *March 18, 2115. Home. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Osman, K.; Chen, T. (asleep). Status: ordinary.* He looks at the last word for a while. Tamsin will be two on Apr 9. She calls the kitchen tap "the river". Kira is dressed first, as she always is. They have been married since June 2112. His prose still has holes in it: he reaches for the name of a street he once walked every day and it isn't there. He writes it down as lost.
- **The lists.** CHECKED, UNCHECKED and HOPES are in his coat, as they have been for nine years. Folded behind them is *QUESTIONS FOR THE WAR*, whose last line is still the one he wrote on May 28, 2110: ***Where is east?*** He has added nothing to it in five years.
- **The world, shown at the sitting and not explained.**
  - Veridica's **Civic Record** is human-run, rebuilt from testimony and journals and backed by paper.
  - **Nikolai's open checking tools** show the chamber two journals that disagree about a warehouse fire in 2109. They compare the two and decide nothing. A clerk will hear both keepers next week.
  - Courts sit with human judges. Chronos's human council is in its second term.
  - Memoria, Axiom and Synthesis still have their Judges.
- **The Commission of Rebuilding** is chaired by **Minister Chen Zhao** (74): silver-haired, tea in a glass, delighted by everyone. Marcus sits at the far end of the table as its **dissenting member**, a seat he chose in Aug 2110 instead of answering.
- **The Counselor proposal.** Chen Zhao presents a paper for an **advisory Counselor**: a machine that "advises and never decides", to help tired clerks keep the record the country has rebuilt. It is warm, humble and very complete. It comes costed, with its architecture sketched and its safeguards listed, as if it had been written before anyone asked the question. The chamber votes to commission a study. Marcus dissents, and asks for it to be minuted exactly: *"The dissenting member records that a machine which advises the record will, in time, be asked to keep it."* Chen Zhao, smiling: "Minute it exactly. Our cousin's dissents are the best-written documents we produce."
- **The chair.** To Chen Zhao's right on the dais is the **deputy chair**, empty since the first sitting in Aug 2110. Everyone knows whose it is. After the session, in the emptying chamber, Chen Zhao rests his hand on its back. "Still yours, Marcus. I keep it open. I'm a patient man." Marcus thanks him and doesn't sit in it. He files it under UNCHECKED and can't have said why.
- **Wei.** **Thomas Wei** (54), technical adviser to the Commission, sits at the technical table: tall, graying, a thin old scar across the back of his left hand. He answers the Minister's one technical question with a page of figures and a nod. His courtesy is complete and he gives nothing away. When their eyes meet he inclines his head, the acknowledgement of a colleague. Marcus nods back, as he has at every sitting for five years. Nobody has ever proved anything.
- **Evening at the flat.** Marcus bathes Tamsin, who counts the taps on the bath: one, two, *river*. Kira works late at the kitchen table on the east traces (Ch 3), with the curtain drawn. She says little: "Still east. Still nothing I can name." Marcus unfolds the question list and looks at the last line again.
- **21:30, the call.** The flat's line rings, which it rarely does at night. It's Tanaka, very precise, as if she were dictating to a clerk. She has waited three days to call because she has been checking: the face, the interface (a flatline spoofed nine years ago, still reporting zero) and the story, which she hasn't been told yet.

## Ending
Tanaka: ***"She's alive. She wants to see all of you. She says she was never murdered, only filed."***
