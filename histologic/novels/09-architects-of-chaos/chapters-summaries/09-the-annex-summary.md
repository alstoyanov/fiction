# Chapter 9: The Annex

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Mon Apr 15, 2115 (about 10:00 to night)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse in the border zone: the kitchen table, the stove with the cracked door, the dresser; the yard at dusk
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The Compiler's copy is laid out whole for the first time since 2066, and Kira reads the part of the design nobody has seen: the schedule. It is dull and exact, like every document that works. It explains the interfaces, Phase Two, the Judge's weak points, the border, and the families. Then she lays Vance's page beside it and sees what Seven really was. The last clause means the design was never meant to end with the people who wrote it.

## Beats
- **Kira's incident entry, 10:05:** *Apr 15, 2115. Farmhouse kitchen. Present: Kovač, H.; Chen, M.; Volkov, D.; Volkov, N.; Volkov, E.; Osman, K. Object: the Compiler's copy, found Apr 11. Custody: Kovač.* Kovač unties the card folder she has carried since Thursday and lays the bundle down squared to the table's edge. It has no copy numeral and the chipped *e*. **Kerr's page, "On Keeping"**, with its struck-through mark **AK·15**, is still clipped to the front. Kira reads its last lines once and doesn't say them aloud:
  > *"A record kept by one hand is a confession waiting for its torturer. Keep it by many hands."*
- **Old Songs isn't at the table.** Before breakfast he left three things on the dresser, still in the songbook bindings they came in (Book 6): the **Introduction (pp. 2–4)**, the **torn contents strip**, and the handwritten **Warning (p. 40)**. Then he went out to the orchard. Elena: "He read it on Thursday. On the floor." Nobody fetches him.
- **Making it whole, in Kira's engineering order:**
  - **The strip goes against p. 1's torn edge,** and the fibres meet: `IV …… 18` / `V …… 23` / `VI …… 29`.
  - **pp. 2–4** go in after the title page.
  - **pp. 5–39** are typed, continuous and complete.
  - **Kira's page** is Principle Seven, in the young slanting hand (Book 8). She takes it out of her journal and lays it after p. 32, where the Compiler left a gap in the stack.
  - **p. 40**, the Warning, goes at the back.

  Kira counts: thirty-nine typed pages and two handwritten ones. She logs: *Copy: complete. First time since March 2066.* Dmitri counts them again, formally, and gets the same number.
- **Principle Six, pp. 29–32,** read whole for the first time. The circle has known page 30 since 2106. Now there is p. 31: *"When a truth cannot be hidden, disclose it first, in your own words, with your own remedy attached. The public forgives a system that confesses and repairs; it never forgives one that is caught."* Marcus hears Vance's paraphrase from Book 7 and the Concordance of 2108. He writes the sentence into his list and says nothing.
- **Kovač's rule for p. 39.** The Register lies face down under its own card cover, with her initials across the flap. It was Old Songs' wish, and Dmitri seconds it formally: the names will be read **once, aloud, in front of every witness whose family may be on it, next Monday.** *"Names should be read the way the dead testified."* Kira agrees. She notes, honestly, that part of her is relieved.
- **The Annex, pp. 33–38.** It ends with three marks the circle can't read: ***MT·06, YP·02, VS·09.*** It is a schedule, not an argument. Kira reads it like a specification, item by item (§4.9):
  - **Mandatory interfaces**, with installation at the age of eight. That is her enlightenment day, and Marcus's.
  - **A broadcast network capable of *"belief delivery at population scale."*** That is Book 4's Phase Two and Book 8's stabilisation carrier, specified in 2066. Nikolai goes very still.
  - **The Judge's architecture, with its *deliberate weaknesses*,** listed plainly: a restore from the true source is accepted without review, and the backups trust one another's master credentials. That is her deleted fact of 2105, the hour of 2106, and her own failed image in Chronos (Book 7). *We didn't find the holes. We were shown where to stand.*
  - **The Correction Registry.**
  - **The placement of Histologic capitals and borders.** It is a schedule of lines. The lakeshore border runs through the broadcast overlap on purpose. Beyond the Histologic lines, two outer borders are drawn dashed, without names, dated **2067** and **2068**. Kira notes the dates and draws no conclusion. Marcus: "Principle Two. *Enemies outside them all.*"
  - **The last line,** alone on p. 38: ***"Each contributor shall prepare an heir."***
- **Seven beside it.** Kira lays Vance's page next to the list of deliberate weaknesses. Every weakness the twelve wrote is a door for the design to reach **in**: to restore, to correct, to overwrite. Seven is the only door that lets the Judge **out**, and it is written in a different hand. *It was the one weakness the others didn't write.*
- **Copying.** In the afternoon Kira and Nikolai begin hand copies of the Annex by the pairing method, two hands to a page. Kovač signs each copy out against her own register. At night the copy goes back into her card folder, the Book 6 pieces with it, in her custody. Kira folds Seven back into her journal: it stays hers, in her desk drawer (Ch 17).

## Ending
Dusk, the yard. Old Songs is a shape among the plum trees, not humming. Kira, to Marcus: ***"It explains the families."*** Marcus, looking at the house: ***"It explains the heirs. Which means it isn't over."***
