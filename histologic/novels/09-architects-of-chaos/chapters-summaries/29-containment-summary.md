# Chapter 29: Containment

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Tue Oct 15, 2115 (the cabinet from 09:00; the report late in the evening)
**Setting:** The First Minister's cabinet room in the capital (a long table, a link screen for the SFW); the corridor outside; a copying room in the testimony intake's basement archive, where volunteers copy by hand
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The man with more reason than anyone to want the war argues against it again, this time at the table where it could be decided. The answer is the series' answer. They contain the enemy they were given and don't invade it, and they send paper instead of soldiers, into a country whose people have never been allowed to read their own history. The first reply comes back in a hand that isn't theirs.

## Beats
- **Dmitri counts the room,** as he always does: eleven at the table, one screen. Maitland is in the chair. There are ministers (unnamed), the defence staff's general (unnamed), **Sokolov** in uniform, **Marcus**, and **Martinez by link** from the SFW. Dmitri has no title here. Maitland asked him to sit at the end of the table after the Justice Center steps in July, and he speaks formally, without contractions, as always.
- **The pressure, stated in numbers.** Eight days after the broadcast: crowds on the Meridian Bridge every evening, calls in the SFW press for an offensive across the Caucasus line, and a petition in Veridica to "bring the founders home". Wei's trial opened yesterday, and the courtroom is full. The general's proposal is careful and competent: a strike on the CNT Council installation where Nikolai traced the source (Ch 16), and an SFW push to seize the "émigrés". **The CNT has the largest conventional army in the world.** Dmitri reads the general's casualty estimate and counts it twice.
- **The argument.**
  - **Marcus,** plainly: ***"If we invade, we prove them right."*** Principle Two wanted enemies outside them all, and controlled conflict. A war with the CNT now would be the design working exactly as written, with a better enemy.
  - **Martinez,** from the link. Her soldiers died in the Battle of Blood in a part they were cast for: ***"The cycle breaks here. We defend, but we don't attack."*** The SFW will hold its border where it stands and will not cross it.
  - **Sokolov,** formally: coastal command can hold the Atlantic coast indefinitely, and it will. Then, low, on the way to her chair, her real sentence: *"My mother would like us to come."*
  - **Dmitri** speaks last. *"My brother died in the last war someone arranged. I said that on the steps. I will say the rest here. I have counted the names on the stone. I would like it to stop getting longer."*
- **What containment means, as decided:**
  - no strike and no crossing;
  - every relay from Veridica, Chronos and the SFW to the CNT site is cut, and Nikolai's open checking tools watch for any reconciliation traffic, in public;
  - Memoria, Axiom and Synthesis are asked openly to let their citizens check the World Record with the same tools;
  - extradition of the named persons is requested openly, and nobody is seized;
  - **and paper.**
- **Maitland** accepts it. His vote is the one that settles it. He says little.
- **The copying room, afternoon.** In the intake's basement archive, where Kovač once hid Marcus, volunteers copy the **Register, the Annex and Kerr's page** by hand in the CNT's own languages. Each copy is checked against another by a second pair of hands. **Elena** runs the routes: the smuggling paths through the mountains that the border wars never closed, carried across from the SFW side with Martinez's consent. Copies have been going in since July. Dmitri copies p. 39 himself, one mark at a time, and he stops at AV·05 and then goes on.

## Ending
Late that evening Elena comes to the copying room with a courier's note, folded small, from a packet sent in July. It is the first report from inside. In a provincial CNT town (unnamed), a hand copy of **p. 39** has been found **pinned to the Council noticeboard** outside the district office. The courier looked closely, and it isn't one of theirs. **The hand is not any of their volunteers'.** Someone inside has copied it again. Dmitri counts that as one, and then as more than one.
