# Chapter 20: Disclosed

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Mon Jul 8, 2115 (about 11:00 to night; the disclosure at 14:00)
**Setting:** The printing house that runs Isaiah's columns, in the capital: the composing room, where the Jul 15 facsimile run is being proofed; the press floor; the street outside; that night, Marcus and Kira's kitchen
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 3.** The witness is scooped by the people he was about to name. A week before the heroes' date, and at their own hour, the heirs publish the Register themselves: calmly, sorrowfully, with a remedy attached. It is the most generous confession anyone has ever made, and Isaiah has seen its shape before.

## Beats
- **11:00, the composing room.** Isaiah, 48, reads the proofs of the eight pages for the Jul 15 run: the Annex, the Register, Kerr's page. He checks each line against Kira's photographs and initials each sheet with his prefix: *IO. 8 JULY. PROOF 3.* The printers are unnamed. He counts: **seven days, 312 sets promised in Veridica, 40 in Chronos, and Elena's 40 for the east.**
- **14:00 exactly, by the capital's clocks.** The printers' radio breaks into its hour. The **Council of Nations' secretariat in Synthesis** has released a document "on behalf of the heirs of the contributors". The street screens carry it within minutes. Every nation's news has it within the hour.
- **What is published:**
  - **facsimiles of the Annex (pp. 33–38) and the Register (p. 39)**, the same pages Isaiah is holding in proof. The chipped *e* is there. **The lower right corners are cropped**, so no copy numeral shows. Isaiah notes it and doesn't know what it means yet.
  - All seventeen marks, the five struck through among them (*AK·15 Anselm Kerr*).
  - **No Kerr's page.** Only the Compiler's copy had it.
- **The *Statement of the Heirs*.** Two pages, unsigned by any individual. It is calm, humane and sorry. Key lines (new, and binding for later chapters):
  - *"We are the children and students of the people who wrote these pages. We did not choose what we inherited. We have chosen what to do with it."*
  - *"Four of the contributors are living. They will answer for themselves."*
  - *"Even the enemy was drawn on the map. We are sorriest for that."* (RA·12 is on the page for everyone to read.)
  - Then **the remedy: the World Record.** Every nation's rebuilt or surviving record would be merged into one record, under **Synthesis's Meta-Judge**, *"so that no nation can ever be written by another again"*. It would be funded by **the Liu Wei Endowment** (LW·03; Ch 13), and offered to the **Council of Nations** for a vote.
  - Attached is a *Technical Schedule*: sixty dense pages that Isaiah can't read. One phrase recurs, a ***"reference source"***. He marks it for Kira and sends the schedule to her by runner at 14:40.
- **The scoop, as arithmetic.** He does it the way his aunt told him he never does (Ch 11). Every paper in the world has the Register today, and his run will be seven days late to its own story. Everything the heroes meant to reveal has been revealed, in words chosen by the people it names, with a repair attached. His facsimile run is now a reprint.
- **The street, 15:30.** He walks. People read the screens with their hands over their mouths, and then read on to the remedy, and their faces change. Relief. He classifies it: *the frightened; the grateful; the ones already saying "at last".* He finds almost nobody angry.
- **Carver Street, from a distance.** The Register names *Ambassador Sarah Okonkwo*. He doesn't go there. He notes that the Statement doesn't name her, or anyone, and that it doesn't need to.
- **17:00, Chen Zhao confesses first.** His name is on p. 39 in every paper. He doesn't hide. On the Ministry steps he makes a short statement before the cameras, grave and fatherly, with no notes: CZ·11 is his mark, he wrote what it closes, and he is sorry. Then comes the turn, warmly: *"I confess it. Now let us make sure it can never be done to anyone again."* He commends the heirs' remedy as the way. He remains Minister, and nobody on the steps asks him to resign. Isaiah watches it on the printers' screen and counts: the second confession with a remedy since 14:00.
- **Evening, the kitchen.** Marcus, Kira and Nikolai. Kira has the Schedule open and says only that it is "very well made" and that she will need days with it. Marcus asks how they knew the hour. Isaiah: a plan carried by many hands is a plan many people can read (Ch 17–18). **Nobody is accused.**
- **The shape.** Isaiah lays it out like an editorial: first the confession, then the sorrow, then the remedy. He has seen it three times:
  - Maitland on Dec 18, 2108, with the Concordance (Book 6);
  - Chen Zhao on the Justice Center steps on May 28, 2110, with the Commission of Rebuilding (Book 8), and again this afternoon;
  - and on paper, in the Compiler's copy, pp. 29–32, which the circle read at the farmhouse table in April (Ch 9). **Principle Six, second sentence, quoted exactly:**
  > *"When a truth cannot be hidden, disclose it first, in your own words, with your own remedy attached. The public forgives a system that confesses and repairs; it never forgives one that is caught."*

## Ending
Late. Isaiah writes the entry with its prefix: *IO. 8 JULY. 14:00. Disclosed by: the named. Remedy: attached.* He looks at the line for a long time. *Disclose first, in your own words, with your own remedy attached.* Everyone at the table knows that page by now. He is the only one who says it aloud.

## Notes
- **(Twist 3 revealed.)** It pays off Book 6 (the Concordance; "the remedy was ready"), Principle Six, Ch 1 (the Counselor proposal), Ch 13 and 16 (LW·03's foundation), and Ch 17–19 (the silence). **The World Record's architecture is not understood here.** "Reference source" is only a phrase Isaiah marks. Ch 23 reveals it.
- **Principle Six's second sentence is quoted** (README decision 8). It isn't in the heirs' publication. The circle knows it from the Compiler's copy.
- **The timing:** 14:00 by the capital's clocks, deliberately the heroes' hour. The leak is structural (Ch 17), and no one betrays anyone.
- **New and binding:** the cropped corners (no copy numeral); the Statement's lines; the unsigned Statement; the *Technical Schedule* and "reference source"; the Council secretariat as channel; **Chen Zhao's 17:00 confession on the Ministry steps** (coordinator: from Jul 8 he is a publicly confessed Architect, still Minister, championing the remedy). He uses no "restore" here. The foundation name matches Ch 13.
- **The CNT says nothing** (Timur Aslanov is silent until Ch 28).
- **Carver Street** is the new house in the rebuilt quarter (Ch 11). Isaiah stays away.
- Isaiah's voice: counts, classifies, files, and never claims more than he saw.
