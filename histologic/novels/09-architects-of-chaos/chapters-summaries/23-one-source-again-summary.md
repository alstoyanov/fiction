# Chapter 23: One Source, Again

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Fri Jul 19, 2115 (about 06:00 to night)
**Setting:** The basement room at the Commission where the image was built in 2109 (Book 7); Nikolai's bench, where the open checking tools run; the flat at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 3's architecture.** The remedy is taken apart by the engineer who once let a scope indicator run past everything she had written. It restores every nation from one source, and that source is in the CNT. The peace everyone wants is Principle One, finished at last.

## Beats
- **Incident entry, 06:00:** *Jul 19, 2115. Basement room B. Present: Osman, K.; Volkov, N.; Reeves, M. Object: World Record, Technical Schedule (60 pp.). Day 11.* She has read it end to end three times since Jul 8 (Ch 20). She has been reciting it under her breath again, and that compulsion only comes back under stress. She notices it and doesn't stop.
- **What the Schedule says,** in her order:
  - Every nation's record, rebuilt or surviving, is ingested as a ***contributing record***.
  - Conflicts between contributing records go to **Synthesis's Meta-Judge**, which settles them, as it already does between Synthesis's own Judges.
  - A contributing record found "corrupted", by outsiders *or by insiders*, undergoes ***integrity restoration from the reference source***.
  - The reference source's location is given only as *"held under distributed custody"*. There is no address and no nation.
  She reads the corruption clause twice. It's **Principle Three** in modern type: *"Should outsiders corrupt the record, it may be restored from the true source. Should insiders reform it, it may be restored from the true source."*
- **The inspection endpoint.** On Tue Jul 16, the day after the heroes' publication, Synthesis opened a **public inspection endpoint** so that any nation could "test the reconciliation in good faith". It's a sample contributing record that you can submit changes to, and it reconciles itself against the reference. It's generous, and Kira has learned that generosity is a design choice.
- **Nikolai probes it** with the open checking tools. They compare and never decide. He submits a trivial correction to the sample record, and it is **restored within minutes**. He traces the reconciliation's route: out through Synthesis, off the continent, and **east through the same CNT relays** he matched to **CNT Council infrastructure** on Jun 10 (Ch 16). Their timing habits match the third site's traffic of May 21, 2110, 13:31–13:35, and the small patient tending of Ch 3.
- **The signature.** The reconciliation notices that come back carry the **Archive key's habits** (Ch 3): the same countersignature profile Nikolai first built in Book 6, from a key that died with the Chronos Judge. Someone kept a copy of the key too, and here it is, signing the world's remedy.
- **Maya** asks the technician's question: can the reference be moved later, and made truly distributed? Kira reads out the schedule's answer: the reference source *"may be relocated only by resolution of its custodians"*. The custodians aren't named.
- **The whole picture, levelly.** Every nation would restore from one source. That source is the true source that escaped the cascade nine minutes early (Book 8), sitting in the country the design built as its enemy (Ch 16). The heirs' remedy would put every record in the world, Veridica's and Chronos's included, back under the design's hand, by vote and with gratitude. It's **Principle One**, which she reads aloud from her photograph of p. 5 of the Compiler's copy (the original is in Kovač's custody): *"Singular truth, mechanically verified, universally enforced."*
- **The Annex beside it.** Kira lays the Schedule next to her hand copy of the Annex (pp. 33–38), *the placement of Histologic capitals and borders*, and thinks of Veridica's own **Concordance** in Book 6: every registered journal held as a sealed master with a **restore path from the master**, the thing the Rewriting came in through (Book 7). *Restorable means editable.* Maya found that component in the drafts in January 2109 and wrote its number down by hand. Now Maya watches Kira write this one down.
- **Evening, the flat.** Marcus has come from the Commission, where Chen Zhao was warm and said nothing about the Schedule. Kira lays it out on the kitchen table as she would lay out a fault report: the corruption clause, the endpoint trace, the key's habits, the relocation clause. Marcus asks one question: could anyone outside the circle check it? Yes. It's all published. Anyone with Nikolai's tools can run the same probe. That's the only good news.

## Ending
Kira: ***"It's the Concordance, for everyone."*** Marcus looks at Tamsin's door, and at the eight hand-copied pages still on the dresser from Monday. ***"Then we say no to peace. That's going to be hard."***
