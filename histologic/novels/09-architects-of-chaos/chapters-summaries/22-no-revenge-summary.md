# Chapter 22: No Revenge

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Wed Jul 17, 2115 (about 08:00 to 22:30)
**Setting:** Carver Street in the capital (morning), outside Sarah Okonkwo's house; the city between; the steps of the Justice Center at dusk, where the dead testified in December 2106
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The names are public, and rage follows, as it had to. The man with more reason for revenge than anyone in the city stands where he once stood as a dead man with his brother, and refuses it. He says it formally, without a single contraction. Nobody cheers. It works anyway.

## Beats
- **08:00, counting.** Dmitri is 40, and his inner voice is still a log. Since Monday the hand copies have confirmed the Register, and people have moved from shame to anger. *Two days. Four broken windows at Ministry addresses. One car burned in the Archive District. Nobody dead.* Numbers stay modest, and he keeps them.
- **Carver Street, morning.** About **two thousand** people are outside the house the Register made famous. **SO·04, Ambassador Sarah Okonkwo**, is inside. Public Order officers, unnamed, hold a thin line at the gate. Placards read her mark and the page number. Someone has chalked *ARITHMETIC* on the pavement. Dmitri doesn't know why, and doesn't ask. A stone goes through a front window at 09:41, and he logs the time. The curtain moves once. An old woman, upright, elegant and silver-haired, looks out at the crowd without fear, the way you might look at weather, and lets the curtain fall. **Isaiah** stands across the street, alone, hands folded. He doesn't go in. Dmitri stands beside him for eleven minutes without speaking, then leaves him there.
- **The city between.** At midday the call spreads through radio and the journal networks: *fetch them back.* The "émigrés" in the CNT include **Viktoria Sokolov**, whose mark is VS·09 on the Register, and RA·12 makes the CNT itself the design's house. Some people want the Architects seized. Some want an army sent east. A retired officer on the radio says Veridica "could". Dmitri counts the people who say *invade* on one call-in hour: **nine callers of fourteen**.
- **His own name.** The Register is in every paper, and **AV·05, Professor Arkady Volkov** is on it. A man on a tram recognises Dmitri from the western corridor in 2110 and says, not unkindly, *"Your lot's on it too."* Dmitri: *"Yes. He was our father's cousin. Our father was corrected for reading his handwriting."* (Ch 10.) The man gets off at the next stop.
- **Dusk, the Justice Center steps.** Some thousands have gathered, perhaps five. Organisers with a loudhailer are reading the Register aloud as a charge sheet, mark by mark, and at every living name the crowd answers *east*. These are the steps where on Wed Dec 8, 2106 the dead testified, Dmitri and Alexei among them. When Officer Kade said he had no warrant for a dead man, Alexei answered: *"Then you'd better prove I'm alive."* (Book 5 Ch 19.)
- **He goes up the steps** without being asked. Nobody introduces him, but enough people know the face. He waits, formally, for the loudhailer to stop, and it does. He doesn't raise his voice. He says who he is, in order: an enforcer once, a corpse once, a company commander in the western corridor. Then his father, Ivan, who was corrected in the spring the boys turned fourteen and died as Test Subject 2471. Then Principle Five, *"their father's death planned on paper"*. Then the cousin on page 39. Then Alexei, on the overpass of the eastern ring road, on Apr 23, 2110, in a war that Principle Two had cast before any of them were born.
- **The line, exact:** ***"My brother died in the last war someone arranged. I will not give them the next one."***
- He adds one more thing, plainly: *"Alexei died so we could be free. I will not waste that on them."* Then he stops. No appeal, no remedy, and nothing for the crowd to do.

## Ending
Silence. Nobody cheers. The loudhailer is lowered. Then people begin to leave, a few and then many, down the steps and into the side streets. Dmitri stays and counts them out until the steps are empty, at 22:30, and he doesn't write the number down.

- **The key line is exact** (plot). "Alexei died so we could be free. I will not waste that." is the usable old line (`volkov-triplets.md`), with "on them" added.
- **Violence stays at the edges and credible:** broken windows, one burned car, a stone. **Nobody dies.** Crowds in thousands (README 22). **No war** (brief).
