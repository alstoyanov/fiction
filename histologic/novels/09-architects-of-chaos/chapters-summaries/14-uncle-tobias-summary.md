# Chapter 14: Uncle Tobias

**POV:** Julian Kovač
**Date:** Mon May 20, 2115 (about 10:00 to 21:00)
**Setting:** The Chronos lakeshore city: Julian's flat near the Temporal Archive; the cardinal's house in the cathedral close, a narrow stone house with a garden; the lake-post office in the evening
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The family that the design used twice goes to see the member of it who helped write the design. The old man is gentle and unafraid, and he confesses as if confession were a courtesy he owes his niece. Julian, whose life was ended by the old man's heir, asks the only question he has left, and gets an answer that is worse for being kind.

## Beats
- **10:00, Julian's flat.** He is a Chronos historian, so he is precise about time: *10:02:14.* His log records everything. The council released him from maximum recording in 2110, but he still feels the log as a second person in the room. **Helena** came across the lake yesterday with a hand copy of p. 39. **Adrian**, who lives two streets away, is at the window, as restless as he was at nineteen. Julian has read one line of the page a hundred times: *TK·07, Cardinal Tobias Kovač.* Great-Uncle Tobias said grace at the family table when Julian was a boy, before he left for Chronos.
- **The walk.** They go together, as the family, and nobody says why. Helena keeps her hands in her coat pockets. Adrian talks too much and then stops.
- **The house.** **Tobias**, 81, is thin and white-haired, dressed in plain black, and sits in a garden chair in the sun. He isn't surprised. *"Helena. You've brought the boys."* There is tea. His log is running too, and he knows that everything he says is recorded. *"Let it keep it. I'd rather it did."*
- **The confession, to Helena.** He addresses her, the niece whose life's work, the Ashford certificates and the intake, grew out of his sentence:
  - He was the **moral cover**. He gave correction a pulpit for forty years: ***"Correction is compassion."*** He preached it in Veridica's churches while the Correction Registry filled.
  - He believed it, and he still half does. *"I told frightened people that being healed was a mercy. Some of them were healed."*
  - **Mera was his heir.** Augustin Mera was the Chronos hand. His key, the old man's keys, countersigned the Gap of Dec 13, 2107. Then he retired in January 2108 and the keys went to Julian. Tobias says nothing about the Rewriting of 2109, and nobody asks him.
  - **The death was staged.** *"I said the office for him. The urn was light."* Helena asks whether he is alive. *"I wasn't told. An heir who is ready doesn't report to his teacher."* That is all.
- **Adrian's turn,** brief and hot. Was he sent after Kira in 2105 on Tobias's word? *"No. You were only useful. That was someone else's arithmetic."* Adrian goes out into the garden.
- **Helena's turn,** flat, in order. She is a Registrar, and she tells him what his sentence cost: the certificates she signed, the ten false deaths, and Julian. He listens to all of it and doesn't defend himself. She writes nothing down. She will afterwards.
- **"Will you go east?"** Viktoria went east in 2110. Tobias, smiling: ***"I'm too old to be anyone's enemy."*** He will stay in Chronos, and he will answer to anyone who asks.

## Ending
At the gate, Julian comes back alone. Mera's key destroyed him, and he asks the only question he has left: ***"Did you know it would be me?"*** Tobias: ***"No. That was Mera's choice. He liked you."***

Coda, evening, the lake-post office. Julian has brought a print from his desk drawer, where it has lain since 2108. It is the Temporal Archive staff photograph of 2107, with Mera in the front row and Julian, a junior historian, beside him, Mera's hand on his shoulder. Julian kept it because Mera liked him. He gives it to Helena in an envelope addressed to **Isaiah Okonkwo**: *"He files things. Ask him to file this."* He doesn't say why, and she doesn't ask.
