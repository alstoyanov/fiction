# Chapter 17: Many Hands, Once More

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Mon Jun 17, 2115 (about 19:30 to after midnight)
**Setting:** Marcus and Kira's flat: the kitchen table where Wei laid down copy X three weeks ago (Ch 15); Tamsin asleep in the next room
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Part Three opens with the plan, and the plan is the series' answer at full size: no upload, no single hand, no one place it can be taken back from. The heroes set a date. Then the one thing that should be happening isn't.

## Beats
- **Kira's incident entry, 19:30:** *Jun 17, 2115. Kitchen. Present: Chen, M.; Volkov, N.; Reeves, M.; Volkov, D.; Okonkwo, I.; Volkov, E.; Kovač, H. Tamsin: asleep, door ajar.* She's 36. Vance's page is in her desk drawer, where it has been since 2110. She doesn't take it out.
- **The pages.** A set is eight sheets: the **Annex (pp. 33–38)**, the **Register (p. 39)** and **Kerr's page, "On Keeping"**. Kira lays out her photographs of the Compiler's copy beside her photographs of copy X (Ch 15). Both originals are in Kovač's custody. They agree page for page, chipped *e* for chipped *e*. Wei's line comes back to her without being asked for: *two copies are a pattern.*
- **Nikolai proposes the obvious thing**, only to have it on the table: put it on the open checking tools at once, in every nation. Kira refuses, levelly: *"If it goes out in one place, it can be taken back in one place."* Principle Three restored three backups from one source, and the Concordance restored every registered journal from a master (Book 6). Nothing they publish will have a master. The photographs go up on the open tools only **after** the paper is out, so that anyone can check a copy. The tools compare. They don't decide.
- **The method,** in Kira's order. It is Book 6's pairing method grown up:
  - every set is **copied by hand** from the photographs;
  - the copyist signs and dates the last sheet, and a **second hand countersigns** it after checking it line by line;
  - no set is copied from another set, only from the photographs, so a mistake can't breed.
- **The routes,** one voice each around the table:
  - **Veridica:** the journal networks, and **Isaiah's press**. Isaiah has the printers set a run of the eight pages in facsimile for the day, for people who want paper and can't copy.
  - **Chronos:** Kovač's letters to the six paper keepers of Book 6, and through them the witness-book people. Tanaka carries the first sets across.
  - **The SFW:** Isaiah will take a set to **President Martinez** in person on Jun 24 (Ch 18).
  - **Memoria, Axiom and Synthesis:** the small journal circles, through the **Synthesis Institute for Historical Methods**, Isaiah's old channel from Book 4. Isaiah notes the irony, and nobody laughs.
  - **The CNT:** Elena's paper routes. She says only: *"I'll tell you what they'll bear by the first."* (Ch 19.)
- **The date.** Dmitri asks for one, formally. Marcus proposes **Mon Jul 15, 14:00**. It's the fifteenth, when the city already gathers at the Lost Hour stone, and it's the hour the design once took. Nobody has to be told where to stand. Everyone agrees. Kira writes it at the top of a clean sheet: *Jul 15. 14:00. By hand. Every nation.*
- **Secrecy.** Maya asks how they keep it quiet with this many people. Kira has already done that arithmetic. They can't, and they don't need to. *"It doesn't have to be secret. It has to be many."* A plan carried by a thousand hands can be read by anyone. That was always the price. **(Twist 3 clue, fair play:** the date will leak, and nobody hides it.)
- **Kovač** takes the copyists' register, meaning the list of who copies and who countersigns, because folders are her element. Kira's first tally: **312 sets** promised in Veridica, 40 in Chronos. Isaiah's run is extra.
- **Late: the silence.** Everyone has gone. Kira opens Nikolai's traffic log from Ch 3 and Ch 16: the small reconciliations at the CNT relays, the patient tending of a garden. It hasn't changed. It hasn't sped up since the copy was found, and it didn't pause when copy X came, or when RA·12 was read on Jun 10. There's no missed beat. At the Commission, Chen Zhao has been as warm as ever for three weeks. Marcus has said nothing to him since Ch 15, and Chen Zhao has asked him nothing. There have been no arrests, no leaks, no statements and no counter-moves. Kira logs it as an incident with no incident in it.

## Ending
01:10. Marcus is in the doorway, glasses pushed up, having looked in on Tamsin. Kira, without looking up from the log: ***"They haven't moved."*** Marcus: ***"That's what worries me."***
