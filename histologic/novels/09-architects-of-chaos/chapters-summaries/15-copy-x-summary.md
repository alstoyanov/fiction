# Chapter 15: Copy X

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon May 27, 2115 (about 09:00 to 02:00 on Tue May 28)
**Setting:** The Commission of Rebuilding's chamber (morning sitting); Marcus and Kira's flat in the capital: the table, the stairwell, and Tamsin's room off the hall
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Marcus spends a day in a room with CZ·11 and says nothing, because he hasn't yet decided what saying something would be for. That evening the other heir in the building comes up his stairs with his father's copy. The man who spared Singh hands Marcus the proof that authenticates everything, and asks for nothing except that Marcus be the one who finishes it.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 09:00:** *May 27, 2115. Commission chamber. Present: the Minister; the commissioners; the technical adviser. Status: sitting.* It is five weeks since p. 39. **Chen Zhao** chairs, silver-haired and warm, with his tea in a glass. He calls Marcus "Cousin" across the table, asks after Tamsin, and says, as he has since March, that "your chair" is still waiting: the deputy chair at his right on the dais, empty since Aug 2110 (Ch 1). He uses "restore" once, about the Civic Record's spring figures. Marcus hears page 30 under it and doesn't look up.
- **What Marcus doesn't do.** He doesn't say *CZ·11*. He votes his dissent on the Counselor, as always, for the record. In his list, under UNCHECKED, he writes: *What would saying it be for?* His prose has its old gaps. He reaches for the Minister's first words to him in 2106, and they aren't there.
- **Wei at the sitting.** **Thomas Wei**, 54, the Commission's technical adviser, is courteous and unreadable, and takes notes with his right hand. The old scar on the back of his left hand has faded to a thin white line. Marcus has known since Ch 6 who stood at Singh's side door at 14:21. Wei inclines his head to him once, at the end, the way he always has.
- **Evening, the flat.** Kira is at the table with hand copies of the Register. Tamsin, two, is asleep. At 20:40 there is a knock. Wei stands on the landing in his coat and doesn't come further than the table. He lays a **flat bundle** on it, tied with tape. Wei, exact: ***"You'll want to check it against hers. Two copies are a pattern."***
- **The bundle.** Typed pages with the chipped *e*, and **copy numeral X** in the lower right of every page. Kira counts: **27 pages.** p. 1 is intact, with no torn strip. pp. 2–4, the Introduction. Principles One to Six. At the foot of **p. 22**, the end of Principle Four: ***WZ·10.*** The Annex. p. 39, with the same seventeen marks and **the same twelve signatures in ink**. **Missing:** pp. 13–17 (Three), p. 19, and pp. 23–28 (Five).
- **Marcus understands the gaps.** Those are the pages that came through Kovač's intake before the doors opened in November 2108 with **no visitor** on the camera, and the page that sat in the Stabilisation Order's file for Maya in 2110 (Book 8). The inside route was always this bundle. He doesn't say so aloud.
- **The exchange.** Marcus: ***"Why now?"*** Wei: ***"Because it's finished. And I would like it to have been finished by you."*** Marcus asks whether he'll go east with the others. Wei smiles and shakes his head once. He won't flee.
- **The door.** Wei, with his hand on the frame: ***"Singh is well?"*** Marcus: ***"Yes."*** Wei: ***"Good."*** He walks down the stairs, lightly, taking the low steps two at a time.
- **Night, the cross-check.** Marcus telephones **Kovač**. She comes at 23:15 with the Compiler's copy in its card folder, and with **the intake pages, pp. 13–17 and 23–28, copy X**, in her custody since Book 6. For p. 19 Kira uses the hand copy Tanaka made for her in 2110; the typed original is in Tanaka's case book in Chronos. Page by page, from p. 1 to p. 39, **copy X matches the Compiler's copy word for word.** The intake pages fill X's gaps exactly: same numeral, same paper, same *e*. Kovač initials each match in her register. When the check ends, a little before two, she signs copy X into her own custody, with its intake pages, beside the Compiler's copy. Kira has photographed every page of X first.
- **Heirs, in Marcus's list:** *WZ·10 — Wei, T. TK·07 — Mera, A. The others: unknown.* He writes a question mark after each of the ten others, including his cousin's, in the same hand.

## Ending
02:00. Kira is asleep at the table with her head on her arms. Marcus writes the entry he didn't write at the sitting: *May 27, 2115. Home. Involved parties: Wei, T. Object: copy X. Verification: two copies. Status: pattern.* Then, below it, the thing he still can't decide: *CZ·11: not yet said.*
