# Chapter 30: The Lakeshore Air

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Fri Nov 15, 2115 (the Archive at 09:00; the plaza from 14:00; the flat at night)
**Setting:** The Historical Archive: the periodicals room and the Governance shelves; the central plaza and the Lost Hour stone; Marcus and Kira's flat
**Length:** ~3,600 words

## Purpose
It has been nine years since the hour was taken. The design's own copy goes back to the shelf its first victim catalogued, under the number that was erased. Then the old man sings the tune the whole series has carried, once, with every word it ever had. The crowd doesn't answer the question in it. They sing it back. The man who thinks in fact entries writes the last one, and it needs no machine to verify it.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 07:00:** *Nov 15, 2115. Home. Involved parties: Chen, M. (38); Osman, K.; Chen, T. (2 years, 7 months).* He has been 38 since earlier this month. Tamsin corrects nothing and wants her shoes.
- **09:00, the Archive.** Present: Kovač, Old Songs, Kira, Marcus, Tamsin, and an archivist (unnamed). Kovač brings everything from her custody. The Compiler's copy is laid in a plain archive box, **whole**: p. 1 with its torn strip laid back against the tear, pp. 2–4, pp. 5–39 with the Annex and the Register, the handwritten Warning (p. 40), and **Kira's page**, Principle Seven, front and back, which she puts in last herself. **Kerr's "On Keeping"** is clipped to the front, where Vance left it. (Copy X, with its intake pages, stays with the court as evidence.)
- **The card.** Old Songs writes the catalogue card **in his own hand**, as he did in 2063. His hands are very bad and it takes a long time. Kovač offers once and doesn't offer again. The card reads: *Periodicals, Governance, 114.* The box goes on the shelf, and the numbers run **113, 114, 115**. He hums one phrase of the ferry verse, and it lifts at the end. Anyone may request it. Hand copies of every page are already everywhere.
- **The week, in passing.** Wei was sentenced last week by a human court: twelve years. He pleaded to everything and said nothing else. Containment holds. More reports have come back from Elena's routes, each copied in a hand that isn't theirs. Marcus files each one under CHECKED.
- **14:00, the plaza.** It is the Day of Testimony. The stone has the Lost Hour names and the war's names cut in its side, Alexei Volkov and Nora Chen among them. The crowd is some thousands. Tamsin is on Marcus's shoulders, and Kira is beside him. The circle is spread through the crowd: Dmitri at the old man's elbow; Nikolai and Maya with their son; Elena counting heads left to right; Isaiah, very tall at the back; Tanaka; **Singh** at the colonnade in her gray coat, reading glasses on their cord; **Sokolov** at the edge in a long dark coat, hands in her pockets. Julian and Adrian have come from Chronos.
- **Old Songs,** 77, officially dead since 2106 and very frail, stands at the stone. He doesn't speak. He sings **the Lakeshore Air, complete, once**, in this order, with the exact lyrics:
  1. **The ferry verses** (§1): *"Down by the water the ferry is late, / lanterns are low on the pier; / who will remember the name of the boat / when there is no one left here?"* and *"Down by the water the fog's coming in, / nobody sees the far shore; / who will remember the boat and the name / when there's no pier any more?"*
  2. **The lullaby, "Hush Now, the River Is Long"** (§2), all three verses. At the second verse (*"Hush now, the boatman is kind…"*) Marcus finds he is singing it under his breath, as he did in Wing C. He still can't remember his mother's name. He has the verse, and he no longer needs to know whether it is memory or invention.
  3. **"Given Gently"** (§4): *"A people that has lost part of its past / will accept any past it is given — / provided it is given gently, / and by someone it trusts."* The crowd knows by now whose words these are. In the open air they sound like a warning, which is what he set them to be in 2063.
  4. **The last verse, new, written for today** (§8): *"Down by the water the ferry comes in, / lanterns are lit on the pier; / everyone knows the name of the boat — / who will remember we're here?"*
- **The question.** The last phrase lifts like a question, as every phrase of that tune does. **The crowd, who have learned to, don't answer it. They sing it back:** thousands of voices on the last verse, raggedly and then together. Tamsin sings a word of it wrong, loudly. At the stone Old Songs hears it and smiles.

## Ending
Night, at the kitchen table. Marcus takes out the lists: CHECKED / UNCHECKED / HOPES; *QUESTIONS FOR THE WAR*, with *Where is east?* on it, crossed through on Jun 10 (Ch 16); and the page from Jul 22, still blank. He turns to a clean page and writes **the last entry of the series**, in fact-entry form, exactly:
*Nov 15, 2115. Central plaza. Involved parties: everyone present. Event: the song. Verification: we were all there.*
He closes the book.
