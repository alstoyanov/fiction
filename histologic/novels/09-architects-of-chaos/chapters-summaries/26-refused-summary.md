# Chapter 26: Refused

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Thu Aug 15, 2115 (morning to evening, Synthesis time)
**Setting:** The capital of Synthesis: the Council of Nations chamber (a round hall with six delegations' desks and an observers' gallery), a corridor outside it, the delegation's hotel room at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The heir refuses in public, and he does it the way he confessed in Book 7: with the truth and nothing attached. He tells the world what he was prepared for and says no. He offers no counter-plan, no chair of his own and no better record. The vote splits down the middle, and the World Record is born crippled.

## Beats
- **Opening fact entry, 07:10:** *Aug 15, 2115. Synthesis, Council of Nations. Involved parties: six delegations. Agenda: one item.* He looks at *one item* for a while. He hasn't answered Chen Zhao since Jul 22, and the list from that night is still blank.
- **The chamber.** There are six voting delegations: **Axiom, Chronos, Memoria, the Sovereignty of Free Will, Synthesis and Veridica.** The CNT is not a member and has no desk. Marcus notices that **three desks have a Judge panel set into them and three don't**, and that the split in the room was visible before anyone spoke. Kira is with the Veridican delegation, carrying the architecture she took apart in Ch 23, which has already been circulated to every desk. **Chen Zhao sits in the observers' gallery** with the Statement's sponsors, silver-haired and courteous, drinking tea from a glass.
- **The debate, compressed.** Synthesis presents the World Record: one record, the Meta-Judge, and "no nation can ever be written by another again". Memoria speaks warmly of context and Axiom speaks of proof. Chronos's delegate, a council member left unnamed, says Chronos was written once by another nation and will not be written by all of them. **Martinez**, in person, is the one Marcus watches. She was inclined on Jul 15, and she still wants a guarantee against war more than anything she has ever wanted.
- **Marcus has the floor.** Maitland gives it to him as "the commissioner who was offered the chair". Marcus speaks without notes, plainly, in the order things happened, in fact-entry rhythm where it helps. He attaches nothing.
  - The Annex clause: *"Each contributor shall prepare an heir."*
  - A Junior Historian at desk 14-08 who believed. The Believer's index, requested: 0.58 real, 0.73 typed. The restorer of the Lost Hour, appointed by the Minister on Jan 8, 2107. The commissioner. The man who refused KINDRED, carried out RESTORATION and ended the Judge. The seat on the Commission of Rebuilding. The chair kept open.
  - He repeats what was said to him in the empty chamber on Jul 22, word for word, because it is testimony: ***"You are my heir, Marcus. You always were."*** and ***"We made you a hero. We needed you."***
  - ***"I was prepared. I decline."***
  - Axiom's delegate asks what he proposes instead. Marcus: "Nothing. I'm telling you what happened. What you do with it is yours." It is Dec 2 again, and he knows it. There is no remedy, because a remedy is what the design accepts.
- **The vote,** by roll, in alphabetical order:
  - **Axiom:** for.
  - **Chronos:** against.
  - **Memoria:** for.
  - **The Sovereignty of Free Will:** Martinez takes a long breath and votes against. She doesn't look at the gallery.
  - **Synthesis:** for.
  - **Veridica:** Maitland, against.
  - **Three to three.** Under the Council's rules a measure binding every member needs a majority, so the World Record is not adopted. Memoria, Axiom and Synthesis sign it that afternoon as a compact among themselves: three nations on one source, and the others keeping their own records, by many hands.
- **The corridor.** Isaiah, who covered the session, files the count in his head. Kira says nothing. She takes Marcus's hand, and it's allowed.

## Ending
Chen Zhao comes down from the gallery and passes Marcus in the doorway, unhurried. He doesn't speak. He **inclines his head to Marcus the way Wei once did**, the acknowledgement of a good move, and walks on. That night Marcus opens the blank list from Jul 22 and still writes nothing.
