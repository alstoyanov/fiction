# Chapter 12: The Administrator

**POV:** Colonel Yevgenia Sokolov
**Date:** Mon May 6, 2115 (about 07:00 to 23:00)
**Setting:** Coastal command headquarters in a port town on the Atlantic coast: her office, the guardroom by the gate, and the harbour wall at noon and again at dusk
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Every promotion, every command and every sentence she said at her mother's table was placed. Her mother writes to tell her so, calmly and with love, as a report of work done. Sokolov answers in the only register she has ever trusted, and in one line gives back the career and keeps herself.

## Beats
- **07:00, her log.** Coastal command. There is fog on the harbour, which is real weather. Her daily entries are formal and in the first person, as always. **VS·09** has been in her safe for thirteen days. Dmitri drove out on **Tue Apr 23** with a hand copy of p. 39 in a card folder. She read it standing, initialled the folder, and said: *"Thank you, Agent Volkov."* He hasn't been her agent for more than ten years, and she noticed that she said it.
- **09:40, the courier.** An unnamed courier on **Synthesis diplomatic papers** asks for her by name at the gate and won't give the envelope to anyone else. There is **no Council frank and no stamp**, only her name in her mother's hand. He says he will wait for a reply until 18:00. She has him wait in the guardroom and logs his arrival.
- **The letter** is read in the register of an order, as she read the SFW annex in Book 8. It is two typed pages in her mother's style, exact and without flourish, signed *V.* **It is undated.** Her mother dated everything. *Written in advance.* She notes that and doesn't interpret it. The substance:
  - *"When you read this, you will have read my name."*
  - Every promotion, listed by date as in a personnel file: the storm test programme; Captain; **Major in February 2109**; **Colonel on Dec 20, 2109**, with the insignia engraved in advance; **coastal command in January 2110**.
  - **The leak,** stated plainly: the plan and the phrase, *"the beaches are a door, not a wall"*. It was a door. *"The war needed a beach. You drew the best one."*
  - *"I didn't control you. I guided you. That is what a mother is for."*
  - The canonical line, alone near the end: ***"I gave you everything, Yevgenia. Even your career."***
  - Viktoria describes the CNT only as "quiet", and says nothing of anyone else in it.
- **The check.** She takes her own file from the safe and compares the dates line by line. They match. She writes two columns, as she did in March 2110: *what I earned* and *what was placed*. The second column is longer. She crosses out the heading of the first. Then she writes it back in.
- **Noon, the harbour wall.** She walks, hands in her coat pockets, and looks east over the water. The narration gives the wind, the gulls and the time, and not her feelings.
- **Dusk, the harbour wall.** **Dmitri** has driven out again. He has come once a week since Apr 23 and has never said why. She shows him the letter and he reads it through once, formally. Neither of them uses a contraction. He asks whether she will answer. She says: *"I am entitled to."* He asks nothing else. On the way back to the car, her quiet line: *"I was apparently very good at it. She has sent me the reasons."*
- **Night, her office.** She writes a reply on a single sheet of command paper. She drafts it three times. The first two go into the burn bag unread by anyone.

## Ending
22:50. The courier, still waiting in the guardroom well past 18:00, is handed the sheet folded once. It is one line, formal, in her own hand: ***"Mother. I am where I should be. It is not where you put me."*** She logs its dispatch, the time and the courier's papers, and initials the entry.
