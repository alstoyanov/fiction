# Chapter 3: The Third Site

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Mon Mar 25, 2115 (about 08:00 to 23:00)
**Setting:** The basement room of the Commission where she, Nikolai and Maya built the image in 2109; Kira and Marcus's flat at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
For five years she has chased one word, *east*, through relays she can't enter. The chapter shows what patient work finds: not an enemy, but a gardener. Then one intercepted notice proves that something everyone buried in 2110 is still in use.

## Beats
- **Incident-format opening:** *March 25, 2115. 08:10. Commission basement, room B-4. Present: Osman, K.; Volkov, N. Subject: third site. Status: open, day 1,769.* She has counted the days since May 21, 2110. She doesn't tell anyone that she has.
- **What five years have given them.** The route comes from the maintenance log store's printout of May 28, 2110: the restore of 13:31–13:35 and the relays it went out through. The trail leaves the continent, crosses unaligned relays and enters **CNT relays**. Past the border, their view is limited to **timing and volume, never content**. Journal-network volunteers run Nikolai's open tools at relay points in the unaligned strips, and the tools record when certain paths are busy. It is passive listening; nobody breaks in anywhere. The CNT has the best cyber warfare in the world, and Kira assumes they are seen.
- **The pattern, laid out in her voice as a report:** small reconciliations every three to five days at irregular hours, tiny in volume. They aren't restores or attacks. There is patient maintenance, and pruning. She has a sentence for it in her notes that she thinks isn't an engineer's sentence: ***"Like someone tending a garden."*** Nikolai reads it over her shoulder. "It's the right sentence." She leaves it in.
- **Who might be tending it.** Viktoria Sokolov went east in March 2110. The CNT has taken in "émigrés" from Veridica since the war. The design has people, and they are still working. **Nobody can name one of them.** Kira refuses to write a name she can't verify, and the column in her notes where names would go stays empty.
- **Singh, briefly.** Tanaka's news reached them a week ago; they will all see her on Thu Apr 4. Kira thinks about it once, as an engineer would: every operation in the lost hour carried Singh's credentials (Book 5), and a woman who was filed has come back unfiled. She doesn't speculate beyond that.
- **Vance's page.** It is in the top drawer of her desk here, folded inside the journal it has lived in since May 2110. She takes it out, as she does some mornings, and doesn't unfold it. Its back says *everything that restores from it ends with it*. What ran east at 13:35 didn't restore from the source. It became the source.
- **Nikolai and home.** He is 40, and married to Maya since 2111. Their son is small and unnamed on the page. Nikolai is funny and tired and keeps a copy of everything in three places. Maya brings them lunch at one and goes back to the Civic Record office. It's an ordinary day, and Kira lets it be one for an hour.
- **16:52, the fault.** A relay in an unaligned strip, badly maintained, **misroutes one packet**. For once Nikolai's tool logs it whole instead of as a timing mark. It is a single **reconciliation notice**: one short, structured record of the kind a record store emits when it reconciles with another. Its content is opaque, a hash and a sequence, but it carries a **signature**.
- **The signature.** Kira reads its structure as she read the Gap's seam in 2108 and the Rewriting's countersignatures in 2109. She recognises the rotation habit and the padding, the particular way it leaves nothing to contradict. Nikolai pulls up his 2108 profile. It matches the habits of the **Chronos Temporal Archive's senior reconciliation key**, the key that countersigned the Gap and the Rewriting. It was Julian's key from January 2108, when it was handed to him. **That key died with Chronos's Judge on May 21, 2110**, because everything that restored from the source ended.
- **Night, the flat.** She tells Marcus plainly, at the kitchen table, with the curtain drawn. Tamsin is asleep. Marcus opens *QUESTIONS FOR THE WAR* and doesn't write anything under *Where is east?* He only looks at it.

## Ending
Kira, levelly, because she is very angry: ***"Someone kept a copy of the key too."***
