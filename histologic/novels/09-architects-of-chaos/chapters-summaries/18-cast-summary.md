# Chapter 18: Cast

**POV:** President Beatriz Martinez (SFW)
**Date:** Mon Jun 24, 2115 (morning to evening)
**Setting:** The SFW capital (unnamed): the President's working office in an old stone building with thick walls and no screens on them; the courtyard; the council room where she meets her ministers
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The first time we stand inside the "enemy outside them all". A woman who has buried soldiers reads the page that cast them, and finds that it cast the other enemy too. She says yes to the truth. She is also, deeply and honestly, tempted by anything that would mean no more burying.

## Beats
- **Morning. The SFW from inside.** Martinez has been President since 2111. Before that she spent twenty years in intelligence, and she still reads everything twice. Her country has **no interfaces**: human judges, paper minutes, oral testimony in the town assemblies. She writes her own notes by hand, and she has **no Judge to ask**. It has never occurred to her to want one. *(Optional, one line: her grandmother's family came east over the water in the Fracture Years and kept the name.)*
- **Isaiah arrives** by the diplomatic air route, as agreed in Ch 17, with one hand-copied set in his coat lining: the Annex, the Register and Kerr's page. The copyist's and countersigner's names are on the last sheet. He's courteous and formal, "Madam President". He folds his hands and doesn't touch the pages once he has put them down. She notes that he has sewn his coat again.
- **She reads.** She reads it all through once without comment, then again with a pencil.
  - **Principle Two** she already knows, from his prints in May 2110 (Book 8 Ch 30): *"let there be enemies outside them all."* Now she sees whose mark closes it: **SO·04, Ambassador Sarah Okonkwo**. She looks up at Isaiah. He says: *"My aunt."* He doesn't say anything else, and she doesn't ask.
  - **The Annex:** *the placement of Histologic capitals and borders.* She thinks of the maps in the council room.
  - **RA·12, Rustam Aslanov,** founder of the CNT's Supreme Council in 2068. She knows the name the way every SFW officer does, from the history of the enemy. The **SFW–CNT border wars of 2091–93**, the Caucasus line and the minefields: the rivalry her service was built around was drawn on the same forty pages as the SFW's own part. The design needed two enemies outside, and it needed them to hate each other.
- **"We were cast."** She remembers saying *"Then we were cast"* to Isaiah at the withdrawal table in 2110, and *"Before I was born."* She was right, and she hadn't known the half of it. The SFW's dead from the Battle of Blood (Book 8) played a part. So did Farahani, killed on the coast road by a battery that never got the ceasefire order. She negotiated the rest of the withdrawal herself, from May 27, 2110, and she can still list the prisoners' names.
- **Kerr's page** she reads slowly: *"A record kept by one hand is a confession waiting for its torturer. Keep it by many hands."* She says it is the only page of the eight she would have signed.
- **The council room, afternoon.** Her ministers, unnamed, take two views. One says to publish at once, since the SFW was right about the Histologic nations from the start. Another says to publish and invade: the CNT was built by them, so it's theirs, and so it's a target. Martinez shuts that down flatly. The SFW will publish on **Jul 15 at 14:00**: printed, and **read aloud in every town assembly**, which is the SFW's own way of keeping a record. She gives Isaiah her word.
- **The temptation, honestly.** Alone, she lets herself think it through. If the enemies were designed, then peace can be designed too. Some arrangement, some guarantee, that means no mother in her country gets another folded letter. She has signed four hundred of those letters. She would sign almost anything to never sign another. She recognises the thought as dangerous and doesn't make it go away.
- **Evening, the courtyard.** Isaiah leaves for the airfield. At the gate he says, in his filing voice, that a plan carried by many hands isn't secret, and that she should expect people to know the date. She files that too.

## Ending
Her secretary brings a card that came through the Synthesis mission: an envoy of Synthesis, unnamed, respectfully asks the President for a private meeting, at her convenience, ***"on a durable peace"***. Martinez reads it twice, then puts it on top of the Register.
