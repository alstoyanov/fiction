# Chapter 24: The Heir

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Jul 22, 2115 (about 18:40 to 21:15)
**Setting:** The Commission of Rebuilding's chamber after hours: the long table, the dais with the chairman's seat and the empty deputy chair at its right, tall windows, the building empty; the stairs and the street afterwards
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
**Twist 4.** The clause said every contributor shall prepare an heir. Chen Zhao's heir is the man who spent the series refusing things. In a warm voice and an empty room, the old man tells Marcus that every step of his life since the fourteenth floor was a step in a succession, and offers him the chair at the end of it. Marcus has kept a record of everything. For once, he writes nothing.

## Beats
- **The note.** The Commission rose at 17:00. A handwritten card from the Minister's office asks Marcus to stay "for a word, if he can spare it". Kira is at home with Tamsin. Marcus tells her where he is, and she says: *"Write it down."*
- **18:40, the chamber.** The lamps are on and the cleaners have gone. **Chen Zhao** is 74, silver-haired, warm, old and unafraid, with tea in a glass. He sits in his own seat on the dais. At his right is the **deputy chair**, empty since the first sitting in Aug 2110 (Ch 1), and he rests his hand on its back as he did in March. Marcus doesn't take it. He sits where he always sits, at the far end: the dissenting member's place.
- **The offer.** The Council of Nations votes on Aug 15 (README 12). The World Record will need a human chair, *"someone every nation has watched refuse something"*. Chen Zhao offers him **the chair of the World Record**. He knows that Kira has read the Schedule, and says so kindly: *"Mrs. Osman has been reading. Good. It will bear her."*
- **Then, simply:** ***"You are my heir, Marcus. You always were."***
- **The succession, as Chen Zhao tells it,** gently and in order. Each step lands on something Marcus has in his own record, exact:
  - **The clause** (Annex, Ch 9): *"Each contributor shall prepare an heir."* He chose a Chen boy, distant enough that no one would call it favour and near enough that he would be family. He won't give the degree of kinship.
  - **The Believer, "requested".** *"I asked for a believer. Thomas brought me names. I chose the one who was family."* Marcus hears the voice through the assessment-room door on **Oct 7, 2105** (Book 4 Prologue): ***"he's exactly what was requested."*** He has had *Requested by whom?* on a list for nine years.
  - **0.58 → 0.73.** *"A believer at fifty-eight goes home, and a believer who goes home is no use to anyone."* Marcus sees the line in the acquisition file exactly: ***"Acquisition: contamination index 0.58 → 0.73 (adjusted; threshold compliance)."*** Chen Zhao doesn't say who typed it. Marcus knows who sat at 14-09.
  - **The restorer of the Lost Hour.** The order that took him off the Unit 3 van on **Mon Dec 6, 2106** carried Chen Zhao's signature (Book 5). So did the appointment on **Sat Jan 8, 2107**, and the toast: ***"My cousin. No, truly. The Chen families of this city are all related somewhere, Mr. Chen."***
  - **KINDRED refused** (Book 6), **RESTORATION carried out** (Book 7). *"Either would have served."* The reception on **Mon Nov 4, 2109**: ***"To our restorers."*** And on Dec 2, 2109, after the confession with no remedy: *"Then let me help you… find this Register of theirs."* *"You found it without me. That was better."*
  - **The Commission offered:** the foot of the Justice Center steps on **Wed May 28, 2110**: ***"Cousin. We'll need you on it."*** Since then Chen Zhao has kept the deputy chair open. Marcus understands it now: a deputy is the one who succeeds. It was never a seat on the Commission. It was the heir's seat, and the World Record's chair is where it leads.
  - **The Judge's end.** *"That I did not arrange. I watched it, and let it run. It made you trusted in two nations. Then in all of them."*
- **Principle Six, in person.** Marcus sees it whole, as a fact entry he doesn't write. The Commission was *"Arrange to be the one who restores it."* in the flesh, and he was its restorer. Every door he held had been left open for him to hold.
- **Nora.** Chen Zhao says *cousin* once more, fondly, and Marcus thinks of **Nora Chen**, the real cousin, holding a door in a burning room (Book 8). She never called him anything.
- **The line, exact:** ***"We made you a hero. We needed you."*** Chen Zhao says it without triumph, as a man states the weather. He says the World Record will be kept by someone who has refused everything, *"and that is exactly why they will let you keep it."*
- **Marcus doesn't answer.** Chen Zhao doesn't press him. He finishes his tea, rises slowly and rests a hand lightly on Marcus's shoulder in passing. At the door: *"Take your time. You always check."*

## Ending
Alone in the chamber. Marcus takes out his list. It has CHECKED, UNCHECKED and HOPES, and near the bottom, crossed through on Jun 10, *Where is east?* He uncaps the pencil and holds it over the page. The Minister's glass stands on the table with a ring of tea at the bottom. **For the first time in the series, he writes nothing on it.**
