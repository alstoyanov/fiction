# Chapter 10: The Register

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Mon Apr 22, 2115 (about 14:00 to after midnight)
**Setting:** Elena's farmhouse: the kitchen table with the lamp lit in daylight; the yard and the barn roof at night
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The most dangerous page in the world is one sheet of names, and Dmitri reads it the only way he knows how to read anything that matters: formally, in order, counting. Almost every name belongs to someone at the table. One belongs to him. The old man asks the one question the page can't answer.

## Beats
- **Dmitri's count, 14:00.** Present, in his own log: Elena, Nikolai, Maya, Marcus, Kira, Kovač, Isaiah and Tanaka, with Old Songs at the stove. Ten people, and one page. Kovač breaks her initials on the card cover and slides **p. 39** to him. It is the page of **seventeen marks**, each beside a typed name. Twelve are **signed in ink**, each with its copy numeral beside it, I to XII. Five are **struck through** with a single line, legible, unsigned, with no numeral. He reads aloud, one at a time, mark first, without contractions. Kira keeps the table, matching each mark to its page.
- **HV·01, Dr. Hedda Voss.** Principle One.
- **YP·02, Dr. Yuri Petrov.** Principle Three and the Annex. Old Songs hums one falling phrase: *no*. Elena says it for him: "Not his family. Petrov is a common name."
- **LW·03, Chairman Liu Wei.** He wrote no principle. Kira: "Liu is the family name. Nothing to do with Wei." Nobody at the table knows what he was chairman of.
- **SO·04, Ambassador Sarah Okonkwo.** Principle Two. Isaiah folds his hands on his knee and says nothing, because he has had a letter since March (Ch 4). Dmitri waits exactly as long as a witness is owed, and then goes on.
- **AV·05, Professor Arkady Volkov.** Dmitri stops.
  - **Elena, flat:** Arkady was Ivan's cousin, their grandfather's brother's son, a historian in the capital. He came to the lakeshore flat once, when she was small. He did not come to Ivan's funeral in 2090.
  - **The Annex:** Kira turns back to the Correction Registry schedule and finds the line they read past on Apr 15: *calibration record: method per AV·05.* Every calibration run is kept twice, once as a natural storm in the factorepo and once as a numbered run. Old Songs' family's storm was *"Calibration run 14"* (Book 7).
  - **Ivan's record**, which Nikolai found in Book 4: *REFERRED: UNAUTHORISED INQUIRY INTO BROADCAST DISCREPANCY.* In 2089 their father found the two entries that didn't match. That was his cousin's design. Dmitri doesn't finish the thought aloud. He says: "Continuing."
- **MT·06, Director Michiko Tanaka.** Tanaka sets her cup down very carefully. "My grandfather's sister." She says nothing more; that is Ch 13.
- **TK·07, Cardinal Tobias Kovač.** Kovač pushes her glasses up her nose. "My uncle." A pause. "I'll tell Julian myself."
- **JM·08, Colonel Jerome Morrison.** Principle Five, their father's death planned on paper (Book 6). Tanaka: "James never speaks of a grandfather." She writes the name in her case book.
- **VS·09, Chancellor Viktoria Sokolov.** The Annex. Dmitri notes that he will drive to coastal command tomorrow. Nobody else will tell her.
- **WZ·10, Dr. Wei Zhang.** Principle Four. Maya: "His father." Nobody says Thomas's name aloud.
- **CZ·11, Minister Chen Zhao.** Principle Six. It is **copy XI**, and the torn page was his, and so was Kira's folder of 2105, *RESTORATION / XI*. Marcus writes the mark in his list, beside the entry he keeps for the Minister, and underlines nothing.
- **RA·12, Rustam Aslanov.** This one has no page, no principle, and no link. **Nobody at the table can place the name.** It isn't in any record west of the lake that anyone has searched. Nikolai writes it under UNCHECKED.
- **Twelve.** Dmitri says the number aloud because it is the count: "Twelve signed." In the silence Old Songs speaks from the stove, the book's one riddle, exact:
  > ***"Twelve shepherds tend the flock. Who tends the shepherds?"***
  Nobody answers it, and he doesn't want them to.
- **The five, struck through.** SA·13, Dr. Sigrun Ahlgren. EO·14, Professor Emeka Obi. **AK·15, Anselm Kerr**: the same mark as on the page clipped to the copy. LB·16, Dr. Lorenzo Bassi. MR·17, Dr. Matthias Roe. Old Songs comes to the table and writes on the back of an envelope in a shaking hand: *Ahlgren. Bassi. They asked with him. Spring 2067.* The "two others" of Book 5. Of Obi and Roe he writes nothing: they withdrew, and nobody knows what became of them.
- **Evening.** The copying starts at once, by many hands: each person copies the page once, and Kovač logs every copy. Dmitri copies his in block capitals and counts the lines twice.

## Ending
After midnight, on the barn roof, where Nikolai goes when he can't sleep. It is the nearest thing to the parapet (Story 03). Dmitri sits on his left. They don't look at each other. Dmitri, without a contraction: ***"Our father was corrected for reading his cousin's handwriting."***
