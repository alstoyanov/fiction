# Architects of Chaos (Histologic, Book 9, Series Finale)

**Status:** plot v2 and chapter summaries done (Sept 2026). The chapters are still to be drafted. The earlier Nov 2025 draft, its summaries and its documents have been deleted, and git history keeps them.
**Set:** Friday, March 15 to Friday, November 15, 2115. The epilogue is on Tuesday, November 15, 2135.
**Follows:** *Battle of Blood* (Book 8).

## Premise
Singh returns from the dead. Old Songs solves Vance's riddle, and the Compiler's copy turns up behind Anselm Kerr's second letter, with the Annex, the Register and the names. Every name belongs to someone's family, and the twelfth name founded the enemy. The heirs publish first and offer the world one record. At its centre is the heir they prepared, Marcus Chen, and he refuses. The Architects flee to the enemy they built. The heroes answer with containment and with paper. Old Songs sings the Lakeshore Air one last time.

## The Twists
1. **The man at 14:21 let her live** (Ch 6).
2. **The enemy was built** (Ch 16): RA·12 founded the CNT.
3. **They disclose first** (Ch 20): the World Record.
4. **The heir** (Ch 24): Marcus.
- **Closing:** "You just changed the enemy." Containment, not invasion.
