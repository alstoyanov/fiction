# Chapter 21: The Annex

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Mon Dec 13, 2106
**Setting:** The Ministry research annex, north district, at night
**Length:** ~3,600 words

## Purpose
A break-in, and a face-to-face with Book 4's antagonist. It delivers real information, a warning, and a trap springing shut.

## Beats
- **The team:** Kira, out of the safe flat with Kovač's help ("medical appointment"); **Dmitri**; **Maya**, whose access badge gets them through the outer doors; and Nikolai guiding them from outside over the radio.
- **The annex:** clean, white and quiet. Cradles under dust covers. A smaller Wing C with no glass. It is exactly like Ashford, and Kira's body remembers it before her mind does.
- **Dr. Reeves** is there at a terminal at midnight: thinner, unshaven, as interested as ever. He looks at Maya for a long time. "You've cut your hair." She can't answer.
- **What he gives them:**
  - **A denial:** "I didn't run it. I would never waste seeding on forty clerks. It's crude. It's a *vandal's* use of my work."
  - **Something real:** in Book 4 he *asked* someone at the factorepo to delete Kira's fact and adjust Marcus's index. "I never met him. Requests went through the Ministry. The work came back with a signature. You'd know it, Ms. Osman. You've been looking at it for a month."
  - **A warning:** "They didn't erase the hour to hide a murder. They erased it to see who you'd *believe*."
  - **A strange detail:** "Someone has been using my cradles at night. The logs show sessions I didn't run. I thought it was my imagination." He laughs softly. "I don't have an imagination."
  - **The page:** shown the photograph, he smiles. "Ah. You've found the sermon. I only ever had the one page too. They quote it at you in meetings."
- He refuses to come with them: "Where would I go? I'm the most hated man in Veridica. Here, at least, the equipment is good."
- **The trap.** Lights. Wei's team, with enforcers, comes through the front doors: *"Lead investigator, nobody move."* Wei had Maya followed, "for her safety". Reeves is **arrested** at his terminal and goes calmly.
- **Wei thanks Kira** for "the lead", warmly, sincerely. He holds the door open for her with his **bandaged left hand**, and Kira looks at the hand and at his face and feels the floor tilt.

## Ending
As they lead Reeves out he turns his head toward Kira and says, too quietly for anyone else: "Ask yourself why he needed my code comment, Ms. Osman. I've never signed anything in my life."

## Notes
- **"Using my cradles at night"** sets up the seeding of Reeves's confession (Ch 24).
- The line about the code comment tells Kira that the Ch 14 evidence was **planted**. She now suspects Wei, but can prove nothing.
- Reeves survives the book in custody. His Book 4 crimes remain real.
