# Chapter 20: Confessions

**POV:** Nikolai Volkov (as "Pieter Lang")
**Date:** Fri Dec 10, 2106
**Setting:** The investigation room; the Ministry's research annex logs; a late-night café
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
A thriller beat, the most frightening scene for Nikolai, and a love story beginning. Two secrets are traded and the hunt gets its target.

## Beats
- **Two days after the steps,** the team room is quieter. People keep glancing at Pieter's face. His beard and glasses hold, barely.
- **The trace:** Nikolai and Maya follow the carrier's injection point back through the node's routing. It came from a terminal registered to a **Ministry research annex** in the capital's north district. According to Maya's logs, Continuity's work carried on there quietly after August. It is presumably where her father is.
- **The terror.** Late in the day Wei stops at Pieter's desk and puts a **folded, worn page** on it. "You dropped this, I think. By the lift." It looks like the page from Pieter's wallet: **the rooftop in pinpricks**, Dmitri's letter from Book 4. It is an **exact copy**, down to the pinpricks. Wei, pleasantly: "Arithmetic for young citizens. Charming. My father collected old primers." He walks away.
  - *Did he read it? Can he read pinpricks? Does he know?*
  - Nikolai checks his wallet. It's zipped shut and was never opened, and the original is still inside. He did **not** drop anything. Somebody has seen the original closely enough to copy it.
- **Maya:** that night in a café she tells Pieter the whole truth. Reeves is her father. She found his code. He answered her message ("It wasn't me"). She wants to find him before Wei's team does, because she wants to hear it from his mouth.
- **Nikolai's decision.** He has spent his life standing in the middle, keeping secrets. He tells her: "My name isn't Pieter. It's Nikolai Volkov. I was C-5. Your father studied me." A long silence. Maya: "…The third face." She'd seen the steps on the news.
- **The beginning of something:** two people defined by the Continuity file, sitting in a café choosing to trust each other anyway. She takes his hand. He lets her.

## Ending
Walking her home, Nikolai sees a gray coat on the other side of the street, stopped, reading nothing on a tablet. He doesn't turn his head. He says, "Maya. Tomorrow we find your father. Before someone else does."

## Notes
- **The returned page** is the key Twist 3 misdirection beat. Wei knows exactly who Pieter is and wants him to know it. That connects to the Epilogue coda ("the old man was not in the design").
- **"My father collected old primers"** is a hint to Wei's father, Dr. Wei Zhang (a later book).
- The romance continues into later books.
