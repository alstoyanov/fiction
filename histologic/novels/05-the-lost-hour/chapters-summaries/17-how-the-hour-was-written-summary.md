# Chapter 17: How the Hour Was Written

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Fri Dec 3, 2106
**Setting:** The SFW mission in Chronos; a video link to the lakeshore
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
The world learns about seeding, and Isaiah's status is decided. End Part Two with the story going international, and with a warning about who benefits.

## Beats
- **The asylum hearing** at the SFW mission. An SFW panel, a Chronos observer, and a Veridican representative who insists Isaiah "absconded".
- **Sokolov testifies by video link** from the border. She is formal and unreadable. At 14:30 on Nov 15 she personally escorted Isaiah Okonkwo across the lakeshore checkpoint under a lawful order. She was outside the Ministry district's broadcast range, so **her memory can't have been seeded**. She is a Border Security captain contradicting her own Ministry in public.
  - Asked whether she has "anything further": "No." A long pause. "Nothing that is relevant to Mr. Okonkwo."
- **Asylum is granted.** The SFW gets its witness.
- **Isaiah publishes "How the Hour Was Written"** through Synthesis and SFW outlets. It is the seeding story, in his voice:
  - forty identical memories;
  - Tanaka's diagnosis, attributed to "a clinician";
  - Phase Two used on a capital district.
  - *"In Veridica, for one hour, memory itself was issued by the state."*
- **The reaction:** an international outcry. Veridica denies it: "outlaw fabrication". Pell's tribunal comes under pressure, but it is still scheduled.
- **What he leaves out:** Kira has sent him the torn page's sentence and Old Songs' passage. He doesn't publish them. *"One fragment is a curiosity. Two is a pattern. I'll wait for three."*
- **The uncomfortable question** he does print, in the last paragraph: *who benefits from the world believing that memory can be written?* He doesn't know the answer, and says so.

## Ending
That night a mission clerk hands Isaiah a sealed note that came "from the Veridican side". Sokolov's handwriting: *"You asked who chose 14:30. At 09:00 on the 15th I called the Deputy Minister's office to confirm the slot. Her aide said she had never requested it. At 14:02 it was confirmed anyway, from her own credentials."* At 14:02, the hour had already begun.

## Notes
- **Sokolov's note:** a confirmation sent from Singh's credentials at 14:02, inside the erased hour, which only Sokolov's own unseeded memory and paper log preserve. It corroborates Twist 2 from outside the resistance and deepens both mysteries: was Singh alive at 14:02, or was someone else using her credentials?
- The Isaiah article named here is Book 5's first major public turn.
- End of Part Two.
