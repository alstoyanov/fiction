# Chapter 15: The Doctrine

**POV:** Director Helena Kovač
**Date:** Wed Dec 1, 2106
**Setting:** Her inquiry; Minister Chen Zhao's office; the Ministry corridors
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
The legal battlefield, and the **red herring**: suspicion turns toward Kovač herself. Introduce Chen Zhao as a warm, too-receptive ally.

## Beats
- **The problem:** Veridican law has no mechanism for a missing record. Pell's tribunal will rule on the forty statements alone. There are forty seeded witnesses against one dead man, and there is no category in law for "seeded".
- **Kovač's idea:** a hearing before The Judge on a single question: *where the record is absent, may testimony establish a fact?* If testimony counts, it has to be weighed, and the Volkovs' unseeded account, Sokolov's, and the defectors' could outweigh forty compromised witnesses.
- **The red herring closes in.** At her inquiry, Halvorsen produces a line from the calendar: **the Director's office requested the 14:30 border slot** on Nov 11. The inference: Kovač arranged a prisoner's release at the exact time of a murder in the same building. Pell adds that she certified six "deaths" in August on the night of an interior fact storm, and asks whether those deaths are also in doubt.
- Kovač realises that **someone is building a case against her** from real facts, arranged to look damning.
- **Minister Chen Zhao:**
  - He is 65, silver-haired, courteous, with tea in a glass. He listens to her proposal with open delight.
  - "A doctrine of testimony. How very humane. How very *restorative*." (Kovač notices the word and doesn't know why it bothers her.)
  - He mentions, pleasantly, that he has been following "young Chen's case". "The Chen families of the capital are all related somewhere, you know."
  - He agrees to put the hearing before The Judge. **Far too easily.**
- Kovač's instinct: she shows him nothing about the torn page or the seeding. She doesn't know why.
- **In the corridor,** Pell stops her. "The Minister likes you. Enjoy it." And then: "Did you know Deputy Minister Singh opened a *second* file in October? On you. And on Ashford."

## Ending
In her car, a message from Alexei on a resistance channel, blunt as always: *"Your office booked Isaiah's slot. Your building. Your dead. Tell me why I should trust you."* Kovač has no answer she can prove.

## Notes
- **The red herring** is resolved in Ch 16: the slot request came through Singh's credentials.
- **"Restorative"** echoes Principle Six ("the one who restores it"), a manuscript clue.
- The Chen Zhao lines are hints for a later book. Don't overplay them.
