# Chapter 3: The Upload

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Nov 15, 2106, 13:45–14:58 (flashback into the lost hour)
**Setting:** The public archive annex, basement of the Ministry of Historical Integrity; then the twelfth floor
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Take the reader *inside* the missing hour with Marcus, so that we know what he did, and then take that certainty away in Ch 4. This is a heist chapter: tense, methodical and triumphant.

## Beats
- **13:45:** Marcus and Kira enter through the public annex. They are dead people using a visitor's pass in a false name, and Marcus is wearing glasses he doesn't need. The annex is a quiet room of public terminals, researchers and schoolchildren. Cameras everywhere. Everything he taught himself in Wing C about looking empty is useful again.
- **The plan:** from a public terminal authorised by Singh's pass, feed Elena's drive (the Continuity core) and **the 214 border statements** into the official factorepo, *as facts*, so that The Judge itself must read them. Kira does the technical work and Marcus does the entries. They are a historian and an architect doing, illegally, the jobs they used to do legally.
- **14:10:** the upload begins. It is written as a fact-entry ritual: *"Statement 1 of 214. Border district 7. Witness: …"* Marcus feels, for the first time since August, **useful**.
- **14:21:** a brief, faint *wrongness*. The room seems too bright for a moment, like a word almost remembered. There is no flicker, no pressure and nothing like a storm. Kira doesn't feel it, or doesn't say. Marcus files it: *14:21. Unexplained. UNCHECKED.* (This is seeding at the edge of its range. The annex is in the basement and partly shielded.)
- **The ticking clock:** progress bars, a security guard wandering past twice, a child asking Marcus what he's typing ("History"), Kira's jaw set.
- **14:52:** the upload completes. For three seconds they simply look at each other.
- **14:55:** up to the twelfth floor for the 14:30 meeting, which is running late. The corridor is quiet. Singh's door is **locked**. Her aide's desk is empty. They wait four minutes. Marcus notices an office door ajar down the corridor and a smell of cut flowers. They leave by the stairs at 14:59.
- On the stairs he checks every step with Kira, out loud, in order. Their accounts **match** completely. *CHECKED.* In his coat pocket is Singh's photograph of the torn page, which he hasn't really looked at yet.

## Ending
**15:01**, in the street. Every screen on the boulevard shows the same alert. Kira reads it, and turns white: *PRIMARY REPOSITORY FAILURE, 14:00–15:00.* She whispers the arithmetic: "The upload was *inside* the hour." Everything they just entered, all 214 statements and the whole core, has been erased with the rest.

## Notes
- **It is essential that the chapter reads as true.** It *is* true. Marcus's memory of this hour is accurate, and the book must make the reader trust it before Ch 4 makes Marcus doubt it.
- The 14:21 "wrongness" is a clue to Twist 1. Keep it clearly distinct from a fact storm (`worldbuilding/fact-storms.md` §8).
- The empty aide's desk and the locked door are clues for Twist 2 and for Singh's disappearance.
