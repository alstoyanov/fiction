# Prologue: 13:59

**POV:** Dr. Amara Singh
**Date:** Mon Nov 15, 2106, 13:52–13:59:58
**Setting:** The Deputy Minister's office, twelfth floor, Ministry of Historical Integrity
**Length:** ~2,000 words

## Purpose
The last recorded minutes before the hole. Introduce a sympathetic, intelligent, frightened woman and make the reader like her, so that her death lands. Plant the torn page. End on the last second the record will ever hold.

## Beats
- Singh at her desk: 49, precise, sleeves rolled up, reading glasses on a cord. A career official who read Isaiah's August exposé three times and opened an internal inquiry the next morning. Her colleagues think she's naive. She has stopped caring what they think.
- On the desk is a **visitor's pass** in the name of an archive researcher, made out for a man the Registry says has been dead since August. She arranged it herself. At 14:30 Marcus Chen is going to walk into her office, and at 14:10 his evidence is going to start entering the official record through a public terminal in the annex below. *"If it's in our record, The Judge must read it."*
- **Her safe:** one typed page from Continuity's Ministry correspondence, found weeks ago. Its heading is torn: *PRINCIPLE —*. Page number 31, a small numeral *XI* in the corner, a typed mark she can't read at the foot. One sentence: *"When the record fails, the people will believe whoever restores it. Arrange to be the one who restores it."* Yesterday she photographed it and sent it with the pass: *"Have you seen this before? A.S."*
- **Small unsettling details** that only matter later:
  - Her aide, Lucian Bell, mentions that "your 14:30 has been confirmed", and she frowns. Did she confirm it, or did he?
  - Her calendar shows three other entries at 14:00–15:00 that she doesn't remember adding: a border transfer sign-off, a "deep-cover review", and a "medical crossing waiver".
  - She decides it is overwork.
- Through the window: the capital, the Spire's light pulsing through its core, an ordinary gray Monday.
- She thinks about the page. *Arrange to be the one who restores it.* She has begun to suspect who in this building believes that sentence. She writes a note in the margin of the original: ***"Who restores it? Watch who restores it."***
- A knock. Someone she knows. She says, "Come in." (The reader does not see who it is.)

## Ending
She glances at the clock on her wall: **13:59:58.** The last image is the second hand moving toward the top of the hour.

## Notes
- **Never show who knocks.** Keep it ambiguous whether Singh is about to be murdered, or is about to leave.
- The three calendar entries are the first clue for Twist 2 (her credentials).
- The margin note pays off in Ch 29.
