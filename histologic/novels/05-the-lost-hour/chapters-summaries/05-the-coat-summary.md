# Chapter 5: The Coat

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Flashback Mon Nov 15, 14:05–14:50; present Tue Nov 16 – Wed Nov 17
**Setting:** A prison transport, the lakeshore checkpoint, a Chronos holding facility, the SFW mission in Chronos
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
Isaiah leaves prison and ends up with no nationality. His operation inside the hour made him a legal ghost. It also brings back Sokolov and plants the "only available slot" (Twist 2).

## Beats
- **The flashback: the expulsion.** Thirteen months of captivity end in a transport van. Isaiah wears his coat, with the strips of newsprint sewn into its lining (Book 4 Epilogue). **Captain Sokolov** escorts him in person. It's unexpected and unexplained, and her face gives nothing away.
- **The lakeshore checkpoint in the fog**, the same one where she let the others through. They sit in the van for a minute, silent. She says only: "The order says 14:30. The Director's office requested this slot. It was the only one available." She sounds as if she doesn't believe it either.
- **14:30:** the barrier goes up. Isaiah walks across the line into the Chronos side with the order in his hand. Behind him, very faintly, Sokolov says: "Write carefully, Mr. Okonkwo." Then the van leaves.
- **The present, Nov 16:** Chronos border officials. There is **no record** of his lawful expulsion, since the order's execution falls in the erased hour, and Veridica's Ministry now *denies expelling him*: "Subject absconded from secure care." Chronos has no record of his entry either. **He is stateless**, a man who came out of nowhere, and he is detained.
- **The SFW mission** sends an envoy. It offers asylum and a platform, at a price: he becomes their witness, their propaganda. Isaiah recognises the offer for what it is, and knows he has no other.
- **The notes in the coat:** he checks the lining in the holding cell and the strips are there, every one. His palace's cellar is intact. It is the one thing nobody erased.
- **His reporter's instinct:** why was the transfer slot "the only one available"? He writes it down: *Who chose 14:30?*

## Ending
A Chronos guard slides a newspaper through the bars. The front page shows Marcus's face under the word MURDERER. Isaiah reads it twice, then takes out the burned match he has kept since Unit 3 and starts writing in the paper's margin.

## Notes
- Sokolov's line about the "only slot" is a clue for Twist 2. Her presence also sets up her testimony in Ch 17 and 25: she was outside the seeded district.
- Keep Sokolov deniable and unreadable, as in Book 4.
- Isaiah was expelled into **Chronos**. Veridica and the SFW share no border (canon).
