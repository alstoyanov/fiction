# Chapter 22: The Body

**POV:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka
**Date:** Wed Dec 15, 2106
**Setting:** Tanaka's hospital office in Chronos; the case file by secure link; her trafficking hearing
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
Clinical unease: the "murder" itself begins to look wrong. Plant the later book's biggest clue without resolving it, and win a small victory for the Santos family.

## Beats
- **The trafficking hearing** in Chronos. **Lia Santos**, 16 and furious, testifies that her family *chose* to leave, and says so better than any adult could. The charges are suspended, pending Veridica's own ruling on testimony. It's a small win, and a human one.
- **The case file,** obtained through Kovač's channel. Tanaka reviews it as a physician:
  - **The autopsy:** stab wound, a letter opener, time of death "during the unrecorded interval".
  - **The identification** rests on the **testimony of Singh's staff**. Every one of them was in the seeded district.
  - **Cremation within 24 hours** "at the family's request". Nobody can locate the family. The request was signed through the Deputy Minister's own office.
  - There are no photographs of the face in the file. "Withheld for dignity."
- **Tanaka writes it in the place where she keeps things she can't explain:** ***"Nobody who can't be seeded has seen her body."***
- **Reeves's night cradles** (from Kira's report): Tanaka thinks it through clinically. Someone with access used the annex cradles after hours. On whom?
- **The medical wing:** Reeves is being held in a **Ministry medical wing** with "cradle-equipped stabilisation". She finds that detail in the file and it frightens her. *Why does a murder suspect need a cradle?*
- **Morrison** confirms it: a stabilisation cradle can do anything a Wing C cradle can, including seeding, "if someone brings the right calibration".

## Ending
Tanaka calls Kira: "They're going to seed him." Kira: "Seed him with what?" Tanaka: "With whatever they need him to remember." Neither of them says what that is. Both of them know.

## Notes
- **The body clues** (a quick cremation, a family nobody can find, identification only by seeded testimony) are all for a later book. **Do not resolve them.**
- The medical wing and cradle set up Ch 24.
