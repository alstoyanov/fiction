# Chapter 18: Protective Custody

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Dec 6, 2106
**Setting:** The tribunal chamber; a prison transport; a Ministry safe flat
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
The darkest point for Marcus, and then a rescue by the man who is secretly behind everything. Maximum dread, maximum gratitude. Reunite Marcus and Kira, and connect the manuscript to Book 4.

## Beats
- **The tribunal.** Pell presides in spirit if not in title. The forty statements are read into the record. Marcus's defence: an alibi from a dead woman (Kira) and a timeline nobody can verify. The Judge's contamination analysis of the "subject's persistent false beliefs": **88%**. Verdict: guilty, pending "stabilisation". Transfer to **Secure Care Unit 3** tonight.
- **The transport.** Marcus is strapped into a van heading north, toward a cradle. He does what Wing C taught him: fact entries, the vault, *Kira in formats*. He knows exactly what's waiting and can't stop shaking. (This is the most frightening passage in the book for anyone who read Book 4.)
- **The stop.** Forty minutes out, the van is halted at a Ministry checkpoint. **Thomas Wei** is standing in the rain with a signed order from **Minister Chen Zhao**: the subject is transferred into the lead investigator's **protective custody** as "the only material witness to the lost hour". Pell's people protest, and are overruled.
- **Wei, in the car:** tired and wet. "I told you I didn't believe you did it. I'm not going to let them empty you before I can prove it." Marcus, shaking, **thanks him**. He means it with his whole heart. (Twist 3 depends on this moment.)
- **The safe flat:** a Ministry apartment with guards and no cameras inside ("I asked"). And **Kira**, brought in by Wei through Kovač, under guard, "because a witness needs his witness". The reunion is not triumphant. They hold each other for a long time without talking.
- **Night, the manuscript:** Kira shows him the photograph properly for the first time, and the words of Old Songs' song beside it. Marcus, the historian, sees it at once: there is **no record of the journal anywhere**. "Someone erased a *journal* forty years before anyone erased an hour."
- **The folder link:** Kira touches the numeral on the page: ***XI***. She has seen it before. In Ashford's data centre, the Ministry correspondence folder she grabbed was labelled ***RESTORATION / XI***. The page and the folder came from the same set of papers.
- Marcus tells her about "another day in paradise". Neither of them can place it. Kira, carefully: "Did you see his hand?" Marcus: "He cut it on a server rack."

## Ending
Kira doesn't answer. She looks at the door, where the guard Wei posted is standing, and thinks: *protective custody* means custody. Across the city, Nikolai sends a single line: *"W. asked me today whether you two are sleeping well."*

## Notes
- **Chen Zhao signs the order.** Hint for a later book: Principle Six's author is setting up his "restorers".
- **RESTORATION / XI** ties to Book 4, Ch 24 (`plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md` §2).
- Wei's rescue must read as sincere heroism on a first reading.
