# The Lost Hour: Chapter Summaries

Based on `plots/05-the-lost-hour-full.md` (v3.1), with the drafting refinements listed below. The earlier summaries and chapter drafts in this folder followed the abandoned v2 plot. They are replaced, and remain in git history.

**Structure:** Prologue + 31 chapters + Epilogue, in four parts.
**Target length:** ~3,000–3,600 words per chapter (~110,000–115,000 words).
**Tone:** a mystery thriller. The present-day investigation is intercut with flashbacks into the missing hour. Paranoid, clever, intimate. Every chapter ends on a turn.
**Canon rules:** `PROJECT-RULES.md` §4–6 and `worldbuilding/`. Seeding is not a storm, removing an interface kills, and first names are never reused.

## Drafting Refinements (Beyond Plot v3.1)
1. **The two wrong details are different kinds of wrong.** All forty witnesses remember the killer in **Wing C prison gray** (that is **seeded**) and with a **bandaged left hand** (that is **real**). Seeding writes over what a mind finds most important about a memory, such as a face and clothing, and leaves small peripheral details alone. The witnesses really saw the killer; the seeding put Marcus's face on him. **Thomas Wei's left hand is bandaged** from the first chapter he appears in ("cut it on a server rack"). This is the main fair-play clue for Twist 3.
2. **Twist 2 goes deeper.** Every operation that was steered into the hour (Singh's 14:30 meeting, Isaiah's expulsion slot, the deep-cover warning, the defectors' window, Elena's tip) can be traced back to **Deputy Minister Singh's own credentials**. The dead woman scheduled the hour. Was she a victim whose credentials were stolen, or part of the design? **This book does not answer that.** It is resolved when Singh returns in a later book.
3. **A red herring: Kovač** (Ch 15–16). She was in the building, she requested Isaiah's time slot, and Singh's inquiry was aimed at her. The hardliners, and briefly Alexei, suspect her.
4. **The worst threat to Marcus** (Ch 17–18): the hardliners order him moved to **Secure Care Unit 3 for re-correction**. Wei blocks the transfer. Marcus becomes grateful to Wei, which makes the betrayal land harder.
5. **Wei and Nikolai** (Ch 20): Wei hands "Pieter" a folded page he "dropped", Dmitri's rooftop primer page from Book 4. Did he read it? Does he know?
6. **Reeves's warning** (Ch 21): "Someone has been using my cradles at night." This sets up the seeding of his confession.
7. **Dmitri recognises a way of walking** (Ch 31): he sees Wei cross the Commission hall and knows how that body moves. It confirms the man at 14:21.
8. **Officer Kade** (Stories 01–02) makes the arrest in Ch 4: "Mr. Chen. The Registry says you are dead."
9. **Wei's alibi is seeded as well** (Ch 25 and 28). At the hearing he produces dozens of colleagues who remember him at his desk in the Ministry district from 13:30 until the Ministry's call at 15:20. It kills the suspicion. But Maya timed his *arrival* at the Regional Factorepo at 15:32 (Ch 1): twelve minutes, from an office twenty-five minutes away. And his colleagues were inside the seeded district. The alibi was written too.
10. **Wei's scar** (Ch 28): by January the bandage is gone and a thin pink scar remains. The injury was real. How he got it stays open.
