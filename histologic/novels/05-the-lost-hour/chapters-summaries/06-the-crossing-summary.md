# Chapter 6: The Crossing

**POV:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka
**Date:** Flashback Mon Nov 15, 14:00–14:45; present Tue Nov 16 – Thu Nov 18
**Setting:** A gap in the fence on the contested strip, a car, Tanaka's Chronos teaching hospital, a Chronos interrogation room
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
A human-scale crossing during the hour, and its legal aftermath. Establish Tanaka's life in Chronos and Morrison's method, and put a family's future at stake.

## Beats
- **The flashback.** The **Santos family** at a gap in the fence: Davi (a Ministry records clerk), Lucia, and their daughter **Lia** (16), who is furious to be leaving her friends. Their "medical crossing waiver" was arranged through a Ministry contact. (It is one of the entries in Singh's calendar, a Twist 2 clue.)
- **Morrison** spoofs their telemetry at the fence at 14:20, exactly as in Book 4: a real flatline signature, and then zero. Tanaka watches him work. His hands are steady now. Lia asks if it will hurt. "No. You'll just stop existing to them." Lia: "Great."
- **14:44:** they are across and into Tanaka's car. Lia cries, and then sleeps.
- **The present, Nov 16:** Chronos authorities are polite and suspicious. Veridica claims three citizens were **abducted**. There is no record they left voluntarily, because their exit is in the erased hour and their telemetry "died". Tanaka is charged with **trafficking**.
- She cannot prove that the family *chose* to leave. Only they can, and testifying means being seen, being named, and perhaps being returned.
- **Her own situation:** she is officially dead in Veridica and working in Chronos under the protection of the hospital director. Her cover is thin, and the charge could expose it.
- She watches Lia refuse to eat in the detention family room and recognises the look: it is Kira's in 7-K. Anger, which is the good sign.
- **Morrison** is quiet all week. On the third evening he tells her something he has been sitting on: during the crossing, at 14:21, his interface registered a "carrier" in the Veridican broadcast, faint and far off, **with the gain profile he designed**. He thought he'd imagined it.

## Ending
Tanaka puts down her tea very carefully, the way she did at her kitchen table in 2105, reading *to baseline* (Book 4). "James. Say that again."

## Notes
- Morrison's 14:21 carrier sets up Ch 11 (seeding). The crossing is outside the Ministry district, so the family was **not** seeded, which makes them clean witnesses (Ch 25).
- There is **no implant removal** anywhere. Spoofing is canon.
