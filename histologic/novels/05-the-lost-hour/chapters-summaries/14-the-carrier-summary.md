# Chapter 14: The Carrier

**POV:** Maya Reeves
**Date:** Mon Nov 29, 2106
**Setting:** The node logs in Wei's investigation room; Maya's flat
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
A technical discovery that becomes a personal wound. The reader learns Maya's secret with her, and the investigation gets a false trail that points convincingly at Dr. Reeves.

## Beats
- **Wei's assignment:** "The Ministry district node. Every packet at the edges of the hour." He trusts her with it. She's proud, and it shows.
- **The discovery:** in a buffer that survived the erasure, she finds the injection point of a **carrier signal** on the Ministry district node at 14:18, three minutes before the man at the side entrance.
- **The code comment.** In the injected payload's header there is a developer's comment in an idiosyncratic style: a lowercase Latin tag, a joke about bees. Maya grew up with that joke on post-it notes on the fridge. **It is her father's.**
- **The reveal (to the reader):** Maya is **Dr. Reeves's daughter**. Her parents divorced when she was ten, and she took her mother's side and her father's surname out of spite. They haven't spoken in four years. She knew what he did at Ashford from Isaiah's article, and has spent August to November ashamed of her own name.
- **Her conclusion:** her father did this. He's alive and hidden, and he killed a Deputy Minister to stop her inquiry. It fits Continuity perfectly.
- **Her dilemma:** reporting it would make her the daughter who turned in her father, and would end her career by association. Hiding it would make her complicit.
- She tells no one but **Pieter**, in a stairwell, crying and furious. He listens carefully, the way Nikolai does. He doesn't tell her anything about himself.
- **Wei, later:** he passes her desk and glances at her screen for a fraction of a second too long. "Anything?" "Not yet." He nods. "Take your time." She has the sudden, irrational feeling that he already knows.

## Ending
At home Maya opens a message account she hasn't used in four years and types to her father: *"I know it was you."* She deletes it. She types it again. She sends it. **A reply comes in forty seconds:** *"No, Maya. It wasn't. But I know who writes like me now."*

## Notes
- **The code comment** is a deliberate plant: Wei borrowed Reeves's style to frame him. The characters don't learn this until Ch 28.
- Reeves's reply is the hook for Ch 20–21 (the annex).
- Keep Maya sympathetic: she's brave and wrong for the right reasons.
