# Chapter 8: Forty Witnesses

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Tue Nov 16 – Wed Nov 17, 2106
**Setting:** The border-zone cottage, then a resistance safe room; Nikolai's encrypted feed
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
Kira, working alone and hunted, finds the two technical keys to the mystery: the **signature** on the erasure, and the **identical detail** in the testimony. It ends Part One on Twist 1's question.

## Beats
- **Getting out:** from the window on Monday night, through the capital on foot, with the drive under her coat. She is a dead woman who helped build the city's surveillance, and she knows where its blind spots are. At dawn she's in a resistance car heading east.
- **The cottage:** Marcus's empty chair. His lists, left on the table. She can't look at them, so she works.
- **Nikolai's feed:** as "Pieter Lang" he has access to the erasure diagnostics and sends her the raw architecture of the hole.
  - **The erasure has a signature**: a way of structuring the deletion so that it leaves no contradictions.
  - She has seen it once before. **It is the deletion of her own fact** (Story 02), the one she spent Book 4 believing no one could do. **The same hand.** Her fury is ice-cold.
- **The forty statements,** which Nikolai obtained from the enforcement file:
  - She reads them the way she reads logs, for patterns. Every one describes the man in **"gray, like a patient's clothes"**, the colour of Wing C.
  - And every one mentions **a bandaged left hand**. The wording varies, but the detail doesn't. "White bandage." "Wrapped hand." "Holding it like it hurt."
  - Marcus wore his dark coat. His hands are unmarked.
- **Her reasoning, as an engineer:** people misremember *differently*. Forty honest people can't make the *same* mistake down to the hand. Either forty people are lying in unison (impossible to organise in a day), or something gave forty people the same memory.
- A memory from Book 4: Reeves in the data centre, *"erase, then seed"*, and *"test carrier signals are already running."*
- **Two details, two different kinds of wrong?** Kira half-notices something: the gray is *Marcus's* (his Wing C file photo). But the bandage isn't his. It's somebody's. She can't finish the thought yet, and puts it in a note: *Bandage: whose?*

## Ending
She writes three lines on the back of Marcus's list and sends them to Tanaka, the only neural specialist she trusts:
> *Forty identical false memories. Same wrong clothes. Same wrong hand.*
> *Someone didn't just erase the hour.*
> *Someone **wrote** it.*

## Notes
- **The signature** connects Story 02, Book 4 (the 0.58→0.73) and this erasure. It pays off in Ch 28.
- **"Bandage: whose?"** is the reader's fair-play invitation for Twist 3. Wei's bandage was shown in Ch 1.
- End of Part One.
