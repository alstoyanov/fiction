# Chapter 31: The Commission

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Fri Jan 14, 2107
**Setting:** The Commission's inaugural session, the Justice Center; the corridor afterwards
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
The quiet duel. Two men who each know what the other knows, in public and politely. The book closes with the reformers stepping into a role that was designed for them, and choosing to keep their own record.

## Beats
- **The inaugural session:** broadcast nationally. Chen Zhao presides. Wei sits at the technical table, the pink scar on his left hand in the lights.
- **Dmitri at the back of the hall.** Wei crosses the floor to the podium, light and athletic, taking the two steps up at once. Dmitri stops breathing. *He walks well.* It is the man from 14:21. Dmitri catches Marcus's eye and gives a single, tiny nod. (He saw it in Ch 7, and now knows the walk.)
- **Marcus's oath.** Every commissioner takes it. His version is word for word the official text, and **every word is true**: to *"keep the record honestly, to hear every witness, and to trust no single account, however trusted"*. The chamber hears a reformer's pledge. Wei hears it differently. (Isaiah's column is folded in Marcus's pocket.)
- **The first decision:** Marcus proposes, as the Commission's first act, that every Lost Hour testimony be **published with its witnesses' names and cross-checks**, *"so that the record can be checked by many hands."* It passes unanimously. Wei votes for it, smiling. (Both men understand it as a move.)
- **The corridor afterwards:**
  - Marcus: **"Another day in paradise, Thomas."**
  - Wei, pleased and genuinely delighted: **"You remember."**
  - Marcus: **"No. I checked."**
  - Wei's smile holds a beat too long. Neither of them says anything else. Wei inclines his head, the way you acknowledge a good move, and walks away.
- **Kira** is waiting at the end of the corridor with her first written journal under her arm. Its first page is the Journal passage in her handwriting, and the second is Old Songs' ferry verse.

## Ending
They walk out into the cold. Marcus takes out his lists and, at the bottom of CHECKED, writes a new entry in fact-entry format: *"Jan 14, 2107. Justice Center. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Wei, T. Event: he knows that I know. Verification: witnesses — Osman, K.; Volkov, D."* Two witnesses. For now, it's enough.

## Notes
- **Wei is never exposed in this book.** Later books need him free, and he "evolves".
- The corridor exchange is the book's signature scene. Draft it with restraint.
