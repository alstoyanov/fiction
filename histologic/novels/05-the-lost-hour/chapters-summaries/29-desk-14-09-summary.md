# Chapter 29: Desk 14-09

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Jan 10, 2107
**Setting:** The Regional Factorepo staff archive, fourteenth floor; the empty desks; the Commission offices
**Length:** ~3,700 words

## Purpose
**Twist 3, part two.** It turns personal and then cosmic: the man is someone Marcus sat beside for three years, and the victory was the design.

## Beats
- **"Another day in paradise."** Kira's discovery makes Marcus go back to his lists and to the phrase he couldn't place. He goes where a historian goes: to the record. The **Regional Factorepo**, the fourteenth floor where he used to work. It's his first time back since *The Believer's Fall*.
- **The floor:** workstations and soft humming. His old desk, **14-08**, now belongs to someone else. The desk beside it, **14-09**, is empty.
- **The staff roster, 2102–2105:** *WEI, THOMAS. Historian (secondment from Systems Architecture). Desk 14-09.*
- **Thomas.** The colleague who said *"Another day in paradise"*, who asked him to lunch, who joked that he'd die at that desk (Story 01). Marcus's memory kept the name and the voice. **Continuity took the face.** For three years the man sat half a metre away and watched him.
- **The reconstruction,** in Marcus's fact-entry voice and in order:
  - Wei **chose** the Believer.
  - He **typed the 73**.
  - He **deleted Kira's fact** to acquire the Architect.
  - He **erased the hour**, and with it the upload.
  - He **seeded** forty witnesses and his own alibi.
  - He **walked into Singh's office** at 14:21 with a bandaged hand (whatever happened in there).
  - He **seeded Reeves** with the murder.
  - He **rescued Marcus** from Unit 3 so that the restorer would survive to restore.
- **The design.** Kovač brings the original torn page, recovered from Singh's sealed safe, which the case file never opened. In the margin, in Singh's hand: ***"Who restores it? Watch who restores it."*** Marcus lays it beside Old Songs' passage:
  - *"When the record fails, the people will believe whoever restores it. Arrange to be the one who restores it."*
  - *"…provided it is given gently, and by someone it trusts."*
- **The meaning:** the Lost Hour Doctrine, the thing they fought for, turns Veridica into a country where testimony is law, *and testimony can be seeded*. **Whoever can write memory can now write law.** The heroes restored the record and they are its trusted face. They are the gentle hands.
- Marcus reads Isaiah's spiked column (Ch 27) and finally understands it.

## Ending
He goes back to the fourteenth floor at closing time and sits, just for a minute, at desk 14-09. On the underside of the desk, where a bored man might carve something, there is nothing at all. It is perfectly clean. Of course it is.
