# Chapter 28: The Signature

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Sat Jan 8, 2107
**Setting:** The Commission's founding reception at the Ministry; the new Commission archive room
**Length:** ~3,700 words

## Purpose
**Twist 3, part one.** A celebration turns into a discovery. The architect sees the one thing that can't be seeded: code.

## Beats
- **The reception:** chandeliers, speeches, Chen Zhao warm and fatherly. He calls Marcus "cousin" in his toast, half joking. Marcus and Kira are appointed commissioners, and **Thomas Wei is Technical Director**. Wei's bandage is gone, and there is a thin pink scar across the back of his left hand.
- **Maya is there** as the Commission's junior technician. Nikolai beside her, with his own face now. They are an obvious couple and pretending not to be.
- **The archive room.** Wei asks Kira, graciously, to take charge of ingesting the Lost Hour case files into the Commission archive: "Who better?" She begins that night, alone, while the party goes on upstairs.
- **The confession file.** She opens the metadata of Reeves's confession record, the formatted file the medical wing produced. Inside its structure is **the signature**: the same way of arranging data that she found on the erasure (Ch 8), on the deletion of her own fact (Story 02), and on Marcus's adjusted index (Book 4). It's the same hand. Someone wrote Reeves's confession *file*, and not just his memory.
- **The author field.** It has been scrubbed, but not in the cache the archive keeps of the first save: ***T. Wei.***
- **The alibi collapses.** Kira goes to find **Maya** and asks her one question: "On November 15th, where was Wei when you found the gap?" Maya: "He wasn't there. He was appointed at 15:20 and *arrived* at 15:32. I remember because I timed it." Kira: "His alibi says he was at his desk in the Ministry district from 13:30." Maya works it out herself: "…Inside the seeded district." The alibi was written too.
- **The hand:** Kira thinks of forty statements, one detail each, the only true detail left in forty seeded memories. *The killer had a bandaged left hand.*

## Ending
Kira climbs the stairs back to the party with the file on a tablet under her arm. Wei is at the top, smiling, holding two glasses. "Kira. There you are. Come, the Minister wants a photograph of the restorers." She takes the glass. She smiles for the camera. Her hand doesn't shake. It is the hardest thing she's ever done since 7-K.

## Notes
- **The proof is technical** (the signature and the author cache) **and testimonial** (Maya's timing). Both are fragile. The signature is Kira's reading, and Maya's memory is one person's.
- The pink scar: the injury was real. Cut at the scene, perhaps. Leave it open.
