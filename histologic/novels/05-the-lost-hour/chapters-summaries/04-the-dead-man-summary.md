# Chapter 4: The Dead Man

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Mon Nov 15, 2106, 18:40 – Tue Nov 16, 04:00
**Setting:** The rented room in the capital, an Enforcement Center interview room, a holding cell
**Length:** ~3,400 words

## Purpose
The accusation. The book's central tension is set up here: the only person who knows Marcus is innocent is Marcus, and he can't trust his own memory.

## Beats
- **18:40:** the rented room above a laundry. Kira is packing the drive. Marcus is trying to write down the hour while it's fresh. The news: forty witnesses, one face, *his*, taken from the August exposé.
- **The raid.** Enforcers on the stairs. Kira goes out through the window with the drive and the photograph, and Marcus holds the door for the four seconds she needs. He doesn't run. Book 4 taught him that running proves the charge.
- **Officer Kade** (Stories 01–02). Older and grayer, just as matter-of-fact. "Mr. Chen." A pause. "The Registry says you are dead." The echo of Story 01 is deliberate: the same interview room, the same camera, the same courtesy.
- **The interview:**
  - Kade lays out the forty statements. Ministry staff saw Marcus Chen walk into the Deputy Minister's office at 14:20: "gray clothes, like a patient"; "his left hand was bandaged".
  - Marcus: "I was in the annex. I have a witness." Kade: "Your witness is also dead, Mr. Chen." There is no record, because the hour is gone. **Forty living witnesses against two dead ones.**
  - Kade is not cruel. He asks sincerely: "How do you explain forty people?"
- **The horror.** Alone in the holding cell, Marcus takes out his lists (they let him keep them; they're "only paper"): CHECKED / UNCHECKED / HOPES. He looks at the entry for the field, the false memory from Book 4 that he would have sworn to. And he realises he **can't be sure** about today either. What if the upload is a field? What if he went up at 14:20? *He has a false memory that feels exactly like a true one. How would he know?*
- He goes through the hour step by step, as a historian would. It holds. It still holds. *That is exactly what the field did too.*
- **04:00:** he writes a single new line at the bottom of the CHECKED column, and then crosses out the word CHECKED.

## Ending
Morning news, through the cell's slot: *"THE DEAD MAN WHO KILLED."* Marcus Chen is the most famous corpse in Veridica, and the capital wants him corrected again, properly this time. Kade, at the door: "Undersecretary Pell has asked for you to be moved to secure care. For your own stability."

## Notes
- **The witnesses' two details:** gray (seeded) and the bandaged left hand (real). Kade reads both aloud. Marcus's hands are unmarked, and Kade notes it without comment.
- The threat of **Secure Care Unit 3** begins here and pays off in Ch 17–18.
- Kade appears again, briefly, in Ch 25.
