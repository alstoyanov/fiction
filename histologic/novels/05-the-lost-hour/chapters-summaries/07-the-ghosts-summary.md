# Chapter 7: The Ghosts

**POV:** Alexei Volkov
**Date:** Flashback Mon Nov 15, 14:15–14:45; present Tue Nov 16 – Wed Nov 17
**Setting:** The Ministry district, a truck, the resistance barn
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
The extraction, as action. Plant **Dmitri's sighting at 14:21** and **Old Songs' question** (Twist 2). Introduce the "ghosts".

## Beats
- **The flashback.** Alexei drives a delivery truck into the Ministry district. Old Songs sits in the passenger seat, humming the Lakeshore Air. Dmitri is on a rooftop across from the Ministry's side entrance, on overwatch.
- The job is to pull out **Marina Holt** (four years in deep cover as a Ministry archivist) and **Jakob Lind** (an enforcement dispatcher). Elena's source warned that "their covers won't survive the week".
- **14:21:** Dmitri, over the radio, flat and precise: "A man. Dark jacket, staff card. Side entrance. Not one of ours." Alexei: "Ignore it." Dmitri: "…He walks well." Remember that line.
- **14:25–14:43:** the extraction itself. Marina walks out with a box of files as if she's going to lunch. Jakob is late, and there are three minutes of pure tension. At 14:38 the brothers feel a faint *wrongness* through the bond (the seeding). Dmitri, curiously, feels it *less*.
- They're out clean by 14:43. Old Songs is humming the "yes" phrase from Wing C.
- **The present:** Marina and Jakob are **ghosts**. There is no record they left their posts, because it is all in the erased hour. The Ministry calls it kidnapping, Chronos is accused of complicity, and there is a diplomatic incident.
- At the barn Elena coordinates and swears. Alexei, energised, wants to go public at once. Dmitri, quiet, keeps returning to the man at the side entrance.
- **The first time anyone asks it.** In the truck, going home, Old Songs had said nothing for an hour. Now, at the barn table, stirring his tea, he asks: "Who told you their covers wouldn't last the week?" Silence. Elena: "My source." Old Songs: "And who told your source?"

## Ending
Marina, unpacking the box of files she carried out, finds a Ministry diary page from Singh's office: tomorrow's schedule for the Deputy Minister, printed on the morning of the 15th. At 14:00–15:00 it lists four meetings, not one. Alexei reads them. **They are all of them:** the upload, the border transfer, the medical crossing, the deep-cover review.

- The diary page is the concrete lead for Twist 2 (Ch 16). Keep it unexplained for now.
- The triplets feel the seeding *less* (the bond), which sets up Ch 13.
