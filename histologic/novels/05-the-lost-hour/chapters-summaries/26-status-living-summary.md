# Chapter 26: Status: Living

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Wed Dec 29, 2106
**Setting:** A Registry office; the capital in celebration; the safe flat
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
The triumph, written as a real, earned, joyful high point, so that its later reversal hurts. Old Songs' refusal adds a note that doesn't fit the celebration.

## Beats
- **The first use of the Doctrine** is on the dead themselves. In a plain Registry office, with witnesses who each swear to the others, the record is corrected:
  > *CHEN, MARCUS. STATUS: LIVING.*
  - It is the first fact in Veridica's history entered on human testimony alone. Marcus, the Junior Historian, watches it verify, green, on a clerk's screen, and has to sit down.
- **Then the others:** Kira, Dmitri, Alexei, Nikolai and Tanaka, by link. Isaiah's legal crossing is affirmed. The Santos family's departure is recorded as voluntary, and Marina and Jakob's as a resignation.
- **The verdicts:**
  - Marcus's tribunal conviction is **quashed**, and the order for Unit 3 cancelled.
  - Reeves is **convicted** of Singh's murder on his own confession and "concordant testimony". He is also held for his Continuity crimes, which are real.
  - Tanaka's trafficking charge is dropped.
- **The capital celebrates:** crowds, the "Day of Testimony" already being proposed, Isaiah's face on Synthesis screens. Marcus is recognised in the street, and a stranger shakes his hand and cries.
- **Old Songs declines.** Offered the same correction, he refuses it, courteously, at the clerk's window: "I've been dead to the record for forty years, one way or another. It suits me. The record can go on thinking I'm furniture." Only Marcus understands that he's keeping himself somewhere the record can't reach, and wonders why he'd need to now.
- **Marcus and Kira** at the safe flat, which is no longer a safe flat. They are free. They are legally alive. For one evening they let themselves be happy, and the chapter lets them.
- **The shadow:** Reeves's words, and "T.W.?" in his UNCHECKED column. Marcus looks at it and, for the first time, **erases** it. Wei testified at the hearing and had an alibi. Wei saved him. *Please be wrong* was answered.

## Ending
A courier at the door with an envelope bearing the Ministry seal: *Minister Chen Zhao requests the honour of your presence, and Ms. Osman's, at the founding of the Historical Integrity Commission, Saturday January 8.* Clipped to it is a handwritten card: *"With gratitude for what you have restored. — T. Wei, Technical Director (designate)."*

## Notes
- Let the joy be real.
- Erasing "T.W.?" is Marcus choosing to trust. Ch 29 makes him regret it.
