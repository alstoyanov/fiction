# Epilogue: One Year Later

**POV:** Rotating vignettes, ending on a coda in Wei's journal
**Date:** Tue Nov 15, 2107, the first Day of Testimony
**Setting:** The Lost Hour memorial in the capital, and elsewhere
**Length:** ~2,600 words

## Purpose
Show where everyone is, the cost carried forward, and the threads pointing onward. End on a chill.

## Vignettes
- **The memorial:** a plain stone in the plaza, near the fountain where Marcus first met Elena. *"To the hour that was lost, and the truth that was found."* The first **Day of Testimony**: citizens read aloud what they remember of one ordinary day.
- **The reunion:** all seven Continuity subjects are together for the first time since Wing C. Isaiah has flown in from SFW, on a Synthesis passport, with his asylum still conditional. Tanaka is here from Chronos, where the first Chronos proposal for "salience therapy" has been **withdrawn** after her eleventh memorandum. The brothers stand side by side. Nikolai has his own face, and Maya is holding his hand.
- **Old Songs** is 69 and still officially dead. At the ceremony he sings **"Given Gently"** to the Lakeshore Air. The crowd hears a lovely old lullaby. Nobody knows the words come from a journal that never existed. Kira mouths them with him.
- **The journals:** Kira's has **thirty-one contributors** who check each other. There are dozens like it now, a quiet and scattered movement. The Commission has published every Lost Hour testimony with its cross-checks.
- **Reeves** is in prison, visited by Maya once a month. He still remembers the letter opener. She has stopped arguing with him about it.
- **The warning:** Nikolai quietly shows the seven a Registry anomaly. An erasure is being prepared, in a **different nation**, with the same signature. "It's happening again." Marcus: "Then we remember again. All of us. Written down."
- **In the crowd:** Tanaka sees a woman in a gray coat at the edge of the plaza, watching Marcus: about fifty, reading glasses on a cord. When Tanaka looks again, she's gone. Tanaka thinks of a body that nobody who couldn't be seeded ever saw. She writes it down.

## Coda: Wei's Journal
In a private hand, the journal a later book will expose:
> *"Nov 15, 2107. The Doctrine is law. The reformers are grateful, and trusted. Chen knows, and says nothing; he has begun keeping records of his own, with many hands, all checking each other. That was not in the design. Neither was the old man. I find I want to see what they build."*

## Final Line
*"History can be erased, but the truth endures in those who lived it, and in those who wrote it down."*

## Notes
- **The woman in the gray coat is Singh** (revealed in a later book). Keep the description to details the Prologue has already planted, such as the reading glasses on a cord.
- **"Chen knows"** in Wei's journal refers to Marcus Chen. It is not a slip about Chen Zhao, but let the double reading stand.
- The erasure in another nation is the hook for the next book.
