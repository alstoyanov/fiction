# Chapter 10: Another Day in Paradise

**POV:** Marcus Chen
**Date:** Fri Nov 19, 2106
**Setting:** Detention at the Enforcement Center
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
Marcus in the dark: doubting himself, under interrogation, and then shown unexpected kindness by the man who is secretly behind all of it. This chapter holds the book's most important planted line.

## Beats
- **Four days of interrogation.** The Ministry's questioners are harsher than Kade. They read him the forty statements again and again. *"You were in gray. Your hand was bandaged."*
- **The wall:** Marcus is allowed a pencil. He draws his three columns on the cell wall: **CHECKED / UNCHECKED / HOPES**. Under CHECKED he writes the upload, step by step. Then, at 03:00 on the third night, he moves it to UNCHECKED, because he can't bear to be as certain as he was about the field.
- **A new fear:** Undersecretary Pell's request to move him to **Secure Care Unit 3 for "re-baselining"** is under review. He dreams of the white room, and wakes up reciting fact entries.
- **Wei visits,** alone, with two cups of tea. He is gentle and precise, and seems genuinely troubled.
  - "I don't believe you did it, Mr. Chen. That isn't the same as being able to prove you didn't. Help me prove it."
  - He asks careful, intelligent questions about the annex, the timing and the stairs. He *listens*. He is the first person in four days to treat Marcus as a witness rather than a suspect.
  - He notices the wall and reads it without asking. "You keep your own record." A pause. "Good."
  - **His left hand** rests on the table, bandaged. Marcus looks at it, and in his mind the forty statements read *bandaged left hand*. He feels a tiny cold drop. Then Wei lifts the cup with his right hand, and the moment passes. *Coincidence. Lots of people cut their hands.*
- As he stands to leave, Wei smiles and says lightly, like an old joke:
  > **"Another day in paradise."**
- **Something moves in Marcus.** It is the recognition without content from Book 4, like the corridor with Kira: *I know you.* He can't place it. He reaches for it and finds a smooth blank place (Continuity took the faces of his old life).
- He writes on the wall, under UNCHECKED: *"'Another day in paradise.' Where have I heard that?"*

## Ending
Kade, at the door an hour later: the transfer to Unit 3 has been **approved** for Monday. "I'm sorry, Mr. Chen." Marcus sits on the bunk with his head in his hands. For the first time since Wing C it isn't a performance.

## Notes
- **"Another day in paradise"** is Thomas's line from Story 01, the first page Marcus ever appears on. Readers of Story 01 may catch it. That's intended.
- The bandage, Wei's kindness and the "paradise" line together make up Twist 3's central clue cluster.
- The transfer to Unit 3 is the ticking clock for Ch 17–18.
