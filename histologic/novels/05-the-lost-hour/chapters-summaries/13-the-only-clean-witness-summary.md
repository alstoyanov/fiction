# Chapter 13: The Only Clean Witness

**POV:** Dmitri Volkov
**Date:** Fri Nov 26, 2106
**Setting:** The resistance barn; a rooftop in the Ministry district, in memory
**Length:** ~3,100 words

## Purpose
Establish that the triplets are the one clean record of the hour, and make that a moral trap: testifying means coming back from the dead.

## Beats
- **Dmitri goes back over 14:21** the way an enforcer reviews a surveillance tape: the angle from the roof, the light, the man's height, his dark jacket, the staff card, **the hand he held slightly away from his body**. And his gait: "He walks well." Economical, athletic, *not old*.
- **Why wasn't he seeded?** He was well within the node's range. Tanaka, on a call, gives the answer: the **bond**. The triplets' shared salience defeated Continuity's correction once, and it seems to have blunted the seeding too. Alexei and Nikolai also felt the wrongness only faintly.
- **Old Songs' test:** he asks Dmitri to *sing the moment back* to him, or at least to say it in rhythm, as a recitation. Dmitri, embarrassed, does it. It holds, detail for detail, with no drift. Old Songs nods: "That one's yours. Nobody wrote it."
- **The trap:**
  - Dmitri's memory may be the only unseeded account of 14:21 in the capital.
  - Legally he is **dead**: a corpse can't testify.
  - To testify he must *prove he's alive*, which means revealing that the Volkovs escaped, that Kovač certified false deaths, and that the others are alive too.
- **The argument:**
  - **Alexei:** "Then we all come back. Let them choke on it."
  - **Nikolai**, by coded message from inside the team: "Not yet. I'm the only one inside. If you come back, they'll look at everyone who shares your face."
  - **Dmitri:** caught between them, as Nikolai always used to be.
- **A turn in Dmitri:** he realises he isn't afraid of arrest. He's afraid of *mattering*, because a witness has to be believed and he has never been believed. The enforcer's truth has always been the system's.

## Ending
Dmitri decides to testify when the time comes. Then the news comes in. Marcus's transfer to Unit 3 was **postponed on Monday at the lead investigator's request** ("the subject is my only material witness"). But Undersecretary Pell has answered by announcing a **tribunal** for the "dead murderer" on **Monday Dec 6**, "without delay for procedural novelties". The transfer will follow the verdict. The clean witness has ten days.

## Notes
- **"He walks well" and "not old"** are clues for Twist 3 (Reeves is 54, Wei 45).
- Nikolai's warning ("everyone who shares your face") raises his danger for Ch 20.
- **Wei postpones the transfer:** a quiet act of protection, which is part of Twist 3's misdirection. It pays off in Ch 18.
