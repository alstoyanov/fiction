# Chapter 2: The Hearing Room

**POV:** Director Helena Kovač
**Date:** Mon Nov 15, 2106, 13:40–17:00 (the hour seen from inside a room)
**Setting:** The fourth-floor hearing room at the Ministry, the building where Singh died
**Length:** ~3,000 words

## Purpose
Show the hour from inside the Ministry, from someone who knows too much. Plant the **first hint of seeding**: twelve people who were in the same room remember it differently. Establish Kovač's danger.

## Beats
- **Kovač at her own inquiry**, the ninth session since August. She answers precisely and gives nothing away, as in Book 4. Inquiry Chair **Halvorsen** is polite and hungry for a conviction. Undersecretary **Iver Pell**, the Ministry hardliner, watches from the side.
- She knows exactly who is upstairs today: Marcus, "dead" and on Singh's pass. She arranged Isaiah's expulsion for this same afternoon, 14:30, in the slot a clerk told her was "the only one available". She feels the whole plan in her chest like a held breath.
- **The hour passes in the hearing room** (she will later realise she can't reconstruct it well). There is questioning. A recess. Water poured. At some point, faintly, a sensation: *the room is slightly too bright*. Nothing flickers. She files it as fatigue.
- **15:01:** alarms, phones, confusion. **15:05:** Singh's aide, Lucian Bell, is heard screaming on the twelfth floor. Word spreads: the Deputy Minister is dead.
- **The disturbing detail.** While the room waits for security, Halvorsen dictates a summary of the session "for the record, since the record is gone". The twelve people present **disagree about small things**: who asked which question, whether the recess was at 14:25 or 14:40, whether Pell left the room. Each of them is *certain*. Kovač is certain too, and finds she's contradicting a clerk she would trust with her life.
- She tells herself it's shock. She doesn't believe it.
- **Pell's first move:** by 16:30 he has stated in the corridor, in front of witnesses, that "the Continuity escapees" are the obvious suspects. Kovač says nothing. She's the Director who certified them dead.
- **Walking out at dusk,** she understands her position: if the escapees are alive, then her certification was a lie, and the inquiry will find out *today*.

## Ending
In her car, her comm lights up with a message from an unknown number, in plain text: *"THEY ARE SAYING HE DID IT. HE DIDN'T. — K."* Then the evening news: forty witnesses, one face. Kovač sits in the dark garage and, for the first time since August, is frightened for herself.

## Notes
- The **disagreements in the hearing room** are Twist 1's earliest clue. They are not identical false memories but small scrambles: people on the edge of a node's range get partial seeding. Tanaka explains this in Ch 11.
- **Pell** and **Halvorsen** are recurring pressure.
