# Chapter 25: The Hearing

**POV:** Director Helena Kovač
**Date:** Thu Dec 23 – Mon Dec 27, 2106
**Setting:** The Judge's hearing chamber at the Justice Center
**Length:** ~3,800 words

## Purpose
The courtroom set piece. Testimony against the record, and the "dead" as witnesses. Wei produces an alibi that seems to clear him of the reader's suspicion.

## Beats
- **The question put to The Judge:** *where the factorepo holds no record, may facts be established by testimony?* Kovač argues for it, and Pell argues against: "A nation of memories is a nation of liars."
- **The witnesses:**
  - **Dmitri, Alexei and Nikolai.** Nikolai comes out of hiding for this, which ends the Pieter Lang cover. They are shown to be three identical men, all "dead", all unseeded, with matching accounts of the hour. Tanaka's written analysis explains why the bond resists seeding.
  - **Captain Sokolov**, in uniform, from outside the seeded district: the 14:30 escort, and the 14:02 confirmation from Singh's credentials (Ch 17).
  - **Marina Holt and Jakob Lind**, the ghosts.
  - **The Santos family**, by link from Chronos. Lia is fierce again.
  - **Reeves's confession**, read into the record.
  - **Old Songs** is called and declines, courteously: "My memory isn't evidence. It's a song." (The chamber laughs. Kovač doesn't.)
- **The dangerous moment:** Pell raises Kovač's certification of the escapees' deaths. If the witnesses are alive, she falsified the record. Kovač answers from the stand: "Yes. And if the record can be wrong about the dead, it can be wrong about anything. That is the question before you." It's her bravest act, and it may end her career.
- **Wei's alibi.** Asked, as lead investigator, to state his own movements "for completeness", Wei calmly presents dozens of statements from colleagues in his Systems Architecture office who remember him **at his desk from 13:30 until the Ministry's call at 15:20**. Nobody questions it. The reader's suspicion wavers.
- **Kovač notices, and doesn't know what to make of it:** his office is in the **Ministry district**, inside the seeded node's range. She files it.
- **Deliberation:** four days over the holidays. The capital waits.
- **The ruling, Mon Dec 27:** *"Where the record is absent, facts may be established by the concordant testimony of multiple independent witnesses, weighted for exposure to known contamination."* This is the **Lost Hour Doctrine**. For the first time, The Judge will accept what people remember.

## Ending
Outside, Chen Zhao shakes Kovač's hand in front of the cameras: "You've restored something precious, Director." *Restored.* Kovač smiles for the photographers and feels the word land in her stomach like a stone.

## Notes
- **Wei's alibi is itself seeded** (his colleagues were in the district). Kira and Maya expose this in Ch 28: Maya saw him *arrive* at the Regional Factorepo at 15:32 (Ch 1).
- Nikolai's cover is now blown publicly. He is legally resurrected with the others in Ch 26.
