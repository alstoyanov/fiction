# Chapter 11: Erase, Then Seed

**POV:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka
**Date:** Mon Nov 22, 2106
**Setting:** Tanaka's office at the Chronos teaching hospital; a secure call
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
**Twist 1 revealed.** A clinician's diagnosis turns the case upside down, and it also gives the book its first scientific key to Twist 3: seeding leaves real peripheral details behind.

## Beats
- **Tanaka lays the forty statements out** on her office floor, as she once laid out case notes. Her trafficking hearing is on Thursday. She works anyway.
- **The diagnosis:**
  - *Confabulation* (Book 4's false field) is individual: every brain fills its own gaps its own way. **Forty identical false details are not confabulation.**
  - Mass hysteria produces vagueness, not precision.
  - What produces identical memories in many heads at once is a **broadcast**.
- **Morrison's carrier,** from Ch 6. At 14:21 his interface picked up a faint signal with *his own* gain profile. He goes gray. "It's my calibration. The one I built for the cradles. Someone put it on a *broadcast node*." Continuity's Phase Two, "erase, then seed", has been used on the Ministry district during the hour.
- **The mechanism (plain and exact):** the record was erased and a memory was *written*. The seeding went for what matters most in a memory, the **face** (Marcus's, from the Continuity file) and the **clothes** (Wing C gray). It left minor, peripheral details alone. So:
  > *"Your witnesses aren't lying. Most of what they remember is real: they saw a man walk in. Someone just painted Marcus's face over him, and the gray. Seeding doesn't bother with small things."*
- **The next question:** "Then which details are *real*?" Tanaka goes back through the statements and circles the one detail that isn't Marcus's and isn't from any file: **the bandaged left hand**. "That one isn't a seed. It's what they actually saw."
- **Kovač's hearing room** (Ch 2) makes sense now: the people at the node's edge received partial, scrambled seeding, which is why they disagreed about small things.
- **The terrible implication:** nobody in the Ministry district can trust their own memory of that hour, and Veridica has no law for that.

## Ending
Tanaka calls Kira on a secure line and says it flatly: "The killer was real, and he had a bandaged left hand." There is a long silence on Kira's end. Then, very quietly: "I've seen a bandaged left hand this week." She doesn't say whose. We cut before she does.

## Notes
- **Twist 1 is revealed here.** Twist 3's clue (the bandage) now has meaning: Kira has seen it in Nikolai's photographs of the team room.
- Kira's not saying who is deliberate. In Ch 16 and 28 she keeps doubting herself, because lots of people cut their hands and Wei is the one protecting Marcus.
