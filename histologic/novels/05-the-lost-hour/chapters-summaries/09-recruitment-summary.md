# Chapter 9: Recruitment

**POV:** Nikolai Volkov (as "Pieter Lang")
**Date:** Wed Nov 17, 2106
**Setting:** The Regional Factorepo; Wei's investigation room; Nikolai's rented room
**Length:** ~3,200 words

## Purpose
Put a dead man inside the investigation. Build Wei's charm and Nikolai's paranoia, and introduce Maya properly.

## Beats
- **Pieter Lang's morning:** beard, unnecessary glasses, hunched shoulders, noodles at his desk. The dullest contractor in the building. Nikolai loves the disguise, and is terrified by how well it works.
- **The summons:** Wei wants the man who flagged the unowned block on Nov 14. "You saw it before anyone. I want people who notice." Nikolai is recruited onto the technical team on the spot.
- **Wei up close:** patient, humorous, generous with credit. He remembers Pieter's name on the first try. **The bandaged left hand**, now a smaller dressing. He writes with his right. When Pieter glances at it: "Server rack. Tuesday. Carelessness." (It was *Monday* in Ch 1. It's a tiny inconsistency. Do not highlight it.)
- **The team room:** screens full of an hour that isn't there. Wei's method is to rebuild it from the **edges**: caches, buffers, devices that were offline and reconnected at 15:01, anything that "didn't hear the hour was over". It's brilliant, and it's exactly what Nikolai would do.
- **Maya:** sharp, prickly, overworked, with a sense of humour. They argue about cache timestamps and enjoy it. She notices he types like someone trained on Registry systems ("Correction contractors type like that"). He deflects.
- **Nikolai's own backup:** fragments he saved from edge caches in the first minutes after 15:01, including traffic sensors, a hospital monitor, and a door log from the Ministry's side entrance that recorded **a staff card at 14:21**. Its name field is corrupted, but the card class is "Systems Architecture, senior". He doesn't give it to Wei. He sends it to Kira.
- **The fear:** in the corridor Wei says, lightly, "Your file's very thin, Pieter. I like that in a man." Nikolai can't tell if it's a joke.

## Ending
That night, in his rented room, Nikolai checks the page in his wallet, the rooftop in pinpricks (Book 4). It's there. He also finds a message from Kira: *"The card class. Who in Systems Architecture has a senior card for the Ministry?"* He runs the query. **Forty-one people.** One of them is Thomas Wei. He tells himself it means nothing, forty-one is a lot, and doesn't sleep.

## Notes
- **The card class** is a real clue that is diluted on purpose (41 names), so the reader can suspect Wei without the characters being able to act on it.
- "Your file's very thin" is the first hint that Wei may know who Nikolai is (see the Epilogue coda).
