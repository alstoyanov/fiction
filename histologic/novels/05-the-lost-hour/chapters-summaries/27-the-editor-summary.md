# Chapter 27: The Editor

**POV:** Isaiah Okonkwo
**Date:** Tue Jan 4, 2107
**Setting:** The SFW mission in Chronos; Isaiah's small asylum flat
**Length:** ~2,900 words

## Purpose
The quiet warning chapter. The one character with distance says what the celebration can't hear, and the world won't let him say it.

## Beats
- **The world hails the Doctrine:** "Veridica learns to listen", "Truth returns to the people". SFW outlets love it too, as proof that even the Histologic system concedes machines can fail.
- **Isaiah writes a column** that none of them want:
  - Two months ago Veridica learned that memory can be seeded.
  - Last week it made memory the law.
  - *"A nation that cannot trust its record has chosen to trust its memory in the same season it learned that memory can be written. We should be asking who is best placed to write it."*
- **The SFW editors spike it:** it spoils the story, and they need the Doctrine to look like a defeat for the Histologic system, not a new weapon for it.
- **Isaiah's asylum is conditional:** a witness who embarrasses his hosts is a witness who can be un-hosted. He understands the terms without being told.
- **His palace:** he walks the cellar and lays out what he has: *two fragments* (the torn page, the song), a restored record, and a "restorer" everyone now trusts. *One fragment is a curiosity. Two is a pattern.* He still waits for three.
- **A letter from Old Songs,** dictated to Kira and sent through the resistance, one line: *"The one who stays is the one who remembers. You stayed. Keep remembering."* (The Book 4 echo.)

## Ending
Isaiah sends the spiked column to one reader, Marcus, with a note: *"Don't publish this. Read it at the Commission. Then decide who you are."*

## Notes
- Isaiah's column is the book's thesis stated early. Its payoff comes in Ch 29–31.
- It keeps Isaiah's SFW relationship uneasy, which later books can develop.
