# Chapter 19: The Resurrection

**POV:** Alexei Volkov
**Date:** Wed Dec 8, 2106
**Setting:** The steps of the Justice Center; the crowd; a resistance car
**Length:** ~3,300 words

## Purpose
A public, spectacular turn: the dead walk into the capital and speak. It is thrilling and dangerous, and it shows how the system short-circuits.

## Beats
- **Elena's decision:** with the tribunal verdict standing and Marcus held "for his own good", only something public will force the hearing. Marina and Jakob, the ghosts, will testify. So will **Dmitri**, and Alexei goes with him.
- **Alexei's plan** is theatre, the Phantom's art: go public at midday on the Justice Center steps, with every camera in the capital pointed at them. The Chronos and Synthesis press have been tipped off by Isaiah.
- **The testimony.** Marina and Jakob state that they left their posts voluntarily at 14:25–14:43, extracted by the resistance. Then **Dmitri** steps forward, and so does Alexei beside him: *the same face twice*, two men the Registry says died in August.
- **Dmitri gives his account of 14:21**, precise as a police report: the side entrance, the staff card, the dark jacket, the hand held away from his body, and the man's gait, "a younger man than the one you are hunting." (Reeves's name is already circulating.)
- **The crowd:** first silence, then noise. Someone shouts: "The dead are testifying!" It becomes the headline.
- **The legal absurdity:** enforcers arrive and hesitate. **Kade**, of all people, is on the steps. "I have no warrant for a dead man, Mr. Volkov." Alexei: "Then you'd better prove I'm alive." Kade almost smiles. **Nobody can arrest them** without first establishing, in law, that they exist, and that is exactly the question the hearing would decide.
- **Old Songs** stands at the back of the crowd in a borrowed coat. He doesn't testify. He watches faces, and hums.
- **The cost:** Pell calls it "a resistance stunt with forged corpses". The Ministry's line hardens. And Alexei sees one face in the crowd that doesn't fit: a man in a good gray coat watching Dmitri, not the speakers. He is gone before Alexei can point him out.

## Ending
In the car afterwards, exhilarated, Alexei laughs until he coughs. Then Dmitri says, quietly: "He looked at the three of us and counted." Alexei: "Who?" Dmitri: "The man in the gray coat. He counted to three, Lyosha. There are only two of us up there."

## Notes
- **The counting man** is Wei's watcher, or Wei himself. It raises the danger to **Nikolai** (the third face), which pays off in Ch 20.
- Isaiah coordinates the foreign press from asylum.
- The public pressure is what finally forces Chen Zhao to schedule the hearing (Ch 25).
