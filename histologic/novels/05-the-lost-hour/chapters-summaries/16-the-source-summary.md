# Chapter 16: The Source

**POV:** Kira Osman
**Date:** Thu Dec 2, 2106
**Setting:** A resistance safe room; a video link with Kovač; Nikolai's feed
**Length:** ~3,500 words

## Purpose
**Twist 2 revealed**, with a darker edge than planned: every operation in the hour was put there on purpose, and every trail leads back to the dead woman.

## Beats
- **Kira confronts Kovač** over a video link, with Alexei in the room. Kovač, cold and exact, shows her calendar: the 14:30 slot was *offered* to her office by the Deputy Minister's scheduling system as "the only one available", on Nov 11 at 09:12.
- **Kira pulls every thread** using the diary page (Ch 7), Nikolai's cache fragments, Tanaka's waiver and Elena's message logs:
  - **Singh's 14:30 meeting with Marcus** was proposed in her calendar by an entry her aide "can't recall making".
  - **Isaiah's border slot** was offered by Singh's scheduling system.
  - **The Santos family's "medical crossing waiver"** was approved from Singh's office.
  - **The warning to Marina and Jakob** ("covers won't survive the week") reached Elena's source through a Ministry "deep-cover review" in Singh's name.
  - **Elena's tip** ("everything between 14:00 and 15:00") came from a source whose credentials, several layers down, are a Deputy Minister's.
- **Twist 2:** *every one of their operations was steered into the hour, and every steer carries Amara Singh's credentials.*
- **The questions this raises:**
  - Were her credentials stolen, and she was killed to cover it?
  - Or was she *part of it*: a woman who drew the escapees into the hour, and then…?
  - Kovač: "Then who died in her office?"
  - Kira: "Someone we were meant to be accused of killing."
- **The experiment:** Kira says what the reader has been feeling. They were chosen as suspects and observed, as in Wing C. "We didn't use the hour. **The hour used us.**"
- Alexei apologises to Kovač, awkwardly, which he has never done to anyone. The red herring is cleared, but the damage is done: the hardliners will keep pushing it.
- **Kira alone:** she thinks of the bandaged hand. She thinks of the man on Nikolai's photographs of the team room, holding his cup in his right hand. She types his name into a note, then deletes it. *He's protecting Marcus. Lots of people cut their hands.*

## Ending
Nikolai's feed: Pell's tribunal is set for **Monday Dec 6**, and the transfer to Unit 3 is authorised for the same evening, *"pending verdict, for the subject's stability."* In four days Marcus will be back in a white room.

## Notes
- **Singh's credentials:** keep both readings alive, victim or accomplice. Her staged death is revealed in a later book.
- Kira's deleted note keeps Twist 3 at arm's length, so that the reader suspects Wei before the characters can.
