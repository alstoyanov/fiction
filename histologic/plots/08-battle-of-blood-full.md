# Battle of Blood: Full Plot Outline (v2)

**Status:** CURRENT. Revision 2, Sept 2026. It replaces the Nov 2025 plot. It is consistent with the short stories, *The Correction*, *The Lost Hour* (drafted), *The Distributed Truth* and *Battle of Truths* (plots and chapter summaries), `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md`, `characters/` and `worldbuilding/`.
**Genre:** war novel and conspiracy thriller. A real war fought by a nation built for wars of facts; the correction of a whole people, carried out under cover of that war; and the machine's death, written into it in 2066.
**Structure:** Prologue + 30 chapters + Epilogue, in four parts. About 110,000 words (about 3,400 words per chapter).
**Timeline:** Wednesday, February 12, 2110 to Wednesday, May 28, 2110. Epilogue on Friday, August 15, 2110.
**Consistency rule:** this book must agree with Books 1–7. Book 9 will be rewritten to fit it.

## High Concept

The fleet comes out of the Atlantic on the morning of February 12, 2110, onto the beaches Colonel Sokolov showed the heroes a month earlier. The Sovereignty of Free Will has no neural interfaces. **No storm can turn its soldiers and no seeding can reach them**, so every weapon Veridica spent forty years perfecting is useless against them. The SFW doesn't want Veridica's cities. It wants **The Judge**, intact, in the Factorepo Spire. After *Battle of Truths*, Veridica's core also holds **Chronos's true source**. Whoever takes it holds the record of 330 million people.

The heroes fight a war they were never built for. Then they find out who invited it. First comes the leak (the SFW has Sokolov's own coastal plan, from her mother's table). Then the reason: behind the front line, the government has switched on **Phase Two**. Continuity's broadcast, which erases doubt and plants loyalty, is running at population scale on the national network, with **Dr. Reeves** released from prison to run it. The preamble to the order quotes **Principle Four** word for word. The war is the justification Principle Two promised. Veridica is being corrected under cover of being invaded.

At the end, with the SFW at the Spire's gates, the First Minister signs the core over to stop the slaughter. That act, and not a bomb, ends The Judge. **Principle Seven**, Vance's hidden contingency, was built to fire when the machine is handed to a master. It needs one more hand: *"the one I did not tell."* The cascade will kill Chronos's Judge too, because the two are one. So this time the heroes do what they didn't do in Book 7. **They ask Chronos first.**

**The question the book asks:** if the machine that decides for you is the thing everyone is fighting over, is it worth keeping, and who has the right to switch it off?

## The Manuscript Thread (Book 8)

Revealed in this book (`00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md` §4.6, §4.12, §5):
- **Principle Four** (pp. 18–22, mark *WZ·10* on its even pages and at its end; p. 19 is odd and carries no mark, and WZ·10 is not seen in this book), in full: *"Do not punish the doubter; heal him. Punishment makes martyrs; correction makes converts. The aim is not obedience but belief: a citizen who, corrected, experiences his certainty as his own. Correction is compassion. Certainty is care."*
  - **The route:** the **Stabilisation of Sympathisers Order** (Mar 2110) carries a preamble that quotes the principle verbatim. In the order's technical file, Maya finds a **typed page, copy numeral X**, with the chipped *e*: page 19 of Principle Four. The same numeral came through the inside route in Book 6. The Continuity survivors read the text their torture was built from. Tanaka recognises Continuity's stated aim as a paraphrase of it.
- **Principle Seven** (handwritten, after p. 32, Vance's copy only), in full: *"The Judge must never become a weapon. Better destroyed than enslaved. I have built into it the means of its own ending: conditions, authorities and a cascade that cannot be reversed. I have told no one. If you are reading this, you are the one I did not tell."*
  - **On the back of the same page**, in the same young hand: three lines setting out **the mechanics** (canon from this book). (1) *The condition:* lawful authority orders the core's custody transferred outside the nation. (2) *The authority:* that order, and **one concurrence from the reader of this page**. (3) *The cascade:* it ends the Judge and **every system that restores from its source**. Nothing else is on the page.
  - **The route:** Vance gives it to **Kira** on Sat May 17 (Ch 25). "I said I would give it when they tried to give it away."

**Rules held:**
- **Nobody decodes a mark.** Nobody knows that WZ·10 is Wei's father, or that copy X belongs to Wei.
- **Nothing of Book 9's material appears:** no Annex text, no names from the Register, no heirs clause.
- **Vance's copy:** she dies without saying where it is. She leaves Old Songs **a riddle only he can solve** (Book 9).

## The Twists

### Twist 1: The wine was open. (Ch 9.)
Elena's guerrillas take an SFW courier behind the coast (Ch 7). His case holds the **SFW landing and advance plan**, and it is **Sokolov's own coastal defence plan**, turned inside out: every battery she placed, every road she meant to hold, and a phrase she used only once, at her mother's dinner table on Dec 20 ("the beaches are a door, not a wall"). She has discussed the plan with no one else outside command. Her promotions were arranged, and the one person who knew the plan was her mother. She drives to **Viktoria Sokolov's** house and finds it empty: two glasses, the good wine, and a note in her mother's hand: *"You were always exactly where you should be."* Viktoria has gone east, to the CNT (Book 9).
- **What the characters learn:** the leak, the invitation and the mother. **They do not learn that she is an Architect** (VS·09 is decoded in Book 9). They guess she was part of "whoever wants to be invaded".
- **Clues:**
  - Book 7 Ch 29 (the wine already open; "exactly where you should be").
  - Book 6 Epilogue and Book 7 (promotions "very quick").
  - Ch 2 (the SFW knew which batteries to avoid).
  - Ch 3 (Nora Chen's handler speaks of "a source at the highest table").

### Twist 2: Certainty is care. (Ch 17.)
Behind the front, Veridica is **being corrected**. Pilot "stabilisation" broadcasts began in **three capital districts on Mon Feb 24**, under emergency information powers, less than two weeks into the war. The **Stabilisation of Sympathisers Order**, signed under war powers on Mon Mar 17 to "treat enemy sympathy", legalised them after the fact, and district-by-district broadcasting followed from Mar 24. That it began before any law existed is part of the horror. It is Continuity's Phase Two ("erase doubt, seed loyalty"), broadcast through the national interface network into whole districts of the capital. **Dr. Reeves**, released from prison under war powers (Ch 14), runs it. Tanaka, back in Veridica with a Chronos field hospital, diagnoses it as she diagnosed seeding in Book 5: identical war fervour in dozens of patients, in identical words, and an absence of doubt that is clinically impossible. Morrison recognises the carrier as his own calibration once again. The order's preamble is **Principle Four**, verbatim. This is why someone in Veridica wanted to be invaded: **war is the justification Principle Two promised**, and Phase Two was always the endgame (Book 4).
- **Clues:**
  - Book 4 (Phase Two defined).
  - Book 7 Twist 3 (*Phase: justification*).
  - Ch 6 (Tanaka's patients, all from three capital districts, say "we must be certain now").
  - Ch 12 (Ashford to be reopened for "sympathisers"; Principle Four's title in the order's file name; the recital's "since 24 February").
  - Ch 14 (Reeves released; the Feb 24 allocation on the schedule).
  - Ch 15 (the capital "strangely calm" under bombardment).

### Twist 3: The surrender is the trigger. (Ch 25.)
With the SFW at the Spire's gates and the city dying, Maitland prepares to **surrender the core** to General Farahani in exchange for a ceasefire. It is humane and it is catastrophic. The heroes race to Vance, and she gives Kira the page. Principle Seven doesn't need a bomb. It needs **exactly what Maitland is about to do**: a lawful order transferring the core's custody outside the nation, plus **one concurrence from "the one I did not tell"**. Kira is now that person. And the back of the page says the cascade ends **every system that restores from its source**. Since Book 7, that includes **Chronos's Judge**.

The heroes decided for Chronos once, and it rewrote a nation. **This time they ask.** Julian, recorded at maximum, and Administrator Aubry, who called what Veridica did "writing us", must answer for Chronos. Aubry: ***"They wrote us once. Nobody writes us again."*** Chronos consents.
- **Clues:**
  - Manuscript §4.12 ("conditions, authorities and a cascade").
  - Book 7 Twist 1 (one source).
  - Ch 8 (Vance: "There is a page").
  - Ch 16 (Vance: "I'll give it when they try to give it away. Not before.").
  - Ch 24 (Maitland drafting surrender terms).

### Closing turn: It moved. (Ch 30.)
The cascade ends both Judges. Veridica and Chronos wake on May 22 without a machine that decides. The SFW's objective is gone, and it withdraws. Then, sorting the dead core's logs, Kira finds what Nikolai half-saw in Ch 11, a **third leg of the join** "that goes nowhere I can see". **Nine minutes before the cascade reached it**, someone restored a full copy of the true source *to a third site, outside both nations*. Principle Three said three backup systems. The third was never in Veridica or Chronos. The signal ran east. *"It's not dead. It moved."* (Book 9: the Architects in the CNT.)

## Structure

### Prologue: Landfall (Sokolov, Wed Feb 12, 2110, dawn)
The headland from Book 7, a month later. Lights on the horizon at 04:40, then landing craft. Sokolov's storm units come up on command, and the SFW infantry walk through the broadcast unaffected: *no interfaces*. Her own soldiers flinch at a contradiction their enemy can't perceive. It is real weather, a hard gray sea and wind. **Ending:** the SFW's first wave comes ashore **exactly between two of her batteries**, where the gap was, as if they'd read her plan. She notes it in a report's register and doesn't let herself think the rest.

### PART ONE: LANDFALL (Feb 12 – Mar 7)
*Theme: a war they weren't built for; the machine as ally; a plan that was read.*

**Ch 1: Questions for the War** (Marcus, Wed Feb 12 – Thu Feb 13). The war cabinet: Maitland, Chen Zhao, Wei as Head of Emergency Information, and Pell. Marcus is made civilian adviser because "you understand The Judge". The Judge coordinates Veridica's defence superbly: every street, every citizen's location, every road. Marcus keeps his list. **Ending:** Farahani's first broadcast to Veridica, in Veridican. *"We are not here for your cities."* Marcus writes: *Then what are they here for?*

**Ch 2: No Storm Will Turn Them** (Dmitri, Mon Feb 17). The first inland battle. Veridica's weapons fail against men with no interfaces. Then the SFW **turns captured storm units on Veridican troops**, who see roads that aren't there. The SFW knew which batteries to avoid *(a Twist 1 clue)*. **Ending:** Dmitri, formally, to his dead lieutenant's pack: "We have fought with facts for forty years. We have forgotten what they cost."

**Ch 3: Embedded** (Isaiah, Thu Feb 20, SFW lines). His asylum is now conditional on embedding with the SFW forces as "the witness". He meets **Colonel Nora Chen**: "I'm his cousin. I left in '95. Tell him I'm sorry about the record." Martinez is cool with him and watching. Farahani is courteous and precise. **Ending:** Nora, of her handler, "a source at the highest table" *(a Twist 1 clue)*. The objective is not a city.

**Ch 4: The Names, Longer** (Old Songs, Mon Feb 24, the farmhouse). Refugees from the coast in the barn. "The Names" grows every evening, with the new dead taken from the journals, Veridican and SFW alike. Vance's tremor now shakes the cup. She begins to say something about "the page" and stops. **Ending:** a boy from the coast asks him to add a soldier he watched die "who wasn't ours". Old Songs does. The song is as long as the dead.

**Ch 5: The Judge at War** (Kira, Fri Feb 28). Kira and Nikolai inside The Judge's war coordination. Its predictions save a town. Its analysis of SFW movements shows the lines bending toward **the capital and the Spire**, not toward ports or factories. Wei appears at her desk and puts down a **maintenance map of the core**: "You'll want this." He leaves. Its route M runs from a service door in a disused transit tunnel under the Spire to the core's lower cable gallery (the core is at or below plaza level). **Ending:** Kira looks at the map's legend and realises it shows how to *remove* the core, not how to defend it.

**Ch 6: Field Hospital** (Tanaka, Mon Mar 3). Chronos, now "friendly" because of the history Marcus wrote, sends a field hospital, and Tanaka comes home with it, legally alive. Chronos orderlies are dying for a past they didn't have. Her case notes record that patients from three of the capital's districts all say the same thing, in the same words: *"We must be certain now."* *(A Twist 2 clue.)* **Ending:** a Chronos nurse asks her why Veridica is grateful to Chronos. She has no honest answer she can give.

**Ch 7: Behind the Lines** (Elena, Wed Mar 5, the occupied coast). Elena's cells, with Alexei. The SFW occupation is disciplined and hands out **paper and pencils** to Veridicans ("write your own history"). The irony is complete. An ambush takes an SFW courier. **Ending:** in his case, the SFW's advance plan. Elena doesn't know Sokolov's hand, but she knows a Veridican military document when she sees one.

**Ch 8: Who Is Invading Whom?** (Marcus, Fri Mar 7). The pattern is clear: the SFW wants the Spire, The Judge, intact. Marcus puts it to Maitland: "We should know how to deny them the core." Maitland: "Destroy our own Judge?" Marcus: "Not yet. But we should know how." He goes to the farmhouse. Vance, at the stove: ***"There is a page."*** She won't give it to him. **Ending:** Marcus writes a new question: *What is on the page?*

### PART TWO: THE LEAK (Mar 10 – Apr 9)
*Theme: the invitation's author; two nations in one core; the siege.*

**Ch 9: The Wine Was Open** (Sokolov, Mon Mar 10). **Twist 1.** Elena's courier document reaches her, and it is her own plan inside out, with the phrase from her mother's table, *"the beaches are a door, not a wall."* She drives to Viktoria's house. It is empty: two glasses, the good wine, a note. *"You were always exactly where you should be."* She sits at the table for an hour and then reports it to Maitland in full, formally, in the first person. **Ending:** "My mother has gone east, First Minister. I would like to stay where I am. I am apparently very good at it."

**Ch 10: Nora** (Isaiah, Fri Mar 14). Nora Chen shows Isaiah the Veridican source's packets, because she wants a witness to her righteousness. The packets describe The Judge's core, its merged source, and the fact that capturing it gives "the record of two nations". Nora believes it will free Veridicans from the machine. Martinez, alone with Isaiah: "We are being given a war. Now we are being given the prize." Isaiah decides to defect. **Ending:** he sews the packets' photographs into his coat's lining, as he did in 2106.

**Ch 11: Two Nations, One Core** (Nikolai, Tue Mar 18). The implication of Book 7 lands: **Chronos's true source is in Veridica's core.** If the SFW takes the Spire, it takes Chronos. In the join Nikolai sees a **third leg** that "goes nowhere I can see". He files it as UNCHECKED *(a closing-turn clue)*. Julian's message comes through Kovač: *"If they take your Judge, they take ours. I would like Chronos to be asked this time."* **Ending:** Nikolai forwards it to Kira and doesn't say what he's thinking, which is Book 7.

**Ch 12: Ashford** (Kovač, Fri Mar 21). The Ministry orders **Ashford reopened** for "enemy sympathisers", meaning journal keepers and people who took SFW paper. The order's file is titled with Principle Four's heading, *Correction Over Punishment*, which she now recognises from the table of contents. She refuses **on paper**, and is removed as Registrar. **Ending:** she walks out with one box, the folders, and leaves every other box behind.

**Ch 13: Artillery** (Alexei, Wed Mar 26). SFW guns on the capital's outskirts are shelling the Chronos field hospital. Alexei's team takes them out at night. It is hard and costly, and he *survives*. The reader expects the old death and doesn't get it *(misdirection)*. Afterwards, with Elena in a ruined barn, wry and exhausted: "I keep thinking this is the one." **Ending:** Nikolai on the radio: Isaiah wants to come across. "Of course he does."

**Ch 14: Release** (Maya, Sat Mar 29). Maya learns from a Ministry list that **her father has been released under war powers** "to direct stabilisation broadcasting". She goes to the broadcast centre and is refused entry. He sends a message out: *"They need someone who knows how. I am the only one who has done it."* **Ending:** she writes in her journal, as every month: *He didn't do it.* She stops, crosses it out, and writes: *He is doing it now.*

**Ch 15: Siege** (Marcus, Fri Apr 4). The capital is encircled from Wed Apr 2. Shelling. And the city is **strangely calm**: queues orderly, volunteers smiling, no panic, no doubt. Marcus's index climbs as he argues in cabinet against Stabilisation: **0.59**. Pell's lists appear. **Ending:** a clerk he has known for years tells him, glowing, "I've never been so sure of anything." Marcus goes home and reads his own journal to check he still disagrees.

**Ch 16: Not Before** (Old Songs, Wed Apr 9). Kira visits the farmhouse. Vance, very frail, still won't give up the page: ***"I'll give it when they try to give it away. Not before. If I give it now, you'll use it to decide."*** That night she gives Old Songs something else, in the only language she trusts him with: **a riddle**, in rhyme, about where she left the rest of her copy. He doesn't solve it. He doesn't try yet. **Ending:** he sets it to the Lakeshore Air, so that he won't lose it. The last line lifts like a question.

### PART THREE: THE BROADCAST (Apr 14 – May 9)
*Theme: a people corrected under cover of war; many hands as antidote; the cost.*

**Ch 17: Certainty Is Care** (Tanaka, Mon Apr 14). **Twist 2.** Tanaka's case notes become a diagnosis: *identical presentation, dozens of patients; absence of doubt, clinically impossible; onset: district by district.* Morrison takes one reading and goes gray: it is his calibration again (Book 5). The file's schedule shows the broadcasting began in three districts on Feb 24, three weeks before the Order. Maya brings the order's technical file. Its preamble is **Principle Four, verbatim**, and inside is a **typed page, copy numeral X**, page 19. Tanaka reads it aloud in the hospital's linen room: *"Correction is compassion. Certainty is care."* It is the text her torture was built from. **Ending:** case note: *Aetiology: a principle. Author: unknown. Treatment:* She leaves it unfinished.

**Ch 18: Read Your Own Book** (Kira, Fri Apr 18). The antidote is the thing they built. **People's own written journals from before the broadcast disagree with what they now feel.** Kira organises "read your own book" nights in cellars under the shelling, with the pairing network running copies between districts. People read their own handwriting from January and weep, because they had doubts then and have none now. **Ending:** a man reads his entry for Jan 3 aloud, *"I don't know if we should fight"*, and looks up. "Who wrote this?" Kira: "You did."

**Ch 19: Across the Lines** (Isaiah, Wed Apr 23). His defection through the siege lines, with Alexei guiding. The packets are in his coat. An overpass, SFW fire, a choice. **Alexei stays** to cover them. *"Tell Elena I love her. Tell Dima he was first by thirty seconds, and I never minded."* Isaiah gets across. **Ending:** he stands in Veridica, alive, with the intelligence, and doesn't feel his hands.

**Ch 20: Thirty Seconds** (Dmitri, Fri Apr 25). Dmitri is told. Grief, spoken formally, without a single contraction. Nikolai, who lost the rooftop memory in Book 4, says, "I don't remember who was born first." Dmitri: ***"I was. By thirty seconds. He never minded."*** They sit together. At the farmhouse Old Songs adds **Alexei** to "The Names", and for the first time his voice breaks. **Ending:** Elena doesn't come in. She stands in the yard until the song is over.

**Ch 21: The Sympathisers** (Marcus, Wed Apr 30). Pell's list is published: journal keepers and "doubters". Ruth Calloway is on it, and Kira, and **Marcus**. His index passes **0.61**, and a custody order is issued. Kovač, removed from office but still holding keys, **hides him in the testimony intake's archive**, among the folders. He is a fugitive in his own capital, a commissioner hunted by his own record. **Ending:** in the dark between the shelves, Marcus writes: *Checked: the record wants me corrected. Unchecked: whether it's wrong.* Then he crosses out "whether".

**Ch 22: Dad** (Maya, Mon May 5). Maya gets into the broadcast centre with Nikolai's help and faces her father among the cradles and relays. He is courteous, interested and proud of the scale. She asks how to stop it. He tells her, because he wants to know what happens: ***"I'd like to see what happens if you turn it off."*** The carrier's weakness is a timing dependency on the national clock. **Ending:** at the door he says, "You still write that I didn't do it." She: "You didn't. You did this."

**Ch 23: Silence** (Nikolai, Fri May 9). Nikolai and Maya cut the Stabilisation carrier across the capital at 03:12, the old minute, taken back. The city wakes over hours from seeded calm into fear, doubt and grief. It is terrible, and it is theirs. Mass correction stops. The government is in crisis. Wei, reported, watched the carrier die from his office and did nothing. **Ending:** a city of people frightened for the right reasons. Nikolai, to Maya: "It's worse. It's better."

### PART FOUR: SEVEN (May 16 – May 28)
*Theme: the machine's end; consent; what survives.*

**Ch 24: Terms** (Marcus, Fri May 16). The SFW is at the Spire's gates, and the dead are in the tens of thousands. Maitland, grieving and humane, prepares to **surrender the core** to Farahani in exchange for a ceasefire. Marcus, in hiding, hears it through Kovač. He sees at once what a transferred Judge means, for Veridica and for Chronos. **Ending:** he goes to the farmhouse, a fugitive crossing a besieged city, to ask for the page.

**Ch 25: The Page** (Kira, Sat May 17). **Twist 3.** Vance gives Kira the page. On the front is Principle Seven, verbatim. On the back are the mechanics: the condition (a lawful order transferring custody outside the nation), the authority (that order plus **one concurrence from its reader**), and the cascade (**every system that restores from its source**). Kira is the one Vance didn't tell until now. And the cascade will end **Chronos's Judge** as well. **Ending:** Kira, to Marcus: "We decided for Chronos once." Marcus: "Then we ask them."

**Ch 26: Consent** (Julian, Mon May 19, Chronos). **Julian's POV.** Aubry comes to him in his recorded room. Chronos must decide whether its Judge dies with Veridica's. Two people who were wronged, one by Marcus and one by the record, deciding together, every word logged. Aubry: ***"They wrote us once. Nobody writes us again."*** Julian agrees. Through the right of self-disclosure, Chronos's consent is published before it is sent, so that no one can say it was forged. **Ending:** Julian sends it to Kira, and adds one line of his own for Marcus: *"This time you asked."*

**Ch 27: The Core** (Nora Chen, Wed May 21). **Nora's POV.** Her SFW team goes into the Spire ahead of the formal handover, to secure the drives before anyone can tamper with them. Marcus and Kira are there, having come in by route M on Wei's map (Kira's Spire access was suspended on Apr 30). The cousins meet among the core racks. She says the Judge must be taken from those who misuse it. He shows her the packets' source and Principle Two. She understands she was handed the prize by the people who wanted the war. **Ending:** Maitland's signature arrives on the core's console, lawful and transferring custody. The condition is met.

**Ch 28: Truth Endures** (Kira, Wed May 21). Kira at the console, the page beside her, with Chronos's consent. She **concurs**. The cascade begins and cannot be reversed. Every Judge panel in Veridica and in Chronos changes register for the last time and displays a final determination. People will argue for decades about whether a mind wrote it: ***"Truth endures. I do not need to."*** In the Spire the core's storage burns out from within. SFW technicians try to cut drives out mid-cascade, and **Nora holds the door** against her own men until the fire takes the room. **She dies** there. Vance and Old Songs hear it at the farmhouse through Nikolai's patched voice line. **Ending:** 14:00, and silence across two nations. No machine decides anything.

**Ch 29: Elise** (Old Songs, Sat May 24 – Tue May 27). Vance heard it through Nikolai's voice line, patched to the farmhouse receiver, and she dies three days later, with Old Songs beside her, the tremor finally still. **His one speech of the book**, at her burial in the farmhouse orchard (plum trees), beside Alexei, on Tue May 27: forty-seven years of arguing with her; the Archive on Thursdays; *"She built the cage, and she built the key into the cage, and she hid it in the one place her own design couldn't look: in someone else's hand."* Then "The Names", with **"Elise Vance"** in it, the name no record in either nation now holds. **Ending:** the riddle, still unsolved, in the Lakeshore Air, hummed low. It lifts like a question.

**Ch 30: It Moved** (Marcus, Wed May 28).
- **The withdrawal.** The SFW's objective is gone. Farahani is killed in the withdrawal to the coast on Mon May 26, and Martinez negotiates the rest.
- **The aftermath.** The custody order on Marcus lapses, because there is no Judge to hold it. Veridica and Chronos are without Judges: courts with only human judges, and Chronos preparing to **elect humans** for the first time. Chen Zhao **confesses first**, warmly, and proposes a *Commission of Rebuilding*. Marcus hears page 30 in it.
- **The turn.** Kira, sorting the dead core's final logs, finds the third leg: **nine minutes before the cascade reached it, a full copy of the true source was restored to a third site, outside both nations.** The signal ran east.

**Ending:** ***"It's not dead,"*** Kira says. ***"It moved."*** Marcus writes the last question on the list: *Where is east?*

### Epilogue: The Names (Fri Aug 15, 2110)
Rotating vignettes:
- **The central plaza**, on the 15th. The Lost Hour stone has new names cut on its side: the war's dead, from both shores and the SFW, taken from the journals.
- **Sokolov** refuses the rank of General: "I would like to stay where I am."
- **Isaiah** begins *The Cost of Certainty* with Tanaka: his words, her case notes.
- **Chronos:** the first human council is elected. Julian is not on it and doesn't want to be, and he is released from maximum recording.
- **Maya** visits her father, who is back in prison: "Was it interesting?" "Yes."
- **Wei**, in a private hand: *"They ended it themselves. That was in no one's design. Not even hers."*
- **Old Songs**, at the stone at 14:00, sings "The Names": Alexei, Elise, Nora, and the long list. Children who don't know who he is join the refrain.

**Final line:** *"The machine is gone. The names remain."*
