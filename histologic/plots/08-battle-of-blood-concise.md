# Battle of Blood: Concise Plot (v2)

**Status:** CURRENT, Sept 2026. This is the short version of `08-battle-of-blood-full.md` (v2), which is authoritative.
**Type:** Book 8. A war novel and conspiracy thriller.
**Length:** Prologue, 30 chapters and an Epilogue, about 110,000 words.
**Timeline:** Feb 12 to May 28, 2110. The Epilogue is set on Aug 15, 2110.

## Core Concept
An SFW fleet lands on Veridica's Atlantic coast. SFW soldiers have **no interfaces**, so no storm can turn them and no seeding can reach them. The SFW doesn't want Veridica's cities. It wants **The Judge**, intact, because since Book 7 it also holds **Chronos's true source**. The heroes fight a real war and find out who invited it: Sokolov's own mother leaked the plan. They also find out why. Under cover of the war, the government runs **Phase Two** at population scale, with Reeves released to run it and **Principle Four** quoted in the order's preamble. At the end the First Minister signs the core over to stop the slaughter, and that act triggers **Principle Seven**, Vance's hidden way of killing The Judge. It needs one more hand: "the one I did not tell." Because the cascade will end Chronos's Judge too, **this time they ask Chronos first.**

## The Twists
1. **The wine was open (Ch 9).** The SFW's advance plan is Sokolov's own coastal plan turned inside out. It includes a phrase she used only at her mother's table. Viktoria's house is empty: two glasses, the good wine, and a note reading "You were always exactly where you should be." She has fled east. The characters learn about the leak, not that she is an Architect; that comes in Book 9.
2. **Certainty is care (Ch 17).** The capital's calm is seeded. The Stabilisation of Sympathisers Order is Phase Two, run by Reeves, and its preamble is Principle Four verbatim, with a **copy X** page inside. The war is the justification that Principle Two promised.
3. **The surrender is the trigger (Ch 25).** Principle Seven needs no bomb. It needs a lawful order transferring the core outside the nation (which Maitland is about to sign) plus one concurrence from the person reading the page. That is Kira. The cascade also ends every system that restores from the core's source, **including Chronos's Judge**. They ask Aubry and Julian, and Chronos consents: "Nobody writes us again."
- **Closing turn, "It moved" (Ch 30):** nine minutes before the cascade, the true source was restored to a **third site to the east**.

## Plot Structure
**Prologue: Landfall.** Sokolov's storm units do nothing to men without interfaces. The first wave lands exactly in the gap in her plan.

**Part One: Landfall (Ch 1–8).**
- The war cabinet makes Marcus an adviser. Farahani: "We are not here for your cities."
- The SFW turns captured storm units on Veridican troops.
- Isaiah is embedded with the SFW and meets Nora Chen.
- "The Names" grows every night at the farmhouse.
- The Judge coordinates Veridica's defence. Wei hands Kira a map showing how to *remove* the core.
- Tanaka's patients all say "We must be certain now."
- Elena captures a courier.
- The SFW wants the Spire. Vance: "There is a page."

**Part Two: The Leak (Ch 9–16).**
- Sokolov's mother (**Twist 1**).
- Nora's packets: the prize is "the record of two nations". Isaiah decides to defect.
- Nikolai finds a "third leg" in the join. Julian: "I would like Chronos to be asked this time."
- Kovač refuses to reopen Ashford and is removed.
- Alexei survives the artillery raid, a deliberate misdirection.
- Reeves is released.
- The siege begins, the city is strangely calm, and Marcus's index reaches 0.59.
- Vance: "I'll give it when they try to give it away. Not before." She gives Old Songs a riddle.

**Part Three: The Broadcast (Ch 17–23).**
- Tanaka's diagnosis, Morrison's calibration, Principle Four (**Twist 2**).
- "Read your own book" nights: people's January journals still hold the doubts they no longer feel.
- **Alexei dies** bringing Isaiah across the lines: "Tell Elena I love her. Tell Dima he was first by thirty seconds, and I never minded."
- Dmitri to Nikolai: "I was. By thirty seconds." Old Songs adds Alexei to "The Names".
- Marcus is listed as a sympathiser and passes 0.61. He hides in Kovač's archive.
- Maya confronts Reeves: "I'd like to see what happens if you turn it off."
- The carrier is cut at 03:12, and the city wakes afraid for the right reasons.

**Part Four: Seven (Ch 24–30).**
- Maitland's surrender terms.
- The page (**Twist 3**). Chronos's consent comes through Julian and Aubry, disclosed publicly before it is sent.
- Nora Chen turns in the Spire.
- Kira concurs, and both Judges end: "Truth endures. I do not need to." **Nora dies** holding the door against her own men.
- **Vance dies**. Old Songs gives **his one speech** and sings her true name in "The Names".
- Farahani is killed in the withdrawal. Chronos will elect humans for the first time. Chen Zhao "confesses first".
- "It's not dead. It moved." Marcus: *Where is east?*

**Epilogue: The Names (Aug 15, 2110).**
- New names are cut into the stone.
- Sokolov refuses to become a general.
- Isaiah and Tanaka begin *The Cost of Certainty*.
- Julian is released from maximum recording.
- Maya visits Reeves.
- Wei: "They ended it themselves. That was in no one's design. Not even hers."
- Old Songs sings, and children join in.
- **Final line:** *"The machine is gone. The names remain."*

## Manuscript Revealed in This Book
- **Principle Four:** full text, from the order's preamble and a copy X page.
- **Principle Seven:** full text, plus the mechanics written on the back of Vance's page.
- **Not revealed:** the Annex, the Register's names, any decoded mark, and where Vance's copy is. That last one is Old Songs' riddle, for Book 9.
