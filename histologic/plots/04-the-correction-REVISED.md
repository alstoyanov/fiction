# The Correction: Revised Plot Outline (v2)

**Status:** CURRENT. Revision 2, Sept 2026. It replaces v1 of this file, and both `04-the-correction-full.md` and `04-the-correction-concise.md`, which describe the abandoned "group therapy" plot.
**Detailed beats:** `novels/04-the-correction/chapters-summaries/` (one file per chapter, plus a README with canon decisions).
**Structure:** Prologue + 29 chapters + Epilogue, in five parts. About 100,000 words.

## Why v1 Was Revised

The first REVISED plot fixed the original's problems (the gentle group therapy, everyone cooperating by Ch 7, an easy escape). But it had problems of its own:
- **The timeline contradicted itself** (the triplets inside for "8 months" in one place and "10" in another, Isaiah arriving twice), and it contradicted the short stories.
- **The facility clashed with the stories.** The triplets' facility is on the border (Story 03). Kira's is on the outskirts of the capital (Story 02).
- **It was predictable.** The structure was a standard prison escape: every ally turned, every plan worked, and the antagonist stayed passive until he explained himself.

v2 fixes the timeline and the facilities, ties all three short stories together, and adds three twists (below).

## Core Concept

Marcus Chen arrives at Ashford Correctional Facility believing he'll be healed. Instead, **Project Continuity** erases him in two months. It is an experimental program that sets out to produce *genuine belief*, not compliance, and it plans to deliver that belief to the whole nation through the neural-interface broadcast network.

Seven prisoners were chosen as test subjects, each representing a different kind of mind. Some can't be erased: the Volkov triplets, whose bond resists correction. One is brought back from nothing: Marcus, by Kira Osman. Together they learn to *appear corrected while preserving themselves*, build a secret network, uncover the program, and escape during a fact storm they create themselves. They get out broken, officially dead, and unsure whether even their love was their own.

**The book's thesis:** belief planted by someone else is manufactured. Belief you keep choosing is yours. It points toward correction *by* people, not *of* people.

## How It Links to the Short Stories

- **Story 01:** "Elena Reyes/Vasquez" is **Elena Volkov**, the triplets' aunt. The brother she says "came back empty" from correction is **Ivan Volkov**, their father, who later became Test Subject 2471.
- **Story 02:** Kira's stolen fact was deleted **by Continuity** to get her convicted. Her encryption protocol (7.3) was then reused on Continuity's own archive, and she uses it to break in (Ch 24). Director Kovač is Adrian Kovač's aunt.
- **Story 03:** After six months of failed correction at the border facility, the triplets were **transferred to Ashford** (June 2105). Nikolai's hidden memories survived in the shared Correction Registry. Sokolov's fact-storm program supplies the stolen storm unit.

## The Three Twists

### Twist 1: Reeves arranged the recovery (revealed Ch 24, answered Ch 28)
Erasure was only Phase One. Protocol **R-2, "Seeding"**, tests whether genuine belief can grow in an emptied mind once an attachment figure is placed in front of it. Reeves chose Kira as Marcus's seed. He stole her fact, put her in the cell facing Marcus, cut Marcus's sessions to give the recovery room, and gave them library privileges in the same week. "Did you think it was luck?" The file proves the *conditions* were arranged. Nothing can prove the *feeling* was. Marcus and Kira have to decide for themselves (Ch 28).
- **Clues:** Ch 3, 4, 7, 10, 12, 16 and 18.
- **At national scale,** the method is **erase, then seed**: remove doubt, plant loyalty.

### Twist 2: The storm leaves a false memory (planted Ch 23, discovered Ch 28)
The fact storm fills Marcus's memory gaps. One false memory sets: lying in the grass with Kira under the stars, in the version of history where Ashford is an empty field. It never happened, and he can't feel that it's false. From now on he has to check his memories against other people. This leads into Book 5's theme of testimony versus the record.

### Twist 3: The plan fails before it starts (Ch 20–21)
Reeves finds Nikolai's February audit trail and runs a **single-node session** on him alone to test the bond. The bond holds, but Nikolai loses the rooftop memory and can no longer answer the family passphrase, *"Who was born first?"*. Integration is moved to Aug 2, and the escape is rushed forward a week. The costs follow: the storm is only partly calibrated, Reeves is in the building, only part of the archive is copied, Isaiah's staying becomes *necessary*, and the improvised van crashes (three dead).

## Structure

### Prologue: The Conviction (Marcus, Oct 2105)
It picks up at the last line of Story 01. Dr. Moss's assessment. Marcus overhears "C-1 profile confirmed. Transfer to Ashford, Wing C." A dawn transport. Intake with Supervisor Hayes. The first sight of Wing C's glass cells, and three identical men in a triangle.

### PART ONE: THE ERASURE (Oct – Dec 2105)
*Horror: what they are doing to us.*

1. **Cell 7-H (Marcus).** Transparent, soundproof cells. Old Songs hums at the meal slot. Samuel Ferris, in 7-G, is taken and returns blank. *This isn't therapy. It's erasure.*
2. **The White Room (Marcus).** The first session. Reeves's calm voice, Moss holding his hand, Morrison's shaking hands. His memories of Elena are examined and rewritten. "These memories are corrupted."
3. **Two Months (Marcus, fragmented).** His fact-entry thoughts decay. Elena, his parents and his belief drain away. Isaiah arrives. Reeves: "Reduce to maintenance… keep 7-K open." He doesn't look up when Kira arrives.
4. **The Arrival (Kira).** She is placed in 7-K ("Dr. Reeves was specific"), facing the broken man. She resists on instinct by reciting Spire specs. A dull library book arrives with **REMEMBER** underlined on page 47.
5. **The Corrected (Dmitri).** In the garden, the triplets are awake. Their history: the border facility, the transfer to Ashford, the privileges, Sokolov's monthly visits. Alexei has broken the rule of silence.
6. **The Library (Alexei).** Old Songs' secret: "hide what you love in what you've memorised." Alexei's guilt over Marcus. He sends the book to Kira.
7. **The Observer (Tanaka).** A former Ashford specialist, now subject C-6. Her clinical casebook: connection resists, isolation erases, and Marcus is near the "floor". 7-K was kept empty for weeks. The staff are divided.
8. **The Pattern (Isaiah).** His memory palace. He glimpses the header "PROJECT CONTINUITY: C-7 (WITNESS)". There are seven kept subjects. Test Subject 2471 was **Ivan Volkov**.

### PART TWO: THE CONNECTION (Dec 2105 – May 2106)
*Mystery: why us? What is really happening?*

9. **The Recognition (Marcus).** His eyes meet Kira's in a corridor. He lifts his head, then lowers it again on purpose.
10. **The Whisper (Kira).** First contact in the library's blind spot, in the week both got privileges. "Don't let them see you coming back." "I'll remember for both of us."
11. **The Garden Meetings (Marcus).** The circle of four forms. Old Songs' tune brings back his mother's lullaby. Marcus hides behind fact-entry formats and fools the machine. Dmitri: "You're doing well. Careful."
12. **The Discovery (Nikolai).** He finds the Continuity files: the mind types, C-1 kept as a "SUCCESS" for the demonstration, the triple cradle, integration on Aug 10 and the demonstration on Aug 11, "Placement 7-K per R-2" (locked). He leaves an audit trail.
13. **The Revelation (Dmitri).** The triplets reveal themselves to Marcus. "August the tenth. They mean to finish all of us." Dmitri apologises.
14. **The Message System (Alexei).** Pinprick codes in books, cart routes, garden stones, Old Songs' humming. Everyone builds a personal memory vault. "We survived because we were three. You can survive because you are seven."
15. **The Triplet Story (Nikolai).** Their history, told through the code. Papa was corrected before he died. Aunt Elena makes contact through Nikolai's hidden partition, signing with the phrase "Who was born first?". Marcus realises who Elena is.
16. **The Conspiracy (Marcus).** Phase Two targets the whole population. Kira's fact was stolen to acquire her. **Marcus's score was raised from 0.58 to 0.73:** he would have gone home. R-2 is misread. "We're lab rats. But some of us can't be broken."
17. **The Ally (Tanaka).** Moss is sincere and immovable. Morrison turns: "I'm sorry. I built the calibration." The triple cradle will "break the bond or kill all three".

### PART THREE: THE PLAN (May – Jul 2106)
*Conspiracy and reversal.*

18. **The Breaking Point (Marcus).** Reeves, in person, probes for Kira. He is measuring, not deleting. Marcus loses the library memory forever. In her office, Director Kovač reveals she is an ally (the camera footage, her nephew Adrian). Kira re-tells him the lost conversation.
19. **The Alliance Forms (Kira).** All seven meet, with Kovač and Morrison. Elena's offer: a storm and an extraction on Aug 9–10. "Escape with the evidence."
20. **The Impossible Plan (Dmitri).** The stolen storm unit. Flatlines faked so they are "officially dead". Sokolov is not to be counted on. **Reversal:** Reeves finds the audit trail, and Nikolai is taken.
21. **The Single Node (Nikolai), new.** Interrogation, then a solo session in the triple cradle. The bond holds, but he loses the rooftop memory. Integration moves to **Aug 2**, and Reeves will be in the building. Dmitri writes the rooftop out for him.
22. **The Sacrifice (Isaiah).** With no time to copy everything, someone must stay and send the rest. Isaiah chooses to stay; Kovač stays too. Old Songs: "The one who stays is the one who remembers."

### PART FOUR: THE STORM (Aug 1–2, 2106)
*Action: chaos and escape.*

23. **The Fact Storm (Marcus).** The first storm inside Veridica: Ashford is a hospital, a prison, a field. Staff break down. The false memories are warm, and one of them sticks: the field with Kira. Kira anchors him with a fact entry. The triplets hold Nikolai together. Samuel sits untouched.
24. **The Breakout (Kira).** Samuel doesn't understand leaving. The archive is opened with Kira's own stolen protocol, and **R-2** is found. Reeves: the border storms were Continuity field trials, the method is erase-then-seed, and **"Did you think it was luck?"** Dmitri subdues him; Tanaka is scarred. Isaiah and Kovač stay. Moss raises the alarm sincerely.
25. **The Cost of Freedom (Dmitri).** Flatlines at the fence. An inmate refuses to go on. Elena arrives with two cars and an improvised van. Nikolai can't answer the passphrase, and Dmitri answers for him. Elena and Marcus: "Kira gave some of it back." The pursuit begins.
26. **The Pursuit (Alexei).** A chase into the border zone's storms. The van crashes and three die. At the checkpoint, Sokolov looks at Dmitri's "dead" face and radios "No contact". The safe house; Old Songs sings for the dead.

### PART FIVE: THE AFTERMATH (Aug – Nov 2106)
*Survival is not victory.*

27. **The Report (Isaiah, the article).** Published Aug 5: Continuity, 0.58 to 0.73, 2471, and the R-2 protocol, with the pairing deliberately left out. The subjects are listed "deceased". It becomes an international scandal. Isaiah is moved to maximum security, and a pinpricked note reaches him: "We're alive. —K."
28. **The Recovery (Kira).** Scars, including Kira's own doubt: *was I placed?* Marcus mentions "the field", and it never happened: storm confabulation. They choose each other anyway: "Reeves arranged a room. He didn't arrange what we did in it." "Not a fact. A hope." "With him. By choice."
29. **The Missions (Marcus).** They scatter. Tanaka and Morrison go to Chronos. Alexei, Dmitri and Old Songs join the Resistance with Elena. Nikolai becomes a mole in Veridica. Marcus and Kira go to the border zone. The brothers retell Nikolai the rooftop. Marcus keeps checked and unchecked memories: "Many witnesses, not one record." Correction by people, not of people.

### Epilogue: Seven Paths, One Truth (Nov 14, 2106)
Vignettes. Isaiah receives an expulsion order for Nov 15 at 14:30. Tanaka's defectors prepare to cross. The brothers plan tomorrow's extraction. Nikolai notices an unowned block of system activity at 14:00–15:00. Kovač sits outside Samuel's cell. Elena: "Tomorrow, 14:00 to 15:00." Marcus: "What if I remember it wrong and never know?" Kira: "Then we'll remember together."
**Final line:** *"Tomorrow, history will be made. And even if they erase it, even if they try to correct it, we'll remember. We survived erasure once. We'll survive it again."*

## Character Arcs

- **Marcus:** From true believer, to erased, to recovering in secret, to learning the Judge was falsified, to escaping with doubt about his own love and his own memories, to *choosing* both. He never gets back what was lost. His mission is desperate, not confident.
- **Kira:** From fighting mind, to anchor, to finding she may have been "placed", to someone who decides her love is her own. She is damaged too (her compulsive reciting).
- **Dmitri:** From controlling planner to letting go. He breaks the silence he enforced, and makes the calculation that costs three lives.
- **Alexei:** From breaking the rule out of conscience, to running the network, to learning what the crossings really cost.
- **Nikolai:** From the frightened bridge, to the one who pays for the discovery (the rooftop), to rebuilding a memory from his brothers' testimony.
- **Tanaka:** From healer turned subject, to clinician of the resistance, to scarred and on her way to Chronos.
- **Isaiah:** From witness, to chronicler, to the one who stays, and who edits the truth only once, out of love.
- **Elena:** The recruiter from Story 01, the aunt from Story 03. Her relationship with Marcus is left unresolved.
- **Staff:** Moss (a sincere believer who raises the alarm), Morrison (turned, becomes the defector), Kovač (ally, survives in her post), Reeves (an antagonist who survives and may connect to the Architects), Sokolov (spares them, with doubled and deniable motives).

## Tone & Themes

- **Part One:** horror. **Part Two:** mystery. **Part Three:** conspiracy and reversal. **Part Four:** action. **Part Five:** aftermath.
- Can you keep your identity while someone is erasing it?
- Is belief genuine if its conditions were manufactured? *(Twist 1)*
- Can you trust a memory you can't feel is false? *(Twist 2)*
- Connection as a counter-technology: three brothers, seven subjects, two lovers.
- Freedom has costs, and survival isn't victory.

## Handoff to Book 5 (*The Lost Hour*)

- The escape happens in August 2106. Book 5 opens three months later.
- Marcus is an outlaw in hiding who plans to upload the evidence during the Lost Hour.
- Isaiah crosses the border on Nov 15 on an expulsion order, with papers he can't prove he legally carried.
- Tanaka is in Chronos helping defectors. Alexei and Dmitri run the extraction. Nikolai keeps a partial Registry backup and flags the 14:00–15:00 block. Elena coordinates.
- Kovač is still Director and able to join the investigation. Morrison is the defector.
- Kira will recognise the "signature" of her stolen fact in the erasure.
- Twist 2's lesson (memories need witnesses) is the human side of the Lost Hour Doctrine.
