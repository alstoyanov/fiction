# Battle of Truths: Full Plot Outline (v3)

**Status:** CURRENT. Revision 3, Sept 2026. It replaces v2 (Nov 2025). It is consistent with the short stories, *The Correction*, *The Lost Hour* (drafted), *The Distributed Truth* (plot v2 and chapter summaries), `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md`, `characters/` and `worldbuilding/`.
**Genre:** spy thriller and moral tragedy. The book is about the betrayal that Book 6 refused, and what it costs when it is committed for good reasons.
**Structure:** Prologue + 30 chapters + Epilogue, in four parts. About 110,000 words (about 3,400 per chapter).
**Timeline:** Wednesday, August 14, 2109 – Monday, December 23, 2109. Epilogue on Friday, January 10, 2110.
**Consistency rule:** this book must agree with Books 1–6. Books 8–9 will be rewritten to fit it.

---

## High Concept

Five months after the heroes refused to give the witness networks a center, somebody uses the center that exists. At **03:12 on August 14, 2109**, the same minute the Chronos Gap began, two million journals registered in Veridica's **Concordance** are quietly **rewritten** overnight, and so are the witness books of Julian Kovač's networks in Chronos. Neighbours' countersignatures now stand under lies. Within a week a man is dead in a lakeshore town because his street's registered book says he informed on it. Chronos's Judge resumes the war of facts. The countersignature on the rewriting is **the key of the Chronos Temporal Archive**: Julian's key, the one Nikolai matched to the Chronos Gap in February.

Marcus Chen has *Julian?* on his UNCHECKED list. He checks the Chronos record, and the record says Julian has held the key since 2104. Marcus goes to the First Minister and asks for the operation he once refused. **RESTORATION:** use Julian's trust to get inside the Temporal Archive, cut the Chronos hand out of the true source, and restore Chronos's account of both attacks. He does it. It works far beyond its purpose, because Veridica's and Chronos's true sources turn out to be **one system**. In one night the heroes don't cut the Chronos hand out. They **write Veridica's history into a nation of 150 million**, and Chronos wakes up believing it.

Then the truth comes in, piece by piece. Julian only received the key *after* the Chronos Gap (the keys in January 2108, the office on Wed Feb 1, 2108). The proof was a paper letter in his aunt's folder the whole time, and nobody asked the witness. The copy of the manuscript Julian found and gave Marcus shows the rivalry between nations was designed. And somebody inside Veridica has been handing the SFW the story of Veridica's crime *before it happened*. The Battle of Truths is won, and the Battle of Blood is being arranged.

**The question the book asks:** what happens when the man who built his life on checking stops checking, because the record said what he was afraid of?

## The Manuscript Thread (Book 7)

Revealed in this book (`00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md` §4–5):
- **The title page:** *THE FOUNDATION OF CERTAINTY. A design for the governance of a species that cannot agree on what happened.* For the first time the characters know its name.
- **The table of contents:** the Introduction, Principles One to Six with their titles, the **Implementation Annex** and the **Register of Contributors**. Seven is handwritten and unlisted, so it doesn't appear. Every fragment found so far can now be placed. **Two things become known: a Register exists, and an Annex exists.** Principle Four's *title* (*Correction Over Punishment*) is now known, but its *text* stays for Book 8.
- **Principle One** (pp. 5–8, mark *HV·01*): *"Singular truth, mechanically verified, universally enforced… Democracy failed because it assumed humans could collectively determine what happened. We remove the assumption."*
- **Principle Two** (pp. 9–12, mark *SO·04*): *"…Let there be several Histologic nations, differing in method and agreeing in substance, and let there be enemies outside them all. Peace would raise questions. Total war would end the system. Controlled conflict answers every question before it is asked."*

**The route:** tracing the Rewriting through the Archive's sealed holdings, **Julian finds a bundle of typed pages with copy numeral VII** (Thu Sep 19): the title page, the table of contents, and Principles One and Two. He lends them to Marcus as an act of trust (Sat Sep 21; Ch 10–11). Marcus returns the originals on Tue Sep 24 to box 204117, so **copy VII stays in Chronos**; afterwards the heroes have only Kira's photographs and their notes. The copy has the chipped *e* and marks that nobody can read. **Vance recognises the numeral and won't say whose it is:** "That copy should not be in Chronos." (VII is Cardinal Tobias Kovač's, TK·07, Julian's great-uncle. That is decoded in Book 9 and never hinted at by name.)

**The 2074 storm:** also in the Archive, Julian finds the Chronos-side log of the lakeshore storm of **Mon Jun 11, 2074**, the storm that killed Anya and Katya Petrov. It is logged as a scheduled **"calibration run"**, coordinated with Veridica's Border Broadcast Division. He sends it to "the old gentleman who sings" (Ch 13).

**Rules held:** no mark is decoded; no contributor or heir is named; the Register's contents and the Annex's text are unseen; Principle Four's text is unseen; Seven is unknown. The characters may **guess**, and do: the Register means there is a list of names. After his confession Marcus privately tells the Minister about copy VII and the Register (Ch 25), and Chen Zhao warmly offers to help trace the copy numerals (dramatic irony).

## The Twists

### Twist 1: One source. (Ch 16.)
The operation is surgical by design. Kira, who joined only to "hold the knife so it only cuts what it should", builds a narrow restoration image. It should revoke the Archive key's rights in the true source and restore Chronos's own records of the Rewriting and the Gap. That night in the Temporal Archive, Chronos's backup accepts the Veridican master credentials as if they were its own. There is nothing to breach and nothing to replace. **Veridica's and Chronos's true sources are one system** ("three backup systems that appear independent and are not": the heroes had published Principle Three believing it meant *within* a nation). The narrow image propagates across the join. By morning, **Chronos's record of every contested event** (the lakeshore, the Temporal War, the Gap, the Rewriting) reads as Veridica's does. Chronos's Judge confirms it, and 150 million people believe it. The heroes set out to cut one hand and have rewritten a nation.
- **Clues:**
  - The Ch 1 puzzle: how could a Chronos key reach the Concordance master in Veridica?
  - Wei's tools are "too clean" (Ch 14).
  - Nikolai: "Their backup structure is like looking in a mirror" (Ch 14).
  - Principle Three's own wording (Book 6).
  - Principle Two, read in Ch 11: "differing in method and agreeing in substance".

### Twist 2: The key wasn't his. (Ch 24.)
Kovač reads Julian's published log and remembers a letter. In her folders (she keeps folders instead of acting; it is her flaw and, this once, her gift) is a **handwritten letter from Julian dated January 2108**: *"They've made me Senior Historian. I start in February. They've given me the old man's keys. I'm frightened of them, Aunt Helena."* **Julian didn't hold the key in December 2107.** He received the keys in January 2108 and took office on Wed Feb 1, 2108. His predecessor held it: **Dr. Augustin Mera**, Senior Historian for twenty years, who retired in January 2108 and "died" in February 2108, cremated within a day. The Chronos record showing Julian as key-holder since 2104 was edited (Principle Three). Marcus, the man who "checked", checked the **record** instead of a **witness**, because the record said what he feared.
- **Clues:**
  - Ch 2: Julian begins "I've only had it since—" and Aubry cuts across him. Marcus checks the Archive's public roll: *Key-holder since 2104.*
  - Ch 10: Julian's POV speaks of "the old man's" habits at the Archive without explaining them.
  - Ch 19: under interrogation Julian says it plainly ("the keys in January, the office in February"), and nobody in Chronos believes a man against his own record. His own log was edited to agree with the roll.
  - The pattern of Mera's death: a staged-looking death, like Singh's in Book 5.
  - Kovač's folders (Book 5; Book 6 Ch 7).

### Twist 3: Enemies outside them all. (Ch 28.)
Colonel **Beatriz Martinez** of SFW intelligence (the officer in `morally-ambiguous-characters.md` §3, who read Isaiah's spiked column and understood it) shows Isaiah the folder he glimpsed in March. The SFW had a **full account of RESTORATION** (targets, dates, the Archive, even the codename) **eleven days before it happened**. The packets came through channels in Veridica, each sealed with a familiar seam. **Someone in Veridica has been building the SFW's case for war**, just as Principle Two says: *"let there be enemies outside them all… controlled conflict."* Isaiah's own condemnation of Marcus ("The Restorer", Ch 22) is in the SFW Council's war brief. Isaiah: *"Someone in Veridica wants to be invaded."* Why is Book 8's question. (The answer: war justifies mass correction, the population-scale Phase Two of Book 4, and Principle Four.)
- **Clues:**
  - Book 6 Epilogue (the folder).
  - Ch 5: the attaché knew the Rewriting's death toll before Veridica published it.
  - Ch 12: the SFW asks Isaiah to authenticate pages he hasn't published.
  - Ch 22: his editors print "The Restorer" within an hour, uncut, which has never happened.

### Two further turns
- **The 2074 storm (Ch 13).** Old Songs learns that the storm that killed his wife and daughter was scheduled. It is the one time in the series we see him angry. There is no speech, only a broken cup, and a song: **"The Two Maps"**. The gift comes from Julian, the man Marcus is using that same month.
- **Self-disclosure (Ch 21).** Julian, arrested and recorded at maximum, publishes his own log, **every hour he spent with Marcus** (Mon Nov 12, 2108 to Fri Oct 25, 2109, uncut), on **Fri Nov 15, 2109 at 14:00**, the Day of Testimony. The betrayal is permanent, timestamped and public. Marcus reads himself lying at nanosecond resolution.

## Structure

### Prologue: The Rewriting (Ruth Calloway, Tue Aug 13 – Wed Aug 14, 2109)
Kestrel Point on a hot night. The ferry season is on, the pier is water, and the Kestrel Book (43 contributors, paper, unregistered) sits in the rebuilt reading room. At **03:12** Ruth's neighbour (**unnamed**), who registered her personal journal with the Concordance in April "to be safe", wakes Ruth hammering on the door. Her journal on the Concordance terminal says things she never wrote, countersigned by people who never signed. By dawn the whole street is comparing screens. The Kestrel Book, on paper, agrees with itself and with nobody's screen. **Ending:** on the ferry-office terminal, a registered journal from the next town up the shore accuses a named man of informing on his street in 2107, with three countersignatures, two of them from the dead.

### PART ONE: THE REWRITING (Aug 14 – Aug 30)
*Theme: the center they refused is used anyway; the record says what they fear.*

**Ch 1: Two Million Journals** (Kira, Wed Aug 14). The Commission in emergency session. About 2.1 million registered journals have been altered: small changes, plausible ones, the kind that turn neighbours against each other. The same thing has happened to the witness books of Julian's networks in Chronos. The paper pairing-network books are untouched, and they prove it. Wei is calm, helpful, "appalled". The edits came through the Concordance's own **restore path from the master** (the Book 6 Ch 23 detail). Nikolai, that night, holds up the countersignature: **the habits of the Chronos Archive's key**. **Ending:** Kira's question, which nobody can answer: *how does a Chronos key reach a Veridican master?* *(A Twist 1 clue.)*

**Ch 2: Julian?** (Marcus, Thu Aug 15). An emergency session under the Lakes Talks' terms (not the Talks proper) at the Old Customs House. Julian is gray with exhaustion, because his networks are wrecked too, and Chronos is blaming Veridica. In the garden he says, "I don't understand how they used the key. I've only had it since—" and **Aubry comes out and cuts across him** on a question of protocol. That night Marcus checks the Archive's public roll: *KOVAČ, J. Senior Historian. Key-holder since 2104.* **Ending:** Marcus looks at *Julian?* on his UNCHECKED list and doesn't cross it out. He underlines it. *(A Twist 2 clue.)*

**Ch 3: Gullhaven** (Ruth, Mon Aug 19). The next town up the shore. The accused man (a ferryman, **unnamed**) is beaten by neighbours who believe their screens. He dies in the night. Ruth brings the Kestrel Book's copy of that street's entries, which prove the accusation false. She arrives four hours late. Wren, fifteen, writes the entry. **Ending:** the first death of the second war. Ruth writes the dead man's name down so that Old Songs can have it.

**Ch 4: Chronos Believes** (Tanaka, Wed Aug 21, Chronos). Chronos's Judge resumes corrective broadcasting at 0.91: Veridica has "attacked the memory of Chronos a second time". The storms return to the lakeshore. At the hospital there are storm casualties again, and a directorate inquiry into Dr. Vale's January death certificate for "Mrs. Kerr" (Book 6). Julian comes to the hospital as liaison and Tanaka sees how far he has come apart. **Ending:** Vale, stooped, beautifully mannered, calm: "They are asking who else I have certified. I have told them: many people, over many years. It is a hospital."

**Ch 5: The Attaché** (Isaiah, Fri Aug 23, SFW). His editors run the Rewriting as proof that "Histologic memory is worthless". The military attaché from Book 6, lunching with the editor-in-chief, quotes **the Gullhaven death toll a day before Veridica publishes it**. Isaiah notes it in the palace. **Ending:** a new line in the attaché's folder, glimpsed: *Phase: justification.* *(A Twist 3 clue.)*

**Ch 6: I Can't Authorise This** (Marcus, Mon Aug 26). A Chronos storm kills a child in a Veridican lakeshore village, and the dead now number seven. Marcus goes to Maitland himself: give him the operation he refused. Use Julian's trust to get into the Temporal Archive, cut the Chronos hand out of the true source, and restore Chronos's own record of both attacks. Wei, present, supplies the tools and a name: **RESTORATION**. Marcus notices the word and accepts it. Maitland: ***"I can't authorise this. But I won't stop you."*** **Ending:** Marcus writes his first fact entry that he knows to be a lie, for his cover, and files it under CHECKED.

**Ch 7: The Falling Phrase** (Old Songs, Wed Aug 28, the farmhouse). Marcus comes to the stove and tells them. Vance, quietly: "They designed you to be the restorer. This is the restoration." Kira: ***"This is wrong, Marcus. This is what we fought against."*** Elena says nothing. Alexei: "If it stops the storms…" Dmitri asks for time. Old Songs doesn't speak. He hums the Lakeshore Air, and ends on the **falling last phrase, sung downward**: the Wing C signal for **no** (Book 4 Ch 14). Everyone who was in Wing C understands. Marcus goes anyway. **Ending:** that night Old Songs writes in the farmhouse book: *He asked. We said no. He went.*

**Ch 8: The Knife** (Kira, Fri Aug 30). Kira can't stop him, and won't leave him to do it with Wei's tools alone. She sets a condition: she builds the image herself, **narrow**, revoking the Archive key and restoring only Chronos's own records of the Rewriting and the Gap, with nothing else touched. Nikolai agrees to help, sick with guilt. Elena agrees to teach Marcus. **Ending:** *"Then I'll hold the knife,"* Kira says, *"so it only cuts what it should."*

*Theme: a betrayal performed with love; gifts given to the man doing it.*

**Ch 13: The Two Maps** (Old Songs, Fri Oct 4). Julian's second gift, sent through Marcus "for the old gentleman who sings": the Chronos log of the **lakeshore storm of Mon Jun 11, 2074**. *"Calibration run 14. Coordinated with V. Border Broadcast Division."* Anya and Katya. **The one time we see him angry:** no words, one cup broken against the stove, then a long silence that Vance sits inside with him. Nikolai, uneasy: "Their backup structure is like looking in a mirror." Maya, a Reeves: "Tools this good are built for the job they're used for." **Ch 15: The Key** (Marcus, Fri Oct 18 – Thu Oct 24). The inquiry's last week inside the Archive. Julian gives him access to the reconciliation rooms, as co-chair, without being asked. Evenings in Julian's flat; the handwritten journal; the Customs House garden, remembered and hoped for; the thing Julian almost says and doesn't. Marcus lets him hope, which is the worst thing he does in the book. **Ending:** CHECKED: *Oct 24, 2109. Chronos Temporal Archive. Involved parties: Chen, M.; Kovač, J. Event: I used him. Verification: me.*

**Ch 16: One Source** (Kira, Fri Oct 25 night – Sat Oct 26). **Twist 1.**
- **The approach:** the operation inside the Temporal Archive. Alexei's route across the Drowned Causeway; Dmitri on the doors; Nikolai on the relay; Marcus with Julian's access.
- **The image goes in**, narrow and clean. Then the Chronos backup **accepts the Veridican master credentials as its own**. There is no breach and nothing to replace.
- **The propagation:** the image crosses the join. Kira watches the scope indicator climb past everything she wrote: the lakeshore, the Temporal War, the Gap, the Rewriting, 150 million records aligning to Veridica's account. She can't stop it without leaving both records half-written.

**Ending:** ***"There was nothing to replace. It was always one."***
