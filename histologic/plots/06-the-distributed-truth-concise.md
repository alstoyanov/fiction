# The Distributed Truth: Concise Plot (v2)

**Status:** CURRENT, Sept 2026. The short version of `06-the-distributed-truth-full.md` (v2), which is authoritative.
**Type:** Book 6. A political thriller and conspiracy mystery.
**Length:** Prologue + 30 chapters + Epilogue, about 110,000 words.
**Timeline:** Oct 30, 2108 – Feb 14, 2109. Epilogue on Mar 15, 2109.

## Core Concept
On Dec 13, 2107, seventeen minutes vanish from the life-logs of Chronos's lakeshore province. Chronos's Judge finds the Lost Hour's technique in the gap and blames Veridica. By autumn 2108 the two nations are fighting a **war of facts**: rival histories broadcast across the border, and fact storms spreading into lakeshore towns. The Continuity survivors know whose hand made the gap. They kept silent in Book 5, and the silence has become a war.

Veridica offers Marcus Chen **Operation KINDRED**: befriend Chronos's senior historian **Julian Kovač** and take his reconciliation key, so that Veridica can "restore" Chronos's record from the true source. Marcus refuses, and publishes everything by many hands, in three nations at once: his orders, Julian's matching orders, and the first fragments of *The Foundation of Certainty*. The war stops. The government confesses first and offers the **Concordance**, a national index of every journal. That is the trap. The last part of the book is a race to keep "many hands" from becoming one record again.

## The Four Twists
1. **MERIDIAN (Ch 8).** When Marcus confesses KINDRED to Julian, Julian produces his own orders: Chronos sent him to turn Marcus. Both states ran R-2 on the same two men. The confession is in Julian's Chronos log for good, so the refusal becomes a double publication.
2. **The remedy was ready (Ch 24).** Vance sent only three of the fragments. Principles Three and Five (copy **X**) were fed through the Commission's intake from inside the Ministry, in the order that made publication inevitable. The Concordance was drafted weeks before the publication it "answers". Page 30, now identified as Principle Six, says it: *"Arrange to be the one who restores it."* "They didn't stop our publication. They finished it."
3. **Mrs. Kerr (Ch 21).** The Compiler is Tanaka's own long-term patient in a Chronos sealed ward, the one place in Chronos where nobody is logged. The director who protected Tanaka is Vance's son. Old Songs recognises the alias: Kerr was their teacher.
4. **Two hands (Ch 30).** The Chronos Gap carries Wei's seam and a Chronos-side countersignature. The countersigning key is Julian Kovač's. Was the war run by people on both sides together? It stays unanswered and sets up Book 7.

## Plot Structure
**Prologue: The Doubled Pier.** Ruth saves Wren from a pier that exists only in Chronos's broadcast, and writes it down. A Border Security van with its mast folded: the storm came from their own side.

**Part One: The War of Facts (Ch 1–8).**
- The Commission blames the Chronos Gap on a Chronos fabrication, while Kira sits on the truth.
- Maitland, Chen Zhao and Wei offer Marcus KINDRED.
- In Chronos, Tanaka's patient M.K. sends songbooks "for the old gentleman".
- Old Songs finds the **Compiler's Introduction** in a binding and names **Elise Vance**.
- The Lakes Talks begin, and Julian is gentle, lonely and too well informed.
- Kovač tells Marcus that Julian is her nephew, Adrian's brother.
- Marcus confesses to Julian, and Julian confesses back (**Twist 1**).

**Part Two: The Fragments (Ch 9–16).**
- Anonymous copy-X deposits bring **Principle Three**, in which Kira sees her stolen fact, and **Principle Five**, in which the Volkovs see their father's death planned and Sokolov reads her job.
- Julian's POV includes his own seventeen minutes "asleep".
- Isaiah gets his third fragment.
- A strip of the table of contents identifies page 30 as **Principle Six**.
- Tanaka stops Chronos's "stabilisation" with M.K.'s help.
- The handwritten **Compiler's Warning** arrives.
- Publication at 14:00 on Mon Dec 17, in three nations. Sokolov stands her transmitters down, Chronos's Judge ends corrective broadcasting, and the first night without a storm follows.

**Part Three: The Concordance (Ch 17–24).**
- Maitland confesses first and launches the Concordance.
- Pell's League burns a journal house.
- The Kestrel Book catches a falsifier who was paid by Concordance outreach.
- Old Songs crosses the lake illegally, and finds Vance (**Twist 3**).
- Her confession and the one page she withholds (Seven, Book 8). Chronos agents come for her.
- Maya finds the Concordance drafts, which predate the publication, and the Ministry origin of the copy-X deposits (**Twist 2**).

**Part Four: Many Hands (Ch 25–30).**
- Marcus argues against the Concordance Act and loses the room. His contamination index climbs live to **0.58**.
- The Night of Copies: Vance dictates her confession to twelve keepers in twelve towns, and nobody holds the original.
- Old Songs teaches her the round "Many Hands", with Kira as the third voice.
- **The New Foundation**, a reply by many hands that mirrors the manuscript, with **blank answers** for the principles nobody has seen yet and an open register.
- The Judge rules unregistered journals below threshold (0.41). The Concordance becomes voluntary.
- Julian: "Thank you for not weaponising my loneliness." Wei hands Marcus a page of his journal: "They refused the index. That was not in the design either." "Did I?"
- Nikolai finds Julian's key in the Chronos Gap (**Twist 4**).

**Epilogue: The Round (Mar 15, 2109).**
- Kestrel Point at peace.
- The crowd picks up the round.
- Sokolov is promoted too fast.
- The SFW has the fragments in a military folder.
- Julian writes that his brother has written to him.
- Marcus leaves *Julian?* on the UNCHECKED list.
- Final line: *"The design could anticipate every reformer. It could not anticipate everyone."*

## Manuscript Revealed in This Book
The Compiler's Introduction · Principle Three · Principle Five · a strip of the table of contents (identifying page 30 as Principle Six) · the Compiler's Warning · Vance herself.
**Not revealed:** Principles One, Two and Four, the Register, the Annex, the heirs, any decoded mark, and Principle Seven, which Vance withholds.
