# Nations of the Histologic World

**Status:** CURRENT canon, Sept 2026. It is consistent with Books 1–5 and `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md`. It replaces `nations-full.md` and `nations-concise.md`.

## Two Truths About Every Nation
1. **The official story** (what citizens believe, from `basic-idea.txt`): Veridica was the first histonation. The others formed as **histobranches**, when large groups disputed facts and split away, or as nations that **rejected** the system outright. Each nation's philosophy is sincere, and each nation's hostility to the others is real.
2. **The truth** (Principle Two, *Legitimacy Through Controlled Opposition*): the **number** of Histologic nations, their **differences** of method, their **borders** and even their **enemies** were planned in 2064–66. *"Differing in method and agreeing in substance."* The splits happened, and people died in them, but they were **engineered**. Nobody in Books 1–5 knows this. It surfaces from Book 7 onward.

Write every nation sincerely from inside. The design shows only in patterns.

## Histologic Nations

### 1. Veridica: "Truth Through Precision" *(the setting of Books 1–5)*
- **Founded:** early 2066, when The Judge was activated. It is the first histonation.
- **Territory:** the former North American East Coast, extending to the **Great Lakes**. The capital is unnamed so far: an ordered city of transit lines, the **Factorepo Spire**, the Justice Center, the Historical Archives, the Meridian Bridge, and the Archive District of preserved pre-Histologic buildings. Ashford Correctional Facility is on its eastern edge.
- **Population:** about 180 million.
- **Factorepo:** the oldest and densest, with the strictest input protocols. Historians verify facts at regional factorepos, and systems architects maintain the Spire. Surveillance is everywhere and is **accepted as comforting**: "In a world where everything was known, nothing could be hidden."
- **Government:** a Chancellor (ceremonial head of state) and a **First Minister** who leads the Cabinet (Oskar Maitland from Book 6), with ministries (Historical Integrity, Correction, Border Security and others). All of it is subordinate to **The Judge**, whose determinations are final.
- **Interfaces:** mandatory since the **2065 Act**, installed at about age 8, with a family "enlightenment day" celebration.
- **Justice:** contamination above 60% means corrective custody. Reporting an outlaw doesn't protect you; exposure is what counts (Story 01).
- **Outlaws:** corrected. Those who flee or are expelled lose citizenship. Expulsion by order exists (Isaiah, Book 5).
- **Travel:** citizens move freely inside Veridica, but international travel needs an extensive background fact-check. Visitors from other Histologic nations get a 30-day visa, with their recent history uploaded and synchronised. Visitors from non-Histologic nations are admitted only to diplomatic zones, with monitoring devices.
- **Stance:** regards other Histologic nations as "corrupted versions" and non-Histologic nations as barbaric.

### 2. Chronos: "Every Moment Matters"
- **Founded:** 2067, the first official **histobranch** from Veridica, in a dispute over temporal precision. Chronos records from 2069 appear in Story 03.
- **Territory:** the former North American Midwest and Great Plains. It borders Veridica across the **Great Lakes**.
- **Population:** about 150 million.
- **Factorepo:** obsessive temporal precision, with nanosecond timestamps and **continuous life-logging**. Chronos interfaces *record and upload* constantly, not just receive. It has the largest data volume per person.
- **Government:** "algorithmic democracy". The Judge reads the collective will from patterns in the facts, and there are no human representatives.
- **Society:** privacy is considered a dangerous illusion. Interfaces are installed early, and outlaws have their recording forced to maximum.
- **Technology:** Chronos builds the best neural-interface hardware. Veridica's correction cradles use Chronos technology ("the irony wasn't lost on Dmitri", Story 03), and Dr. Tanaka works in a Chronos teaching hospital after 2106.
- **Travel:** visitors must accept temporary neural monitoring or stay in quarantine zones. Non-Histologic visitors are refused as "temporal black holes".
- **Versus Veridica:** the **Temporal War**, fought through contradictory facts, especially in the Lake Erie **border zone**. Veridica calls Chronos's life-logging "invasive". Chronos says Veridica's record has "temporal gaps".

### 3. Memoria: "Context Over Data"
- **Founded:** 2069, the first European histonation.
- **Territory:** former Western Europe (France, Germany, the Benelux countries, northern Italy). About 200 million people.
- **Factorepo:** emphasises cultural and artistic facts, with qualitative metadata on every fact. It allows an "interpretive layer" of annotations. The facts themselves are single and binding, but context and commentary are permitted alongside them.
- **Government:** cultural meritocracy, run by a Council of Rememberers (artists, historians and philosophers). The Judge advises and ratifies.
- **Society:** the most humane-seeming of the Histologic nations. Surveillance covers public spaces only, and outlaws often become underground artists.
- **Versus the others:** Veridica calls the interpretive layer "corruption". Chronos calls Memoria's private spaces "blind spots". Memoria has a border dispute with Axiom over northern Italy.

### 4. Axiom: "Truth Through Logic"
- **Founded:** 2071, a histobranch from Memoria over whether facts must be *derivable*.
- **Territory:** Scandinavia, the Baltic states and parts of Eastern Europe. About 120 million people.
- **Factorepo:** the smallest, holding only the "axiomatically necessary" facts. It has the most powerful Judge, which derives everything else. Mathematical proofs count as historical facts.
- **Government:** pure logocracy. The Judge is absolute, and citizens submit formal arguments to it.
- **Society:** the elite are those who reason best. Outlaws are "logical errors" and are detained until they can prove their innocence formally.
- **Versus the others:** Axiom claims to have *proven* that Veridica's record contains contradictions. Relations with Veridica are closed.

### 5. Synthesis: "Unity Through Compromise"
- **Founded:** 2074, formed by people displaced from several histobranches.
- **Territory:** former East Asia (Korea, Japan, Taiwan). About 90 million people.
- **Factorepo:** keeps **parallel factorepos**, one for each origin tradition, under a "synthesis layer" that reconciles them. Each citizen belongs to one lineage. Synthesis is the exception that proves the rule, and every other nation calls it "confused".
- **Government:** a rotating council, several Judges, and a **Meta-Judge** that settles conflicts between them.
- **Role:** the neutral hub. Diplomatic negotiations happen here, and the **Synthesis Institute for Historical Methods** runs an archive exchange with correctional facilities. That exchange is the channel Isaiah used to publish in Book 4.
- **Book 5:** it carries Isaiah's articles and hosts international reaction.

## Non-Histologic Nations
In the official story these are enemies that rejected the system. The design needs them: *"Let there be enemies outside them all."* (Principle Two.)

### 6. The Sovereignty of Free Will (SFW): "Truth Cannot Be Mechanised"
- **Formed:** 2067, when it explicitly rejected the Histologic system.
- **Territory:** the Middle East, North Africa and parts of South Asia (Iran, Turkey, Egypt, the Arabian Peninsula, Pakistan, Afghanistan). About 250 million people.
- **No neural interfaces.** Human judges, an oral-history tradition, and privacy held sacred. A religious basis (many faiths united against the system) and a democratic republic.
- **Relations:** cold but functional. It grants immediate asylum to Histologic outlaws and uses them as **propaganda**. In Book 5 Isaiah claims asylum at an **SFW mission in Chronos**.
- **Versus the CNT:** a bitter rivalry. There were border wars in 2091–93 over the Caucasus and Central Asia, and monthly skirmishes continue.

### 7. The Confederacy of Natural Truth (CNT): "Reality Needs No Recording"
- **Formed:** 2068, a violent rejection of early Histologic expansion.
- **Territory:** Russia, Central Asia and the Caucasus (Russia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan). About 300 million people.
- **No neural interfaces.** An authoritarian confederation run by a Supreme Council. History is officially "what the Council says it is". It has the largest conventional military and the best cyber warfare, and it is openly hostile to the Histologic nations.
- **Versus the SFW:** as above. Both want to lead the anti-Histologic world, and the Histologic nations benefit from their division.

### Unaligned territories
Failed states, remote regions and some disputed strips outside every system. They are home to refugees and smugglers. (There is little canon on them so far.)

## Borders

### The Veridica–Chronos border zone (Lake Erie)
- **Geography:** fog off the lake (real weather), abandoned warehouse districts, lakeshore towns, marshes, causeways and old rail cuttings.
- **Jurisdiction:** **claimed by both** nations and receiving both broadcasts. Buildings have two histories. Safe houses change jurisdiction "three times a day" (Story 03).
- **The neutral zone:** a strip that is officially neutral and in practice claimed by both. It holds a **hospital**, whose wards "flicker between jurisdictions", and the **border facility**, a correctional facility and "the only place both nations agreed on anything" (Story 03).
- **Fact storms** are frequent here, officially natural (see `fact-storms.md`). There are sirens, cognitive trauma wards, and a Border Security wing at the hospital.
- **Checkpoints:** fog-bound, with floodlights and barriers. Border Security on the Veridican side, Chronos enforcement on the other.

### Other border types
- **Histologic–Histologic:** "fact reconciliation zones", with counselling for "historical dissonance". Some are closed entirely (Veridica–Axiom).
- **Histologic–non-Histologic:** militarised buffer zones, broadcast dead zones, and refugee camps.
- **SFW–CNT:** the most dangerous border in the world, from the Black Sea to Afghanistan, with minefields and mountain smuggling routes.

## International Dynamics (Official View)
- **The Temporal War (Veridica vs. Chronos):** fought through contradictions and border storms. The Judges refuse to communicate with each other.
- **The Philosophical War (Veridica vs. Axiom):** a dispute over proofs. Exchange has ceased.
- **The Human War (Memoria vs. Chronos):** each side calls the other's system dystopian.
- **The Unity Question:** every pure nation distrusts Synthesis, and every one of them needs it as a mediator.
- **The Cold War (Histologic vs. non-Histologic):** cyber attacks, propaganda, and proxy conflicts in unaligned territory.
- **"Some theorise the Histologic nations secretly fuel the SFW–CNT conflict."** They are right (Principle Two).
