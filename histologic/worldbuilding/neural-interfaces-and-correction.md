# Neural Interfaces and the Correction System

**Status:** CURRENT canon, Sept 2026. It is consistent with Books 1–5 and the manuscript plan. It replaces `fact-storms/neural-interface-society.md` and brings together the correction mechanics recorded in `novels/04-the-correction/chapters-summaries/README.md`.
**Decided rule:** **removing an interface is always fatal.** No partial removal or disabling exists. The only way to escape its tracking is to **spoof its telemetry**.

---

# Part 1: The Neural Interface

## 1.1 What It Is
- **Hardware:** a microscopic bio-integrated **mesh**. It is inserted through tiny incisions **behind the ears**, assembles itself in the **hippocampus and prefrontal cortex**, and grows with the brain. The installation scars fade to thin white lines.
- **Receiving:** a constant low-level broadcast of facts about the wearer's surroundings, which arrive as things the wearer simply *knows*. Facts from the interface feel exactly like memory or sight, and can't be told apart from them.
- **Reporting:** a continuous thin stream of **telemetry** to the national **Correction Registry**: location, vital signs and cognitive status. Nobody is ever aware of it. *"You can leave your house and your name and your family, but you cannot leave your own head."* (Dmitri, Book 4.)
- **Interaction:** the mesh talks to medical and correction equipment such as assessment bands and correction cradles. It "wakes up and says *yes*", and it can be *eager* for a network.
- **Chronos variant:** records and uploads continuously (life-logging). Chronos makes the best interface hardware in the world.

## 1.2 How It Became Universal
| Year | Step |
|------|------|
| 2050s | **Voluntary.** Sold as protection against the Fracture Years' misinformation: perfect memory, instant knowledge, "the end of propaganda". Adopted first by officials, scientists, the military and the wealthy. |
| 2061 | Required for government employment. |
| 2062 | Required for education. |
| 2063 | Required for healthcare. |
| 2064 | Required for employment. |
| **2065** | **The Mandatory Citizenship Act:** required for citizenship. "Refusal is essentially choosing exile." |
| From 2066 | Standard installation for children at **about age 8** (Dmitri was 8, "the year after the Act"). |

*"Sold as freedom, implemented as control."* (Alexei, Story 03.)

## 1.3 Installation
- **Children (about age 8):** a week of preparation (brain mapping), 3–4 hours of minimally invasive surgery, 1–2 weeks of recovery, and **activation** in week three.
- **"Enlightenment day"** is a family celebration with a cake iced with the national seal. The first morning afterwards the child simply *knows* the date of the Founding, "like a door opening" (Marcus, Book 4 Prologue).
- **Adults** (immigrants, late adopters): harder and slower, with more complications. It never feels natural.
- Interfaces are universal in the Histologic nations (99.9%). The SFW and CNT have none.

## 1.4 Removal
**Removal is always fatal.** The mesh is part of the brain's tissue, and taking it out destroys the brain. No surgery, partial procedure or "disabling" exists. The government says so, and in this case the government is telling the truth.

**What the resistance can do instead:**
- **Spoof telemetry:** feed the Registry a false signal. The canon method is a **flatline**, the recorded signature of a real death, pushed through the telemetry relay at the peak of a fact storm, when interface failures are expected. After that the relay keeps reporting zero (Morrison and Nikolai, Book 4, Ch 25). The same method gets the Santos family across the border in Book 5.
- **Forge an identity** on top of a dead citizen's number (Nikolai as "Pieter Lang"). This needs expert work on the interface signature (Elena's technician at the regional node).
- **Shield:** stay inside shielding, or out of broadcast range.
- A spoofed or shielded person **still receives broadcasts** and is still exposed to fact storms and seeding.

## 1.5 People Without Interfaces
- **Citizens of the non-Histologic nations** (SFW, CNT).
- **Refuseniks** from the transition period: fewer than 0.1% of the Histologic population, stateless, unemployable, and living in "grey zones".
- **Medical exemptions:** fewer than 0.01%, heavily monitored.
- **Children** below installation age.
- **Nobody else.** There are no "removed" people.

# Part 2: The Correction System

## 2.1 Purpose (Official and Real)
- **Official:** healing. *"Correction isn't punishment. It's healing."* Doubt is an illness, and staff like Dr. Moss believe this sincerely.
- **Real:** **Principle Four**, *"Do not punish the doubter; heal him. Punishment makes martyrs; correction makes converts… Correction is compassion. Certainty is care."*

## 2.2 Institutions
- **Department of Correction:** runs the facilities.
- **The Correction Registry:** every facility writes to it (session logs, salience maps, compliance scores, discharges, telemetry), and it is kept forever. It has blocks of **dead space** allocated to archived patients, which is where Nikolai hid his partition. The Registry's outbound link runs to a regional node and then to the national archive.
- **Facilities:**
  - **Ashford Correctional Facility** (outskirts of the capital): white, low buildings, hospital-like, with lawns and a fountain. Ordinary wards with common rooms, a library and a garden. **Wing C** is the Continuity wing: a curved atrium of glass cells, soundproofed, each with two transparent walls, a camera dome and a meal and book slot. Behind it is a treatment corridor with rooms C-1 to C-4, plus the technical basement (B-4, the data centre).
  - **The border facility** (neutral zone, Lake Erie): run jointly with Chronos, "the only place both nations agreed". Triangle cells and isolation cells.
  - **Secure care units** (for example Unit 3, two hours north of the capital): concrete cells with no windows and deeper cradles.
  - **Regional facilities** for ordinary cases (where Elena faked her correction).
  - **Long-term care:** where people "who didn't come back" are sent.
- **Shielding:** every facility can isolate its inmates from outside broadcasts. The system engages automatically when the monitors detect a conflict.

## 2.3 How Correction Works
- **The white room:** a white reclining chair and a **neural cradle** (a hood on a jointed arm) that fits over the installation sites behind the ears. There is a console for the engineer, a stool for the physician who holds the patient's hand, and a mirror with the director of treatment behind it.
- **Ritual:** *"State your name. State your crime. Do you accept your guilt? Do you wish to be corrected?"* Continuity adds *"Do you believe?"*
- **Mechanism:** the cradle finds memories by **emotional salience** (heat) and follows their **associative links** outward. A memory is lifted, turned and examined, then **rubbed** until it blurs and its feeling drains away. What is left is information without meaning, "filed". Memories can be altered as well: several versions are overlaid until the original can't be found.
- **The blind spot:** rote, rhythmic and procedural material (songs, multiplication tables, code, prayers, fact-entry formats, checklists) is **skimmed** and ignored. So *"hide what you love in what you've memorised"* (Old Songs):
  - Kira hides things in protocol specs.
  - Marcus hides things in fact-entry formats.
  - Tanaka uses the surgical checklist.
  - Isaiah uses his memory palace (he feeds the machine the "parlour").
  - The triplets use songs and the bond.
- **Doses:** "intensive" (every 2–3 days) or "maintenance" (weekly). Assessment uses a sensor band and flashed words, and reads *reactions* directly.
- **Outcomes:**
  - **Flattened:** "baseline". Calm, empty, stalling in the middle of movements.
  - **Past "the floor"** (Tanaka's word): no one is left to bring back (Samuel).
  - **Compliant and discharged:** Ivan Volkov was "full compliance" after 12 weeks.
  - **Correction failure:** permanent detention (the Volkovs).
  - **Faking:** a disciplined performance of flattening (lags, planned forgetting, *varied* stalls) can fool the staff. Elena and the triplets managed it.
- **Aftermath:** permanent gaps, flinching, compulsive reciting (Kira). A mind with gaps is highly vulnerable to fact storms and confabulation.
- **The standard death notice:** *"DECEASED. COGNITIVE FAILURE… REMAINS WILL BE PROCESSED ACCORDING TO STANDARD PROTOCOLS. NO FAMILY VIEWING PERMITTED."*

## 2.4 Project Continuity (Books 4–5)
- **Location:** Wing C, Ashford, from spring 2105. Director of treatment **Dr. Reeves**. Cradle calibration by **Dr. Morrison**. Sponsored by the **Ministry of Historical Integrity**.
- **Aim:** *"stable, genuine belief, as distinct from compliance… experienced as authentic and self-originated."*
- **Subjects C-1 to C-7:** Believer (Marcus), Architect (Kira), Bond ×3 (the Volkovs), Clinician (Tanaka), Witness (Isaiah). Before them came a calibration subject, **Samuel Ferris** (from March 2105).
- **Protocols:**
  - **R-1:** Reduction to Baseline.
  - **R-2:** Seeding. An attachment figure is placed in front of an emptied subject, and the result is measured (Kira and Marcus).
  - **The triple cradle** for the Bond.
- **Phase Two:** "erase, then seed" delivered through the **national broadcast network**. Carrier signals were already running in three districts by August 2106. It was first used for real on the Ministry district during the **Lost Hour** (Book 5).
- **Status:** officially suspended after Book 4, and in fact only delayed.

## 2.5 Being "Officially Dead"
- In Book 4 the escapees' telemetry was spoofed to flatline and Director Kovač certified their deaths. The Registry records them as *deceased*, and **in Veridica the record is the truth**. They are untracked and legally nonexistent.
- In Book 5 the **Lost Hour Doctrine** is used to correct the Registry on testimony (*STATUS: LIVING*). Old Songs declines.

## 2.6 Writing Checklist
- Correction is **sincere** at every level below the top. It is kind people doing a kind-seeming thing.
- Always say *why* a technique works or fails, in terms of salience or the blind spot.
- Nobody escapes the interface. People escape the Registry.
