## Order of Authority
When sources disagree, the earlier one wins:
1. `basic-idea.txt`: the founding concept.
2. The short stories (`novels/01-03-stories/`).
3. *The Correction* (`novels/04-the-correction/`, especially `chapters-summaries/README.md`).
4. *The Lost Hour* plot (`plots/05-the-lost-hour-full.md`).
5. `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md` and the character files that were updated in Sept 2026.
6. **This folder:** a summary of the sources above. If it disagrees with them, fix this folder.

Plots and drafts for Books 6–9 are **not** binding. They will be rewritten to fit.

## Rules That Are Easy to Get Wrong
- Fact storms are perceptual. The physical world never changes, and the Lake Erie fog is real weather.
- Removing an interface kills. Spoofing telemetry is the only way out of tracking.
- The Judge works in **probabilities** about people (over 60% means corrective custody). There is no appeal, until Book 5's Doctrine.
- Facts **can** be deleted or adjusted, but only through the hidden "true source" (Principle Three). Citizens believe facts are immutable.
- Neutral zones are claimed by both nations. They are not lawless.
- The aesthetic is clean and clinical, as in the short stories. It is not steampunk: no brass, steam or gears.
