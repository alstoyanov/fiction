# The Histologic System: Core Reference

**Status:** CURRENT canon, Sept 2026. It is consistent with the short stories, *The Correction* and *The Lost Hour* (v3.1), and with `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md`. Books 6–9 will be rewritten to fit this file.
**Origin:** the author's founding concept in `basic-idea.txt`, which remains the root of everything here.

---

## 1. The Idea in One Paragraph
**Histologics** is history treated as an exact science. Every event is a **histofact** with a time, a location and the parties involved. Facts are stored in a **factorepo** and checked so that no fact contradicts any other. A **histonation** is a people defined by nothing but the version of history it believes. Its truth is whatever its factorepo holds, together with whatever can be derived from it. **The Judge**, a supercomputer, decides every dispute from those facts. A citizen who doubts is an **outlaw**. Outlaws are corrected until they believe again, or they leave the nation's territory, the **histocircle**. When enough people dispute a fact, a **histobranch** forms and a new histonation splits away. This is the most common cause of war.

That is the story citizens are told. The truth, revealed across the series, is that the system was **designed** to work exactly like this, including its conflicts, weaknesses and failures (section 7).

## 4. The Factorepo

### Structure
- **Primary repository:** politics, economics, wars, treaties, and anything that concerns other nations. It is what The Judge and senior officials consult.
- **Local repositories:** the life of every citizen, including movements, transactions, meetings, births and deaths. They are continuously synchronised with the primary repository and checked against it.
- **Input:** automated sensors and cameras, authorised historians (Marcus's job at the Regional Factorepo), officials, systems architects (Kira's protocol updates), citizens' own statements (subject to verification), and The Judge's derived facts.
- **Verification:** each new fact is checked against all existing facts for direct contradiction, temporal or physical impossibility, logical inconsistency and statistical anomaly. Facts that pass are **entered**. Facts that fail are rejected, and disputes go to The Judge.
- **Physical form:** regional factorepos (office towers of historians at workstations; Marcus worked on the fourteenth floor) and the **Factorepo Spire** in the capital. The Spire is eighty storeys of glass, with the primary nodes, and data visible as light in its core.

### What citizens believe
Facts are **immutable**. Once entered and verified, a fact is permanent. There are no gaps: "Everything is recorded" (Marcus, Story 01).

### What is actually true (Principle Three)
- The factorepo keeps **backups that appear independent and are not**. There is a hidden **"true source"** from which any record can be *restored*. Whoever holds the true source can **delete**, **adjust** or **erase** records without leaving a contradiction.
- **Examples in canon:**
  - Kira's security protocol entry disappears (Story 02).
  - Marcus's contamination index is changed from 0.58 to 0.73 (Book 4).
  - One hour of national history is erased (Book 5).
- Each of these carries the same technical **signature**, the work of one insider (Thomas Wei, Book 5).

## 5. The Judge

### What it does
- **Decides disputes** by evaluating every relevant fact. Its rulings become facts.
- **Derives facts** by deduction and identifies gaps.
- **Computes probabilities** about people. This is how ordinary citizens meet it. It calculates a **contamination probability** from a person's exposure: time spent with outlaws, visits to unmonitored spaces, fact-storm exposure.
  - **Over 60% means corrective custody** (Story 01, Veridica's threshold).
  - Marcus: 73% (in truth 0.58, adjusted). Kira: 92%.
- **Its authority is final.** "The Judge cannot be wrong. The facts are the facts." There is no appeal against a determination.

### What it cannot do
It cannot weigh intentions, loyalty or love. *"Your belief isn't measurable, Mr. Chen. Your exposure is."* It cannot resolve contradictions *between* nations. It cannot see what is not in the record.

### What changes in Book 5
- **The Lost Hour Doctrine** (Jan 2107): where the record is absent, facts may be established by the concordant testimony of several independent witnesses, weighted for known contamination. This is the first time human testimony can create a fact.
- A **Historical Integrity Commission** is created, with Marcus and Kira as members and Thomas Wei as technical director.

### Designed weaknesses (for later books)
The Judge's architecture includes **deliberate weaknesses** (the manuscript's Annex) and a hidden **self-termination contingency** (Principle Seven, written by Vance), revealed in Book 8.

## 6. The Broadcast Network
- Every citizen carries a **neural interface** (see `neural-interfaces-and-correction.md`). It **receives** a constant low-level stream of facts about the citizen's surroundings: *"This structure built 2067, originally a factory."* Citizens simply *know* these things, as if they were memories.
- The interface also **reports telemetry** (location, vital signs, cognitive status) to the national **Correction Registry**.
- **Towers:** each covers roughly 10–50 km. **Local nodes** serve districts. Coverage overlaps within a nation, so there are no dead spots.
- **At borders,** Veridican and Chronos towers overlap. Officially this is a technical limitation. In truth it is **deliberate** (Principle Five), and it is the root of fact storms.
- **Phase Two (Books 4–5):** the network was designed from the start to carry more than facts. **"Erase, then seed"** is belief delivered at population scale. It was first used on the capital's Ministry district during the Lost Hour.

## 7. The Hidden Truth: A Designed System
Citizens believe the system evolved to meet a crisis. In fact it was **designed from 2064 to 2066** by the authors of *The Foundation of Certainty*, whose twelve signatories and their heirs are **the Architects**. Their principles explain the world:

| Principle | What it explains |
|-----------|------------------|
| 1. Singular Truth | The factorepo and The Judge. Doubt treated as illness. |
| 2. Controlled Opposition | Several Histologic nations, and non-Histologic "enemies". |
| 3. Backup Control | Facts that can be deleted and restored. |
| 4. Correction Over Punishment | The correction system, and Continuity. |
| 5. Controlled Conflict | Border overlaps and fact storms as calibration grounds. |
| 6. The Restorer's Authority | The Lost Hour, the Doctrine and the Commission. |
| 7. Contingency (secret) | The Judge's built-in death. |

Full plan and reveal schedule: `plots/00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md`. **Nobody in Books 1–5 knows this whole picture.** Characters discover it fragment by fragment.

## 8. Language
- **The common language** is the official language, designed to translate directly into machine logic so that facts can be stated without ambiguity. Everyone learns it from childhood, and the novels' prose renders it.
- **Older languages** survive only in fragments: names, place names, old songs and grandmothers' sayings. This is why Old Songs' pre-Histologic songs sound strange, "in a mode Marcus had never heard".

- **Correction Registry**: telemetry and correction records, and the "dead space" where Nikolai hid his partition.
- **Ministry of Historical Integrity**: Minister Chen Zhao and Deputy Minister Dr. Amara Singh. It sponsored Continuity.
- **The Cabinet**, which approved Phase Two "at Ministry level".
- **Historical Integrity Commission** (from 2107).

## 10. Story Tensions
Truth vs. freedom. Record vs. memory. Certainty vs. doubt. A system that can't be wrong, and so can't be fixed. The designed victory (reform absorbed by the design). One record vs. many witnesses.
