# Society in the Histologic World

**Status:** CURRENT canon, Sept 2026. It is consistent with Books 1–5. It replaces `society-structure-full.md` and `society-structure-concise.md`.
**See also:** `core-system.md` (institutions), `neural-interfaces-and-correction.md` (interfaces, the Registry, correction) and `nations.md`.

---

## 1. Identity
Language, ethnicity and religion have been displaced by one question: **which history do you believe?** Within a nation, unity is absolute: citizens "do not question the truthfulness of their history" (`basic-idea.txt`). Between nations, disagreement can't be settled, because each side has its own "truth", backed by its own Judge.

## 2. How Histonations Form (the Official Account)
- **Founding:** Veridica, 2066, the first nation.
- **Histobranching:** when a significant share of a nation disputes a fact, the dissenters organise, a factorepo is branched with the disputed facts changed, and territory is claimed. Parent nations resist, so histobranches cause **the most common wars**.
- **Conquest:** the defeated nation gives up its factorepo and adopts the conqueror's. Those who refuse become outlaws. A classic strategy is to **split an enemy first with false facts**, so that it histobranches, then conquer the pieces (`basic-idea.txt`).
- **Resource wars:** less common and very costly. Rarely does anyone clearly win.
- **The hidden truth:** the major splits were engineered (Principle Two; see `nations.md`).

## 3. Territory and Borders
- **Histocircle:** a nation's territory, defined by coordinates in its factorepo.
- **Agreed borders:** rare. Both factorepos hold the same boundary facts.
- **Disputed borders:** the most common. Each factorepo claims the ground, and both nations' broadcasts overlap there. This is where fact storms occur.
- **Neutral zones** are *not* lawless empty land. They are strips that are **officially neutral and in practice claimed by both** nations. Both broadcasts reach them, and they hold shared institutions: the border **hospital** and the **border correctional facility** on the Veridica–Chronos line (Story 03). Resistance safe houses use their shifting jurisdictions.
- **Unaligned territories:** failed or remote regions outside every system, where refugees and smugglers live. These are the only truly ungoverned places.

## 5. Daily Life
- **Birth:** recorded to the second. The child is given a citizen number, an identity that lasts for life and appears in every record.
- **Enlightenment day (about age 8):** the neural interface is installed. It is a family celebration with a cake iced with the national seal. The first morning afterwards the child simply *knows* things, "like a door opening" (Book 4 Prologue).
- **Education:** history as memorised fact, and science as derivation from fact. Pre-Histologic culture survives in lullabies and old tunes that grandmothers aren't supposed to know.
- **Adulthood:** every meeting, purchase and movement is recorded. Cameras are everywhere. **Unmonitored spaces** (Elena's apartment, Adrian's warehouse) are illegal, and time spent in them counts as contamination.
- **Relationships:** recorded too. Love is not a fact: *"Love is not measurable, Ms. Osman."*
- **Death:** recorded with its cause. *"Remains processed according to standard protocols. No family viewing"* is common for deaths in correction.
- **What citizens feel:** for believers the surveillance is **comforting** ("nothing could be hidden"). Doubt feels like illness, and asking questions feels dangerous.

## 6. Becoming an Outlaw
- **Triggers:** questioning a fact, noticing a contradiction, contact with outlaws, fact-storm exposure (border residents are "always one fact storm away from doubt"), or simply **exposure**. Loyalty is irrelevant: *"Your belief isn't measurable. Your exposure is."*
- **Detection:** surveillance, and The Judge's **contamination probability**. **Above 60%** means corrective custody (Veridica).
- **The paths:**
  1. **Corrective custody.** This is the usual route and it is involuntary (Marcus, Kira, Tanaka, Isaiah, the Volkovs).
  2. **Leaving the histocircle.** The `basic-idea.txt` choice of correction or exile survives as a legal option for citizens who **openly declare** doubt and renounce citizenship before the Judge acts. In practice the paths are **flight** across the border, usually helped by smugglers like "the Phantom" (Alexei), or **expulsion by order** (Isaiah, Book 5). Those who leave become stateless outside their nation, and they **keep their interface**.
  3. **Detention for life** for "correction failures" (the Volkovs at the border facility).
- **After correction:** discharge as "compliant", sometimes flattened (Ivan Volkov, 2089–90).

## 7. Outlaw and Resistance Life
- **Resistance:** a network across the border zone, led by **Commander Elena Volkov**. It has safe houses in contested buildings, smugglers, deep-cover operatives inside ministries, forged identities (Nikolai becomes "Pieter Lang"), and in Book 4 a stolen fact-storm unit.
- **Being "officially dead"** (from Book 4): escapees recorded as *deceased* in the Registry aren't tracked. Their spoofed telemetry reads zero. In Veridica the record *is* the truth, so a dead person can walk through the interior under a new name. In Book 5 the Lost Hour Doctrine is used to bring them back to legal life.
- **Refugees:** some reach the SFW, which uses them as propaganda.
- **Culture:** songs, oral memory, and (from Book 5) **written journals** kept by many hands, which later grow into Book 6's distributed-truth networks.

## 8. International Relations
- There is no shared basis for negotiation: each nation's grievances are "facts".
- **International Judges** see only primary repositories, and their rulings are rarely accepted.
- **Synthesis** is the diplomatic hub, and its Institute runs archive exchanges.
- **Trade** happens only where both factorepos hold compatible facts, which usually means simple exchanges. Black markets operate in unaligned territory.

## 9. Story Tensions
Authority vs. individual. Stability vs. freedom. Can forced belief be real (Continuity)? Record vs. memory (the Lost Hour). One record vs. many witnesses.
