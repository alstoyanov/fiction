## At a Glance
- **Full name:** Maya Reeves.
- **Age:** **24** in Nov 2106 (born about 2082). 26 in Book 6 (2108), 28 in Book 8 (2110), about 33 in Book 9 (~2115).
- **Occupation:** junior technician on the monitoring floor of the **Regional Factorepo**, the most junior person there. From Nov 15, 2106, on **Thomas Wei's** investigation team. From Jan 2107, the **Historical Integrity Commission's junior technician** (Ch 28), under Wei as Technical Director.
- **Family:** the estranged daughter of **Dr. Reeves**, head of Project Continuity (54 in 2106; his first name is never given in the drafted Book 4, so leave it unstated). Her parents divorced when she was ten; she took her mother's side. Her mother is unnamed. She and her father had not spoken for **four years** before Nov 2106.
- **Status after Book 5:** Commission technician; with Nikolai Volkov (an obvious couple "pretending not to be" in Ch 28, holding hands openly by the Epilogue). Visits her father in prison once a month.
- **Appearance:** not described beyond her hair: her father's first words to her in four years are "You've cut your hair" (Ch 21). Keep it that spare.

## Who She Is
Sharp, prickly, overworked and funny. The only person on the monitoring floor who doesn't freeze at 15:01. She spent August to November 2106 ashamed of her own surname, which the Continuity scandal has made famous "in the worst way", and has told no one whose daughter she is. She is brave and wrong for the right reasons: she finds her father's code in the carrier signal and believes he killed a Deputy Minister. When she learns he didn't, the only way to free him is the one thing that would destroy everything her new friends have won. She chooses to wait, and it costs her.

## Voice Rules
1. **She timestamps everything.** It is a technician's habit and it becomes the key to Twist 3: she knows Wei *arrived* at 15:32 "because I timed it." Give her times, cache offsets and log IDs in ordinary speech.
2. **Prickly, quick, dry.** She argues about cache timestamps and enjoys it. Her humour is defensive and fast.
3. **She notices how people type.** "Correction contractors type like that" (to "Pieter", Ch 9). She reads people the way she reads logs.
4. **Her surname is a bruise.** She flinches at "Continuity" (Ch 1; Wei notices). She doesn't say "my father" until Ch 14, and then only to Pieter.
5. **Anger and grief arrive together.** She cries and is furious in the same sentence (the stairwell, Ch 14). Never write her as only sad.
6. **Physical reactions are bodily, not dramatic.** The hole in the record makes her nauseous, "as if a stair were missing" (Ch 1).

### Books 6–9 (provisional; to be rewritten)
- **Nikolai:** the romance continues through the later books. **Provisional: she marries Nikolai** (the old files had this by their "Novel 08", which would be Book 9 here; any book from 7 on works).
- **Wei:** she works for the man who framed her father, and she knows it. That makes her the heroes' closest witness to Wei, and the one most at risk. Suggested: Wei treats her with real kindness, which is worse.
- **Her father:** the question she carries is whether, and when, to free him. Reeves is guilty of Continuity and innocent of Singh. Any book that exposes seeding (the Doctrine's weakness) reopens his conviction, and she will have to choose again.
- **Principle Four (Book 8):** the Continuity survivors read the principle their torture was built from. Her father knew it (Book 4 README). She should be in the room.
- **Technical role:** her skills are logs, buffers and node forensics. In Book 6 she is a natural builder of the verification side of the distributed truth networks.
- **Old line, usable (Book 5 Ch 1 or 14):** "Then who stole it?" The old "The logs show it was deliberate" needs adjusting: the hour is *absent*, not corrupted.

## Continuity Traps
- She is **Dr. Reeves's daughter**, and on **Wei's team**. She is not "recruited by the resistance".
- **15:32** is Wei's arrival time at the Regional Factorepo (appointed 15:20, arrived twelve minutes later). The number must match in Ch 1 and Ch 28.
- **14:18** is the carrier injection, three minutes before the man at 14:21.
- She learns who Pieter is in **Ch 20**, not earlier. She learns her father is innocent of the murder only gradually (Ch 14 reply, Ch 21 "I've never signed anything in my life", Ch 28).
- The **code comment was planted by Wei** in her father's style. The characters don't know this until Ch 28.
- **Dr. Reeves's first name is unstated** in the drafted Book 4. Don't invent it casually.
- **The surname line in the Ch 14 summary is ambiguous:** "she took her mother's side and her father's surname out of spite." Decide in drafting what it means (most likely: she kept her father's surname to spite her mother, or took it back later to spite him), and make the page say it plainly.
- **Name clashes to watch:** the Architect "Director Maya Tanaka" (MT·06) must be renamed (SERIES-OVERVIEW §4). The old Book 6 plot also has a border resident **"Anna Reeves"** with a daughter "Lena"; that surname clashes with Maya's family and "Anna" is close to Anya (Old Songs' wife). Rename or drop them when Book 6 is rewritten.
- She was never at Ashford. Her knowledge of Continuity is Isaiah's article, her father's code, and the annex.
