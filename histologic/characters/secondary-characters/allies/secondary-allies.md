## 1. Dr. James Morrison: The Hands

### At a Glance
- **Full name:** Dr. James Morrison. Tanaka calls him "James" (Book 5, Ch 6).
- **Age:** "late thirties, perhaps" in Oct 2105 (Book 4, Ch 2).
- **Occupation:** Project Continuity's neural-interface engineer at Ashford. He runs the cradles, and he designed their gain calibration.
- **Status after Book 4:** officially **deceased** ("a member of the Continuity technical staff", Ch 27). Living in Chronos, working beside Tanaka.
- **Status after Book 5:** still in Chronos, and still **officially dead** in Veridica (settled, Sept 2026). *STATUS: LIVING* (Ch 26) covers the Continuity subjects who testified. Morrison was staff, not a subject, and legal life would bring charges for his Continuity work. Like Old Songs, he lets the record keep him dead. He lives on Chronos papers.
- **Appearance:** sandy hair that needs cutting, pale eyes that stay on the console. A tremor in the fingers of his **right** hand whenever the probe engages.

### Who He Is
A good technician who built something terrible because he wanted the cradles to "work properly, because a cradle that worked properly hurt people less" (Ch 12, Nikolai's view). He never looks at the patients. Tanaka reads his hands and turns him (Ch 17). He fakes a calibration fault to protect Marcus (Ch 18), spoofs the telemetry at the fence (Ch 25), and leaves for Chronos: *"I can't stay in Veridica. I built half of it."* (Ch 29).

### Canon Appearances
- **Book 4:** Ch 2–3 (at the console), 4 (Kira's first session), 7 (Tanaka notices the tremor), 8 (turns over the tablet Isaiah saw), 12 (sends Nikolai to B-4: *"He's better than our contractor."*), 17 (turned), 18 (the fault), 19 (the night meeting, the sleep-study cover), 20 (the shielding, and Marcus as the most vulnerable mind), 21 (at the single-node session; relays the message to Elena), 22, 23 (releases the lockdown), 24–26 (escape), 27 (on the Registry list), 28 (awake at night, looking at his hands), 29 (goes with Tanaka; his grip is steady), Epilogue (Chronos, hands steady at the keyboard).
- **Book 5:** Ch 6 (the Santos crossing, spoofing at 14:20; he picks up a carrier at 14:21), 11 (*"It's my calibration… Someone put it on a broadcast node."*), 22 (a stabilisation cradle can seed "if someone brings the right calibration").

### Voice
Low, rapid and technical, eyes on the floor. He apologises too much. He comes alive only when a problem interests him (Ch 20: "a technician's interest in a problem").

### Key Lines
- *"Sorry. Calibration fault. The left temporal pad, I think. It's been drifting… Abort for subject safety."* (Book 4, Ch 18)
- *"Mr. Chen's salience map looks like a city after a bombing."* (Ch 20)
- *"Somebody needs to be in Chronos who knows exactly what it looks like when it starts."* (Ch 29)

### Continuity Traps
- **His hands shake until the escape, then stop.** They still shake at the fence (Ch 25). From Ch 29 on they are steady, and Book 5 keeps them steady. If they shake again, it must mean something.
- He **spoofs** telemetry and never removes an interface. Removal is fatal.
- He has **no brother** in canon. "Colonel James Morrison" (JM·08) is an unrelated Architect, rename pending.
- He escaped with the group, but he is **not** one of the seven Continuity subjects. The Doctrine does not revive him, and he doesn't ask it to.

### Provisional (Books 6–9)
- He is the one person outside Veridica who can recognise Continuity hardware and seeding on sight. Use him for the Chronos "salience therapy" proposals (Book 5 Epilogue) and for the "erasure in a different nation".
- The old file had him helping destroy the Judge. Under Principle Seven he could, at most, explain what the Judge's interface broadcasts will do when it dies (Book 8).

## 2. Adrian Kovač: The Recruiter Who Meant It

### At a Glance
- **Full name:** Adrian Kovač. **Age:** "perhaps thirty" in Nov 2105 (Story 02).
- **Occupation:** resistance recruiter. He calls himself a "freelance consultant. Data analysis, system optimization."
- **Family:** son of Helena Kovač's younger brother (Book 4, Ch 18). Great-nephew of Cardinal Tobias Kovač (TK·07). Julian Kovač, the Book 6 historian, is Helena's other nephew.
- **Appearance:** dark hair, startlingly blue eyes, a slight scar above his left eyebrow, strong features. He is fast and strong.
- **Status after Book 5:** at large. He is never seen on the page after Story 02.

### Who He Is
Sent to recruit Kira, he followed her for weeks and used the falling stone on Historian's Lane to make contact. Then he fell in love with her. He did **not** steal her fact: Reeves had it deleted (Book 4, Ch 18), and the acquisition file used Adrian as the "person of interest" who made the contamination finding believable. At the end of Story 02 he starts making calls to find out who took it.

### Canon Appearances
- **Story 02:** the whole story.
- **Book 4 (offstage):** Ch 4 and 10 (Kira hides him inside protocol 7.3), Ch 18 (the acquisition file, and Kovač: "I haven't spoken to him in six years"), Ch 19 (*"He lied to me. And he loved me. Both."*). In Ch 19 an unnamed technician at the regional Registry node, "very good, and very angry", has been trying to get into Ashford since December. The text implies it is Adrian and never confirms it.
- **Book 5:** not mentioned.

### Voice
Easy, amused, a little theatrical: *"They should add that to the factorepo: 'Historic architecture, also a death trap.'"* When it matters he is blunt: *"I'm an outlaw, Kira. I was sent to recruit you."*

### Key Lines
- *"I'm someone who fell in love with you when I wasn't supposed to."* (Story 02)
- Kovač: *"He was a sweet boy. He used to take apart my kitchen clock."* (Book 4, Ch 19)

### Continuity Traps
- He is **not** the Book 6 historian (that is Julian), and Elena does not seduce him.
- He manipulated Kira *and* loved her. Keep both true.
- He is not the thief of the fact.
- He and his aunt have not spoken since about 2099–2100.

### Provisional (Books 6–9)
- Kira has not seen him since Dec 2105, and she is now with Marcus. A reunion is owed, and it should hurt without being a love triangle.
- Possible roles: confirming that he is the Registry-node technician, and serving as the bridge to his cousin or brother Julian in Book 6.

## 3. Supervisor Hayes: The Jobholder

### At a Glance
- **Role:** intake and Wing C supervisor at Ashford, with **eleven years of service** (Book 4, Ch 7).
- **Sex:** **female** (the Book 4 text uses "she" throughout).
- **Appearance:** tall, somewhere in her forties, with reddish hair going gray and "the permanently tired look of someone who had worked night shifts for too many years" (Prologue).
- **Life:** a daughter at school and a mortgage (Ch 7).

### Who She Is
Sympathetic, but she won't answer questions. She is polite, tired and complicit. She looks at a point "somewhere to the left" of your face. Isaiah files her among "the jobholders" (Ch 8). Tanaka: *"Hayes obeys."* Six months before Tanaka's arrest, Hayes **brought her coffee** in the staff room and asked her advice about her daughter's school. Now she signs Tanaka's meal chart and doesn't meet her eyes (Ch 7).

### Canon Appearances
Book 4: Prologue (receives Marcus), Ch 1 (*"Samuel's doing very well. He's one of our best."*), Ch 2–3, Ch 4 (walks Kira in, and reads something twice at the junction), Ch 10 (the library card), Ch 18, Ch 20 (leads Nikolai to the single-node session and looks sick), Ch 23 (sits on the floor in the storm weeping, full of longing for a different life: "a hospital, a ward, a nurse's uniform"), Epilogue ("Hayes perhaps" cuts Samuel's hair). **Books 1–3 and 5:** absent.

### Key Lines
- *"Welcome to Ashford."* (She hears how that sounds.) (Prologue)
- *"Help you… They're going to help you."* (Ch 1)
- *"Keep walking, please."* (Prologue, Ch 4)

### Continuity Traps
- **She is a woman.** The Book 4 cast table doesn't give her sex, so check before drafting.
- She never helps the escape and never betrays it. Her kindness is passive.
- She has no first name on the page.

### Provisional
She is a natural witness at Kovač's inquiry, or under the Doctrine. She could also be the "ordinary person" who comes forward in Book 6.

## 4. Lucian Bell: The Aide Who Can't Recall

### At a Glance
- **Role:** aide to Deputy Minister Amara Singh, twelfth floor, Ministry of Historical Integrity (Book 5).
- **Status after Book 5:** unchanged. His allegiance is **unknown**.

### Canon Appearances (Book 5)
- **Prologue:** *"Your 14:30 has been confirmed."* Singh frowns. Did she confirm it, or did he?
- **Ch 2:** heard screaming on the twelfth floor at 15:05. He finds the body.
- **Ch 3:** his desk is **empty** at 14:55, and Singh's door is locked.
- **Ch 15–16:** he "can't recall" making the calendar entry that proposed the 14:30 meeting with Marcus.
- **Ch 17:** Sokolov's note says that at 09:00, "her aide" told her Singh had never requested the 14:30 border slot.
- **Ch 22:** the identification of the body rests on the testimony of Singh's staff.

### Notes
He is listed as an ally because he serves the "victim". In fact he is the book's most innocent-looking loose end.

### Continuity Traps
- He was inside the seeded district. **His memory of finding the body is not clean evidence**, and nobody should treat it as such.
- Don't resolve whether he was used, seeded or complicit. That belongs to Singh's return (Book 9).
- "Lucian" is a near-clash with **Lucia** Santos. See the list in `../minor-cast.md`.
