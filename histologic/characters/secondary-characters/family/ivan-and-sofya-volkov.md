## At a Glance
- **Ivan Sergeyevich Volkov.** Systems engineer, **Border Broadcast Division** (his correction record, Book 4 Ch 15). Corrected for twelve weeks c. 2089. Died about a year later (c. 2090) in a manufactured fact storm "on a street in the border zone". He is **Test Subject 2471**.
- **Sofya Volkov.** Ivan's wife, mother of Dmitri, Alexei and Nikolai. Died in **Nov 2104**, in a fact storm, in the cognitive trauma ward of the neutral hospital in the border zone (Story 03). Gray hair; her mind was worn down by decades of storm exposure.
- **Elena Sergeyevna Volkov** is Ivan's **younger sister**, and the triplets' "Aunt Lena".
- **Home:** a flat on the lakeshore in the border zone, with a flat tarred roof. Ivan's study had a green lamp and smelled of solder.
- **Ages and birth years:** not given. Keep them unstated.

## Who They Were
An ordinary border-zone couple who believed the interface promises of the 2050s ("My parents believed them. Got their interfaces early," Alexei, Story 03). Ivan worked on the broadcast system and noticed that the border overlap was not an accident. For that he was corrected, sent home empty, and then used to measure how much a mind can bear. Sofya kept the household together through it and never told her sons the word *correction*. Both died of the storms. Between them they gave the boys the three things that saved them: Ivan's *question things*, Sofya's *save each other*, and the promise that they would stay together.

### The Discrepancy (c. 2089–90)
What Ivan questioned was the **border broadcast overlap**. According to the Foundation Manuscript, the overlap was planned in the Implementation Annex and justified by **Principle Five** (controlled conflict; the border as "calibration grounds", mark JM·08). Elena described the same event to Marcus in Story 01, without names: *"He questioned a minor discrepancy in the factorepo. Nothing major, just a small contradiction he noticed… When he came back, he wasn't my brother anymore. He was empty."* She was describing Ivan.

### His Death, as Later Explained
Reeves in Book 4 Ch 24: "Your father. 2471. I'm sorry about him, truly. He was very early data. We didn't understand, then, how much we were measuring." The unit that made the storm was a vehicle-mounted transmitter of the kind stolen for the Ashford escape (Ch 25). He was corrected until he was compliant, and then, once he "was quiet, and no longer asked questions", used.

### The Correction: Rule for Writing It
**The boys never knew he had been corrected** until Nikolai found the record in April 2106 (Book 4 Ch 15). Alexei knew about 2471 in Story 03; the correction was news to all three. Nikolai's realisation: "They are living their father's fate, and resisting it where he couldn't." Elena, on the road (Ch 25): "Your father didn't change. They *changed* him. I should have told you."

## Sofya Volkov

### Canon
- **The house.** She sets the table and says "Wash your hands" automatically (Story 03). She forbade sitting on the parapet. She hummed the pre-Histologic **Lakeshore Air** over the washing-up, with the fog against the windows. It is the tune Old Songs hums at Ashford, and it is how Alexei recognises that songs survive correction (Book 4 Ch 6).
- **She taught the promise sign**, forefinger hooked over middle finger, "before the interfaces" (Book 4 Ch 5). She wept when the interfaces were installed.
- **She kept the correction from the boys.** "Mama had told them it was the work." After Elena's last visit, Sofya "would not say her name" (Ch 15). *Whether Sofya knew the "work course" was a correction is left open.* Recommendation: she knew, and chose silence to keep the boys from going looking, the same reason Elena gives.
- **Her decline.** She stayed in the border zone after Ivan died. Nikolai stayed with her ("Mama needs me"). By Nov 2104 she is in the cognitive trauma ward on the third floor of the neutral hospital, with "cognitive coherence below thirty percent". She sometimes remembers Alexei leaving, sometimes dying, sometimes never existing.
- **Her death (Story 03).** With all three sons in the room for the first time in years, she is lucid: "My beautiful boys. My triplets. I knew you'd come together." The storm siren sounds, the building locks down, and she seizes. "Ivan, I see you. I see—" Then: **"Save each other. Promise me. Save each other."**
- **The notice** (delivered to the brothers in their triangle cells): *"SOFYA VOLKOV. DECEASED. COGNITIVE FAILURE SECONDARY TO PROLONGED FACT STORM EXPOSURE. REMAINS WILL BE PROCESSED ACCORDING TO STANDARD PROTOCOLS. NO FAMILY VIEWING PERMITTED."* The Ashford escapees' official deaths deliberately echo it (Book 4 README decision #8; Isaiah's article, Ch 27).
- **She was the birth-order authority.** "Mama said I came out first." Dmitri, by thirty seconds.

### What Her Death Was
Alexei in Story 03: "Collateral damage. Acceptable losses. That's what the reports call people like her." Reeves in Book 4: "Every storm on that border for fifteen years has been one of ours." By implication, the storm that killed her was a trial too. Dmitri's unspoken question to Sokolov: *"Was the data worth my mother?"*

## Continuity Traps
- **Ivan's job.** Story 03 says both "systems engineer during the transition… helped build the broadcast towers" and "worked for the factorepo maintenance division". The record says Border Broadcast Division. Use the record. The boys' version was the family's understanding.
- **Timing.** Corrected the spring the boys turned fourteen; died about a year later, when they were fifteen. Book 4 Ch 15's *summary* says "two years before his death", but the drafted chapter and Ch 27 say a year. Use a year.
- **"Fifteen years."** Book 4 dates his death to "fifteen years ago" (c. 2090). Story 03 says both that and that the rooftop (age 12) was fifteen years earlier and he died three years after it. Don't print the arithmetic.
- **The mother's name is Sofya.** An old draft of Story 03's notice said "ELENA VOLKOV"; that is fixed.
- **Elena is younger than Ivan.** She is 42 in 2106.
