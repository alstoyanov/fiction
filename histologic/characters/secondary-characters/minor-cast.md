# Minor Cast: Walk-Ons and the Named Dead

**Status:** CURRENT, Sept 2026. Rewritten to match Stories 01–03, *The Correction* (drafted) and *The Lost Hour* (summaries). Later books will be rewritten to fit this file.
**Related:** `allies/secondary-allies.md`, `opposition/secondary-antagonists.md`, `../main-characters/protagonists/old-songs-character.md` ("The Names"), `../main-characters/protagonists/volkov-triplets.md` (the Volkov family).

Each entry gives the character's role, the chapters they appear in, and anything a writer must not get wrong. "B4" means *The Correction* and "B5" means *The Lost Hour*.

---

## Stories 01–03
- **Priya.** Kira's colleague at the Spire (Story 02). She reviewed Kira's protocol 7.3 designs, ran the same searches and got the same empty result: *"Kira, this doesn't make sense. I saw you working on this."* She warns Kira about Adrian ("Just be careful"). She was renamed from "Yuki" to avoid clashing with Tanaka. She is a living witness to the stolen fact (possible Book 6 use).
- **Kira's supervisor.** Unnamed and suspicious. See the opposition file.
- **The Story 03 border-facility director.** Unnamed and **male** ("the director returned to his work"). He recommends permanent detention for the triplets. He is not Kovač, whose facility is Ashford.
- **Marcus's parents.** Unnamed and dead before Story 01. His father was an enforcer and his mother taught history (Story 01). His mother sang him "Hush Now, the River Is Long" (B4 Ch 11, 28).

## Book 4: *The Correction*
- **Samuel Ferris.** Cell **7-G**, next to Marcus. About 25, with curly brown hair and a thin, animated face. A student who "had written an essay". *CONTINUITY PRE-SERIES (CALIBRATION)*, admitted March 2105 (Kovač's folder, Ch 18). He waves and presses his palm to the glass on Marcus's first night, and comes back blank four hours later (Ch 1). Marcus counts to five and leaves him (Ch 23–24). He is still at Ashford in the Epilogue: Kovač sits outside his cell, and "Hayes perhaps" cuts his hair. **He is alive**, but he is still sung for in "The Names" (Ch 26).
- **Hanna Marsh.** Cell **7-R**, Wing C overflow, admitted April 2106. Small, in her forties, with cropped gray hair and "a nurse's forearms". *"Take me. Please… I won't make a sound."* (Ch 24.) She dies in the van crash (Ch 26). Ch 24–27.
- **Pavel Ionescu.** Cell **7-N**, admitted May 2106. Twenty, with a narrow, clever face and his gray top on inside out. He never speaks. He dies in the van crash (Ch 24–26).
- **Teo.** Elena's driver. Twenty-three, with a shaved head, and in the resistance for a year. He drives the improvised laundry van, doesn't know the lakeshore road, and follows the storm's four false lanes off the embankment (Ch 25–26). *Trap:* he was never an Ashford inmate, so he is **not** in the Registry list of flatlines (Ch 27). Hanna and Pavel are, recorded as "cognitive failure during fact storm", which is the **wrong cause**. They really died in the crash.
- **The old man at the fence.** Unnamed. About seventy, bald and stooped, in general-ward gray. He follows the group down the tunnel and won't go through the gate: *"It's not there… I don't know what's out there."* Dmitri leaves him (Ch 25). Old Songs sings for him (Ch 26).
- **Brandt.** Offstage. The contractor who built the Wing C systems and wired the cradles into the Registry. Nobody revoked his certificate. Nikolai uses it to open CONTINUITY on Feb 24 at 21:40 (Ch 12), and Reeves finds that in the audit table (Ch 20–21). Also mentioned in Ch 15.
- **Ines.** Ashford orderly: "the older woman with the braid". She talks about her grandchildren and her grandson's school play (Ch 2, 9, 10). Her partner orderly, a broad young man with a shaved head and very clean fingernails, is unnamed.
- **The loading-bay enforcer.** Unnamed. Young, with a shaved head and a tired face. *"Ashford… Wing C? What did you do?"* (Prologue.)
- **Wing C "furniture".** A woman of about sixty sitting on the floor like a doll, and a man with his palms on the glass (Ch 1). The patient in 7-M with his arms outstretched, and a staff member of about thirty who "reports he has never worked here" (Ch 23). All unnamed.
- **"Aunt Sarah."** Isaiah's aunt in his memory palace, "sharp and elegant, already talking about the diplomatic service" (Ch 8). She is the Architect Sarah Okonkwo. See the Architects file.

## Book 5: *The Lost Hour*
- **Marina Holt.** A resistance operative, four years in deep cover as a Ministry archivist. She walks out at 14:25 with a box of files "as if she's going to lunch" and finds the diary page listing four meetings (Ch 7). She testifies on the Justice Center steps (Ch 19) and at the hearing (Ch 25). Her departure is recorded as a resignation (Ch 26).
- **Jakob Lind.** An enforcement dispatcher in deep cover. He is three minutes late at the extraction (Ch 7). His appearances match Marina's (Ch 19, 25, 26). *Trap:* Lind is a near-clash with **Lang** (Pieter Lang, Nikolai's alias).
- **The Santos family.** They cross into Chronos at about 14:20 through a gap in the fence. Morrison spoofs their telemetry, and the "medical crossing waiver" came from Singh's office (Ch 6, 16). They are **not seeded**, because the crossing was outside the Ministry district.
  - **Davi Santos:** a Ministry records clerk.
  - **Lucia Santos:** his wife. There is no detail on the page yet.
  - **Lia Santos (16):** furious to be leaving her friends. *"Great."* She testifies at Tanaka's trafficking hearing and gets the charges suspended (Ch 22). She is fierce again by link at the hearing (Ch 25). The family's departure is recorded as voluntary (Ch 26).
  - **Name note:** Lucia and Lia are a near-clash inside one household, and Lucia also sits next to Lucian Bell. **Suggestion (not applied):** rename the mother **Rosa Santos**, since Lia has the lines and should keep her name.
- **The SFW envoy** (Ch 5), **the Chronos hospital director** who protects Tanaka (Ch 6), and **Elena's source** (Ch 7, 16). All unnamed. Keep them so for now.

## The Dead and the Remembered
- **Test Subject 2471.** **Ivan Volkov**, the triplets' father. *"Cause of death: cerebral hemorrhage induced by controlled fact storm exposure. Result: successful demonstration of lethal threshold."* (Story 03.) Isaiah has the number (B4 Ch 8: *2471 — VOLKOV, I.*). It appears again in Ch 15 and 27, and Reeves: *"He was very early data."* (Ch 24.) See `../main-characters/protagonists/volkov-triplets.md` for Ivan and **Sofya Volkov**.
- **Anselm Kerr.** Old Songs' mentor at the Historical Archive in 2062–67. He is corrected in 2067 and "never comes back as himself". He is one of the five struck-through marks in the Register (Book 9). B5 Ch 12, 30. See the Old Songs file.
- **Anya and Katya Petrov.** Old Songs' wife and daughter (Katya born 2071). Both die in an early border storm in 2074, which was manufactured (Book 7). **So far they exist only in the Old Songs file.** They are not named in the Book 4 chapters or the Book 5 summaries.
