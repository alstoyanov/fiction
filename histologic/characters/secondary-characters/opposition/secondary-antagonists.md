# Secondary Opposition: The System's Human Faces

**Status:** CURRENT, Sept 2026. Rewritten to match Stories 01–03, *The Correction* (drafted) and *The Lost Hour* (summaries). Later books will be rewritten to fit this file.
**Related:** `../allies/secondary-allies.md`, `../minor-cast.md`, `../../main-characters/antagonists/thomas-wei.md` (Wei has his own file), `../../main-characters/antagonists/the-architects.md`, Book 4's `chapters-summaries/README.md` and Book 5's `chapters-summaries/README.md`.

Almost nobody here is cruel. As Tanaka puts it in Book 4, Ch 7: *"almost no one in Ashford was cruel. That was the most frightening discovery of all."* Write the opposition as sincere, procedural and tired, not as villains.

## 1. Dr. Reyna Moss: The Gentle Face

### At a Glance
- **Role:** assessment and correction physician. She does intake at the Enforcement Center (holding room 4, the "city intake wards") and at Ashford, and she holds the patient's hand in Wing C sessions.
- **Age and appearance:** "perhaps fifty", with gray hair cut short and practical, and a face "built for reassurance" (Book 4, Prologue). Her hands are warm and dry.
- **Status after Book 4:** at Ashford. She sets off the alarm during the escape. Her fate is not stated, and she is absent from Book 5.
- **Birthday:** in March. Tanaka still remembers it (Ch 17).

### Who She Is
A sincere believer. She meant every moment of holding Marcus's hand while the machine took him (Ch 2). She shared an office with Tanaka for six years. When Tanaka appeals to her doctor to doctor, Reyna answers with tears and pity, *"But you're ill, Yuki"*, and doesn't report her, "because you do not report the sick" (Ch 17). In the tunnel mouth, in a dressing gown and slippers, she presses the alarm to save them: *"I'm so sorry. I love you. I can't let you go out there and die."* (Ch 24).

### Canon Appearances
- **Story 01:** processes Marcus. *"Correction isn't punishment, Mr. Chen. It's healing."*
- **Story 02:** processes Kira. *"The system doesn't make mistakes, Ms. Osman."* Something flickers in her eyes.
- **Book 4:** Prologue (assessment: *"You're a strong believer. Stronger than most."*; on the comm: *"C-1 profile confirmed… he's exactly what was requested"*), Ch 2–3 (Marcus's sessions), Ch 4 (Kira's word test), Ch 17 (Tanaka's attempt fails), Ch 24 (the alarm), Ch 25 ("Moss's alarm went to the district command"), Ch 28.

### Voice
Warm, certain and maternal. Everything is healing, illness or progress. She never raises her voice.

### Continuity Traps
- **She knew Marcus was "requested"** for Wing C and routed him to Reeves. What she doesn't believe is that it harms him. Don't write her as ignorant of Continuity.
- She is never cynical and never takes a bribe.
- "Reyna" is a near-clash with "Reyes" (Elena's Story 01 alias) and with **Reeves**. Keep her as "Dr. Moss" in narration where possible.

### Provisional
A witness at Kovač's inquiry, or a believer who takes the Doctrine at face value. In Book 8 she could be one of the staff running the mass correction of "sympathisers", sincerely.

## 2. Officer Kade: The Courteous Enforcer

### At a Glance
- **Title:** the texts use both. Story 01 has "a senior officer named Kade"; Story 02 has "**Enforcer** Kade"; Book 4's Prologue and Book 5 have "**Officer** Kade". **Use "Officer Kade" as his rank, and call him an enforcer as his job.** Both forms are canon.
- **First name and age:** never given. In Book 5 he is "older and grayer" (Ch 4).
- **Workplace:** the Enforcement Center interview room with the camera. Story 02 calls it the "Enforcement Tower" (see Traps).

### Who He Is
He is the procedure in person: *"Your belief isn't measurable, Mr. Chen. Your exposure is."* To Marcus he is "not unkind", and to Kira "professional, cold, efficient". He doesn't enjoy it, and he doesn't doubt it either, until Book 5, where he asks sincerely: *"How do you explain forty people?"*

### Canon Appearances
- **Story 01:** takes the report, and at 09:00 the next day delivers the 73%.
- **Story 02:** investigates the missing fact, and delivers the 92%. *"Love is not measurable, Ms. Osman. Your actions are."*
- **Book 4:** Prologue ("Officer Kade presiding"). He is remembered in Ch 1, 2, 9 and 10.
- **Book 5:** Ch 4 (the arrest: *"Mr. Chen. The Registry says you are dead."*, and *"Your witness is also dead, Mr. Chen."*. He notes Marcus's unmarked hands without comment), Ch 10 (*"I'm sorry, Mr. Chen."* The Unit 3 transfer is approved), Ch 19 (on the Justice Center steps: *"I have no warrant for a dead man, Mr. Volkov."* He almost smiles).

### Continuity Traps
- There is **no reform arc** in canon. At most he hesitates.
- The Story 01 and Book 5 interviews happen in the **same room with the same camera**. The echo is deliberate.
- The Book 5 Ch 4 notes say he appears "briefly, in Ch 25", but the Ch 25 summary doesn't include him. His confirmed later appearances are Ch 10 and 19.

### Provisional
Book 5 never says where he was during the hour. If he was outside the Ministry district, he could be a clean witness in Book 6. Or he could be the enforcer ordered to carry out the Book 8 mass arrests.

## 3. Undersecretary Iver Pell: The Hardliner

### At a Glance
- **Role:** Undersecretary at the Ministry of Historical Integrity. He wants a quick trial for the "dead murderer" and a return to re-correction. Age not given.
- **Status after Book 5:** still in office. He lost the Doctrine fight.

### Canon Appearances (Book 5)
Ch 2 (watches Kovač's inquiry; by 16:30 names "the Continuity escapees" as suspects), Ch 4 (asks for Marcus's move to secure care), Ch 10 (the Unit 3 request), Ch 13 (announces the **Dec 6 tribunal**, "without delay for procedural novelties"), Ch 15 (asks whether Kovač's six certified deaths are also in doubt; in the corridor: *"The Minister likes you. Enjoy it."*, and he tells her Singh opened a second file on her), Ch 16–18 (the tribunal; he "presides in spirit if not in title"), Ch 19 ("a resistance stunt with forged corpses"), Ch 24 (triumphant at the confession), Ch 25 (*"A nation of memories is a nation of liars."*; he raises Kovač's false certifications), Ch 30 (the heroes fear handing him the argument to abolish testimony).

### Notes
He is the foil that makes Wei look good: Pell attacks and Wei rescues. Whether Pell knows he is being used is open. **He is right about one thing** (memory can be written), and he is right for the wrong reasons.

### Continuity Traps
- In the Ch 2 hearing room, witnesses disagree about **"whether Pell left the room"** during the hour. Keep his movements in the hour ambiguous. It is a free thread for later.
- "Iver" is a near-clash with **Ivan** (and with Ines, the Ashford orderly).
- "Pell" is a near-clash with Pavel Ionescu.

## 4. Inquiry Chair Halvorsen: The Procedure

### At a Glance
- **Role:** chairs the inquiry into Director Kovač, which has been running since August 2106. The Nov 15 session is the ninth.
- **Sex, first name and age:** never given. Keep them open.

### Neutral or Hostile?
**Procedural, and leaning hostile.** Ch 2 has Halvorsen "polite and hungry for a conviction". In Ch 15 Halvorsen produces the damning calendar line (that the Director's office requested the 14:30 border slot) from real facts arranged to look bad.

### Canon Appearances (Book 5)
Ch 2 (dictates a summary of the session "for the record, since the record is gone", and the twelve people present disagree about details), Ch 15.

### Continuity Traps
Halvorsen was **in the Ministry district** during the hour (the fourth-floor hearing room), so Halvorsen's own recollections are partly seeded. Tanaka explains this in Ch 11.

## 5. Kira's Supervisor (Story 02): The Man Who Made the Call

- **Name:** **unnamed.** Don't name him. The old "Supervisor Chen" is wrong, and it adds another Chen.
- **Canon:** *"She went to her supervisor's office, explained the situation, watched his expression change from confusion to concern to suspicion. He made calls. Within an hour, Enforcer Kade arrived."* (Story 02.) In Book 4, Ch 10, Kira counts him first among her accusers: "accused by her supervisor, by an enforcer, by The Judge…"
- **Trap:** he does **not** believe her, and he does **not** testify for her.

## 6. The Dec 6 Tribunal (Book 5, Ch 18)

- **Named members:** **none.** Pell announced it, and "presides in spirit if not in title". The forty statements are read into the record. The Judge's contamination analysis of the "subject's persistent false beliefs" is **88%**. The verdict is guilty, pending "stabilisation", with transfer to Secure Care Unit 3. The conviction is quashed on Dec 29 (Ch 26).
- **If a presiding officer has to speak on the page**, keep them anonymous ("the presiding magistrate"). If a name is unavoidable, the suggestion is **Magistrate Oren Falk**.
