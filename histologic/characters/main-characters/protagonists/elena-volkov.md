**Status:** CURRENT, Sept 2026. Rewritten to match Story 01 (*The Believer's Fall*), Story 03, the drafted *The Correction* and *The Lost Hour* (plot v3.1 with the v3.2 refinements and the chapter summaries). Later books will be rewritten to fit this file.

## At a Glance
- **Full name:** Elena Sergeyevna Volkov (Ivan's correction record, Book 4 Ch 15). Her nephews call her **"Aunt Lena"**, and the resistance calls her **Commander**.
- **Story 01 names:** she gave Marcus the name **"Elena Reyes"**, which is how the café fact records her. The enforcers' file names her **"Elena Vasquez"**. Kade calls that "her real name", but it is only a deeper cover.
- **Age:** 42 in Aug 2106 (Book 4 Ch 25). **Born ~2064.** She is 41 or 42 in Story 01.
- **Family:** the younger sister of **Ivan Volkov** and the aunt of Dmitri, Alexei and Nikolai. Her sister-in-law was **Sofya**. The next-of-kin record lists only Sofya and Elena: **no brother "Professor Adrian Volkov"**.
- **Occupation:** a trained resistance **recruiter**, who "successfully turned seven citizens over the past two years" (Story 01). Since 2106 she has been the commander who coordinates operations. She was never in "military intelligence".
- **Status after Book 4:** a fugitive. She faked her correction, was released under surveillance, and slipped it. She is **not** among the officially dead.
- **Status after Book 5:** still an outlaw. She is not part of the Doctrine's revivals, because she was never logged dead.
- **Appearance:** tall, with dark hair (in Story 01 it catches the light by the fountain; by Book 4 it is "pulled back hard") and **dark eyes**. She has sharp cheekbones, and the triplets have them too. By 2106 her face is "all bones and angles… lined at the eyes". Her voice is low, hoarse and very controlled. In Story 01 she wears a blue dress at the plaza and a green one at dinner. In Book 4 she wears dark tactical clothing and a heavy jacket.

## Who She Is
Elena is what Marcus was taught to report: a woman who asks questions for a living and uses love as a tool. She watched the system take her brother apart and send him home "empty", and she went to fight it. She became very good at the part of the fight that costs the most. She recruited seven people, and by the time she met Marcus she no longer knew whether she had saved them or destroyed them.

Story 01 shows her cold. The novels show what the coldness costs. She is the resistance's best planner, and she counts heads like a soldier because she has lost people. She knew her brother had been changed and didn't tell his sons; she came back fifteen years later to get them out. She is not a mentor, and she is not a legend yet. She is a tired professional who keeps doing the necessary thing and never lets herself off.

## Voice Rules
1. **Fast, flat, no wasted word.** She briefs; she doesn't explain. "Here's where we are." She uses short declaratives and dry asides ("People noticed").
2. **She counts first and feels second.** Her eyes go over a group left to right like a soldier checking a patrol. "Eleven. I was told eight." Feeling comes afterwards, and all at once when it comes (Book 4 Ch 25).
3. **She remembers everything and writes nothing down.** She carries a notebook she never writes in. She reads files ("I know. I read your file.").
4. **Bitter precision about the system.** "There's no such thing as a real one. There's just the ones we didn't make."
5. **She tests people by habit.** The passphrase, "Who was born first?", is her first words to her nephews in fifteen years.
6. **She can't say the direct thing to Marcus.** With him she speaks to Kira instead ("Is he…?"), or keeps to one line. Keep it that way until the relationship is deliberately resolved.
7. **Two registers.** As the recruiter (Story 01) she is warm, curious and self-deprecating ("I'm terrible with directions"), and it is all technique. As the commander she is cold and hoarse. The reader should be able to feel her switch between them.

## Appearances

### Story 01: *The Believer's Fall*
The antagonist, and then the warning. As "Elena Reyes, freelance data analyst, new to the capital", she courts Marcus with questions about the factorepo and The Judge. Unmasked, she is clinical: "You were a perfect target: lonely, idealistic, trusting." "Seduction is just a tool." Then her brother story breaks through. She tells Marcus the numbers won't care about his loyalty, and waits for the enforcers on the couch. Her parting line: "I'm sorry. Not for the mission. But for making you think you were loved. That was cruel."

## Continuity Traps
- **Dark eyes**, not steel gray. Tall. Dark hair.
- **She is the Elena of Story 01.** Her appearances start there, not in Book 4.
- **She is a recruiter**, with seven turned and Marcus as the eighth. She is not ex-military intelligence.
- **"Vasquez" is not her real name**, whatever Kade said. The state's records have never linked Vasquez to Volkov on the page. If they ever do, it should be a plot point.
- **Faked correction, not escape.** Regional facility, released under surveillance in March 2106, slipped it. She is **not** in the Registry as dead, so the Lost Hour Doctrine doesn't "revive" her.
- **She didn't run the storm unit's calibration herself.** Her technician did, and lost two weeks. She didn't drive the van; Teo did.
- **She didn't extract Marina and Jakob herself.** Alexei and Dmitri did, and she coordinated.
- **Her Book 5 source is "he"**, and his credentials lead to Singh. Don't resolve who he is before Singh returns.
- **The next of kin is only Sofya and Elena.** No Volkov uncle.
- **Never give the triplets' birth years.** Use relative time: fifteen years since her last visit.
- **Whether she knew about 2471** before Isaiah's exposé (Aug 5, 2106) is unstated. In Book 4 she knows only that Ivan was *changed*.
- **Name rule:** there is only one Elena in the cast. The old drafts' other Elenas (Sokolov, Marcus's daughter) are renamed.

## Canon Conflicts Noted
- **Book 4 Ch 25** says she "spent eight months planning how to get into a building", but she was only released in March 2106, five months before the escape. It works if the planning began in her correction cell (Dec 2105). Say so if it is ever referenced.
- **Book 4 Ch 16** calls Ivan "a man who would die a year later as Test Subject 2471". That reads as a year after Story 01, but he died about fifteen years earlier, a year after his correction. Suggested wording: "a man who had died, a year after they sent him home, as Test Subject 2471."
- **Book 4 Ch 15 summary** dates Ivan's correction record "two years before his death". The drafted chapter says the spring before, "a year later". The draft wins.
