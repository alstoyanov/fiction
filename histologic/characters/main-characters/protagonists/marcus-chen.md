## At a Glance
- **Full name:** Marcus Chen.
- **Age:** 27 in Story 01 (Aug–Oct 2105) and 29 in *The Lost Hour* (Nov 2106), per `SERIES-OVERVIEW.md`. Both figures hold only if he was born in 2077 with a birthday between about Oct 8 and Nov 14. **Suggested:** early November 2077. He is then 28 through almost all of Book 4.
- **Occupation:** Junior Historian at Veridica's Regional Factorepo, fourteenth floor, **desk 14-08** (Story 01). He was never an "analyst".
- **File code:** Project Continuity **C-1, BELIEVER**. Cell **7-H**, Wing C, Ashford.
- **Status after Book 4:** officially *deceased: cognitive failure during fact storm* (Aug 2, 2106). Living in a border-zone cottage with Kira.
- **Status after Book 5:** *CHEN, MARCUS. STATUS: LIVING* (Dec 29, 2106). His tribunal conviction was quashed. He was appointed a commissioner of the Historical Integrity Commission on Jan 8, 2107.
- **Appearance:** thin, with dark hair that goes uncombed in Wing C. He wears **glasses**, usually pushed up on his forehead or sitting crooked. His face in sleep is "very young". His **hands are unmarked**, and in Book 5 that matters. His interface was installed at eight, and the scars behind his ears have faded to thin white lines.
- **Habits:** black coffee, no sugar (Story 01). He writes in a small, precise historian's hand, in pencil. He keeps lists.

## Who He Is
Marcus is the man the system was built to produce, and the first man it was used to prove something on. He believed completely: he reported the woman he loved because the facts required it, and the system condemned him anyway. At Ashford it emptied him in two months, down to Tanaka's "floor". He came back because one person decided to remember him, and because he learned to hide her inside the dullest thing he knew, the fact-entry formats of his old job.

What comes out is not the old believer. It is a damaged, careful man who cannot trust his own memory, who checks his life against other people's, and who has swapped faith in the record for a harder faith: *correction by people, not of people*. In Book 5 he learns that even this faith was foreseen. The man who sat at the next desk for three years designed his downfall and then his rise. Marcus keeps going anyway, and keeps his own record.

## Voice Rules
1. **He thinks in fact entries.** Date, time, location, involved parties, status. It is his reflex, his comfort and, from Ch 11 of Book 4, his armour: *"Traffic incident, corner of Fifth and Main, fourteen-oh-two…"*
2. **He never finishes Kira's entry himself.** *"Personnel entry: Osman, K. Senior Architect. Location: Wing C, 7-K. Status:"* Finishing it would make it salient. Kira finishes it for him in the storm: *"Status: with you."*
3. **The prose never fully recovers.** After Book 4 Ch 3 his POV has holes. He reaches for a word and it is gone, or loses the end of a story about the fourteenth floor. Keep a trace of this in every later book.
4. **CHECKED / UNCHECKED / HOPES.** He sorts what he remembers into columns and asks witnesses to confirm it. He moves items between columns under stress.
5. **"Both. I think it's allowed."** His emotional growth is learning that contradictory feelings aren't a malfunction (anger, gratitude and fear about Elena, Book 4 Ch 16). He uses the phrase sparingly.
6. **Earnest and precise, with dry, quiet humour** that dates back to Story 01 ("There are worse epitaphs"). No catchphrases, no speeches. His longest statements are arguments about the record ("If you entered it, it happened").
7. **He does not run.** Running proves the charge (Story 01, Book 5 Ch 4). He holds doors so that others can run.

## Continuity Traps
- **His hands are unmarked.** No scar, no bandage, in any book. The bandaged left hand is Wei's.
- **Black coffee, no sugar.** Not tea. (Kira also orders black coffee, no sugar, in Story 02. Keep it as a quiet echo, not a coincidence to explain.)
- **Junior Historian, desk 14-08.** Thomas Wei sat at **14-09, 2102–2105.** Continuity took Thomas's face; Marcus kept the name and the voice.
- **Glasses are canon.** Story 01 has him adjust them; Book 4 has them crooked, pushed up and folded by his head. See the conflict with Book 5 Ch 3 below.
- **He did not lead the escape plan.** Dmitri planned it and Elena ran the storm; Marcus was still damaged. He did not stay behind to reform from inside; Isaiah stayed. Marcus went to the border zone.
- **Permanently lost:** his mother's first name, the first library conversation (lost May 27, 2106, now "held by K."), the three versions of Elena's last words, the plaza dress colour, and the feeling of belief. They never come back. Other people may give him the facts back as testimony.
- **The field never happened.** It stays filed as a hope. He can't feel that it's false, and he never will.
- **The lullaby verse** may be memory or invention. Never settle it.
- **Legally:** dead from Aug 2, 2106 to Dec 29, 2106. Convicted Dec 6; conviction quashed. Commissioner from Jan 8, 2107 (the Historical Integrity **Commission**, not an "Institute", and he is not its Director).
- **Wei is not exposed in Book 5.** Marcus's only proof is "No. I checked."
- **Contamination figures:** 0.58 real, 0.73 typed (Book 4); 88% at the tribunal (Book 5).
