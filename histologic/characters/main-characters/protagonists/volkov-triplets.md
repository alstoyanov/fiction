## Who They Are
Three brothers who were "one person in three bodies" as boys and split into three opposite lives: Dmitri carried the order, Alexei the questions, Nikolai the guilt (Story 03). Correction failed on them because it could not correct one without correcting all three. The bond is not telepathy. They never hear each other's thoughts. They feel each other as **weight**: Alexei's impatience as pressure behind the eyes, Nikolai's fear as cold hands, Dmitri as a steady cold weight at the edge of the mind. "We only know where the others are. The way you know where your own hand is in the dark" (Nikolai, Ch 15).

## The Bond: Rules for Writing It
1. **Sensation, never voices.** Weight, pull, pressure, cold hands. The technicians see it as three peaks on a screen where there should be one ("distributed resistance via shared salience architecture").
2. **It resists correction and seeding, but not completely.** It cost Nikolai the rooftop (Book 4, Ch 21). In Book 5 they feel the seeding at 14:38 as a faint wrongness; Dmitri feels it least.
3. **It needs no line of sight.** Isolation did not break it at the border, and in Book 4 Ch 29 they choose to separate.
4. **In a fact storm they are the only stable thing in the building.** They call each other's names in rhythm ("Dima. Kolya. Lyosha.") and people follow them.
5. **The passphrase is "Who was born first?"** The answer is "I was. By thirty seconds." and "That doesn't count." Elena signs with it, and it is how she tests them on the road (Ch 25).
6. **Their private signs:** the promise sign. "Save each other" (Mama's last words). "Mama said save each other. She didn't say only each other" (Alexei, Ch 6).

## Dmitri Volkov: The Enforcer
*C-3, cell 7-A. Garden detail at Ashford.*

### Voice Rules
1. **Formal, with almost no contractions.** "Negative. I am close." "It is tactically sound." "We do not leave him." He uses contractions only when quoting the boys on the roof, or when something breaks through.
2. **He counts.** Camera arcs (the east pole camera: 140 degrees, a period of 9 minutes 20 seconds, a four-minute blind arc), orderly rotations, heads in a group. His inner voice is a surveillance log.
3. **Short declaratives under pressure.** "Do not move. Look at the bench. Good." He still uses the voice he used on suspects.
4. **His face does nothing when he feels the most** (Nikolai, Ch 12).
5. **Deadpan, never jokes.** "Thirty seconds counts." Alexei supplies the jokes. Do not give Dmitri the old "combat humour".
6. **"Lyosha."** Said alone, it is a whole reprimand.

### Arc in Book 4
The man who hunted his brother for three years without knowing him. At Ashford he rakes the garden with a blank face and counts cameras, and he made the brothers' rule: *survive, wait, trust no one*. Sokolov's monthly visits are the hardest acting of his life: *"Was the data worth my mother?"* (Ch 5). When the rule breaks, he insists he be the one to reveal them to Marcus, because "it is the only use left for what I was", and he apologises: "I watched you for five months and did not help you" (Ch 13). He plans the escape "the way he once planned to catch The Phantom" (Ch 20), refuses to build any plan on Sokolov ("Then let that be a surprise, not a strategy"), and insists on bringing Old Songs. He writes the rooftop out in full in a primer for Nikolai (Ch 21). During the escape he subdues Reeves, leaves the old man who will not pass the gate, and assigns Hanna and Pavel to the van, a choice he will remember for the rest of his life (Ch 25). He answers Elena's passphrase for Nikolai. At the checkpoint: "She let us go." He goes to the resistance with Alexei and Elena.

### Arc in Book 5
**The only clean witness.** On overwatch at 14:21 he sees a man with a staff card enter Singh's side entrance: "A man. Dark jacket, staff card. Side entrance. Not one of ours… He walks well" (Ch 7). The bond blunted the seeding, so his memory is the one unseeded account of that minute, and legally he is a corpse. His fear is not of arrest but "of *mattering*": the enforcer's truth had always been the system's (Ch 13). He testifies on the Justice Center steps (Ch 19: "a younger man than the one you are hunting"). He is on the annex team (Ch 21), and he distrusts Wei's case against Reeves: "It is a very good case. I have built cases like this. I built them when I already knew the answer" (Ch 23). He testifies at the hearing (Ch 25) and wants to wait and build the case (Ch 30). At the Commission he recognises Wei's walk and gives Marcus a single nod (Ch 31). He is Marcus's second named witness in the CHECKED entry.

### Provisional Fate (Books 6–9; to be rewritten)
He survives the series. The old file had him chief of defence, and then a "peacemaker" who chooses peace over revenge after Alexei's death. Keep the shape (an enforcer who ends by refusing the cycle), but keep any ministerial title provisional. In Book 6 Principle Five is revealed: their father's death "planned on paper". That is Dmitri's reckoning with the service he gave. Rewrite the old quotes without contractions: "Alexei died so we could be free. I will not waste that."

## Alexei Volkov: The Phantom
*C-4, cell 7-B. Library detail at Ashford.*

### Voice Rules
1. **Warm, wry, uses contractions.** The contrast with Dmitri is deliberate (Book 4 Ch 6 note). "Well. Isn't this nice."
2. **He teases Dmitri about being the eldest.** "You're the bossiest. That's different." "You're like a dog with a map." "You go first because you're bossy."
3. **Moral urgency comes before caution.** "We don't leave people. That's the whole point of me." He wants to act *tonight* (Book 4 Ch 19; Book 5 Ch 7, 13, 30).
4. **Theatre is his craft.** Messages on walls ("WE'RE NOT SO DIFFERENT, AGENT VOLKOV"), the public resurrection on the Justice Center steps. He thinks in routes, jurisdictions and exits.
5. **Blunt with outsiders, gentle with the frightened.** To Kovač: "Tell me why I should trust you." To the woman over tea: "It's the hardest choice you'll ever make. I can't make it for you."
6. **He grins.** A "sudden wide boyish grin that changed his whole face" (Ch 19). He laughs until he coughs.

### Arc in Book 4
A model reformed librarian who is still the Phantom inside. The library is his map of which minds are still reaching. Old Songs hums the tune their mother hummed over the washing-up, and tells him, "They look for what you love. So I hide what I love in what I've memorised." Alexei folds Papa's study into the tune and teaches the method to his brothers (Ch 6). Guilt over watching Marcus go under makes him break the rule: he underlines REMEMBER on page 47 of a water-systems manual for Kira. He builds the pinprick code in arithmetic primers (Ch 13–14): "We survived because we were three. You can survive because you are seven." He protests Isaiah's staying: "I've never once left someone at the fence and driven away. I'm not starting with you" (Ch 22). He drives the lead car through the storm ("There's always a way round") and sees the van go over the embankment in his mirror. He cannot turn back (Ch 26). Of Sokolov: "She let *herself* go. We were just in the way." At dawn he finally knows the cost he always told people about.

### Arc in Book 5
He drives the delivery truck into the Ministry district with Old Songs beside him and extracts Marina Holt and Jakob Lind at 14:25–14:43 (Ch 7). He wants to go public at once, and suspects Kovač over the 14:30 slot, then apologises to her, awkwardly, "which he has never done to anyone" (Ch 15–16). He stages the "resurrection" on the Justice Center steps: "Then you'd better prove I'm alive" to Kade. He spots the man in the gray coat, whom Dmitri says "counted to three" (Ch 19). At the dilemma he argues for exposing Wei "with fire" (Ch 30), and accepts the silence.

### Provisional Fate (Books 6–9; to be rewritten)
**Death is the leading option, in Book 7 or Book 8.** The old file killed him in *Battle of Blood* (now Book 8), covering a team's escape against SFW artillery. Recommendation: make his death an extraction, the thing he is for. He stays at the fence so that others can cross, reversing "I have never once left someone at the fence." Old Songs sings him into "The Names" (see `old-songs-character.md`). Usable old last words: "Tell Dmitri… tell him I'm proud we were brothers." Drop the old quips ("Error 404"-style jokes, "Rules are suggestions for people without imagination").

## Nikolai Volkov: The Bridge
*C-5, cell 7-C. Technical detail at Ashford. Alias **Pieter Lang** (Book 4 Epilogue – Book 5 Ch 25).*

### Voice Rules
1. **Quiet, anxious, precise.** He lives "inside *what if*". He looks at his hands when he speaks, like Morrison.
2. **Technical language, used plainly.** Dead space, blocks, partitions, audit tables. In writing he is careful and exact: "I want to say that clearly, because Dr. Tanaka will want to know."
3. **One flash of dark humour per scene, no more.** "If they shoot me, at least it will be for something more interesting than a pressure sensor" (Ch 12).
4. **The middle is his theme.** "Somebody stays in the middle. For once let it be someone who chose it" (Ch 22). "At least this time I'm choosing which middle" (Ch 29).
5. **He keeps copies.** "I've learned to keep a copy of everything" (Ch 29). "I've kept a copy of everything. As always" (Book 5, Ch 30).
6. **He is the peacemaker between brothers.** "Stop. Please, both of you, stop. This is what they want" (Story 03).

### Arc in Book 4
The best systems technician at Ashford, "the Volkov who fixes things", trusted for being incurious. Each night in maintenance room B-4 he visits his hidden **Registry partition** and restores their memories. In Ch 12 he restores the rooftop, and then opens CONTINUITY with Brandt's forgotten certificate. He finds the seven files, the triple cradle and the date. In Ch 15 he writes the brothers' story in pinpricks and finds his father's correction record and next-of-kin field. Then Elena's message appears inside his partition, signed "Who was born first?". Reeves finds the audit trail (Ch 20) and runs the **single-node session** on him (Ch 21). The bond holds, but the rooftop memory is gone for good. He accepts Dmitri's primer as a story, not a memory. On the road he cannot answer his aunt's passphrase, though he keeps "Aunt Lena". In Ch 29 his brothers tell him the roof aloud and he answers from their telling: "Dima. By thirty seconds. It doesn't count." He is "a memory rebuilt from testimony". He goes back into Veridica as Elena's mole, on a dead man's number, with the rooftop page folded in his wallet. In the Epilogue he flags the unowned 14:00–15:00 block.

### Arc in Book 5
"Pieter Lang": a beard, glasses he does not need, hunched shoulders, noodles at his desk. Wei recruits him because he flagged the block ("I want people who notice"), and says, "Your file's very thin, Pieter. I like that in a man" (Ch 9). He feeds Kira the erasure architecture and the forty statements (Ch 8), and keeps the edge-cache fragment: a staff card at 14:21, class "Systems Architecture, senior", one of 41 people, one of them Wei. Maya confides in him (Ch 14). Wei returns the rooftop page he never dropped: "My father collected old primers" (Ch 20). Nikolai tells Maya the truth: "My name isn't Pieter. It's Nikolai Volkov. I was C-5. Your father studied me." He comes out of hiding to testify at the hearing, which ends the cover (Ch 25), and keeps the proof on Wei (Ch 30). In the Epilogue he holds Maya's hand and shows the seven a new erasure being prepared in another nation, with the same signature.

### Provisional Fate (Books 6–9; to be rewritten)
He survives. His romance with **Maya Reeves** is canon from Book 5, and it replaces any old romance. The old plan had him help destroy The Judge in Book 8 with the old "Sarah Chen" character (rename pending; suggested **Nora Chen**, see `../complex/morally-ambiguous-characters.md`), and become the architect of a rebuilt factorepo in Book 9. Both fit if the destruction runs through **Principle Seven**, Vance's contingency (SERIES-OVERVIEW). His "keep a copy of everything" habit makes him the natural keeper of the distributed-truth networks' backups in Book 6.

## Continuity Traps
- **Dmitri first, by thirty seconds.** Nobody is "the middle child" by birth. On the parapet Alexei sits in the middle and Nikolai "always on the right".
- **Gray-blue eyes.** Cropped hair at Ashford.
- **Born 2075 (settled, Sept 2026).** Story 03 was corrected to match: the interface is fitted at eight, "the age the Mandatory Citizenship Act of 2065 set for every child" (not in 2066), and the rooftop is "seventeen years earlier". Sokolov (born 2070) is five years older than them. "Twelve years as one person" means until the rooftop; they drifted from twelve to fifteen and split after Ivan's death. "Fifteen years" apart and since Papa died is a round number (about fourteen and a half in Nov 2104).
- **The rooftop memory is gone from Nikolai permanently.** He answers the passphrase from testimony, never from memory. He keeps the primer page in his wallet; Wei has handled it.
- **The bond is not telepathy.** No heard thoughts.
- **They never knew their father was corrected** until Book 4 Ch 15. Alexei knew about 2471 in Story 03; the correction record was news to all three.
- **Nikolai escapes with the others** in Book 4. He goes back into Veridica only afterwards (Ch 29).
- **Book 5: Dmitri is the only unseeded witness to the man at 14:21.** The man was younger than Reeves.
- **Interfaces are never removed.** They are spoofed (flatlined). Removal kills.
- **They are officially dead from Aug 2 to Dec 29, 2106.**
- **Never "brass armour" or a "brass tool belt".** Overalls, cell gray, the resistance's dark clothing.
