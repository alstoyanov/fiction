## At a Glance
- **Full name:** Kira Osman.
- **Age:** 27 in 2106 (`SERIES-OVERVIEW.md`). **Suggested:** born 2079, which makes her 26 at her arrest (Nov–Dec 2105) and 27 through Books 4–5.
- **Occupation:** **Senior Architect**, a systems architect at the Factorepo Spire, on the sixtieth floor. She "helped design parts of" the Spire and wrote transmission-layer standards. She was never a "technician" or "recorder".
- **File code:** Project Continuity **C-2, ARCHITECT**. Cell **7-K**, facing Marcus in 7-H. *"Acquired via record anomaly (ref. R.). Placement 7-K per protocol R-2."*
- **Conviction:** The Judge found a **92%** probability of compromise (Story 02).
- **Status after Book 4:** officially *deceased: cognitive failure during fact storm* (Aug 2, 2106). She lives with Marcus in the border zone.
- **Status after Book 5:** STATUS: LIVING (Dec 29, 2106). Commissioner of the Historical Integrity Commission (Jan 8, 2107). Keeper of the first written journal, which has **31 contributors** by Nov 2107.
- **Appearance:** dark hair worn in a knot (pinned in her Spire days), and **hazel** eyes, "very steady". In Wing C she wears cell gray. Clean, practical and plain; the old file's brass, copper, rings, vest and tattoo are gone.
- **Habits:** black coffee, no sugar (Story 02). She **counts**: tiles, steps, nodes. She reduces the world to its components when it gets too loud.

## Who She Is
Kira built part of the machine that says facts can't be changed, and then watched one of her own facts vanish. She is an engineer to the bone. She treats her own mind as a system under attack, and she is the first person in Wing C to work out that the machine "follows feeling, not information". Her armour is the dullest thing she knows, her own specifications. The only person she can't fully debug is herself.

The shape of her arc is holding two true things at once. Adrian lied to her *and* loved her. Reeves may have placed her in front of Marcus *and* she chose him. The record was wrong *and* it can be corrected, "not by them, by us". In Book 5 she is the one who reads the one thing that can't be seeded, **code**, and finds the hand behind all of it.

## Voice Rules
1. **She thinks like an engineer.** Hypothesis, model, test. "Assume the attacker knows your architecture… Don't make anything impregnable; make it *boring*." Her POV logs things in incident-report format: *"Occupant 7-D… Returned pale… Unchanged."*
2. **The technical vault.** She recites 7.3 in the white chair: *"Layer one: key exchange… rotation interval ninety seconds… block size sixty-four kilobytes, chain depth twelve."* The people she loves live inside the specs: Adrian in 7.3 (the bridge in key rotation, the warehouse in hash chaining), her mother in the node address tables, the November 18 fact in the verification layer.
3. **After Ashford the vault won't close.** From Book 4 Ch 28 she can't write code without reciting it aloud. By the Book 4 Epilogue she has started practising in silence ("That was new"). Let this compulsion fade slowly, and let it come back under stress.
4. **"Both."** She holds contradictions that the factorepo would reject. "You can need it, and it can be real. Both." Give it one use per book.
5. **Her anger is cold.** It is hot in the first session, then "deep water under ice" from Book 4 Ch 16 on. When she is most furious she speaks most levelly.
6. **She enters facts aloud when it matters,** in the Story 02 cadence: name, date, time, place, status. *"Kira Osman. August twenty-third, 2106. Twenty-three fifty hours. Border zone. With him. By choice."*
7. **Short, precise sentences.** She is not a quipper. No catchphrases ("That doesn't compute" is gone). Any wit is dry and comes out of engineering, and it is rare.

## Continuity Traps
- **Senior Architect at the Spire**, sixtieth floor. Not a technician.
- **Protocol 7.3** was entered **Nov 18, 2105, 22:00**, the night before she met Adrian (Nov 19). Its spec is fixed: lattice-based key exchange, 90-second rotation, hash chaining with a 64 KB block size and chain depth 12. Don't change the numbers.
- **Adrian did not steal the fact.** Continuity deleted it; Wei's hand did the work. Adrian's contact was *used* to support the 92%.
- **Kovač is Adrian's aunt**, and Adrian is the son of her younger brother. She hasn't spoken to him in six years.
- **Hazel eyes, dark hair.** No brass anything.
- **R-2 proves the conditions were arranged, never the feeling.** No book may settle it.
- **She was never in any field** on the night of Aug 1–2. She was with Marcus every second.
- **In Book 5 she is never on Wei's team.** Nikolai is the insider.
- **Her proof against Wei is fragile:** her reading of a signature, an author cache, and Maya's memory of a time. Wei is not exposed in Book 5.
- **The XI link:** the folder is **RESTORATION / XI** (Book 4 Ch 24), and she makes the connection in Book 5 Ch 18. She doesn't know what XI means (Chen Zhao's copy number) until the manuscript schedule allows it.
- **The reciting compulsion** begins in Book 4 Ch 28. It isn't there before.

## Canon Conflicts Noted
- **Story 01 vs Story 02:** Marcus's interview is at the "Enforcement Center" (Story 01; Book 4 also uses "Enforcement Center"), but Story 02 sends Kira to the "Enforcement Tower" and calls it "the same room where Marcus Chen had sat". Suggested fix: make it one building. In Story 02, "Enforcement Tower" could be a common name for the Enforcement Center, or change the one word.
