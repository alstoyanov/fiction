## At a Glance
- **Full name:** Dr. Yuki Tanaka. The Ashford orderlies' tablets call her *C-6*. Moss calls her "Yuki".
- **Age:** **45** in *The Correction* (Oct 2105 – Aug 2106), **46** in *The Lost Hour*.
- **Occupation:** correction specialist in Ashford's ordinary wards for eleven years. Then Project Continuity subject **C-6 CLINICIAN**, in cell **7-D**. Then a physician in a Chronos teaching hospital, working under the hospital director's protection.
- **Status after Book 4:** officially **deceased** ("cognitive failure during fact storm"). She lives in Chronos with Dr. James Morrison and gets defectors across the fence.
- **Status after Book 5:** legally alive (*STATUS: LIVING*, by link, Dec 29, 2106). The trafficking charge in Chronos is dropped.
- **Appearance:** small (she "came up barely to Kira's shoulder"). Mid-forties, Japanese features, silver-streaked hair "pinned up in a tidy bun despite the prison clothes". She sits and stands very straight, with her hands folded. Her voice is "very quiet and very precise, with a slight roughness to it, like an instrument that has been used a great deal". After Book 4 Ch 24 she has a **thin pale scar on her right temple**.

## Who She Is
A doctor imprisoned in the building where she practised. She knows exactly what the white chair does, because she used to run the ordinary version of it. That makes her sessions worse, and it gives her the one defence nobody else has: she can *narrate*. While the machine works on her, a part of her stands "very slightly, to one side, with a clipboard", dictating case notes. She is the first person in Wing C to see that the Volkovs are awake, the one who names "the floor", and the one who finds the theory the whole series rests on: **isolation accelerates, connection resists.** She is kind without being soft. Her gentlest discovery is also her most frightening: almost no one at Ashford is cruel.

## Voice Rules
1. **Case notes.** In her POV her thinking comes as italic clinical entries: *"Patient: Chen, Marcus. Wing C, 7-H. Admitted October 8. Presenting profile: high-conviction believer… Course: rapid."* She leaves lines unfinished when she can't bear to finish them (*Prognosis,* and stop).
2. **"The place where she keeps things."** What she can't explain she files, unlabelled: 7-K kept empty, Moss's sincerity, and in Book 5 *"Nobody who can't be seeded has seen her body."*
3. **She asks permission and tells the truth.** "Mr. Chen. I want to tell you what they did to you. May I?" And: "No. Not everything. I won't lie to you. Some things will not come back. I'm sorry."
4. **Exact distinctions.** "I'm telling you it didn't happen. That isn't quite the same thing." She says *confabulation*, *salience*, *baseline*, and translates them at once.
5. **Formal address** ("Mr. Chen", "Dr. Morrison", "Reyna"), which softens only under strain ("James. Say that again.").
6. **Dry, rare wit.** Kira glares at the treatment door "as if it owed her money". "Thank you for the work order." Never jokes, riddles or aphorisms. She is a physician, not a sage.
7. **Physical tells.** She folds her hands. She sets a cup down very carefully when frightened (at the kitchen table in 2105, and again in Book 5 Ch 6). She does not cry where a camera can see. After the storm she flinches at hissing doors and touches her temple.
8. **Her vault is the checklist.** *Step one: confirm patient identity… Step nine: confirm allergies.* The cello. *Step ten: confirm anaesthetic plan.* His hands on the bow. It ends on *"Step forty-one. Confirm the patient is safe to leave."*

## Continuity Traps
- **She refused the Continuity trials**, and it was **Reeves** who asked. She did not refuse to treat "an innocent patient", and Kovač was never her adversary.
- **78% contamination.** Convicted in summer 2105, subject since August.
- **The scar** is on the **right** temple, from a falling steel rack in the data centre on Aug 2, 2106. It is a scalp wound, a thin pale line afterwards.
- **She drinks tea.**
- **The floor** is her private word. She first says it aloud to Marcus in Ch 11.
- **The checklist has 41 steps.** Her husband is folded into it.
- **In Chronos she is officially dead**, protected by the hospital director, with a thin cover. There is no implant removal anywhere: Morrison *spoofs* telemetry.
- **Seeding is not confabulation.** Confabulation is individual; forty identical details come from a broadcast. She makes this distinction in Book 5 Ch 11 and must not blur it later.
- **She is the one who doubts the body** (Book 5 Ch 22 and Epilogue). Do not resolve Singh in Books 5–8.
- **No sister appears in Books 4–5.** The sister is provisional (Book 9).
- **No brass pendant.**
