## At a Glance
- **Full name:** Thomas Wei. "Thomas" to Marcus in Story 01; "Wei" or "the lead investigator" to almost everyone in Book 5. His colleagues' shorthand in Nikolai's messages is "W."
- **Born:** about 2061. He is **45** in *The Lost Hour* (Nov 2106 – Jan 2107) and about 44 in Story 01 (2105).
- **Occupation:** senior factorepo systems architect (Systems Architecture, Ministry district). 2102–2105: seconded as a Historian to the Regional Factorepo, fourteenth floor, **desk 14-09**, next to Marcus at 14-08. Book 5: lead investigator of the Lost Hour (from Nov 15, 15:20). From Jan 8, 2107: **Technical Director** of the Historical Integrity Commission.
- **Status after Book 5:** free, trusted, honoured. **Never exposed.** Marcus, Kira and Dmitri know. Nobody can prove it.
- **Family:** son and heir of **Dr. Wei Zhang**, mark **WZ·10**, author of Principle Four. "Wei" is the family name; his father's name is written family-name-first, which is why the mark is WZ.
- **Appearance:** tall, graying at the temples, a quiet voice, calm. Right-handed. He moves lightly and athletically ("He walks well"), and takes low steps two at a time. **Left hand bandaged** from Nov 15, 2106, a smaller dressing by Nov 17, still bandaged on Dec 13. By Jan 8, 2107 the bandage is gone and **a thin pink scar** runs across the back of his left hand.
- **File code:** none. The signature he leaves on records is his real file.

## Who He Is
Thomas Wei is the kindest man in *The Lost Hour*, and its author. He is patient, fair and generous with credit, he knows everyone's name, and he protects Marcus against a Ministry that wants him emptied a second time. Every one of those things is true. He is also the insider who typed Marcus's 73, deleted Kira's fact, erased the hour, seeded forty witnesses and his own alibi, walked into Singh's office at 14:21, and wrote a murder into Dr. Reeves's memory. He is an heir: raised by an Architect to carry a sixty-year-old design, and the first of the heirs we meet. What makes him frightening is not cruelty. He is sincere about the design, and he is sincerely interested in the people it uses. At the end of Book 5 he finds the reformers have built something he did not plan, and he wants to see what it becomes.

### The heirs principle and his father
- **Canon:** Thomas is Dr. Wei Zhang's son (Foundation Manuscript §4.6, §4.9, §6). Continuity is his father's principle modernised: its stated aim, "genuine belief… experienced as authentic and self-originated", paraphrases Principle Four. The son ran the Lost Hour, which is Principle Six (Chen Zhao's) performed. The heirs clause is how a 2066 design is still being run in 2106.
- **Principle Four, his father's page:** *"Do not punish the doubter; heal him… Correction is compassion. Certainty is care."* Thomas's kindness is not a mask over the design. It *is* the design, as his father wrote it: he rescues Marcus from Unit 3 because a restorer who has been emptied is no use, and because he doesn't believe in punishment.
- **Not in Book 5:** whether Wei Zhang is alive in 2106 (he would be about 70 or older; see the ages check in `the-architects.md`), and what Thomas thinks of him. "My father collected old primers" is past tense. Keep it that way until Book 8 or 9 decides.
- Thomas is **an heir, not an Architect.** He was a small child in 2066. He did not write or sign anything in the manuscript.

- **What happened in Singh's office.** Singh's death is staged; the woman in the gray coat in the Epilogue is Singh. Whether Wei staged it with her, did it to her, or was used by her is a later book's question.

- **Whether he let Kira find the author cache.** He gave her the files ("Who better?"). The pattern (the returned page, "paradise", his delight at "You remember") supports the reading that he wanted these people to know. Never state it.

## The Bandage and the Scar
This is the main fair-play clue for Twist 3 (README refinement 1).
- **The mechanism** (Ch 11): seeding writes over what a mind finds most important in a memory, the face and the clothes, and leaves small peripheral details alone. The forty witnesses really saw a man walk in. **Wing C gray is seeded. The bandaged left hand is real.** Tanaka circles it: "That one isn't a seed. It's what they actually saw."
- **The witnesses' wording varies; the detail does not:** "White bandage." "Wrapped hand." "Holding it like it hurt." (Ch 8.) Dmitri, unseeded: "the hand he held slightly away from his body" (Ch 13, 19).
- **Where the bandage appears:** Ch 1 (a clean white bandage, "Server rack. My own fault."), Ch 9 (a smaller dressing, "Server rack. Tuesday. Carelessness."), Ch 10 (resting on the table; he lifts the cup with his right), Ch 16 (Kira pictures him holding his cup in his right hand), Ch 18 (Marcus: "He cut it on a server rack."), Ch 21 (he holds the annex door for Kira with it). Ch 28: gone, a thin pink scar across the back of the hand. Ch 31: the scar in the hall lights.
- **How it is written:** one detail among several. **Never in close-up.** No POV character dwells on it for more than a beat, and each one explains it away ("Lots of people cut their hands").
- **How he got the cut stays open.** The injury was real. The server-rack story may be true, or not. The Ch 9 "Tuesday" is a tiny slip that is never highlighted. See the Continuity Traps for the timing problem with "cut at the scene".

## The Alibi Seeding
- **Ch 25:** asked "for completeness" to state his movements, he calmly presents dozens of statements from colleagues in his **Systems Architecture office** who remember him **at his desk from 13:30 until the Ministry's call at 15:20**. Nobody questions it. The reader's suspicion wavers. Kovač alone notices that his office is **in the Ministry district, inside the seeded node's range**, and files it.
- **Ch 28:** Kira asks Maya where Wei was when she found the gap. Maya: "He wasn't there. He was appointed at 15:20 and *arrived* at 15:32. I remember because I timed it." Kira: "His alibi says he was at his desk in the Ministry district from 13:30." Maya: "…Inside the seeded district." **The alibi was written too.**
- **Why it matters:** the witnesses who clear him are exactly as reliable as the forty who accused Marcus. Seeding his own colleagues is the same crime, done quietly, in his favour.
- **The proof stays fragile** (Ch 28 note): the signature is Kira's reading, the author cache "can be called forged", and Maya's timing is one person's memory.

## Voice Rules
These apply to every book until he is exposed.

1. **No POV in Books 1–5, except the Epilogue coda.** He is only ever seen from outside: by Maya, Nikolai, Marcus, Kira, Kovač and Dmitri. The narration never enters his head, never gives his thoughts in italics, and never lets a POV character read his mind correctly.
2. **His kindness is real.** He is patient, fair, precise, and generous with credit ("You found the gap first. Walk me through it."). He never sneers, never threatens, and never gloats. Book 5 contains no villain speech from him. The reconstruction in Ch 29 is Marcus's, not his.
3. **He says true things that have a second meaning.** Every line should read as decency on a first pass and as the design on a second:
   - "Everything that happened in that hour… is now legally *nothing*. We have to rebuild it from whatever survived." A beat. "Or from whoever." (Ch 1)
   - "I don't believe you did it, Mr. Chen. That isn't the same as being able to prove you didn't. Help me prove it." (Ch 10)
   - "You're my only honest suspect. I need you alive." (plot, Ch 18)
   - "I'm not going to let them empty you before I can prove it." (Ch 18)
4. **He notices people, and says so.** He knows everyone's name and gets Pieter's right the first time. "I want people who notice." He reads Marcus's cell wall without asking: "You keep your own record." A pause. "Good."
5. **Dry, light jokes, never cruel ones.** "Another day in paradise" is an old office joke, said like one. "Your file's very thin, Pieter. I like that in a man." "Arithmetic for young citizens. Charming."
6. **His menace is only ever excess:** a glance at Maya's screen a fraction too long; knowing where a page was "dropped"; asking whether Marcus and Kira are "sleeping well". Never more than one such beat per scene.
7. **When he is outplayed, he is pleased.** "You remember," pleased and genuinely delighted. His smile holds a beat too long. He inclines his head "the way you acknowledge a good move" and walks away. He never loses his temper on the page.
8. **The journal voice** (Epilogue coda, and later books): a private hand, dated entries, short declarative sentences with no flourish, the same register as the manuscript he inherited. It is the only place he admits surprise: "That was not in the design."
9. **Physical shorthand:** tall, graying temples, quiet voice, right-handed, light on his feet. Describe his walk neutrally, or through Dmitri. Dmitri is the only character who reads it.

### The Epilogue journal (Book 5)
In a private hand, "the journal a later book will expose". The summary wording is canon:
> *"Nov 15, 2107. The Doctrine is law. The reformers are grateful, and trusted. Chen knows, and says nothing; he has begun keeping records of his own, with many hands, all checking each other. That was not in the design. Neither was the old man. I find I want to see what they build."*
- **"Chen"** is **Marcus**. Let the double reading with Chen Zhao stand; never resolve it.
- **"The old man"** is Old Songs. How and when Wei learned what the old man carried is not stated.
- Plot v3.1 has a slightly different wording ("The old man was not in the design either"). Use the summary's.
- This is his **only** interior moment in Book 5. It shows interest, not repentance.

## Continuity Traps
- **He is 45, not 30.** Born about 2061. Old enough to have been prepared as an heir from childhood, and far too young to be an Architect.
- **Never exposed, arrested or accused in public in Book 5.** Three people know. The Commission keeps him.
- **Marcus's hands are unmarked.** The bandage is Wei's. The witnesses' gray is seeded; the hand is real.
- **Left hand, and he is right-handed.** He writes and lifts cups with his right.
- **Bandage timeline:** on by 14:20–14:21 on Nov 15 (the witnesses and Dmitri see it as he goes in); "clean white" at 15:32; smaller on Nov 17; still on Dec 13; gone with a thin pink scar by Jan 8.
- **Marcus never recognises his face.** Continuity took it. The recognition is "without content", only the phrase. Even in Ch 31 Marcus does not remember him: "No. I checked." Don't let Marcus have a flash of memory later that undoes this.
- **Desk numbers:** Marcus 14-08, Wei 14-09. Fourteenth floor, Regional Factorepo. **2102–2105.** Roster title: *Historian (secondment from Systems Architecture)*.
- **Two different gray coats:** the man in the good gray coat who counts the Volkovs (Ch 19–20) is Wei or his watcher. The woman in the gray coat with reading glasses on a cord (Epilogue) is Singh.
- **He is not the author of the manuscript** and he cannot know Principle Seven.
