## At a Glance
- **Who they are:** the twelve contributors who **signed the Register** of *The Foundation of Certainty* in 2066, marks **·01 to ·12**. Seventeen contributed; five are struck through. The Compiler, **Dr. Elise Vance**, did **not** sign and is not an Architect.
- **When:** the ideas appeared as anonymous essays in the *Journal of Post-Democratic Governance* (2062–2066). Vance began compiling in winter 2064. Thirteen copies were typed in early 2066, around the time The Judge was activated.
- **Why:** they had lived through the **Fracture Years** (the collapse of shared reality in the 2040s–50s) and believed humanity had to be governed through its record, or it would destroy itself. *"Whoever holds the record holds the present. Whoever holds the present will not be asked about the past."*
- **How they last:** the Annex's succession clause, *"Each contributor shall prepare an heir."* A 2066 design is still being run in 2106 by the Architects who are left and by their heirs. Thomas Wei is the first heir the reader meets.
- **Status after Book 5:** unknown to every character. Nobody in Books 1–5 learns who wrote the manuscript. Book 5 establishes only that **it exists, it is old, and the present is following it.** One Architect, Chen Zhao, is on the page as a Minister.
- **Who knows the name "Architects":** nobody in Books 1–5. It is the series' name for them, used from Book 9.

## Who They Are
They are not villains in their own minds and must never be written that way. They are scholars, engineers and politicians who watched the world tear itself apart over what had happened, and wrote a calm, lucid, humane-sounding manual for making people agree. *It is not a manifesto. It is a manual.* Almost nothing in the Histologic world happened by accident: the nations, their borders, the fact storms, correction, the enemies outside, and even the routes by which reformers would try to fix the system were anticipated on their pages. Their tragedy, and the series' answer to them, is that a design this complete cannot be reformed from inside. It can only be replaced "by many hands".

### The Register of Contributors (p. 39, signed 2066)
- **Seventeen marks, each beside a name.** Twelve are signed: the Architects. Five are **struck through**, unsigned and without copy numerals.
- **Marks** are a two-letter cipher and a number (*CZ·11*). Every principle ends with its author's mark. The Register is the only key to the marks.
- **Copy numerals** run I–XII for the signatories; the copy numeral matches the mark number (page 30 carries *CZ·11* and is from copy *XI*, Chen Zhao's). The Compiler's copy has none.
- **Anselm Kerr**, the senior archivist who mentored Mikhail Petrov, holds one struck-through mark. The other four are unnamed.
- **Revealed:** Book 9. It is "the single most dangerous page in the world."

### The heirs principle
- The Implementation Annex: *"Each contributor shall prepare an heir."*
- It explains why the Architects are **families**, and why a conspiracy signed in 2066 is still active in 2106.
- **Canon heir:** **Thomas Wei** (45), son of Dr. Wei Zhang (WZ·10). He erased the hour.
- **Book 9** reveals the heirs as a group (SERIES-OVERVIEW §4: "the Architects' families are connected to the heroes"). Heirs other than Thomas are not named yet.

## The Twelve
Each entry: mark, role, principle, link to the heroes, and philosophy and voice where the old file has usable material. **Old-file lines are provisional** and are for Books 7–9 only, when the Architects can speak on the page. Their manuscript voices are fixed by Foundation §8: calm, lucid, humane-sounding and terrifying; short declarative sentences with no flourishes.

### HV·01 — The Visionary: Dr. Hedda Voss
- **Principle:** **One, The Necessity of Singular Truth** (pp. 5–8). *"Singular truth, mechanically verified, universally enforced. All other principles derive from this necessity. Democracy failed because it assumed humans could collectively determine what happened. We remove the assumption."*
- **Role:** the leader. Explains the factorepo and The Judge, and why doubt is treated as illness (Stories 01–02).
- **Link to the heroes:** none.
- **Name:** **renamed** from "Helena Voss" (Sept 2026) to avoid a clash with Helena Kovač.
- **Philosophy and voice (old file, usable):** brilliant, unrepentant, cold logic, charismatic. *"We saved humanity from itself."* *"You think revealing us changes anything? Humans need enemies. We just provided them."* *"We didn't fail. We succeeded. You just changed the enemy."* Replace the old origin ("after World War III") with the Fracture Years.
- **Revealed:** Principle One in Book 7; name in Book 9.

### YP·02 — The Scientist: Dr. Yuri Petrov
- **Principle:** **Three, Strategic Ambiguity Through Backup Control** (pp. 13–17), and co-author of the **Annex** (which specifies The Judge's architecture, including its **deliberate weaknesses**). *"Maintain three backup systems that appear independent and are not… Ambiguity, controlled, is both defence and instrument."*
- **Explains:** how a fact can be deleted (Kira), an index adjusted (Marcus's 0.58→0.73), and an hour erased (Book 5).
- **Link to the heroes:** none. **Not related to Mikhail "Old Songs" Petrov**; Petrov is a common surname. Say so on the page if they ever meet, or rename.
- **Philosophy and voice (old file, usable):** remorseless, technical, sees humans as variables. *"A perfect system cannot be controlled. An imperfect system can."* *"We made it corruptible on purpose. That was the point."*
- **Dropped:** the old "Director Yuri Petrov", his son (a reused first name). His heir, if needed, is unnamed.
- **Revealed:** Principle Three in Book 6; name in Book 9.

### LW·03 — The Financier: Chairman Liu Wei
- **Principle:** none. He **funded** the design.
- **Link to the heroes:** none.
- **Name note:** "Wei" is his given name. He is **not related** to Thomas Wei or Wei Zhang. Keep him away from their scenes, or say so.
- **Philosophy and voice (old file, usable):** cold, transactional, no ideology. *"I don't choose sides. I fund all sides. That way, I always win."* *"Money doesn't care about ideology. Neither do I."* Drop the old "can crash any economy at will"; it is too large for the world's scale.
- **Revealed:** Book 9.

### SO·04 — The Diplomat: Ambassador Sarah Okonkwo
- **Principle:** **Two, Legitimacy Through Controlled Opposition** (pp. 9–12). *"…Let there be several Histologic nations, differing in method and agreeing in substance, and let there be enemies outside them all. Peace would raise questions. Total war would end the system. Controlled conflict answers every question before it is asked."*
- **Explains:** Veridica, Chronos, Memoria, Axiom and Synthesis as variations on one design; the SFW and CNT as "enemies".
- **Link to the heroes:** **Isaiah Okonkwo's aunt.** Book 4, Ch 8 (drafted): in Isaiah's memory palace, "Aunt Sarah at the head of the table, sharp and elegant, already talking about the diplomatic service." Her parlour stays innocent-seeming until Book 9.

We maintained them. There's a difference."* *"I gave you your career, Isaiah. Every connection, every source."* (Use this last one only if Book 9 decides she really did.)
- **Revealed:** Principle Two in Book 7; name in Book 9.

## Voice Rules (For Every Architect)
1. **Never moustache-twirling.** They believe they saved humanity. Write them as calm, lucid and humane-sounding.
2. **Short declarative sentences.** No rhetorical flourishes, in the manuscript or in speech.
3. **Each voice differs slightly:** Chen Zhao epigrammatic, Wei Zhang clinical, Vance precise with a hidden tremor. Give each of the others one distinguishing habit when their book comes.
4. **Their power is procedural,** exercised by signing, appointing, scheduling and funding. None of them does violence on the page.
5. **Before Book 9 they are never named as Architects.** Characters may guess, but not know (Foundation §8.4).
