## At a Glance
- **Name:** Dr. Reeves. **No first name is given** in the drafted chapters or the summaries. Leave it unstated. If one is ever needed, check the taken-names list first.
- **Age:** **54** in August 2106 ("There are eleven of you and one of me, and I'm fifty-four", Book 4, Ch 24). Born about 2052. Book 5 still treats him as 54.
- **Occupation:** head of **Project Continuity**, Ashford Correctional Facility, Wing C (Book 4). After the escape, working unofficially in a **Ministry research annex** in the capital's north district (Book 5).
- **Status after Book 4:** missing. "Reeves has vanished. Nobody will say where" (Ch 29). Continuity is officially suspended.
- **Status after Book 5:** **in prison**, convicted of Singh's murder on his own seeded confession, and also held for his Continuity crimes, which are real.
- **Appearance** (Book 4, Ch 18, his first appearance in person): "in his fifties. Medium height. Thinning brown hair, neatly cut. A gray suit, not a white coat, well made and unremarkable. A pleasant, even-featured face that you would forget in a crowd. He wore no jewellery. His hands were very clean." His eyes are the only thing that stays with you: **light brown, and *interested***, "the way a man's eyes are interested in a book he has been looking forward to reading." He sits, crosses his legs and folds his clean hands on his knee. In Book 5 he is thinner and unshaven.
- **Family:** his daughter **Maya Reeves** (24 in 2106), a junior factorepo technician. Estranged; her parents divorced when she was ten.

## Who He Is
Reeves is the scientist who built "erase, then seed". He is not cruel in any way a report could name. He is courteous, candid and endlessly curious, the most certain man Tanaka has ever met, and certain of something other than Veridica. He emptied Marcus, arranged the room where Marcus and Kira found each other, ran the border storms as a laboratory, and admits to killing people without pretending otherwise. In Book 5 his own method is turned on him: someone uses his cradles, his code comment and his reputation, and writes a murder into his memory. The man who invented seeding confesses sincerely to a crime he did not commit, and believes every word.

## Voice Rules
1. **Courteous, conversational, unhurried.** He speaks like a colleague showing a visitor round. "Good morning, Mr. Chen." "May I call you Nikolai?"
2. **His favourite word is "interesting".** Curiosity is his whole temperament. When Kira's sessions go well, "Reeves's voice says 'interesting' less often, which frightens her more" (Book 4, Ch 10).
3. **Candid about the worst things.** "You killed people." "Yes. I'm not going to pretend I didn't." He never justifies himself by patriotism.
4. **Tender, and that is the horror.** "I was so proud of you." "Oh, Marcus. I already have." The kindest smile Kira has ever seen.
5. **He laughs softly** when he is caught out or when something surprises him ("A pleasant, delighted laugh", Ch 21). He never raises his voice.
6. **He is precise about method and dismissive of crude work.** "It's crude. It's a *vandal's* use of my work."
7. **Only a voice for most of Book 4.** His face is first seen in Ch 18. Keep the voice behind the mirror as his signature: pleasant, just behind you.

### Books 6–9 (provisional; to be rewritten)
- He stays in prison for as long as the Doctrine stands. Exposing Wei would void the Doctrine and free him. Every later book that threatens the Doctrine threatens to release him, and that should be felt.
- **The seeded memory is permanent evidence.** He is the living proof that seeding works: its inventor carries a murder he didn't commit. If the heroes ever need a demonstration that testimony can be written, he is it, and he would find that interesting.
- **"I know who writes like me now."** He recognised his imitator. He could name Wei, or point to the signature, in a later book. Decide whether he ever does, and at what price.
- **Maya's visits** continue into the Nikolai–Maya storyline.
- **Not an Architect.** He was born about 2052. Whether he is anyone's heir, or simply the believer the design hired, is open. The Book 4 summary calls him "a possible Architects link"; the current plan treats him as the instrument of Principle Four (WZ·10) and Principle Five (JM·08), not an author.
- **"Anna Reeves"** in the old Book 6 plot (a civilian journal-keeper) is not canon and is not related. If that character is kept, both names must change: Anna is too close to Anya, and Reeves is his surname.

## Continuity Traps
- **No first name.** Never "Dr. X Reeves" unless it is chosen deliberately and checked.
- **54**, medium height, thinning brown hair, gray suit, light brown eyes, **clean hands**. In Book 5 his hands are unmarked ("His hands were fine", Ch 23). The bandage is Wei's.
- **He did not seed the forty witnesses** and did not kill Singh. His Book 4 crimes are real: the storms, 2471, Marcus, Kira, the triple cradle.
- **"I stole her fact" (Book 4) and "I asked someone… to delete" (Book 5) are the same act.** He commissioned it; the factorepo work was Wei's.
- **He knew the escapees were alive** (Book 4, Ch 27) and it made no difference: his word could not stand against a certified Registry entry. Don't have him expose Kovač's certification later without addressing this.
- **The confession is seeded in the Ministry medical wing** (v3.2 refinement 6), not in the annex. The annex night sessions are a separate, open question.
- **"The sermon":** he "only ever had the one page too". Which page is not stated. Continuity's aim paraphrases Principle Four, and he knew it (Foundation §4.6), so if the page is ever named, Principle Four is the natural choice. He must not know the manuscript's name or authors in Book 5.
- **Maya took his surname out of spite** after the divorce (Ch 14 summary). The wording is odd; keep it, or clarify when drafting.
- **Twist 1 must stay unresolved.** The R-2 file proves he arranged the conditions. Nothing proves he caused the feeling. Never let a later book settle it in his favour.

### Source conflicts to settle when drafting
- **Singh's second file:** Ch 15 (Pell) says it was opened "on you", meaning Kovač. Ch 23 calls it "a second file, on Ashford". They can be the same file (Kovač is Ashford's Director), but say so.
