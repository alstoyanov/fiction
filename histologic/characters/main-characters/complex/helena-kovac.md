## At a Glance
- **Full name:** Helena Kovač. Staff and inmates call her "the Director". Only Tanaka calls her Helena.
- **Born:** 2054. She is **52** in 2106 (Book 4 Ch 18 summary; SERIES-OVERVIEW). The drafted text gives "in her early fifties".
- **Occupation:** Director of Ashford Correctional Facility, on the eastern edge of the capital, for **eleven years** as of 2106 (so since about 2095). Ashford is mostly an ordinary correction facility; Wing C was Project Continuity's.
- **Status after Book 4:** still Director, under a Ministry inquiry. She certified the escapees dead.
- **Status after Book 5:** she has admitted from the stand at the Lost Hour Hearing (Ch 25) that she falsified the death certificates. What that costs her is not settled in Book 5. She is at the dilemma meeting in Ch 30 and does not appear in the Epilogue.
- **Appearance (Book 4 Ch 18–19):** thin, precise face; dark hair going gray, pulled back hard; deep lines at the corners of the mouth; very tired eyes behind **rimless glasses**. A plain dark suit, and a dark coat over it when she goes down to the basement. She stands by doors with her **hands in her coat pockets**. "A woman who had not slept properly in a long time and had stopped expecting to."
- **Her office:** small. A desk, a window onto the pond with the fountain, two chairs, a shelf of files, one slightly overwatered potted plant. A panel on the desk turns off the room's recording hum.

## Who She Is
Kovač believed in correction for most of her career, and she still believes it heals some people: "There are patients downstairs right now who came in unable to sleep for the nightmares, and who will go home able to sleep. That's real." For four years before Book 4 she kept a paper folder of the patients the protocols went too far with, "because on paper nobody can adjust them", and did nothing else. When she turns, she does it the only way she knows: through signatures, work orders, rosters and procedure. She is not a rescuer and not a jailer. Kira's verdict (Book 4 Ch 19) is the key to her: "a woman who had spent eleven years in charge of a building that had quietly become a prison around her, and she was as trapped in it as any of them."

## Voice Rules
1. **She briefs; she doesn't make speeches.** Flat, dry, in order, "the flat, dry voice of a woman briefing a committee" (Ch 19). Even her fear is delivered as an item on an agenda: "Reeves has backing much higher than me. I don't know how much higher. That frightens me."
2. **Her humour is aimed at herself.** "I kept a folder. That's what I did. I kept a folder." She laughs "once, without any humour at all."
3. **She has two voices, and the switch is a tell.** Privately, low and exact. The moment the recording comes back on, "brisk and administrative": "Thank you, Mr. Chen. Your stability interview is complete. You may go." Use the switch whenever she is being watched.
4. **She asks, she doesn't promise.** Her offer to Marcus is a question: "If there were a way out, could you all be ready by August?"
5. **She knows exactly what the forms say.** "Cognitive failure during a fact storm. Remains processed according to standard protocols. No family viewing. I've signed that form a hundred times." Her weapon is procedure: work orders ("AUTHORISED: H. KOVAČ"), travel orders, overrides, a routine quarterly data channel.
6. **Titles and surnames.** "Mr. Chen", "Ms. Osman", "Mr. Okonkwo". First names only with Tanaka, and once, about family: "Adrian."
7. **Small physical business, never big gestures.** Hands in her coat pockets; pushes her glasses up her nose before she decides something; takes them off to rub her eyes; looks at people over them. She does not reach across a desk to take a hand "the way Dr. Moss would have."
8. **Paper over screens.** She trusts what can't be adjusted. This makes her a natural ally of Book 5's written journals, and she should notice the echo.

## Continuity Traps
- She is Adrian's **aunt**, not his cousin. Adrian is her **younger brother's** son. Tobias is her **uncle**, not her brother.
- She turns in **Ch 18** (June 4, 2106), not earlier. She had kept the folder for four years without acting.
- She **did not know about R-2**. She never claims to have arranged Marcus and Kira.
- During the escape she is **alone in B-4**, keeping the shielding down by re-entering the override every four seconds. She is not at the gate, the cells or the vehicles. She speaks only over the intercom.
- She certified **ten** deaths (six subjects, Old Songs, Morrison, Hanna Marsh, Pavel Ionescu), and one survivor. The plan in Ch 22 speaks of eight because Hanna and Pavel joined later. Book 5 Ch 15 has Pell say "six", meaning the subjects.
- Eleven years as Director in 2106. Four years of the folder. Six years of silence with Adrian.
- She stays **Director through Book 4 and into Book 5**, under inquiry. The inquiry is chaired by **Halvorsen**.
- In Book 5 she is a **red herring, not a culprit**. The 14:30 slot was offered to her office by Singh's scheduling system on Nov 11 at 09:12.
- She never shows Chen Zhao the torn page.
- She is the one who brings the **original** page out of Singh's safe in Ch 29. Until then everyone has worked from Singh's photograph. (How she gets into a sealed safe needs a line when Ch 29 is drafted.)
- The Story 03 / Book 4 Ch 5 border-facility "director" is someone else.
- The look is clinical: rimless glasses, plain dark suit, dark coat. No uniforms, no insignia, nothing ornate.
