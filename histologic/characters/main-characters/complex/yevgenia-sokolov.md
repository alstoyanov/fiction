## At a Glance
- **Full name:** Captain Yevgenia Sokolov, Border Security, Veridica. There is **one** Sokolov officer; the old "Captain Sokolov (different from General Yevgenia)" never existed.
- **Age:** the drafted Book 4 calls her **thirty-five** both in December 2105 (Ch 5) and on Aug 2, 2106 (Ch 26). That fixes her birthday between early August and December, so she was **born in the second half of 2070**:
  - Story 03 (Nov 2104): **33 or 34**. (The old file's "35 in Story 03" is wrong.)
  - Book 4 (Oct 2105 – Aug 2106): **35**.
  - Book 5 (Nov 2106 – Jan 2107): **35 or 36**, matching SERIES-OVERVIEW's "35–36 in 2106".
  - Book 6 (2108): 37–38. Book 9 (~2115): 44–45.
- **Occupation:** captain in Border Security. She commands units in the Lake Erie border zone and runs the **fact-storm test programme** (vehicle-mounted transmitter units that broadcast a false history of a district over the true one). Dmitri worked under her for four years.
- **Status after Book 4:** in post. Nobody knows she let the escapees through the lakeshore checkpoint, except the people in the cars and her own people at the barrier.
- **Status after Book 5:** in post. She has contradicted her own Ministry twice in public (Ch 17 and 25). Still a captain.
- **Appearance:** tall. Gray uniform with a captain's insignia, a long dark coat over it. "The hard, fine-boned face of someone who had been promoted young and had never once been allowed to look it" (Book 4 Ch 5 and 26). Stands with her hands in her coat pockets. Carries a radio.
- **Mother:** **Chancellor Viktoria Sokolov**, the Architect **VS·09** (the Administrator; the Implementation Annex). Nobody in Books 1–5 knows this.

## Who She Is
In Story 03 she is a believer who knows the storms are manufactured and says so without embarrassment: "I'm a patriot. I serve Veridica." Over two books she watches the three brothers she arrested refuse to be erased, and something in her moves that she never names. Her mercy, when it comes, is always procedural and deniable: a radio call, a lawful escort, a paper log, a sealed note. She is the series' clearest case of a person who changes without confessing it, and the writing must never explain her from inside.

## Voice Rules
1. **Formal, flat, short.** She speaks in the register of an order or a report: "Agent Volkov. Step away from the outlaws." "Sokolov. Lakeshore checkpoint. No contact. I repeat, no contact. Checkpoint clear."
2. **Her real sentence comes last, quietly, on the way out.** This is her signature. The formal line, then a pause, then one low line nobody is sure they heard: "Or the solution." "Write carefully, Mr. Okonkwo." "Nothing that is relevant to Mr. Okonkwo." Keep this pattern in every book.
3. **She never explains herself.** No interior monologue, no confession, no speech about conscience. Other characters read her (Dmitri: "never once known what she was thinking") and get it wrong or only half right.
4. **Mercy is always deniable.** It takes the form of something she was entitled to do anyway: clear a checkpoint, carry out a lawful escort, testify to what she saw, keep a paper log.
5. **In Story 03 she can be cold and even smile at cruelty** ("If necessary."). Don't soften that retroactively. The change is slow and never announced.
6. **She keeps records the Ministry doesn't control.** Her paper log of the 14:02 confirmation (Book 5 Ch 17) is in character: an officer who writes things down.

## Continuity Traps
- **One Sokolov.** Captain through Book 5. Don't call her General before Book 7.
- Born in the second half of 2070. **Thirty-five** throughout Book 4. Don't give her a precise birthday unless it falls between August and December.
- Her mother is **Viktoria**, never Elena.
- **She did not personally run the ~2090 test that killed Ivan.** She was about 20. Dmitri's "She ran the programme that killed our father" (Book 4 Ch 20) is true of the programme and his belief about her; Story 03's "helped us refine the protocols" is institutional.
- **Storm units are vehicle-mounted transmitters**, and storms are perceptual. Nothing she does changes the weather. The fog is real fog.
- At the Ch 26 checkpoint **Alexei is driving**, and she does not know which brother she is looking at. (The Ch 26 summary's "she looks at Dmitri's face" is superseded by the draft.)
- She had read on her screen that all three were dead, "and come anyway".
- In Book 5 she is **outside the seeded district** at 14:30. Her memory of the hour is clean, and her paper log is real.
- **Isaiah crosses into Chronos.** Veridica and the SFW share no border.
- She never mentions August in Book 5, not even obliquely.
- Keep her unreadable. No POV chapters through Book 5. If she ever gets one in a later book, it should be one only.
