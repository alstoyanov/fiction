## At a Glance
- **Full name:** Dr. Amara Singh. Signs her notes *A.S.* (Her doctorate's field is not stated anywhere; leave it unstated or choose one that fits a career Ministry official.)
- **Born:** about 2057. She is **49** on Nov 15, 2106, "about fifty" in the Book 5 Epilogue (Nov 2107), and **about 58** when she returns (~2115).
- **Occupation:** Deputy Minister of Historical Integrity, under Minister Chen Zhao. Her office is on the **twelfth floor** of the Ministry. Her aide is **Lucian Bell**. Her Ministry sponsored Project Continuity (`worldbuilding/core-system.md` §9).
- **Status after Book 5:** officially **murdered** at about 14:23 on Nov 15, 2106, during the lost hour. Dr. Reeves is convicted of it on his own (seeded) confession. **In truth her death was staged.** Nobody in Book 5 can prove it, and the book only hints at it.
- **Appearance:** precise; sleeves rolled up at her desk; **reading glasses on a cord**. In the Epilogue: a woman of about fifty in a **gray coat**, reading glasses on a cord, watching Marcus from the edge of the plaza.

## Who She Is
On the page for one chapter only, she has to be liked at once and understood never. The Prologue gives a career official who read Isaiah's August exposé three times and opened an internal inquiry into Continuity the next morning, whose colleagues think she's naive and who has stopped caring what they think. She believes in the record's procedures ("If it's in our record, The Judge must read it") and uses them to open a door for a dead man. Then the book shows that **every operation steered into the hour carries her credentials**. She is either the victim whose identity was used to build the trap, or one of its builders. **Book 5 does not answer this.** Everything written about her must keep both readings alive until Book 9.

## Voice Rules (Prologue, and her return)
1. **Precise, self-checking, privately wry.** She notices a small wrongness (the "confirmed" 14:30, the calendar entries) and argues herself out of it: *overwork*. The reader should see her intelligence and her blind spot in the same paragraph.
2. **A procedural idealist.** Her faith is in process: "If it's in our record, The Judge must read it." She doesn't make speeches about truth; she arranges passes.
3. **Terse notes, initials.** *"Have you seen this before? A.S."* *"Who restores it? Watch who restores it."* Her written voice is questions.
4. **Every Singh line must read two ways.** "She has begun to suspect who in this building believes that sentence" works whether she is a whistleblower closing in or an insider who knows her colleagues too well. Test each line against both readings before keeping it.
5. **The glasses on a cord are her mark.** They are the one detail the Epilogue may use. Don't add a second identifying detail there.

### Books 6–9 (provisional; to be rewritten)
- **Books 6–8:** she stays off the page. At most, a glimpse that one of the heroes writes down in a journal. Her absence should be felt as the question Tanaka left open.
- **Book 9, *Architects of Chaos* (~2115):** she **returns**, about 58, **nine years** after the hour (not the old file's "five"). She is the living answer to Twist 2. Book 9 must decide:
  - **Victim, complicit, or both.** Her credentials scheduled the hour. Did someone take them, did she lend them, or did she build the hour and then find she couldn't control it? The strongest option may be "both": she was drawn in, understood too late, and chose to disappear rather than be used again.
  - **Who knocked** at 13:59:58, and what happened when the man with the bandaged hand came in at 14:21.
  - **Who, if anyone, died** in her office. (A body was cremated. Whose?)
  - **Where she has been.** Principle Six's own logic ("disclose it first, in your own words") suggests her return could itself be staged by the design; decide whether it is.
  - **What she brings.** Her testimony about the hour, her own inquiry's papers, possibly more of the Continuity–Ministry correspondence. **She must not bring the Register or the Annex**: by the manuscript schedule those come from Vance's copy (`00-FOUNDATION-MANUSCRIPT.md` §5).
- **Old lines, usable only if Book 9 chooses the victim reading:** "I faked my death because I knew they'd kill me if I spoke. Now I have protection. Now I speak." "I've been hiding for nine years. Time to speak." (the old line said five).

## What the Reader May and May Not Learn in Book 5

**May learn (and should):**
- That she was sympathetic, capable and brave in what she did openly: the inquiry, the pass, the upload.
- That she found page 30 in Continuity's Ministry correspondence and wrote *"Who restores it? Watch who restores it."* in its margin. (Ch 29 reads this as "she suspected", which leaves both readings open.)
- That **every** operation in the hour was steered by her credentials, including a confirmation at 14:02, inside the hour.
- That she had three calendar entries she didn't remember adding, and that her aide can't recall who proposed the 14:30.
- That the identification of her body is weak: cremation within 24 hours, a family no one can find, no face photographs, identification only by seeded witnesses.
- That a woman in a gray coat with reading glasses on a cord was seen a year later. The reader may connect it to the Prologue.

**May not learn:**
- **That she is alive.** It is a hint, never a confirmation. No character states it; Tanaka only writes down what she saw.
- **Whether she was a victim or complicit.**
- Who knocked at 13:59:58. Never show the visitor.
- What happened in her office between 14:00 and 15:00, beyond the reconstruction that Wei went in "whatever happened in there".
- Whether anyone died there, and whose body was cremated.
- Her relationship, if any, to Thomas Wei or Chen Zhao.
- Who wrote page 30, or what *CZ·11* means (Book 9).
- Why she opened a file on Kovač.

## Continuity Traps
- She is **49**, not 48. She returns in **Book 9 (~2115)**, about **58**, not "five years later".
- She is **Deputy** Minister. The Minister is Chen Zhao.
- She is on the page (as POV) **only in the Prologue** in Book 5. The Epilogue glimpse is Tanaka's POV.
- **Never show who knocks.** Never show her body, or her face in the file.
- **The gray-coat woman** (Epilogue) is Singh. The **gray-coat man** who counts to three at the Justice Center steps (Ch 19) and stands across the street from Nikolai (Ch 20) is Wei's watcher, or Wei. Keep them visibly distinct: the woman is identified only by her reading glasses on a cord.
- She found the page **in Continuity's Ministry correspondence**, weeks before Nov 15. She sent Marcus a **photograph**. The **original** stayed in her sealed safe until Kovač brings it out in Ch 29, and the margin note exists only on the original.
- The page's number (30), numeral (XI) and mark (CZ·11) are visible but unreadable in Book 5. Nobody identifies it as Principle Six until Book 6, or decodes the mark before Book 9.
- The 14:02 confirmation comes from **her credentials**, which is not the same as from her.
- Reeves's confession (letter opener, cut flowers) is **seeded**. Don't treat its details as evidence of what happened.
